# Save Woogaroo Forest — observer species-occurrence addendum

## Purpose

This addendum records observer-supplied FrogID/iNaturalist occurrence evidence as a separate Snowball evidence fibre. It must not be collapsed into proponent ecology, expert validation, agency findings or statutory conclusions.

- Capture 948284 — 4:37 pm — Water Body: Stream or creek.

A separate FrogID result screenshot for 948283 shows the observer selection `Adelotus brevis` (Tusked Frog). Until FrogID validation is complete, the correct provenance is:

```text
observer-selected identification
!=
FrogID expert validation
!=
agency occurrence finding
```

The supplied 948284 detail screenshot does not independently show the taxon result. Do not inherit the 948283 taxon identification across captures merely because the locations and times are close.

## Current bounded legal significance

Queensland's current environmental-offset material classifies *Adelotus brevis* / Tusked Frog as NCA class V (Vulnerable). If capture 948283 is later expert-validated, and its exact coordinates are shown to intersect the relevant Opossum/Woogaroo creek or project/corridor object, it may become useful same-landscape occurrence evidence.

That still does not by itself establish:

```text
species occurrence
-> critical habitat
-> significant detrimental effect
-> EPBC controlled matter
-> injunction / refusal
```

Each legal consumer requires its own statutory and factual coordinates.
