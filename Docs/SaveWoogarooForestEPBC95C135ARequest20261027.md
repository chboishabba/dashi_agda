## A. Separate statutory request — EPBC Act s 135A(2)

**Subject: Request under EPBC Act s 135A(2) — section 95C recommendation report — Springfield Residential Development, EPBC 2019/8575**

> To the Secretary of the administering Department,
>
> I request a copy of the recommendation report given to the Minister under section 95C of the *Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999* for the Springfield Residential Development, EPBC referral 2019/8575, approved with conditions on 15 October 2026, pursuant to section 135A(2).
>
> The request is for the original report as provided to the statutory decision-maker, including any attachments, recommended conditions and any corrections or final versions that formed part of the report.
>
> Please identify the report's preparation and transmission dates and its version or document-control identifier. If any material is withheld, please indicate the specific subsection 135A(4) category relied upon and the scope of each omission, and supply all segregable material. Please advise of any charge determined under subsection 135A(2) before incurring it.
>
> As the statutory decision has already been announced, I ask that this request be dealt with promptly. The absence of this report from the public project document display is not represented as evidence that the report was not prepared or considered.
>
> Thank you.

**Why targeted:** Section 95C(1) requires the Secretary's recommendation on approval and conditions. Section 135A(1)(b), (2) establishes a post-decision request route (subject to subsections (3)–(5)); one need not infer the exact report's content from public portal status or comments. Cite the Act rather than a retrospective standard or broad "Freedom of Information" headline. Don't describe this as a guaranteed fully unredacted disclosure.

## C. Structured three-file legal cross-examination once records arrive

| Exact same-action comparison | Required joined original | Questions counsel can test |
|---|---|---|
| **s95B proponent evidence** | full revised PD, comment set and response, species/offset/cumulative appendices | Was Item 4.6(c) actually answered with functional rather than mapped-only connectivity? Which competing scientific conclusions were presented? |
| **s95C recommendation** | Secretary's original report, recommended conditions, attachment/version/time | Which controlling species and biodiversity impacts were addressed? Was local/regional cumulative impact incorporated? What scientific uncertainties or residual matters were expressed? |
| **Part 9 signed decision** | dated approval, signatory/delegation, conditions/reasons, parcel plans, subsequent changes | Did required s136/s139 considerations enter the decision? Which conditions respond to actual impacts, offsets and the recommended conditions? |

Section **136(1)(a) and (b)** mandates both protected environmental considerations and economic/social matters; housing benefit as a factor is not itself unlawful influence. Section **136(2)(bc)** requires relevant section 95B and 95C documents as applicable to preliminary-documentation assessment. Any purported legal error requires the *actual facts and law*, not a documentary nonappearance on the portal.
