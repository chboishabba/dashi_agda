## Provenance graph and explicit no-equivalence edges

Programmatic DARPA funding is a relationship of **sponsor / project**, not proof that N3, Insect Allies, BETR, HI-MEMS and RadioBio constitute one system. A remote radio command to **an insect backpack** is not field-mediated neural communication in a human. Optically read **tissue oxygen** is not directly read neural intent or voltage. A skin-stimulation **clinical pain endpoint** is not regeneration. Delivering modified **plant viruses through insect vectors** is a biochemical/gene-delivery mechanism, not a bioelectric transmitter.

For every claim, keep separate records: original programme identifier; original device or laboratory model; actual organism/tissue; transmitter/receiver apparatus; stimulation/sensing direction; signal/energy modality; physical observation; measured endpoint and negative controls; source date and date of later marketing/interpretation; regulatory jurisdiction and device generation; independent replication or sham control. Grant- or participant-level overlap does not constitute same manifestation, same treatment or same mechanism.

## Exact high-value P1 boundaries and source-specific next gates

- **N3:** original six performer reports, independently verified channels, spatial and temporal resolution, signal-to-noise, route of administration (noninvasive versus injectables), measured read/write accuracy and human-safety/ethics oversight. The specified 16/16 mm³/50 ms is a target until an explicit trial/hardware receipt passes.
- **Profusa:** NCT04514861 actual regulatory record, PAD participant flow and ground truth against transcutaneous pO2, sensor drift, calibration, adverse events, reader optical power and device-specific regulatory documents. **Single tissue oxygen measurement cannot identify both oxygen delivery and oxygen consumption independently** without additional physiological observations.
- **SCENAR:** source-specific active versus sham effect, trial-arm accounting, pre-registered outcomes, device pulse parameters and reproducibility. Carry **null active-vs-sham** in the 2021 pain trial alongside other experiments; never aggregate dissimilar indications without a defendable statistical model.
- **HI-MEMS:** animal welfare, actual stimulation site and RF receiver, trial-level turn probability/trajectory, source power, GPS-versus-radio interface and negative command controls. Remote command exists via conventional electronics, not direct ambient field-to-brain transfer.
- **Insect Allies:** plant-virus vector identity, crop target, environmental containment, controlled trait expression and independent biosafety risk; avoid labelling it bioelectric or assuming field control.
- **Cross-programme BETR contact:** a proposed *engineering architecture* combines independent sensing, actuation and tissue outcome. Its full-state observability requires independent physiological readouts, actuation and actual healing measurements in a common device with a common experimental timeline. A conceptual connection is not evidence that such an integrated device was deployed by these programmes.
