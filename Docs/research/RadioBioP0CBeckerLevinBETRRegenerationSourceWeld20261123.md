## Original source identities and bounded evidence

- **Becker 1972 mammal DC stimulation:** Robert O. Becker, *Stimulation of Partial Limb Regeneration in Rats*, *Nature* **235**, 109–111 (1972), DOI [10.1038/235109a0](https://doi.org/10.1038/235109a0), [PubMed 4550399](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/4550399/). Further original Becker and Joseph A. Spadaro, *Electrical stimulation of partial limb regeneration in mammals*, *Bulletin of the New York Academy of Medicine* **48**, 627–641 (1972), [original full text PMCID PMC1806700](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1806700/). This is **reported partial limb regeneration in rats after low-voltage DC**; not established human full-limb regrowth, a generic electromagnetic cure or a complete functional restoration.
- **Becker later independent scrutiny:** *Effects of electrical stimulation on rat limb regeneration, a new look at an old model* (2016), *Scientific Reports*, [original full text PMCID PMC4683620](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4683620/). Distinguish independent reenactment, experimental organism, amputation site, stimulation, negative controls and resulting histology; it is not automatically the exact same intervention or a successful human trial. Exact quantitative endpoint and experimental arm counts remain to be extracted from source tables before claiming a measured cross-study effect.
- **Murugan et al. 2022 frog BioDome:** Nirosha J. Murugan, Hannah J. Vigran, Kelsie A. Miller, Annie Golding, Quang L. Pham, Megan M. Sperry, Cody Rasmussen-Ivey, Anna W. Kane, David L. Kaplan, Michael Levin, *Acute multidrug delivery via a wearable bioreactor facilitates long-term limb regeneration and functional recovery in adult Xenopus laevis*, *Science Advances* **8**, eabj2164 (2022), DOI [10.1126/sciadv.abj2164](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.abj2164), [PMCID PMC8791464](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8791464/). Published comparison includes **no device**, **BioDome only**, and **BioDome with five-drug treatment**; drug contents include BDNF, growth hormone, 1,4-DPCA, retinoic acid and resolvin D5. A **one-day** local treatment preceded long-term regenerative outcomes in frogs. **This is not simply an electrical-stimulation experiment** and not a clinical demonstration in humans. Any source figures, histology, motor outcomes, sample sizes and follow-up intervals must be independently extracted with their original definitions before a quantitative reproduction claim.
- **Levin 2021 mechanistic review:** Michael Levin, *Bioelectric signaling: Reprogrammable circuits underlying embryogenesis, regeneration, and cancer*, *Cell* **184** (2021), DOI [10.1016/j.cell.2021.02.034](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2021.02.034), [PubMed 33826908](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33826908/). It surveys endogenous ion channels, gap junctions, transmembrane voltage patterns, signalling and morphogenesis. It is a **review**; its figures and referenced primary studies must not be misrepresented as new experiments by Levin in this article.
- **DARPA BETR 2019 programme goals:** [official BETR page](https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/bioelectronics-for-tissue-regeneration) and [2019 announcement](https://www.darpa.mil/news/2019/intelligent-healing-complex-wounds); original solicitation **HR001119S0027**. Proposed sensing of changing wound physiology, biochemical/biophysical actuation and adaptive feedback to improve wound healing, especially skin/muscle/nerve injuries. This is **research programme intent**, not demonstration of a marketed human regeneration device. Named BETR contractors/outcomes, FDA clearances, trial registry IDs and patient measures require separate original evidence before promotion.

## Completed P0-C synthetic exact inference

[New executable SymPy source-audit](../../scripts/radiobio_p0c_regenerative_source_controls_sympy.py) constructs a **hypothetical factorial** with untreated, device only, and device+drug; four possible coefficients are (baseline, device, drug main, device×drug interaction). The actual *three-arm design structure* has design rank **3 of 4**; adding a fourth drug-only arm would give rank **4 of 4**. The exact unidentifiable direction is (0,0,+1,−1): only **drug+interaction given device** is identifiable in the simple contrast. This does not deny that Murugan's experimentally measured three arms show differences; it limits the **separate attribution** of drug main effects from the interaction under this *specific linear factorial decomposition*, absent independent mechanistic assumptions or additional arms.

A complementary **synthetic** RC membrane-voltage system obeys exact implicit-midpoint energy accounting:

    Δ(1/2 C V²) + dt g V_mid² = dt V_mid u.

The finite test has exact residual zero and an unforced decay control. **No relation between this voltage trajectory and tissue morphology was assumed or discovered.** For a proposed BETR feedback system with hidden (membrane potential, tissue defect), a voltage-only *instantaneous* readout has rank 1/2; adding an independent tissue-morphology endpoint makes the direct observation rank 2/2. This is **not** a dynamical observability theorem, clinical proof, nor evidence that two real sensors have noise-free, calibrated independent outputs.

Structural feedback/energy similarity is a reusable *mathematical* pattern only.
