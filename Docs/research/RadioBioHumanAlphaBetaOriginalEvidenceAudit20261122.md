## B. W19 is a direct original funding/analysis link to DARPA RadioBio

The original publication explicitly credits **Human Frontier Science Program HFSP-RGP0054/2014** for its original research, and **DARPA RadioBio grant D17AC00019** to Kirschvink and Shimojo for *more recent analysis of data*, plus **JSPS KAKENHI 18H03500** to Matani. See [original publisher funding and source record](https://www.eneuro.org/content/6/2/ENEURO.0483-18.2019/tab-article-info) and [Caltech institutional publication](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/whbb7-57e98). **A grant supporting analysis does NOT establish that DARPA funded each earlier experiment, that RadioBio observed radiation-mediated cell signalling, or that any implementation was deployed.**

The original investigators published their raw EEG datasets and MATLAB scripts at:
- **Dataset 1:** Kirschvink, *Human Magnetoreception Dataset 1*, CaltechDATA **DOI [10.22002/D1.930](https://doi.org/10.22002/D1.930)**, [source catalogue](https://data.caltech.edu/records/qyhmj-96g11), nominal 2.9 GB; files include analyzeDecDn.m **MD5 a9edb02840112e7b375a43ea116cb94b**, analyzeDecTest.m **MD5 7570e3b33e255bb048cc20505a5b6fb9**, DecDn.Data.01.mat **MD5 93028ab6077e64e6ed92344a90aad472**.
- **Dataset 2:** Kirschvink, *Human Magnetoreception Dataset 2*, CaltechDATA **DOI [10.22002/D1.931](https://doi.org/10.22002/D1.931)**, [source catalogue](https://data.caltech.edu/records/fx074-jee09), nominal 3.1 GB; files include analyzeIncTest.m **MD5 43e334036fe79ad0671991d5899a8949**, Inc.Data.01.mat **MD5 15305bea702a5e5702575d67da2d1c63**; IncSham.Data series also deposited.

## C. Original source denominators and model-specific contrast invariants

**W19 inclination (N=29 participants):** source conditions active SWEEP, active FIXED, sham SWEEP, sham FIXED. Original authors report an active/sham by sweep/fixed interaction, p<.05. Independent *proposed* per-participant contrast for replay:

I_i = (Y_i(active,sweep) - Y_i(active,fixed)) - (Y_i(sham,sweep) - Y_i(sham,fixed)).

**W19 declination (N=26 participants):** downward static inclination with counterclockwise (CCW), clockwise (CW), FIXED horizontal-direction comparisons. Original reports both CCW versus CW and CCW versus FIXED p<.001, while CW was similar to FIXED. Declination **sham is available for only 18/26**, not for all 26; do not impute the other 8. Comparisons: C_i = Y_i(CCW)-Y_i(CW); F_i=Y_i(CCW)-Y_i(FIXED). These are *DASHI proposed arithmetic auditors*, not a claim that source authors published these exact formulas/numerical values. The N=29/N=26 pools may overlap; **55 is not an established number of unique people**.

W19 selected DecDn physical exposure: 0.1-s sweep, total field about **35 µT**, downward vertical **33.8 µT**, horizontal **9.1 µT**; the coils are about 2 m square in 3 axes with double-wrapped active-sham design. EEG alpha ERD is a **post-stimulus response**, not a transmitter's alpha-frequency field. The original paper correlates EEG/fluxgate streams to within ±2 ms at 512 samples/s.

**B26:** sham-mounted coil, 100 volunteers but 98 WAIS-IV analysed, 35 male +65 female counts refer to enrolled total. Post-stimulation 10-s EEG snapshots, alpha ROI left IFG, theta-burst active. Original reports left-IFG high-alpha p=.002 vs sham; no right IFG / bilateral DIPC effects. Raw data **not openly available**, only on reasonable request from authors. Post-hoc and multiple-testing adjustments to be independently audited.

**M26:** original abstract counts **31** participants, but listed sex counts **32**, as do related original 26 GHz exposure studies. Flag a denominator mismatch; do not silently 'correct' it or label deception. Request original exclusion flow and same-cohort membership.

**D26:** 10 Hz sawtooth has higher Fourier harmonics, and power redistribution under a one-subject pre/post intervention is not blind causal evidence. No independent population dose response is established.

## D. Acceptance contract and original-source first execution

P0-A1 **ACQUIRE & REPRODUCE**: download from Caltech directly under its licence, verify per-file MD5, inventory source MATLAB/EEGLAB versions/participant IDs and source code, then replay **source-defined** alpha-ERD with per-participant mixed condition contrasts. Record original hash, immutable source ID, exact contrast, baseline, electrode/time/frequency, sample count, and original vs new computational outputs.

P0-A2 **NO FALSE INDEPENDENCE**: require matched participant IDs in inclination four-cell contrast (N <=29). For declination active CCW/CW/FIXED (N <=26), include 18 or fewer participants when sham comparisons are required; never implicitly treat stimulus trials or recording files as participants. Audit missing cells explicitly rather than pairwise/imputed subject means.

P0-A3 **CONTRADICTORY SIGNALS FIRST**: active-fixed, sham-sweep/fixed, CW, upward-inclination negative controls, coil thermal/acoustic pickup and EEG artefacts (latter *proposed additional audit*, not an assertion originals tested them all). Separate true alpha (8–13/10–12 Hz) and beta (13–30/12–35 Hz) across original-specific definitions.

P0-A4 **ORIGINAL FIELD CHECK**: every claimed physiological link carries exposure carrier, modulation, vector field, slew rate, polarization, measured local E/B, electronics, tissue medium, exact sample point, response measurement and uncertainties. 26 **GHz** RF and 10 **Hz** stimulation cannot be pooled; 8–13 Hz EEG alpha is an observation, not an environmental Schumann source.

P0-A5 **DATA AVAILABILITY DISTINCTION**: W19 raw+code deposited but not yet replayed; B26 original data by author request; M26 original individual data not acquired; D26 one-case public report only. Final experimental closure needs independently repeated observed control contrasts, not agreement of synthetic SymPy expressions.

## E. Explicit mathematical-only test owner

[SymPy contrast and denominator guard](../../scripts/radiobio_alpha_contrast_sympy.py) is a new **synthetic mathematical check**, to be run locally without GitHub CI. It tests rank and contrast independence and rejects missing/sham/improperly pooled participant denominators. It does **not** ingest 6 GB of original data, compute observed neural effects, validate mechanisms or imply independent publication replication.

**Bibliography and provenance:** every original paper, DOI and repository record above; no claim that all 4 research teams tested the same mechanism. This is an audit of **four distinct original studies and a documented DARPA analysis-grant edge**, not four replications of one phenomenon.
