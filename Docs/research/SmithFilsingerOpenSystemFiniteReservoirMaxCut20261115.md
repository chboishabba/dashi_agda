**M2 — Source-versus-controller work proved constructively and numerically.** Model initial reservoir energy 178.605 normalised units (not the measured global atmosphere). **M3 — Delayed FM→2fm conversion near opposition.** A single continuously phase/frequency-modulated carrier entering two non-identical delays has fast-averaged intensity `A²+B²+2AB cos[θ(t)−θ(t−τ)]`. Near `A=B, Δθ≈π+u cos(2π fm t)`, exact series gives `I(t)≈A²u²/4 [1+cos(4π fm t)]`. The 3.915Hz→7.83Hz path follows without positing an independent electrical 3.915Hz oscillator. Verified symbolically and as a high-resolution 7.8kHz illustrative FM input. A *memoryless single-path* quadratic detector applied to pure constant-envelope FM has no such slow harmonic after carrier averaging. **The delayed path or an equivalent dispersive/nonlinear member is a mathematical obligation.** The illustrative phase delay τ≈0.303013s was deliberately chosen near an opposition condition, NOT inferred as Filsinger's electromagnetic flight time from an acoustic response-peak separation. Nonstationary phase, interference and finite response kernels permit intermittent rather than permanent 2:1 locking.

**M4 — Explicit Earth-source *access* scale; separate from its immense global capacity.** Atmospheric global circuit literature gives V≈250kV, typical fair-weather J≈2pA/m², global current ≈1–2kA (250–500MW global throughput), and model-dependent effective Earth-ionosphere C≈0.7F. At V=250kV its global electrostatic storage estimate ≈21.9GJ. A naive concentric ideal 80km vacuum spherical shell instead has C≈0.057F and ≈1.79GJ; these are **different capacitance models**, not a measurement discrepancy to silently reconcile. Under a deliberately generous *full-column DC* assumption, the undisturbed return throughput per horizontal area `VJ=0.5 µW/m²`. For 0.4W/6.7W/1kW hypothetical useful targets, equivalent 100%-collection/zero-loss fair-weather footprints are 0.8/13.4/2000 km². **6.7W in the reel is a reported driver power, not demonstrated natural output.** Nonlinear current diversion or new source supply is possible only if the receiver actually changes the source-boundary solution; these numbers do not bound RF/near-field/galvanic/active sources. They turn the funnel conjecture into the concrete task of proving a large effective source-response cross-section/transport. `physical_source_bounds.py` additionally evaluates a 0.5/2/15m *hypothetical* electrically short open dipole at 7.83Hz and 7.8kHz: direct far-field radiation is tiny for 1 A RMS, without ruling out near-field or conductive Earth coupling.

## 2. Distinct source ports — never collapse to one Earth capacitor

- **Global atmospheric DC:** charged potential + fair-weather return current; nonlinear conductivity/transport, with distinct charging thunderstorm currents and local current divergence.
- **Schumann / lightning ELF modes:** standing/dispersive/lossy EM waveguide fields and local modal response at ~7.8, 14, 20Hz etc. These are not the global DC current and have different extractable power.
- **Local telluric/electrochemical Earth:** potential gradients, available Thevenin impedance, chemical capacity and local current paths.
- **Local RF / induced mains / other fields:** separately resolved radiative/near-field work, scattering and backaction.
- **Driver, time-varying modulator, spark-dump and storage:** independently metered real input and storage changes. Spark gap is an energy-transfer switch, not its own source.
