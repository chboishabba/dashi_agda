## Original-source observations (different experiments)

**E1 — modulated-electrojet ELF (original observed).** R. C. Moore and coauthors, *ELF waves generated by modulated HF heating of the auroral electrojet and observed at a ground distance of ~4400 km*, **Journal of Geophysical Research: Space Physics** (2007), DOI [10.1029/2006JA012063](https://doi.org/10.1029/2006JA012063). Original experimental record: **11 February 2005**, 08:00–12:00 UT, HF heater **3.25 MHz X-mode** directed vertically, **960 kW input**, amplitude-modulated at **575 and 2125 Hz** alternating hourly; receivers at Chistochina (~36 km) and Midway Atoll (**4461 km**). Receiver uses orthogonal ground magnetic loops, RFI suppression and calibrated synchronized digitization, with instrument-level efforts to distinguish received ELF from nonlinear HF demodulation in the receiver. For **2125 Hz**, the authors infer effective ELF radiation **~4–32 W** and **HF-to-ELF conversion ~0.0004–0.0032%**, based on model-informed far-field observations, NOT direct useful receiver terminal power and NOT an all-conditions ceiling. Funding includes HAARP, AFRL, DARPA and ONR, including ONR grants documented in paper. This is original physical propagation evidence, not a biological mechanism.

**E2 — electrojet-current quantitative model (independent source).** *HF modulated ionospheric currents* (2007), DOI [10.1029/2007GL031724](https://doi.org/10.1029/2007GL031724); *100 days of ELF/VLF generation via HF heating with HAARP* (2013), DOI [10.1002/jgra.50558](https://doi.org/10.1002/jgra.50558); *HF beam parameters in ELF/VLF wave generation via modulated heating of the ionosphere* (2012), DOI [10.1029/2012JA017585](https://doi.org/10.1029/2012JA017585). Mechanism: HF-driven changes in lower-ionosphere conductivity modulate **existing natural electrojet current** and produce secondary ELF/VLF in the waveguide. Do not count both an existing naturally generated current's power and the same source as independent extracted energy.

**P1 — SURA upper-hybrid/lower-hybrid/plasma conversion (original observed and interpreted).** A. A. Chernyshov and collaborators, *HF Heating Effects as Seen From Below and Above: First Results of Joint SURA–Ionosfera-M Experiment*, **Geophysical Research Letters** 53 (2026), DOI [10.1029/2026GL123503](https://doi.org/10.1029/2026GL123503). The coordinated SURA and Ionosfera-M2 experiment reports artificial 630 nm red-line airglow around **300–305 km**, a ~**5% total electron content depletion**, pump penetration/field signatures, and bands around **8–9 kHz** and **17–18 kHz** in satellite E/B channels, interpreted as lower-hybrid and harmonic whistler-related coupling. Its authors note background/geomagnetic contributions, conditions for possible O-Z-O conversion and the need for more observations. These are not 7.83 Hz Schumann waves, receiver load work, or a demonstration of 100% conversion.

**P2 — HAARP 2026 structured plasma spectroscopy.** Salman A. Nejad and colleagues, *Lower-Hybrid-Driven Whistler Generation During Active Ionospheric Heating at HAARP*, **Journal of Geophysical Research: Space Physics** 131 (2026), DOI [10.1029/2026JA035171](https://doi.org/10.1029/2026JA035171). Two simultaneous HF beams, one heating and one probing, with source-experiment stepped VLF tones, ELF/VLF receiver and SuperDARN radar. Heat-on enhancements plus radar structuring support a plausible lower-hybrid/whistler path; the observational correlation and proposed mechanism are not an independently calibrated universal nonlinearity. Author-referenced data sources: [SuperDARN](https://superdarn.thayer.dartmouth.edu/data.html), [HAARP transmissions](https://haarp.gi.alaska.edu/transmissions), [HAARP diagnostics](https://haarp.gi.alaska.edu/diagnostic-suite).

## Exact source and observer separation

Define a transport experiment by source *identity*, not frequency alone:

- E1 lower-ionosphere electrojet branch: HF heater envelope **m(t)** -> conductivity perturbation δΣ(x,t) -> perturbation δJ=δΣ E_background + Σ δE -> retarded / waveguide ELF magnetic observation. Natural electrojet J_background must be present and separately metered as source.
- P1/P2 upper-plasma branch: incident HF pump -> upper-hybrid and lower-hybrid candidate daughter modes -> field-aligned irregularity scattering / mode conversion -> whistler VLF electric/magnetic spectra. This branch requires plasma density, temperature, geomagnetic angle, turbulence and gyro/lower-hybrid frequency measurements. A generic fixed-gain linear mixer cannot represent it as proved physics.
- Receiver instrument branch: downconversion in actual plasma and **instrument-induced HF demodulation** are alternative physical models. The 2007 source contains specific RFI suppressor and receiver validation; do not assume every new receiver has equivalent protection.
- Smith/Filsinger reuse: [Lorentz–ADE exact midpoint/Maxwell source](../../scripts/smith_filsinger_lorentz_ade_sympy.py) and [existing source/energy owner](SmithFilsingerLorentzADEMaxwellGreenMaxCut20261117.md) preserve E/H/P/V storage, natural controller currents, useful load, Ohmic and Lorentz relaxation. **No ionospheric material has yet been fitted** to these coefficients; finite passive exact mathematics is a *candidate model*, not a space-plasma data match.

## New algebraic inverse and energy gates

[Exact source-separated discriminators](../../scripts/radiobio_p0b_ionosphere_sympy.py) check a deliberately simplified four-parameter real transfer. A one-channel receiver observation
**y = h × j × (e+p)** has **Jacobian rank 1** for unknown heater gain h, natural current j, electrojet coupling e, and upper-plasma coupling p. Even three distinguished observable channels **(hj, hje, hjp)** have rank **3 rather than 4**; unknown h and j remain under the symmetry **(h,j) -> (λh,j/λ)**. Such a model cannot backsolve individual source strengths without an independently calibrated source gain/current anchor.

This is a **structural non-identifiability witness**; it does NOT claim the two mechanisms superpose linearly in real ionospheric plasma. For a single source/receiver control volume, the exact passive ledger is:

    W_pump + W_natural - ΔU = Q_loss + W_useful + W_radiation.

All terms need disjoint boundary/custody definitions. A source-free positive receiver with no depletion/other inputs and positive losses violates the ledger; a negative ΔU represents storage depletion, not replenishment. Exact rational illustrative work example uses pump 7/5, natural 1/2, ΔU 1/4, loss 2/5, radiation 1/5 and useful 21/20, all **synthetic dimensionless**.

Source-quoted E1 range conversion: **0.0004–0.0032% = 4×10^-6 to 3.2×10^-5**. At illustrative **1 MW** heating these would correspond to **4–32 W** *estimated ELF radiated*, not measured terminal energy at any receiver; the original 2005 experiment used **960 kW**, with model/range uncertainties. Do not infer efficiency at 7.83 Hz, a different atmospheric state, an F-region experiment, or any biological exposure.

### Empirical forward/backsolve — open measurements

1. **E1 original artifact acquisition:** obtain the author's receiver B_h(t), polarization and phase spectra, 3.25 MHz heater modulation log, contemporaneous Kp/electrojet magnetometry, waveguide model inputs, two receiver geometries and RFI-control calibration. Attempt original data custodians; the published article's extracted tables/plots are not source time-series or independent metrology. Recover estimated radiated power uncertainty and source-model inverse settings from actual paper.
2. **P1/P2 actual data:** time-align SURA pump-on/off with satellite E/B, radar, optical emission and density, and 2026 HAARP two-beam stepped tones with SuperDARN observations. Retain all source timings, instrument bandwidths and independent plasma background.
3. **Model ordering:** first instrument and waveguide propagator, then electrojet conductivity, then lower-hybrid/upper-hybrid plasma, using parameter ranges constrained by original data; inherit Smith/Filsinger passive ADE only after source-specific constitutive calibration. Separate heater work from natural electrojet current and from any externally applied probe beams.
4. **Pre-registered negative controls:** heater-off, independent sky/noise sources, off-resonance frequency, opposing polarization, distant and near receiver, receiver RFI-only injection, source-free storage depletion, and independent energy flux/return.
5. **Inverse identifiability admission:** calibrate one of heater gain and natural current separately; enough independent spectral/polarization/distance observations to resolve chosen finite parameterization; check Jacobian singular values, confidence intervals and sensitivity. A symbolic rank is not an empirical fit.
6. **Transfer to human bioelectricity:** P0-A human EEG source remains a separate physiological mechanism. No plasma VLF similarity alone can bridge P0-B to human alpha/beta or regeneration.

**No new external experimental data were replayed.** The exact finite algebra is not a proof of a plasma-specific Lorentz material or of an observed power-transfer efficiency.
