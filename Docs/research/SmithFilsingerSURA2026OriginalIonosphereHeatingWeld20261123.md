## SURA 2026: original observations and competing mechanisms

The experiment was **20 August 2025**. SURA transmitted a **5,350 kHz ordinary-mode HF pump** beginning **18:01 UTC** in a repeated **8-minute-on / 4-minute-off** sequence, steered ~12° towards magnetic zenith. The original paper reports an approximately **150 MW effective radiated power (ERP)** — a directional antenna quantity, **not facility electrical input, plasma-absorbed energy, or useful recovered power**. Ionosfera-M2 passed above SURA at about **18:41 UTC**, within the pump-on **18:37–18:45** interval. Background geomagnetic conditions were **Kp ~4–5**.

Original independent observational channels: **CADI** ionograms and ground stimulated electromagnetic emission (SEE) spectra; optical red 630-nm airglow/photometers; onboard **LAERT** HF sounder/spectrometer (0.1–20 MHz), **NVK** E/B low-frequency observations (10 Hz–23 kHz sampled at **46,875 samples/s**), and **MAYAK** 150/400 MHz beacons used for relative slant TEC. The authors report **~5% TEC depletion**, red-line airglow near **300–305 km**, pump-frequency signatures reaching the topside (~820-km spacecraft altitude), and NVK bands around **8–9 kHz** and **17–18 kHz**, in electric and magnetic components. The lower-hybrid / second-harmonic and whistler-mode conversion interpretation is attributed to the original authors.

**Original negative/alternate causes:** main-ionospheric-trough and geomagnetic background variability change satellite spectra before flux-tube entry; the 2026 paper reports a separate ~**21 kHz** line when **LAERT radiation pauses**; some green-line optical changes are described as natural rather than attributable to HF heating. The **SURA pump clock, LAERT instrument operating clock, and satellite spatial flux-tube crossing** are three different variables. The same pump-on orbit slice alone cannot establish unique plasma-conversion coefficients.

The authors' **Availability Statement** identifies [original optical, ionosonde, SEE and Ionosfera-M satellite-data archive](https://links.cosmos.ru/87-3f-d4-49). The direct archive link **timed out** during this audit; file contents, schema, hashes and licence have **not been verified** and no original time series has been replayed.

**Mechanism relevance to Filsinger:** HF excitation of an upper-hybrid plasma region, daughter-wave conversion near field-aligned irregularities and propagation of VLF whistler modes is a real research pathway for source-dependent nonlinear frequency conversion. It is *mechanistically relevant* to Filsinger's proposed nonlinear, dispersive environmental conduit. It neither identifies the Filsinger coil/capacitor wiring, nor demonstrates a Schumann-frequency ground receiver, nor supplies efficient net electrical extraction. Distinguish this **F-region pump / whistler** case from **lower-ionosphere electrojet current modulation**, documented in separate HAARP work, even when both produce VLF fields.
