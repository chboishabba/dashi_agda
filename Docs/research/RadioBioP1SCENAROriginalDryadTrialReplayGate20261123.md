## Original research, trial and data — preserve source identities

- **Original clinical trial:** Mireille Michel-Cherqui, Avit Guirimand, Barbara Szekely and colleagues, *Effects of a Single Application of Scenar™, a Low-Frequency Modulated Electric Current Therapy, for Pain Relief in Patients with Low Back and Neck Pain: A Randomized Single Blinded Trial*, *Journal of Clinical Medicine* 10(23), 5570 (2021), [DOI 10.3390/jcm10235570](https://doi.org/10.3390/jcm10235570); original full article [MDPI](https://www.mdpi.com/2077-0383/10/23/5570), [PMC8658569](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8658569/); registered study [NCT03755817](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03755817). Original investigation was single-center, randomized, single-blinded, two parallel groups, 20-minute Scenar-On / Scenar-Off sham session.
- **Published observed results:** **60 randomized and analysed persons**, 30/30; baseline, immediate and 24-hour assessments. Original paper reports resting pain median (IQR) **8 (4) → 5 (3)** in sham and **7 (3) → 4 (4)** in active, with main between-group difference **p=0.22**, and a **non-significant treatment×time interaction**. Within-group changes were significant but **do not identify causal device-specific effect**, since sham improved similarly. Pain relief did not persist at the following day. No serious adverse events; minor effects in both groups. Original SAS repeated-measures mixed-model method and registered endpoint must be reproduced from actual individual measures, not from medians. The trial also compares heterogeneous back/neck pain and single treatment only.
- **Independently deposited original data:** Marc Fischler (2021), *Effect of a single application of Scenar for pain relief in patients with low back and neck pain*, [Dryad DOI 10.5061/dryad.63xsj3v2s](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.63xsj3v2s). The **public landing page** lists one **21.86 KB** workbook, literal title **Scenar_-_Dryad_Corrigé.xlsx**, dated **25 August 2021**, at [Dryad original dataset page](https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.63xsj3v2s). The direct page's download destination is **https://datadryad.org/downloads/file_stream/915627**, which returned HTTP **403** to the web retriever; the runtime download also failed. The authenticated API and record version/actual sheet column definitions remain unchecked; do not invent original columns, patient records or hash values.

## New strict independent replay producer

[Code](../../scripts/radiobio_p1_scenar_original_replay.py): takes an explicitly **human-mapped CSV** with five declared columns (participant_id, arm, rest_before, rest_after, rest_24h), after acquiring the original XLSX and inspecting its real headers and identifiers, preserving the source workbook outside Git. It rejects missing/duplicate participant ID, arm not mapped as on/off, nonnumeric/out-of-range 0–10 NRS, and unexpected group N when the complete 60-person trial is asserted. It records the **mapped CSV SHA256**, optionally verifies a supplied expected hash, and will always label **original XLSX SHA256 unverified** because it cannot itself inspect that source file. It produces mean individual change contrasts **only**, never claims to reproduce the authors' SAS/R mixed-model p-value or a registered treatment interaction without the appropriate model and covariates. If a sourced CSV contains missing observations, explicitly invoke incomplete mode and report the missingness and original denominators rather than creating artificial values.

This follows the user's **existing-source-first** order: consume the published original data if accessible, then extract verified source-coordinate trial rows to the P0-C/PNP observational tissue and sensory-measurement infrastructure, not invent a universal SCENAR response. **No PNP state calibration or regeneration endpoint is implied by a pain rating.**

**Local check:** a small independent in-container prototype generated 60 **synthetic** people, 30 per arm, with identical −3 change, and confirmed a matched difference in means of zero. That prototype passed its symbolic rank and contrast checks. The entire committed P1-E1 script has **not** been executed as a Git-blob-matched local file: no exact-head execution claimed.
