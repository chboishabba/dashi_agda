## 1. Why obtain the original rules urgently?

**Original statute:** [EPBC Act 1999 C70 as at 24 August 2026, s487(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A00485/2026-08-24/2026-08-24/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_3/document_3.html) states that an organisation or association **whether incorporated or not** may qualify if (a) incorporated or otherwise established in Australia, (b) it engaged in a **series** of Australian environmental conservation/protection/research activities in the two years immediately before the decision, and (c) **at the time of decision** its objects/purposes included environmental protection/conservation/research. s487 extends, does not limit, ordinary ADJR standing. No requirement that each qualifying activity concern the *same referral* is stated in this subsection. Different project activities can be relevant to **the applicant's standing** without becoming evidence on the other project's merits. This is a statutory reading, not a judicial determination for SWFG.
