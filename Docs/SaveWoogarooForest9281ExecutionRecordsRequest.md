# Save Woogaroo Forest — request for current 9281/2024/OW execution and compliance records

## Purpose

This is a short records request for the current execution/compliance state of **9281/2024/OW — Kalina Village 2 Stages 1–16, earthworks, vegetation clearing and stormwater**.

We already have the negotiated decision notice (`A12705835`) and negotiated approved plans (`A12705838`). The remaining question is not whether the works are approved. It is whether the approval has moved into a present execution state and whether the relevant prerequisite records have been supplied.

## Suggested request to Ipswich City Council

**Subject:** Request for current compliance / prestart records — 9281/2024/OW

Dear Ipswich City Council,

I am seeking copies of current records relating to the implementation of **development application 9281/2024/OW — Kalina Village 2 Stages 1–16 — Earthworks, Clearing Vegetation and Stormwater**.

Could Council please provide, or identify how to access, the following records if held:

1. **Condition 6(a) evidence** — the document supplied to Council to satisfy the Commonwealth-related prerequisite before the prestart meeting, including any DCCEEW / Commonwealth letter or approval relied on and any Council acceptance record;
2. **Prestart records** — any prestart request, booking, agenda, minutes, attendance record, inspection record or confirmation that the prestart requirement has been completed;
3. **Current fauna-management records** — any pre-clearance fauna management plan, fauna assessment, spotter-catcher details, licences/authorities, or Council review/acceptance records associated with the approved clearing;
4. **Arboricultural records** — any arborist assessment required before clearing near Open Space areas, together with revised tree-protection or retention drawings if applicable;
5. **Erosion and sediment-control records** required before or at commencement;
6. **Access and Works Licence records** relevant to works on Council-controlled land;
7. **Current construction / field plan set**, if a later issue-for-construction or current field set exists in addition to the negotiated approved plans;
8. **Commencement records** — any notice of intention to commence, contractor mobilisation notice, site possession/handover record, construction program, clearing schedule, inspection record or other dated record showing whether earthworks or vegetation clearing are imminent, underway or completed; and
9. any **post-clearance fauna reports or compliance inspection records** already lodged for this approval.

For clarity, this request does not assume that any condition has been breached or that works have commenced. We are seeking the current documentary status of the approval.

If some records are not publicly releasable, please identify the record type and the appropriate access process where possible.

Kind regards,

[Name]
[Organisation / Save Woogaroo Forest, if applicable]
[Contact details]

## Why these records matter

The negotiated approval is already known. These records answer the separate factual questions:

- Has the Commonwealth-related prerequisite been satisfied?
- Has the prestart process occurred?
- Are the required fauna/arborist controls in place?
- Are physical works merely approved, imminent, underway or completed?

The absence of any of these records from Development.i does **not** prove that the record does not exist. That is why the request asks Council directly.

## Current public-web check

A fresh public search on 11 September 2026 still surfaces the application as **Decided / Approved — Negotiated Decision Approved** and the same 21-document application index. That public surface does not itself provide a prestart/compliance/commencement record. This is an acquisition finding only, not evidence that no such record exists.
