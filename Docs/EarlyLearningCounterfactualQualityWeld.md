# Early-learning counterfactual, quality and evidence-horizon weld

This stacked tranche extends the comparative architecture in PR #573 without replacing its core conclusion. The professional/public early-learning floor remains an entitlement and capacity coordinate; it is not promoted into a guarantee that every centre-based exposure improves every child outcome.

The comparative evidence now forces a stronger carrier.

## 1. Intervention labels do not determine effects

`EarlyLearningCounterfactualHeterogeneityExact.agda` treats the counterfactual care environment as an explicit coordinate. The same nominal expanded-subsidised-ECEC intervention can have different situated effect directions in the finite theorem witness. Therefore no interpretation of the intervention label alone reconstructs effect direction.

The same module separately blocks turning a demographic/subgroup label into an individual treatment rule. Heterogeneous-effect evidence justifies retaining finer counterfactual coordinates; it does not justify demographic determinism.

## 2. Policy evidence is vector-valued

`EarlyLearningMultiOutcomeVectorExact.agda` separates:

- child development;
- caregiver labour;
- caregiver well-being;
- fiscal balance;
- retained entitlement.

The Quebec evidence is therefore representable as mixed rather than as one good/bad bit. Labour gains cannot determine child effects, and a child-outcome signal cannot determine the fiscal axis. A whole-policy verdict requires an explicit aggregation rule rather than accidental scalarisation.

## 3. Quality and workforce are load-bearing

`EarlyLearningQualityWorkforceLoadBearingExact.agda` makes explicit consequences already latent in the comparative architecture:

```text
professional relation != actual quality
public entitlement != mature rollout capacity
professional presence != process quality
```

The positive `QualityCapacityGate` retains workforce sustainability, process quality, adaptive contextual review, mature rollout and public entitlement as separate coordinates. The gate is not an outcome theorem.

## 4. Universal entitlement does not prescribe one route

`EarlyLearningEntitlementRouteSeparationExact.agda` proves two independent non-factorability results:

```text
universal entitlement != uniform delivery architecture
parallel family-care route != professional-floor removal
```

The Nordic comparison is represented by **country-neutral policy architecture types**:

```text
universal access without parallel cash-care
universal access with parallel cash-care.
```

This is deliberate. Nordic cash-for-childcare schemes have followed different historical paths and have been introduced, amended, reduced or abolished. Finland supplies a particularly clear historical hybrid example in which a daycare guarantee and home-care subsidy were legislated together. The formal theorem therefore uses national evidence to motivate architecture coordinates without turning a country name into a timeless policy type.

## 5. Formal availability does not determine uptake

`EarlyLearningTakeupStratificationExact.agda` separates legal availability of a cash/home-care option from its uptake distribution and labour incidence. The finite witness does not assign a real demographic group to an outcome. Its theorem is only that nominally equal availability cannot carry a stratified uptake/effect analysis by itself.

## 6. Evidence is versioned and reopenable

`EarlyLearningEvidenceRevisionHorizonExact.agda` cross-pollinates the existing PNF `ReopenableEvidenceFibre` and the existing developmental measurement-horizon carrier.

Evidence is indexed by both axis and vintage. Later maternal/fiscal evidence can revise policy assessment without erasing child-outcome receipts on another axis. Existing PNF boundaries are reused directly:

```text
reweighting != refutation
projection residual != semantic erasure
later evidence != authority to erase another evidence axis
```

The same horizon discipline used for developmental trajectories therefore applies to policy evaluation: an early snapshot does not exhaust a trajectory, and one evidence vintage does not close the future evidence space.

## 7. Universal rights can be too coarse for intervention routing

`EarlyLearningSituatedPolicyRoutingSafetyExact.agda` specializes the existing `PolicyRelativeProjectionSafety` theorem pattern.

Two situated states share the same current coarse entitlement/route-fit observation. A coarse policy selects the same support action for both. Their post-action route-fit observations differ, yielding a literal `PolicyExposedQuotientDefect`.

The result is deliberately scoped:

```text
universal entitlement can remain a valid rights surface
AND
universal entitlement alone can be an unsafe complete action-routing carrier.
```

A dedicated empty permission prevents the routing defect from being promoted into a refutation of universal entitlement.

- Ann-Zofie Duvander and Anne Lise Ellingsaeter, *Cash for childcare schemes in the Nordic welfare states: diverse paths, diverse outcomes*, European Societies 18(1), 2016, DOI `10.1080/14616696.2015.1124903`;

- Jonathan Gruber, Tuomas Kosonen and Kristiina Huttunen, *Paying moms to stay home: Short and long run effects on parents and children*, Journal of Public Economics 251, 2025, DOI `10.1016/j.jpubeco.2025.105496`;

These sources supplement, rather than replace, PR #573's existing Baker-Gruber-Milligan, Kottelenberg-Lehrer, Haeck-Lebihan-Merrigan, Waldorf, homeschool/unschooling and normative-range-parenting evidence surfaces.

## Compact result

```text
intervention label != treatment effect
subgroup label != individual effect
professional floor != outcome
professional presence != quality
entitlement != rollout capacity
labour gain != child benefit
child signal != fiscal verdict
universal entitlement != uniform route
parallel family support != professional exit
formal option availability != uptake distribution
evidence vintage != whole evidence state
early snapshot != full trajectory
later evidence != erasure of earlier evidence
country label != permanent policy architecture
rights surface != complete intervention router
```

Positive synthesis:

```text
preserve the entitlement;
keep delivery routes plural;
make quality/workforce/process capacity explicit;
audit uptake and effects stratified by situated counterfactuals;
keep outcome axes and evidence vintages reopenable;
route support on the fine situated carrier, not the public slogan.
```
