# Save Woogaroo Forest — gain/loss snowball audit

This note is for Save Woogaroo Forest and assisting/pro-bono lawyers. It is not legal advice. It adapts the repository's Amy Eskridge / disruption-benefit discipline and snowball acquisition rule to the Woogaroo planning chain.

## Attribution boundary

The purpose is to identify **distributional effects and exposures**, not motives or wrongdoing.

```text
beneficiary != perpetrator
beneficiary != improper motive
burden bearer != proof of unlawfulness
approval != realised profit
planning entitlement != federal approval
works approval != proof works have occurred
```

The Amy-derived canonical owner requires benefit, visibility, disruption-effect, threat perception, opportunity/access and operational evidence to remain distinct before any actor-specific attribution. This Woogaroo audit uses only the much weaker and safer question: **who gains an entitlement/option, and who becomes exposed to loss?**

The Snowball rule is also retained: evidence can arrive out of dependency order and be kept. A later operational-works receipt does not automatically pay an earlier federal, NCA, merits or contravention requirement.

## Who gains — bounded reading

### 1. Development proponent / relevant land interests

The strongest presently supportable gain is **option value / entitlement value**.

The local approvals reduce local-planning uncertainty and permit a sequence from precinct planning to subdivision/development plans and then operational works. That can increase the practical ability to proceed, finance, stage or transact around the development.

What is **not** proved:

- realised developer profit;
- exact land-value uplift;
- financing gains;
- identity of every economic beneficiary;
- that local approval overcomes Commonwealth law;
- any improper motive or coordination.

The correct acquisition demand is ownership, project-entity, financing, development-agreement and transaction evidence if actual economic capture matters to counsel.

### 2. Future purchasers / residents

`4272/2020/ADP` and `5547/2020/ADP` describe substantial residential subdivision outcomes, together with roads, drainage and open-space components. If implemented, future residents/purchasers may gain access to housing and associated infrastructure.

This is a **contingent future benefit**, not a realised outcome and not proof of net social benefit.

### 3. Construction / infrastructure participants

Approved operational works create potential future work for contractors, suppliers and infrastructure providers. No specific contractor or monetary benefit should be attributed without contracts or procurement evidence.

## Who loses or bears exposure — bounded reading

### 1. Vegetation / habitat inside the exact works footprint

`9281/2024/OW` moves the record beyond abstract zoning: **Vegetation Clearing** is expressly within the approved operational works.

That source-pays **exposure to clearing**.

It does **not** yet source-pay:

```text
exact hectares cleared
exact trees/species affected
exact commencement date
actual clearing already performed
NCA s13 critical-habitat status
NCA s102 significant detrimental effect
EPBC contravention
```

The next high-alpha object is therefore the exact approved clearing plan/polygon.

### 2. Species, local populations and corridor function

Koalas and other controlled matters may bear ecological loss **if** the approved clearing intersects their functional habitat. The DA summaries alone do not pay that same-object join.

Required join:

```text
9281/9293 approved works polygon
  x
species-use / habitat-quality evidence
  x
official corridor geometry
  x
mature-tree / structural-habitat evidence
  ->
exact ecological burden
```

This same join can feed the EPBC merits consumer, the NCA s13 essentiality consumer and the s102 detrimental-effect consumer separately.

### 3. Public participation

Every supplied local application summary records **Public Notification Required: No**.

The source-paid loss is narrow: the statutory public-notification opportunity attached to an impact-assessable pathway was not present for these recorded applications.

It would be WrongType to say:

```text
no statutory public notification = no consultation whatsoever
```

because referral, agency, councillor, community or voluntary engagement may have occurred through other channels.

For counsel, the question is whether the governing Springfield/superseded planning framework lawfully placed each decision in a pathway with no public notification and what review/participation rights nevertheless existed.

### 4. Community/ecosystem option value

Once mature connected habitat is cleared, some ecological options may be difficult or impossible to recover on decision-relevant timescales. This is presently a **cross-source inference**, supported conceptually by the offset/maturity work, but it requires the exact mature-habitat and clearing footprint evidence before being presented as a project-specific fact.

## Who may bear costs

Potential cost bearers include the proponent, landholders, future lot purchasers, public infrastructure providers and government, depending on conditions, offsets and development agreements. None of that allocation is paid by these one-page DA summaries.

Do not infer:

```text
developer gets approval -> taxpayers necessarily bear infrastructure cost
```

or:

```text
offset condition -> developer alone ultimately bears economic incidence
```

without the actual agreements/conditions and an incidence analysis.

**Net-benefit arithmetic / no naive transfer:** gross entitlements and historical estimated implicit financing assistance cannot be summed with an entire asset's market valuation and infrastructure-charge notices to produce a common 2026 cash benefit. Required net-incidence join = **same legal person + same parcel/financial instrument + valid rate/version + actual paid receipts + true financing/equity exposure + counterfactual + temporal realisation**. A private uplift can coexist with public roads/services, paid developer contributions, costs to households, and habitat loss. Model all separately.

**Critical close gate:** actual signed federal approval and 2024/25 council decision documents, original **both** ICNs, original water/sewer ICNs, infrastructure agreements and charge register, 1994 loan settlement ledger, precise sale/option/financing agreements and same-parcel habitat clearing/offset costs. Do not infer any specific lobbying instruction or Murray Watt personal benefit from shared party, donor, investment or regional-development adjacency.
