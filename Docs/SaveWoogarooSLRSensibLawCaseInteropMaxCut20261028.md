## The two new original-source legal discriminators

**One: mandatory 2022 approved conservation advice**. The actual EPBC advice took effect **12 February 2022**; its existence is source-positive. But did the officer consider it when making *this* approval? Original approved advice plus actual brief/reasons, s139 trigger, applicable 2025–2026 transition, and final 95C must be compared. This is a potential **legality** inquiry, not a request to substitute this repository's scientific preferred outcome.

**Two: exact historical legal outcomes and counterlaw**. Different *Tarkine* cases [2013] FCA 694 (Court judgment concerning approved conservation advice) versus [2015] FCAFC 89 (adverse blanket cumulative consequences theory) must never be conflated. [Parliamentary original 2015 EPBC Standing report](https://www.aph.gov.au/Parliamentary_Business/Committees/Senate/Environment_and_Communications/EPBC_Standing_Bill/Report/c01) states proposed **2013 legislative Schedule 1** to insulate failures to consider conservation advice was **removed before enactment**. The same original reports **Mackay Conservation Group NSD33/2015** approval set aside **by consent 4 August 2015** following absence of Yakka Skink/Ornamental Snake approved advice. [Senators' original dissent](https://www.aph.gov.au/Parliamentary_Business/Committees/Senate/Environment_and_Communications/EPBC_Standing_Bill/Report/d01) makes explicit that **no contested judicial judgment or findings were made**. The project was re-approved **14 October 2015**. These sources are important to counsel but **not** evidence the 2019/8575 decision-maker similarly omitted koala advice.
