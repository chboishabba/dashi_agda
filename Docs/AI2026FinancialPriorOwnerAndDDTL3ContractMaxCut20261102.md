## Newly read original legal text: DDTL 3.0, not a generic CDO analogy

1. **2025-07-28 initial agreement**, original SEC exhibit
   https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1769628/000176962825000033/coreweave30-ex101xtermlo.htm
   §2.20(b)(i)-(viii), pp.56–59, gives an **eight-step priority**:
   eligible operating costs first subject to $50m per calendar-month cap for
   that priority; agent/administrative fees; contractual interest and swaps;
   liquidity-account satisfaction; scheduled principal and certain swap
   terminations; *only upon cash trap event* cash trapping; other operating
   and capital costs with **OpEx Payment Condition**; remainder to
   **Distribution Reserve Account**, not immediately to shareholder.
   §2.20(d), §6.06 restrict actual release. Cash accounts may receive
   permitted **parent deposits and account transfers**. Actual deposit source,
   cash-flow date and optional Interparty waterfall not published here.
2. **2025-12-31 first amendment**, original SEC exhibit
   https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1769628/000176962826000104/ex10311.htm
   §1.01 defines CRR numerator as billings or receipts **without double
   counting** and equity `CRR Cure Proceeds`. Section 6.12 floors ratio at
   0.85 (with special zero-denominator rule). CRR 0.85 does **NOT** imply
   85% of projected cash actually collected; numerator may be billed and
   cure-included. No historical cure event, default or actual ratio is
   inferred from the *permitted* contractual mechanism.
3. **Hedge / GPU-specific sourcing**: original §5.22(a) requires
   financed **GPU rack** serial numbers to lenders 60 days after
   draw; §5.22(b) provides conditional reasonable-effort delivery of
   individual GPU documents from Nvidia/Dell. §5.23 requires swaps/caps on
   at least **75% of projected floating-rate principal**, subject to the
   agreement's timing and basis. This **invalidates** extrapolating a
   universal unhedged floating-rate shock to *this* facility. §6.06 is
   a distinct legally constrained distribution/affiliate-payment gate.
