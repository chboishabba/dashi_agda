**Original study design as source-described:** n=19 experienced participants, two within-participant inhaled 20mg/40mg freebase DMT sessions, eyes-closed RS baseline and 20-minute DMT period each session, continuous ECG/EDA/respiration (simultaneously with EEG), retrospectively traced **15 TET experience dimensions** at 0.25 Hz/4 s. This is neither the 25-person intranasal DMT+buccal harmine microstate study nor Erowid report analysis. **Cohort overlap with Lewis-Healey et al. (2026) is unresolved**; shared authors and matching n=19 are dependencies to review, not proof of independent replication or matched identity.

**The exact 15-dimension codebook** (original `config.py`, in declared order): pleasantness; unpleasantness; emotional_intensity; elementary_imagery; complex_imagery; auditory; interoception; bliss; anxiety; entity; selfhood; disembodiment; salience; temporality; general_intensity. Original author definition of `entity` is subjective perceived presence of autonomous entities, **not an independent physical entity detector**. An onset difference between `complex_imagery` and `entity` is a conditional retrospective report statistic; culture, expectation, sequence reconstruction, missingness, timing anchors and measurement imprecision remain live alternatives.

## Implemented original-source-specific consumer (without raw data)

`scripts/audit_dmt_tet_presence_synthetic.py` pins the original author code commit, config blob, exactly 15 author-defined dimensions, 0–10 self-rating scale, 4-second source resolution, 20/40mg dose fields and RS vs DMT stage. It **refuses real participant data** and accepts only explicit synthetic person IDs and complete synthetic within-person/treatment/stage sequences; it rejects untyped source fields, raw narratives, out-of-scale values, missing/duplicated samples, unauthorized modalities and order ambiguity. For predeclared synthetic threshold, it produces first-crossing times for `elementary_imagery`, `complex_imagery`, `entity` and their entity-versus-complex-imagery lag at the per-participant/dose level. No temporal inference arises from Erowid summary fractions or from EEG G1.

`tests/test_dmt_tet_presence_synthetic.py` adds **15 source-written** synthetic contract tests including both positive and negative onset lags, a missing/zero distinction, order-invariant row processing, field/scale/source/date limits, and human-data fail-closed admission. Tests are written but not yet run at exact GitHub head; no statistical power or prospective confirmation is claimed.

## Highest-value Pareto choices (axes not scalar ranks)

- **P0a, human-source admission:** verify Zenodo version/concept DOI, actual TET time-series and source consent/reuse restrictions; pin local SHA and a privacy/ethics scope receipt. Then verify code columns/time alignment, missingness, subject/dose/session crosswalk and clock-vs-retrospective-anchor semantics. The source says retrospective TET traces with two-minute auditory time anchors; this is not prospective observation and not an objectively timestamped neuron-level event.
- **P0b, preregistered restricted M1a baseline:** first specify source-measured sensory/imagery covariates, intensity, salience, selfhood and dose, with held-out **person** prediction of subjective entity timing/presence. Compare M0 (selection/intensity/time), mechanistically constrained M1a, observationally expressive latent-state M1b, and proposed M2 at equal admissible outcome, missingness and capacity. First-crossing toy threshold alone is not an implemented M1a model.
- **P0c, original Niedernhuber G1:** continue separate participant/arm/epoch and +microstate source execution recovery. The exact six-permutation synthetic paired-variance certificate in `scripts/dmt_g1_pairing_permutation_certificate.py` establishes why unkeyed marginals cannot reconstruct pairing; the new registered `DMTG1PairingNonFactorabilityExact.agda` reuses the existing `IntersectionalNonFactorability` theorem. Source MATLAB jointly excludes NaN rows and no original mispairing is claimed.
- **P1, cross-cohort independence:** Lewis-Healey n19 and the D'Amelio n19 TET programme share several authors and similar study design, but recruitment/data overlap has not been proved. Establish cohort DOI/participant lineage before any independent replication claim.
- **P2, external ontology:** any improvement predicting a *reported* presence cannot identify independent conscious agents. M2 needs a new, source-specified counterfactual restriction or a separately approved blinded-information channel, with strong endogenous controls.
