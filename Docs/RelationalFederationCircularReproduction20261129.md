## Constructive model
Three distinct typed loops: physical stock/use/recovery/loss/virgin input; financial cash/receivables/debt/reserves/settlement; authority and revision/exit. Do not equate them by topology. Internal transactions cancel when consolidating equal, same-currency, same-period realised debits/credits across a declared boundary (not across arbitrary currencies, accrual or incomplete entity coverage). Local liquidity constraints survive consolidation; distinguish ordinary cooperative internal trade from related-party transactions without genuine delivery. No requirement for all services to earn commercial revenue: explicit sustainable grants, member contributions, public provisioning or nonmonetary support are legitimate candidate funding modes, each with actual commitment and expiry. No perpetual refinancing assumption. In-kind care hours cannot settle cash debt unless independently contracted; resource recovery is not energy creation.
For each entity, period and currency: opening cash + external realised service receipts + grants/contributions + debt draws + internal receipts = internal payments + operating costs + maintenance + paid care + interest + principal + closing cash. Track principal opening + draw = repayment + closing (writeoffs, capitalised interest, valuation and currency changes require separate ledger edges in generalised case). Each resource/site/unit ledger: opening + new inputs + received recovered stock = use + outflows + loss + closing. Distinguish virgin throughput, quality/downcycling, recycling energy and external harms. Rebound may erase absolute resource reduction even when recovery increases. Avoided cost is not liquid cash unless actually realised and accessible. Track contracts, loss allocation, maturity, guarantees, independence, reserves, distribution and funding cliff.
Candidate revolving fund: openly scoped contributions/capital -> durable repair/reuse/energy/food assets -> delivered and verified services or public benefits -> actual repayments where applicable -> funded upkeep/reserves -> new lending only within liquidity, capacity and ecological limits. Grant-funded services need no forced commercial payer; report recurrent source and expiry explicitly. Cooperative ownership, audited related-party decisions, accessible refusal/exit, external accountability and no permanent founder command are independent conditions; no real authority or solvency asserted. Compare alternative models (linear procurement, extractive reflexive finance, genuine cooperative internal clearing, grants/public provisioning, mixed systems) on same declared resource and service obligations rather than political labels.
Australia accountability cross-pollination: record financial flows, procurement and disclosure provenance, independent conflicts review, beneficial ownership/control, actual decision and burden incidence, appeal and remedy; no policy similarity -> covert command. Legal status must be independently sourced per jurisdiction.
