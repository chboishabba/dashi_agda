# Early learning: developmental affordance and attunement

## Why `quality x structure x autonomy x context` was still too coarse

The comparative early-learning lane already proved that neither professional entitlement nor home delivery determines outcome. Repo archaeology now adds a deeper correction from three existing lines of work:

1. `DASHI.Reasoning.DevelopmentalAttunementPNFBridge` proves that equal attention quantity and equal uptake quantity can coexist with different sequence-dependent burden when response timing is differently fragmented.
2. `DASHI.Biology.EcologyOfDataHyperfabricBridge`, grounded in Finn/Brown's ecology-of-data work, keeps person, place, affordance, activity, value-flow and time as co-constituted coordinates.
3. `DASHI.Education.RoseContextLearningSourceBoundary` permits olfactory/sensory context to be represented while explicitly blocking the inference that exposure alone proves universal learning or therapeutic benefit.

Together these imply that a policy cannot safely replace the old `home versus centre` scalar with another scalar such as `more resources`, `more stimulation`, `more autonomy`, `more structure`, or `more teacher contact`.

The relevant object is the fit and temporal topology of developmental affordances.

## Exact theorem 1: attention quantity does not determine attunement burden

The existing developmental-attunement module provides two traces with:

```text
attentionMass stable     = 4
attentionMass fragmented = 4

uptakeMass stable        = 2
uptakeMass fragmented    = 2
```

but:

```text
traceTotalBurden stable     = 5
traceTotalBurden fragmented = 7.
```

`DevelopmentalAffordanceAttunementExact` turns this into the generic intersectional non-factorability result:

```text
attentionQuantityCannotDetermineAttunementBurden
```

This matters directly for eKindy, ECEC and nudge policy. Equal scheduled teacher-contact minutes, equal message counts, or equal nominal interaction opportunities do not reconstruct temporal contingency, synchrony, uptake, switching burden or repair.

## Exact theorem 2: equal affordance inventory does not determine developmental fit

The new module constructs two finite developmental-affordance cells with the same nominal inventory:

```text
relational
sensory
olfactory
spatial
motor
social
language
rest/recovery
```

while retaining different:

```text
temporal organisation
child-preference visibility
relational review
overload regulation
affordance fit.
```

Therefore:

```text
affordanceInventoryCannotDetermineDevelopmentalFit
```

This is a structural theorem, not an empirical claim that the particular illustrative inventory is universally sufficient or that one child would respond in a specified way.

The point is that counting available resources or experiences is not enough to reconstruct whether they are usable, timely, safe, meaningful, contestable or developmentally fitted.

## `Smell the roses` means represent context, not maximise stimulation

The rose source remains deliberately narrow:

Keisuke Kokubun, Kiyotaka Nemoto, Yoshinori Yamakawa,
"Continuous inhalation of essential oil increases gray matter volume",
*Brain Research Bulletin* 208 (2024), 110896,
DOI `10.1016/j.brainresbull.2024.110896`.

Its role is to justify keeping olfactory exposure as a possible contextual coordinate. It does not license:

```text
more scent -> more learning
sensory enrichment -> universal benefit
MRI difference -> therapeutic effect
```

Hence `DevelopmentalAffordanceAttunementExact` explicitly blocks monotone promotion from more affordances, more sensory exposure, more autonomy, more structure or professional presence to a better/attuned outcome.

## Finn/Brown: person-place-affordance-time is the stronger carrier

`EcologyOfDataHyperfabricBridge` already represents an ecological cell as:

```text
person
x place
x affordance
x activity
x value flow
x time.
```

For early learning this is stronger than a delivery-site classification. A farm during harvest, an urban apartment, a centre room, a community-controlled service, a forest kindergarten, an online eKindy session and a grandparent's home are not merely competing `locations`. Each makes different actions, relations, sensations, cultural practices, burdens and learning opportunities reachable.

The policy question becomes:

```text
What can this child actually do, perceive, initiate, refuse, revisit and integrate here,
at this developmental phase, with these people and this temporal organisation?
```

rather than:

```text
How many resources/teacher minutes/activities does the route provide?
```

## Developmental phase and attunement

The new carrier retains:

```text
developmental phase
affordance inventory
affordance fit
temporal organisation
child-preference visibility
relational review
overload regulation
local lifeworld
common entitlement.
```

This does not imply that a formal model can determine an individual child's developmental needs. It means those distinctions may not be erased before the relevant consumer has justified that they are irrelevant.

The developmental-attunement sources already in `RelationalFormalismSourceAtlas` provide bounded provenance for this distinction:

- Elysia Poggi Davis et al., "Exposure to unpredictable maternal sensory signals influences cognitive development across species", DOI `10.1073/pnas.1703444114`;
- Ruth Feldman, "Parent-infant synchrony and the construction of shared timing; physiological precursors, developmental outcomes, and risk conditions", DOI `10.1111/j.1469-7610.2006.01701.x`;
- Edward Tronick et al., "The infant's response to entrapment between contradictory messages in face-to-face interaction", DOI `10.1016/S0002-7138(09)62273-1`;
- Autumn S. Ivy et al., "Dysfunctional nurturing behavior in rat dams with limited access to nesting material: A clinically relevant model for early-life stress", DOI `10.1016/j.neuroscience.2008.04.019`.

These sources motivate temporal predictability, synchrony, mismatch and fragmentation as candidate dimensions. They do not diagnose children or caregivers and do not turn animal paradigms into direct human causal claims.

## Implications for the comparative cases

### eKindy

The strength of eKindy is no longer described merely as `home + professional floor`. Its high-value feature is the repeated contextual loop:

```text
common aim
x local lifeworld
x child response
x teacher/family interpretation
-> revised situated activity.
```

The harvest is not noise around a standard lesson. It is part of the affordance field through which a common learning aim becomes actionable.

### Quebec

The Quebec evidence already blocks `professional floor -> good outcome`. The affordance/attunement layer explains what that theorem was missing: rapid scale-up can preserve entitlement while changing educator capacity, child-adult temporal organisation, peer ecology, setting quality and the counterfactual affordance field.

### Waldorf / Steiner

Waldorf need not be represented as simply `alternative pedagogy`. Its distinctive hypothesis can be decomposed into temporal pacing, sensory/motor affordances, continuity of relationship, delayed formalisation and structured curriculum. Whether those coordinates improve a particular outcome remains empirical; the formalism simply stops the pedagogical label from doing all explanatory work. The implementation deliberately uses the current Waldorf source only as a measurement-horizon warning; there is no invented canonical Steiner ontology in the repo.

### Structured home education and unschooling

`home` does not determine outcome. `autonomy` does not determine outcome either. A home-learning environment can differ in available affordances, scaffolding, relational attunement, structure, social topology, child preference and feedback loops. The framework therefore does not infer that unschooling fails because it is free, or succeeds because it is free.

### Free-range parenting

The same logic runs in the opposite direction. Institutional restriction can itself remove developmentally useful autonomy affordances. The repo therefore blocks both `institutional authority = automatic protection` and `parental autonomy = automatic risk`.

## Child authority is part of the affordance topology

The existing `ChildAuthorityTraumaHyperfabric` already blocks scalar-capacity sufficiency and compliance-as-consent promotion. The early-learning consequence is important: an environment is not fully described by what adults make available. It also matters whether the child can initiate, refuse, contest, pause, redirect and return.

This gives a stronger reading of child agency:

```text
agency != nominal menu size
agency != parent choosing on child's behalf
agency != exposure to many activities
```

Instead, agency partly concerns the topology of viable, developmentally appropriate child actions within a governed relational field.

## Availability, reachability and contestability

The deeper pass through `DevelopmentalAgencyState`, `AutonomyReopeningCriterion`, `TraumaMemoryHypervoxelBridge`, `ChildAuthorityTraumaHyperfabric`, `PsychogeographicFieldExact`, `PatternPreservingTeaching`, and `NicheConstructionProblemSpace` sharpens the carrier further:

```text
available != reachable != contestable/agentic.
```

`SituatedRelationalLearningAffordanceExact` now gives two exact finite non-factorability witnesses:

```text
availableAffordanceCannotDetermineReachability
reachabilityCannotDetermineDevelopmentalAgency
```

The first holds nominal availability fixed while varying whether the opportunity is actually reachable through the child's relational, temporal and historical topology. The second holds reachability fixed while varying whether the child has a route to accept, decline or modify participation.

The child-agency lane adds an important normative correction. `DevelopmentalAgencyState` makes competence domain- and capability-relative: guardian permission is not child competence, assent is not mature consent, and refusal and withdrawal remain distinct. `ConsentAgencyGovernanceCore` requires pacing, review, refusal and withdrawal. Consequently, an adult making an opportunity available cannot by itself establish child agency.

The autonomy-reopening lane prevents the opposite mistake. Temporary structure, protection or scaffolding is not automatically anti-autonomous. The relevant question is whether reasons, alternatives, independent verification and revision capacity are reopened over development. This prevents the framework from silently equating `more freedom now` with `more autonomy over time`.

The trauma-memory lane adds path dependence without diagnostic determinism. Versioned developmental history and residuals may remain relevant to current accessibility, while `residualAloneProvesTrauma = false` and `formalCarrierDiagnosesPerson = false`. A nominal opportunity can therefore be historically difficult to reach without the formalism declaring a clinical diagnosis or fixed character.

The psychogeographic and niche-construction lanes add two further distinctions:

```text
same physical endpoint != same lived affordance
future affordance expansion requires longitudinal evidence.
```

Route history may alter what a place affords, and a child's actions may participate in changing a future problem space; neither claim licenses a one-step developmental outcome theorem.

Finally, measurement horizon becomes explicit:

```text
immediate snapshot
intermediate horizon
longitudinal horizon.
```

This is the disciplined lesson drawn from comparative cases such as Waldorf: an early measurement and a later developmental trajectory are different observation surfaces. The source does not become a causal Steiner theorem.

The resulting positive design gate requires the dimensions to travel together before policy language promotes an offered opportunity into a genuinely developmental affordance:

```text
nominal availability
+ actual reachability
+ child accept/decline/modify route
+ refusal and withdrawal
+ safe mediation
+ structured scaffolding compatible with later autonomy
+ relational review
+ place and sensory context
+ developmental history
+ declared measurement horizon
+ public entitlement
+ longitudinal evidence for future-affordance claims.
```

This is stronger than `more choice`, `more resources`, `more professional contact`, or even `more affordances`.

## Compact result

The high-alpha compression is now:

```text
same delivery route       != same developmental opportunity
same professional floor   != same developmental opportunity
same resource inventory   != same developmental fit
same attention quantity   != same attunement burden
same nominal availability != same reachability
same reachability         != same contestability/agency
adult offer               != child assent
more sensory exposure     != automatically better
more autonomy             != automatically better
more structure            != automatically better
temporary structure       != automatic loss of autonomy
professional presence     != automatic attunement
developmental history     != diagnosis
early snapshot            != whole developmental trajectory
```

The positive target is:

> Preserve public entitlements, pluralise pathways, and govern a reachable-and-contestable developmental affordance field so that child, family, kin/community and professional knowledge can continuously test whether what is available is actually usable, timely, meaningful, revisable and safe.

Or more compactly:

```text
preserve entitlements; make affordances reachable; preserve contestability; declare the horizon.
```
