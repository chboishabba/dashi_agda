# Save Woogaroo Forest — Scenic / Peninsula visual Snowball addendum

## Purpose

This addendum records what is paid by rendered page images in the uploaded 2020/8629 Peninsula and 2020/8651 Scenic ecological-assessment PDFs. It is intended for the Save Woogaroo Forest legal handoff.

Attribution is strict throughout:

- the maps are Saunders Havill Group technical material prepared for Springfield City Group;
- they are not Commonwealth findings merely because they were used in EPBC referral material;
- a rendered-map observation is separate from an exact GIS intersection;
- one project's fact does not automatically pay another project's legal consumer.

## 1. Peninsula — EPBC 2020/8629

### Historical imagery

Plan 1 displays the referral boundary against 1969, 1982, 1990 and 2002 aerial imagery. The visual sequence shows the project area within a long-standing wooded landscape while substantial surrounding land-use change becomes visible by 2002.

This is a visual temporal carrier, not a stand-age theorem. Persistent canopy appearance in historical aerials does not by itself prove the age of individual trees.

### Field survey and vegetation

Plan 2 spatially locates habitat transects, SAT surveys, photographs and GPS survey effort within the referral area.

Plan 3 maps 1.84 ha of RE 12.3.11 and 17.03 ha of the RE 12.9-10.2 / 12.9-10.7 / 12.9-10.19 composite across the site.

### Critical Koala habitat

Plan 4 labels **17.08 ha habitat to remove** within the mapped potential critical Koala habitat. This visually binds the proposed removal footprint to the habitat classification used by SHG.

### Contiguous landscape

Plan 5 depicts the Peninsula referral polygon at the southern end of a green **675 ha contiguous landscape** carrier extending substantially northward through the Brookwater/Springfield landscape.

## 2. Scenic — EPBC 2020/8651

### Historical imagery

Plan 2 displays the Scenic referral boundary against 1969, 1982, 1990 and 2002 aerial imagery. The visual sequence again places the site within a historically wooded landscape while showing increasing urban development around it by 2002.

### Field survey

Plan 3 spatially records SAT survey locations, modified habitat-quality transects, threatened-flora search areas/meanders, rocky outcrops and GPS tracklogs. This is important because survey coverage can be compared with mapped habitat and the proposed development footprint instead of treating survey conclusions as spatially uniform by assumption.

### Critical Koala habitat

Plan 5 maps potential critical Koala habitat across the referral area and labels **23.52 ha habitat to remove**.

### Contiguous landscape

Plan 6 uses the same **675 ha contiguous landscape** label and depicts Scenic within that green landscape, north of the Peninsula site position.

## 3. New cross-project Snowball payment

Before viewing the PDF figures, the strongest cross-project case was a manually aligned campaign map plus official corridor screenshots.

The two SHG plans now give a stronger source-bound proposition:

```text
Peninsula SHG contiguous-landscape analysis
       +
Scenic SHG contiguous-landscape analysis
       ->
common 675 ha landscape carrier
```

This supports the bounded inference that **Scenic and Peninsula were assessed by the same consultant against a common connected-landscape object**, rather than merely being adjacent project names.

It does **not** yet establish:

```text
same 675 ha label == byte-identical GIS polygon
same landscape == Commonwealth finding
same landscape == cumulative legal effect proved
same landscape == Queensland NCA s 13 critical habitat
```

The exact source GIS geometry remains the cleanest payment for those stronger statements.

## 4. Why this matters to Springview / EPBC 2019/8575

The legal question is now sharper. Instead of asking only whether the campaign's Springview–Scenic–Peninsula chain appears continuous, counsel can ask whether Springview 2019/8575 intersects or severs the **same connected habitat system already used in the SHG Scenic/Peninsula analyses**.

Highest-value next join:

```text
SHG 675 ha Scenic/Peninsula landscape geometry
        ×
2019/8575 Springview action / clearing polygon
        ×
Queensland biodiversity-corridor geometry
        ×
koala / listed-species habitat use
```

Potential consumers, if the exact join is paid:

- EPBC cumulative and fragmentation analysis;
- adequacy of avoidance / mitigation and offsets;
- Queensland NCA s 13 habitat-function / essentiality evidence;
- NCA ss 102–107 detrimental-effect analysis.

## 5. Important proponent-side tension for counsel

The same 2020 technical corpus gives both:

1. a high connectivity score / common 675 ha landscape carrier; and
2. project-level reasoning that parts of the habitat are fragmented, under urban pressure, or of lower koala-use quality.

That is not necessarily a contradiction. But it is worth testing precisely:

```text
How can a site be part of a 675 ha connected landscape
while the significance analysis discounts loss because of fragmentation,
transience or availability of habitat elsewhere?
```

Counsel/ecologists should compare the definitions, spatial scales and counterfactual assumptions used in those statements rather than treating either one as dispositive.

## 6. Attribution / WrongType firewalls

```text
SHG technical map != Commonwealth finding
potential critical Koala habitat != NCA s 13 critical habitat
habitat to remove != habitat already cleared
visual continuity != exact GIS intersection
historic wooded appearance != exact tree age
common landscape carrier != cumulative legal consequence automatically proved
```
