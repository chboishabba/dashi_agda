## Hodge: exact generated chart, not assumed full affine ring
Let A be the original open ring, B the subalgebra generated by all original coordinates over Q. In A[T], the cone image R generated by T and a_i T has R_d=B_{<=d} T^d, where B_{<=d} is the span of coordinate monomials of degree at most d. The equality follows by padding with powers of T. The degree-zero subring of R[T^-1] is exactly B: each homogeneous fraction r/T^d maps to its coefficient in B, and every finite polynomial in a_i lifts by padding to its maximum degree. ## PNP: exact sparse cut with cost boundary
For an original topologically ordered single-output DAG split after k gates, let L contain every distinct input or prefix gate referenced by a suffix gate or by the final output (if it is in the prefix/input). Retain precisely L. By induction in suffix order, evaluating with the restricted prefix values gives every original suffix wire value. Conversely substituting the original prefix DAG into the sparse suffix recovers the original output and uses at most the original gate count, because each retained reference is shared rather than expanded. Any two inputs with equal L values therefore have equal output. The converse is not guaranteed; an output-equivalent quotient can be coarser. A lower bound on |L| for a chosen DAG or partition cannot establish SAT lower bounds for all DAGs. This proof is the sparse specialization of the existing full-boundary PNPDAGCutsetFactorizationExact, not a universal complexity result.
