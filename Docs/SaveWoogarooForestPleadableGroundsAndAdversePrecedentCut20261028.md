# Woogaroo EPBC 2019/8575 — pleadable-grounds and adverse-authority cut

**Prepared 28 October 2026. Case counsel issue paper, source-written; NOT counsel's signed opinion, originating process, filed pleading, order, or a guarantee of success. No new allegation should be sent, pleaded or published as an established legal violation merely because it appears here.**

## Executive case theory

The Commonwealth [original decision register](https://eap-cp.powerappsportals.com/all-notices/) states Springfield Residential Development **EPBC 2019/8575 approved with conditions on 15 October 2026**. Portal document lists still do not pay the signed approval, actual conditions, decision-maker, s95C recommendation and final s95B comment-transmission bundle. Without these, neither a material legal defect, actual prestart breach nor an urgent injunction claim is proved.

**P0 case focus:** whether the authorized decision-maker genuinely dealt with the **correct operative 2022 endangered-koala approved conservation advice**, relevant 2022 koala recovery plan and *this action's own incremental physical effect* on a pre-existing fragmented koala habitat network, in the terms required by the applicable transitional EPBC Act ss133–139. Separately test the exact s95B comments/95C recommendation and Queensland prestart condition 6(a).

**Hard adverse authority:** [Tarkine National Coalition v Minister [2015] FCAFC 89](https://www.judgments.fedcourt.gov.au/judgments/Judgments/fca/full/2024/2024fcafc0056), as discussed in the later Court's 2024 official judgment, rejects a general s136(2)(e) duty to aggregate all *other projects'* harms as consequences of THIS project. Do not plead a stand-alone blanket cumulative-impact mandatory statutory obligation merely because surrounding projects contribute pressure. [Australian Government Solicitor's 23 February 2024 original legal briefing](https://www.ags.gov.au/publications/legal-briefing/lb-20240219) draws the critical line: other clearing and population decline may form the **existing ecological baseline** and inform whether *Springview's action-specific loss* is significant, but external impacts are not automatically consequences attributable to Springview. This is a better, more defensible case theory. The 2024 Court case concerned coal project reconsideration, not final Springview conditions.

**Original ecological regulator language:** DCCEEW's [2024 endangered-koala referral guidance](https://www.dcceew.gov.au/environment/biodiversity/threatened/publications/referral-guidelines-endangered-koala) specifically recognizes connection between habitat patches and local/wider koala populations, and warns even **1 ha** loss can have a significant impact in appropriate context. This is *nonbinding species guidance for assessment*, **not an automatic one-hectare prohibition**, proof of on-site local population size, or a substitute for s139 conservation advice. The [2022 recovery plan](https://www.dcceew.gov.au/environment/biodiversity/threatened/publications/recovery/koala-2022) identifies connectivity and genetic resilience. Their **original ecological status** must be reconciled with project-specific field surveys, approved corridor functioning and conditions.

## Physical causation evidence needed — no model inference as measurement

The **baseline / marginal-impact model** is a falsifiable ordered graph:

1. **Before 2019/8575**: actual mapped remnants, pre-existing approved/cleared areas and the connected Woogaroo Creek–Opossum Creek koala corridor, with vegetation type, tree age/canopy, habitat-use detections and barriers.
2. **Action itself**: exact signed federal approved footprint/stages plus Council 9281/9293 clearing polygons, tree/fencing/roads, fauna crossing measures and prestart clearances.
3. **Predicted marginal physical response**: changes in viable movement routes, mature feeding/shade/thermal refuge trees, habitat capacity, road/dog risk and local population connectivity, with expert uncertainty and effect sizes.
4. **Mitigation/conditions**: operative corridor geometry and function, not just hectares, and the time-lag/uncertainty between mature-tree loss and future offset benefits.
5. **Decision comparison**: what the final 95B response and 95C/reasons actually considered against each physical factor; trace errors to the controlling EPBC provision and materiality.
6. **Field protection trigger**: credible dated works/contractor/start evidence and actual condition mismatch → focused regulator or court relief, not general stop-work allegation.

Do NOT pool the letter's **136ha direct/26ha indirect** claim, developer's **128ha** limit, visually estimated Stage 1–16 **~41.74ha**, GIS analytical overlap **~8.28ha**, and separate Scenic **24.2ha** into a single measured impact denominator. They are different author, directness, stage, plan and legal action identity. Field presence or death toll has not been measured in this repo.

**Strongest falsifying defence:** the correct approved koala advice and recovery plan were indeed before the 15 Oct decision-maker; the final PD and full 95C report actually dealt with local baseline and functional corridors, the lawful conditions and offsets responded rationally, and works follow accepted Queensland prestart conditions. If that original chain is paid, do not claim a federal legal defect solely from preference for a stronger conservation outcome.
