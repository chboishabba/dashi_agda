Examination of the complete **section 95B preliminary documentation/public comment set**, the Secretary's **section 95C recommendation report** obtainable via **section 135A(2)**, and the actual final conditions under **sections 136/139**. Whether any **source-supported likely federal contravention** and imminent serious damage to a protected matter warrants a *separate National EPA compliance referral*, under the **already-operative section 474A Environment Protection Order power** (effective 24 August 2026), with National EPA rather than a private applicant deciding if the test is met.
