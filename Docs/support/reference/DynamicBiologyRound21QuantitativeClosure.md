# Round 21 quantitative closure

This addendum records the theorem-bearing pieces added after the first Round-21 reference note.

## DNA chemistry -> regulatory operator

`DNAChemistryRegulatoryOperatorBridgeExact` reuses the repository owner `DNAChemistryEnergyAlgebra` rather than inventing an unrelated DNA-energy layer.

The finite operational regression gives

```text
AT -> H = 0
GC -> H = 2
```

under one explicit GC-weighted operational model, then compiles

```text
(accessibility, H) -> regulatory parameter -> cell channel state.
```

The same DNA sequence/energy with different accessibility produces different regulatory cell parameters. The module explicitly records that the operational energy is not a complete quantum Hamiltonian and that energy alone does not determine expression.

This closes the requested architectural bridge

```text
DNA sequence -> local chemistry/operator field -> context-sensitive regulation.
```

## Morphogenetic target capacity

`MorphogeneticControlFieldCapacityExact` gives three independent binary high-level target constraints:

```text
attachment × differentiation × junction.
```

The target carrier has exactly eight explicit states and is exactly equivalent to a three-bit control word. Forgetting the junction bit merges two distinct targets; retaining that one fibre-local residual reopens the full target exactly.

This is a finite exact information-capacity theorem, not a Shannon capacity result for biological tissue.

## Iterated attractor scale lift

`IteratedAttractorScaleLiftExact` proves a finite composition

```text
molecular repair attractor
  -> effective cellular regime
  -> tissue connectivity attractor
  -> integrated organ observation.
```

Every molecular/tissue starting pair in the finite regression reaches the integrated organ regime. The construction is intentionally one-way: a higher-scale attractor does not uniquely reconstruct the lower-scale microstate.

## Abiogenesis inevitability boundary

`AbiogenesisInevitabilityBoundaryExact` supplies explicit countermodels:

```text
driven + persistent + not replicating
```

and

```text
replicating + heritable variation + no corrective feedback.
```

Thus neither driven persistence nor replication is silently promoted to life/agency. A separate finite kinetic-selection example has two populations tied after one step but the faster self-amplifier strictly ahead after two. This still does not imply probability-one abiogenesis.

Sources:

- Jeremy L. England, *Statistical physics of self-replication*, DOI `10.1063/1.4818538`.
- Sumantra Sarkar and Jeremy L. England, *Design of conditions for self-replication*, DOI `10.1103/PhysRevE.100.022414`.

## Four-state DNA phase -> ternary observation + residual

`DNAHelixTernaryQuantizationResidualExact` formalizes the requested anti-numerological bridge.

The fine finite helix phase has four states. The coarse observation has three C3 states. Two fine phases share the third trit, so the projection is genuinely lossy. A dependent residual is empty/singleton on the first two fibres and two-valued on the overloaded third fibre. The exact reopening theorem is

```text
reopen (quantizePhase p) (residual p) = p.
```

Therefore the repo does not assert `4 = 3`; it proves

```text
fine phase -> ternary observation + minimal local distinction needed for reopening.
```

This is the DNA-specific analogue of the general PNF residual principle.
