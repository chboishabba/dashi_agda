# Conscious-access round five: SSP weave completion

This completion replaces the isolated `53 -> 47` reading with the stronger
geometric and executable construction developed after PR #399 was opened.

## Canonical 53-sector

The repository already contains the `6 x 9 = 54` carrier. The completion splits
its mode space into one invariant mode and the reduced carrier:

```text
54 = 1 + 53.
```

Thus the canonical DASHI residual is the reduced/mean-zero/nonconstant sector,
not an independently inserted Ogg-prime lane.

The resulting arithmetic is exact:

```text
196884 = 10 * 3^9 + 54,
196883 = 10 * 3^9 + (54 - 1).
```

Monster equivariance, irreducibility, graded characters, and modularity remain
explicitly open.

## Signed fifteen-SSP valuations

All fifteen Ogg/SSP lanes are represented. Each lane has negative, zero, and
positive multiplicity. Negative multiplicity is the typed analogue of a
FRACTRAN denominator or inverse fibre transport, not a sixteenth prime.

Alternative arithmetic compilations such as

```text
53 = 47 + 5 + 1,
59 + 1 = 53 + 7,
71 + 1 = 53 + 19
```

remain available, while `54 - 1` is selected by the declared geometric
normal-form cost.

Program length, execution length, normal-form length, type-witness length, and
residual-witness length are separate quantities. The finite cost is not
promoted to exact Kolmogorov complexity.

## Fifteen-SSP hyperfibre symmetry atlas

The SSPs now index an actual dependent family rather than only arithmetic
coordinates. Each lane has its own:

- fibre carrier;
- symmetry carrier and action;
- residual carrier;
- path type and transport;
- `3/6/9` resolution;
- declared structural-complexity rank.

The finite witness assigns lower lanes to resolution 3, middle lanes to
resolution 6, and upper lanes to resolution 9. Inverse transport reverses
polarity while retaining an inverse-path residual. The generic atlas permits
heterogeneous fibres; numerical prime size alone is not promoted to a theorem
of physical complexity.

## Self-indexing hyperfabric

The strong tower is represented at the type level:

```text
I_0       = Unit,
I_(n + 1) = I_n -> Fin 9.
```

Its cardinality recursion agrees with fixed-base nine tetration. This remains
distinct from ordinary product growth and does not supply physical dynamics by
itself.

## Equivariant spectral sectors

A reusable commuting-action theorem proves that an operator eigenspace is
stable under any symmetry action commuting with both the operator and scalar
action. The interface is reusable in grokking, neural, diffusion, NS, YM, and
quantum-excitation lanes, but it does not replace their concrete operators,
support geometry, coefficients, or analytic estimates.

The five `D4` species have explicit role labels:

```text
A1  invariant/global;
A2  orientation/pseudoscalar;
B1  axial contrast;
B2  diagonal contrast;
E   two-dimensional directional pair.
```

Candidate application records type an `A1` global/vacuum-like lane, opposite
`A2` orientations for positive/negative NS helicity, an `A1` gauge-invariant
candidate, and an `E` neural directional pair. These are reusable mode roles,
not identifications of concrete physics across domains.

## Oriented-zero jets

The oriented-zero carrier is refined with finite velocity, acceleration, and
passage data. The checked passage kinds distinguish:

- approach without crossing;
- negative-to-positive and positive-to-negative crossing;
- touch-and-reflect on either side;
- sticking at zero.

Reflection reverses the oriented carrier and derivative signs. Coarse zero can
therefore agree while the first/second jet and passage history differ. This is
a finite germ interface, not a solved continuum ODE or hysteresis model.

## Reachable sectors and entropy ceilings

Raw syntactic configurations are separated from the orbit generated by legal
object-level moves. Direct pole jumps, arbitrary component manipulation, and
carrier mismatches are represented as typed violation witnesses rather than
silently admitted transitions.

The finite witness has four raw configurations and three object-level reachable
configurations. This checks the accessible-state ceiling but deliberately does
not identify a state count with Shannon entropy without a probability measure.

## Exceptional-lattice grokking protocol

E8 and Leech comparisons now have fixed benchmark records:

```text
E8:    dimension 8,  shortest-vector count 240;
```

A valid comparison must declare the embedding dimension, potential family,
task loss, geometry metric, null geometries, held-out evaluation, seeds, and
multiple-comparison treatment before observing a match. Matching dimensions or
vector counts alone does not prove lattice isometry.
