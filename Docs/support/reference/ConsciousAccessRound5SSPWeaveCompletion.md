# Conscious-access round five: SSP weave completion

This completion replaces the isolated `53 -> 47` reading with the stronger
geometric and executable construction developed after PR #399 was opened.

## Canonical 53-sector

The repository already contains the `6 x 9 = 54` carrier. The completion splits
its mode space into one invariant mode and the reduced carrier:

```text
54 = 1 + 53.
```

Thus the canonical DASHI residual is the reduced/mean-zero/nonconstant sector,
not an independently inserted Ogg-prime lane.

The resulting arithmetic is exact:

```text
196884 = 10 * 3^9 + 54,
196883 = 10 * 3^9 + (54 - 1).
```

Monster equivariance, irreducibility, graded characters, and modularity remain
explicitly open.

## Signed fifteen-SSP valuations

All fifteen Ogg/SSP lanes are represented. Each lane has negative, zero, and
positive multiplicity. Negative multiplicity is the typed analogue of a
FRACTRAN denominator or inverse fibre transport, not a sixteenth prime.

Alternative arithmetic compilations such as

```text
53 = 47 + 5 + 1,
59 + 1 = 53 + 7,
71 + 1 = 53 + 19
```

remain available, while `54 - 1` is selected by the declared geometric
normal-form cost.

Program length, execution length, normal-form length, type-witness length, and
residual-witness length are separate quantities. The finite cost is not
promoted to exact Kolmogorov complexity.

## Self-indexing hyperfabric

The strong tower is represented at the type level:

```text

I_(n + 1) = I_n -> Fin 9.
```

Its cardinality recursion agrees with fixed-base nine tetration. This remains
distinct from ordinary product growth and does not supply physical dynamics by

## Equivariant spectral sectors

A reusable commuting-action theorem proves that an operator eigenspace is
stable under any symmetry action commuting with both the operator and scalar
action. The interface is reusable in grokking, neural, diffusion, NS, YM, and
quantum-excitation lanes, but it does not replace their concrete operators,
support geometry, coefficients, or analytic estimates.
