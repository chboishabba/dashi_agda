# Narrative provenance, phase capture, and cultural boundary repair — Round 15

This tranche uses the Kendrick Lamar / Drake battle and the consecutive Kendrick Lamar / Bad Bunny Super Bowl cultural stages as worked stress tests for the existing PNF / terminalisation / dynamic-sufficiency machinery.  It does not promote disputed lyrical allegations into theorem premises.

## 1. Claim hypergraph and evidence lineage

`DASHI.Cognition.PNF.NarrativeClaimProvenanceExact` makes a narrative claim an edge with separate subject, proposition, source, evidence, provenance, and modality.

The key exact result is that repetition is not independent corroboration.  `replicateEvidence` increments repetition count but preserves `originId`; `replicationDoesNotCreateIndependentEvidence` proves that a copied item cannot simultaneously satisfy the supplied distinct-origin witness against its own source lineage.

`NarrativeSignal` adds a salience coordinate.  Replication raises salience while preserving the original evidence lineage.  This is the finite theorem behind the distinction

`same allegation repeated widely != independent evidence`.

The same module keeps factual, rhetorical, cultural, and institutional state in separate types.  Updating rhetorical, cultural, or institutional state preserves the factual coordinate definitionally.

## 2. The diss-track corpus as an operator sequence

`DASHI.Cognition.PNF.DissTrackNarrativeDynamicsExact` separates three corpus rings:

- causal prehistory;
- direct 2024 battle;
- adjacent war.

The prehistory ring includes the artifacts explicitly identified in the supplied analysis, including `Buried Alive Interlude`, `Poetic Justice`, `Fuckin' Problems`, the `Control` verse, the 2013 BET cypher, `The Language`, `King Kunta`, `100`, `ELEMENT.`, `The Hillbillies`, and `First Person Shooter`.

The direct ring is

`Like That -> Push Ups -> Taylor Made Freestyle -> Euphoria -> 6:16 in LA -> Family Matters -> Meet the Grahams -> Not Like Us -> The Heart Part 6`.

The module does not treat all artifacts as performing the same operation.  It assigns distinct operator roles: ranking proposal/rejection, commercial counter, response-phase pressure, authenticity reframe, provenance attack, hidden-fibre attack, relational-fibre attack, categorical compression, and evidentiary reopening.

The exact finite phase-capture witness models `Family Matters` as opening one public response window and `Meet the Grahams` as moving that window to a distinct captured phase before stabilization.  `phaseCaptureChangesPublicState` proves the composite state is not the state produced by the first update alone.  `orderChangesOutcome` separately proves that reversing two abstract update orders changes the public phase in the model.

## 3. Consumer-indexed projection

`DASHI.Cognition.PNF.ConsumerIndexedNarrativeProjectionExact` gives one fine cultural artifact four independent codomains:

- factual;
- rhetorical;
- cultural;
- institutional.

The `Not Like Us` worked surface has a factual coordinate deliberately left `factUnresolved` by this module, a high-impact rhetorical coordinate, a replicated-anthem cultural coordinate, and the `nonActionableOpinion` institutional disposition corresponding to the 2025 district-court ruling.

There is no constructor from `nonActionableOpinion` to either factual support or factual refutation.  Therefore

`not actionable defamation != allegations judicially proven true or false`

is enforced at the type boundary rather than merely stated in prose.

## 4. Description-length transmission asymmetry

`DASHI.Cognition.PNF.NarrativeMDLReplicationExact` gives a deliberately tiny MDL witness.  A three-unit compact classifier fits a three-unit broadcast budget while a four-unit reopening explanation does not.  The code-length values are a finite toy, not a measured semantic-complexity claim.

The module separately makes `CompressionWinner` and `TruthCoordinate` independent.  A shorter description has no constructor that settles factual truth.

Reference:

Jorma Rissanen, *Modeling by shortest data description*, Automatica 14(5) (1978), 465-471. DOI `10.1016/0005-1098(78)90005-5`.

## 5. Amplification without evidence promotion

`DASHI.Cognition.PNF.NarrativeAmplificationWithoutEvidencePromotionExact` composes the claim-lineage and four-plane structures.

Starting from one unresolved low-salience narrative, one amplification step:

- preserves the evidence origin;
- raises salience;
- preserves the factual coordinate;
- changes rhetorical state;
- changes cultural state.

`canonicalAmplificationSeparation` packages the exact witness.  This is the strongest formal version in this tranche of the observation that participatory repetition can move a cultural classifier without manufacturing new independent evidence.

## 6. Kendrick / Bad Bunny / state-boundary cross-pollination

`DASHI.Cognition.PNF.CulturalMembershipBoundaryExact` keeps coarse membership sign, provenance, membership move, and downstream action separately typed.

The Kendrick-like cultural-boundary model and a state-enforcement boundary share the same coarse `outside` sign, but have different provenance and different action projections.  `coarseBoundaryCannotDetermineAllActions` proves there is no single action function factoring through the inside/outside sign for the model roles.  This is the exact theorem behind

`same us/them classifier != same politics, provenance, or consequence`.

The Bad Bunny-inspired operator instead widens the evaluation domain while preserving local Puerto Rican/Caribbean provenance.  `wideningPreservesProvenance` proves expansion need not erase difference.

Threatened, planned, and actual enforcement are three distinct constructors; no transition among them is silently promoted.

## 7. New master separation

The combined finite result is now stronger than `compression does not commute with causal inference`.

A narrative system has at least four independent quantities:

1. proposition-local evidentiary state;
2. provenance/source independence;
3. rhetorical/cultural replication state;
4. action licensed by a membership classifier.

A change in 3 does not entail a change in 1 or 2, and equality of a coarse membership sign does not determine 4.

That gives a precise anti-terminalisation requirement for public narratives:

**high replication and low description length may explain why a classifier stabilizes, but they cannot substitute for proposition-local evidence, and a coarse boundary label is dynamically unsafe whenever its collapsed provenance changes downstream action.**

## Boundary

No theorem here establishes the truth of disputed allegations in the Kendrick/Drake battle, exhaustively enumerates every song ever interpreted as part of the feud, proves a sociological law of audience behaviour, identifies Kendrick Lamar with state coercion, or asserts that Bad Bunny's cultural inclusion strategy is the unique healthy political model.  The tranche formalizes the distinctions required to keep those questions separate.
