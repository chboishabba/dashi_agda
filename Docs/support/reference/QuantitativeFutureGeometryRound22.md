# Quantitative Future Geometry — Round 22

Round 22 moves the cumulative PNF/dynamic-biology branch from architecture to quantitative theorem surfaces.  The organizing distinction is

`future quotient -> minimum rate -> geometric realization -> update/reopen cost`.

It deliberately keeps several authority boundaries separate: finite stochastic chemistry is not a probability-one abiogenesis theorem; finite conservative electrodiffusion is not continuum PNP well-posedness; finite control regressions are not measured universal organ-control capacity; ideal B-DNA parameters are not universal sequence-independent DNA geometry; finite clopen Cantor premeasure is not sigma-additive measure extension; and exact C3 algebra is not a claim that arbitrary neural networks learn C3 characters.

## Stochastic chemistry and committors

`FiniteStochasticReactionCommittorExact` provides a normalized rational finite Markov regression with extinction and proto-life absorbing outcomes and an exact committor. `FiniteChemicalMasterGeneratorExact` gives the continuous-time jump generator in the form

`L f(x) = sum_y r(x,y) (f(y) - f(x))`

and proves the embedded committor is harmonic at the transient states. `CommittorConsumerFutureStatisticExact` then connects this to PNF: equality of the declared hitting-event future law need not determine richer catalytic provenance.

Primary source metadata retained in the Agda lane:

- Daniel T. Gillespie, **Exact stochastic simulation of coupled chemical reactions**, *The Journal of Physical Chemistry* 81 (1977), 2340–2361. DOI `10.1021/j100540a008`.
- Jeremy L. England, **Statistical physics of self-replication**, *The Journal of Chemical Physics* 139 (2013). DOI `10.1063/1.4818538`.

`FiniteFluctuationRelationExact` proves a multiplicative finite path-likelihood ratio from local forward/reverse factors. It does not identify the rational factor with `exp(Delta S_tot)`; that remains an analytic stochastic-thermodynamics obligation.

## Electrodiffusion, moving boundaries and free-energy bookkeeping

`FiniteElectrodiffusiveMovingBoundaryExact` proves exact finite-volume conservation of material and fixed-valence charge under transport, Reynolds-style material/volume accounting under boundary growth, passive stored-energy descent in a concrete regression, and active energy balance with explicit metabolic input.

`FinitePNPFreeEnergyDissipationExact` separates conservative transport from passive free-energy descent and metabolically driven restoration of a concentration gradient.

Source-facing anchors:

- A. L. Hodgkin and A. F. Huxley, **A quantitative description of membrane current and its application to conduction and excitation in nerve**, *Journal of Physiology* 117 (1952). DOI `10.1113/jphysiol.1952.sp004764`.
- Alan M. Turing, **The Chemical Basis of Morphogenesis**, *Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society B* 237 (1952). DOI `10.1098/rstb.1952.0012`.

These finite laws do not assert Poisson–Nernst–Planck existence, uniqueness, positivity, moving-domain regularity, or topology-changing PDE well-posedness.

## B-DNA calibration

`BDNACalibratedHelicalGeometryExact` replaces the earlier four-phase physical reading by the six conventional base-pair-step slots `(shift, slide, rise, tilt, roll, twist)`, with an ideal-reference exact regression at rise 3.4 Å, twist 36 degrees, 10 bp/turn and 34 Å pitch. It proves the ten-step rise and twist identities and supplies sequence/environment-dependent and SE(3)-realization interfaces.

Source metadata:

- Wilma K. Olson et al., **A standard reference frame for the description of nucleic acid base-pair geometry**, *Journal of Molecular Biology* 313 (2001), 229–237. DOI `10.1006/jmbi.2001.4987`.
- Li-Yan Yu et al., **Structures of an all-alpha protein running along the DNA major groove**, *Nucleic Acids Research* 44 (2016), 3936–3946. DOI `10.1093/nar/gkw133`.

The present exact composition is the axial screw subgroup used for the calibration regression. Full noncommutative SE(3), sequence-specific empirical step tables, elastic-rod energetics and the Călugăreanu–White–Fuller linking theorem remain separate geometric/analytic owners.

## Exact C3^3 Fourier closure

`C3CubeCyclotomicFourierExact` constructs `Q(omega)` algebraically in the basis `1, omega` with `omega^2 + omega + 1 = 0`. The one-dimensional three-point transform is proved invertible in both directions and tensorized to arbitrary 27-value signals on `C3^3`.

`C3CubeCyclotomicParsevalExact` proves the unnormalised identity

`energy(F f) = 27 * energy(f)`

and normalized Parseval.

`C3DirectGroupConvolutionExact` proves the ordinary direct cyclic convolution theorem on C3. `C3CubeDirectGroupConvolutionExact` lifts it tensorially to ordinary product-group convolution on C3^3 and proves

`F(f * g) = F(f) pointwise* F(g)`

followed by exact equality of direct and inverse-transform-defined spectral convolution.

This closes the earlier gap between a 27-character indexing scaffold and an actual lossless Fourier coordinate transform with orthogonality, inversion, Parseval and convolution diagonalization.

## Task-action geometry and grokking

`TaskActionRepresentationGeometryExact` defines a generic equivariant representation square

`CyclotomicGrokkingActionLinearizationExact` connects the existing finite modular-addition grokking task to the exact cyclotomic carrier: for every C3 input pair, modular addition becomes multiplication of roots of unity. Training-set agreement of the memorizer still does not determine the held-out structural extension.

## Rate–future-distortion–geometry optimization

`FinitePathTransitionOptimalityExact` proves the generic positive-edge path lower bound and unit-edge attainment. `WaveGrayLocalGlobalGeometryExact` adds the important separation that Gray is locally transition-optimal on Wave4 while contracting the long-range endpoint path distance. Thus one-step update geometry and all-pairs metric preservation are distinct objectives.

`FutureGeometryEmbeddingExact` supplies abstract Lipschitz, co-Lipschitz and bi-Lipschitz representation certificates and proves composition of upper geometry bounds with multiplicative constants.

`RateFutureTransitionParetoExact` implements the nested optimization:

1. minimize representation rate subject to future distortion tolerance;
2. among equal-rate feasible candidates, minimize transition cost;
3. among remaining ties, optionally minimize reopen cost.

The exact Wave4 regression proves that binary and Gray are both rate-2 / zero-future-distortion candidates while Gray is strictly better under the transition objective.

This realizes the Round-19 frontier distinction `quotient + rate + geometry` rather than collapsing all representation quality into MDL.

## Expected fibre-local rates

`GenericExpectedFibreRateExact` generalizes the signed-zero `0/1/0` calculation to finite rational fibre-rate laws. It proves constant local rate factors through total probability mass and, for the oriented-zero local widths, the normalized expected additional residual rate equals the probability mass of the zero fibre.

This is finite expected fixed-width coding. It is deliberately not a Shannon prefix-code theorem and does not yet identify the optimum with `H(Q_future | Y_coarse)`.

## Dynamic multimodal and cache geometry

`DynamicMultimodalTransitionGeometryExact` adds a fourth notion beyond reconstruction/current-task/future-output equivalence: if every latent query update is non-expansive, initial text/visual latent proximity is preserved through every finite update trace.

`CacheUpdateReopenGeometryExact` gives an exact counterexample showing that equal storage and equal future-output distortion do not determine operational cost: two finite cache schemes have identical storage and output distortion but different update/reopen costs. The numerical regression is not an empirical LLM benchmark.

## Probabilistic future refinement

`StableProbabilisticObservationFutureBridgeExact` uses probability-valued observations with deterministic action dynamics. A stable finite partition relation is proved to imply equality of the complete trace-indexed rational probability future kernel. This is a probabilistic-observation future quotient; full stochastic-transition probabilistic bisimulation remains stronger.

## Cantor finite-clopen extension

`CantorFiniteClopenPremeasureExact` moves beyond individual cylinders. At arbitrary finite depth it represents every union of binary cylinders, defines complement and union, proves complement mass, finite additivity for proof-relevant disjoint events, and proves that the canonical refinement into the next layer preserves mass and Boolean structure.

Thus the repository now has a directed system of finite clopen Boolean algebras carrying a consistent finitely additive rational probability premeasure. The remaining Cantor wall is specifically extension to the generated sigma-algebra with countable additivity; that theorem is not inferred from finite consistency.
