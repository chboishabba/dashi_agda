## Genuine source and mathematical result

The existing `DASHI/Moonshine/Y443CoxeterPresentationExact.agda` defines a 12-node connected Coxeter graph with **11 edges**, **55 nonedges**, 12 involution squares, 66 pair relators, one length-90 spider relator, and the length-63 extra central relation; the 79/80 relator word-length totals are 400/463.

In an abelian `C₂` target, every node involution has Boolean parity `χ(n)`. In a pair relator `(xy)^k=e`, an **odd** exponent gives `χ(x)+χ(y)=0 mod 2`, while even exponents impose no condition. Thus the 11 original Coxeter edges force equal parity across their connected tree, leaving one possible common bit among all 12 node values. The length-90 spider relation is even and adds no `C₂` condition.

The original central relator `(a b3 c3 d3 b1 c1 b2)^9=e` has an **odd** number (7) of node occurrences repeated an odd number of times. Once the Coxeter edges identify all node bits, it forces `7χ(a)=χ(a)=0 mod2`. Therefore:

- Original 79-relator presentation: **exactly two group homomorphisms to C₂** (the trivial and all-generator-sign maps).
- Original 80-relator presentation: **only the trivial homomorphism to C₂**.

The latter does **not** establish the full abelianization is trivial over every abelian group by itself; for a group generated by involutions, though, its abelianization is an elementary abelian 2-group, so the absence of nontrivial `C₂` characters does imply **trivial abelianization / perfectness** of the universal 80-relator group. This is an actual consequence of its source relations. It is consistent with the Monster being perfect, but also with many other perfect groups, so is not an isomorphism theorem.
