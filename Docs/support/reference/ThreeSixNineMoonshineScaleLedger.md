## Distinct presentations

The equalities do not identify their structure.

- `10 = 1 + 9` is realised by one unmarked ternary sheet plus nine choices of marked site.
- `10 = 5 * 2` is realised by five D4 irrep species with two declared orientations.
- `54 = 6 * 9` is the existing six-by-nine mode carrier.
- `54 = 2 * 3^3` is a triadic arithmetic presentation.
- `54 = 1 + 53` is the invariant/reduced-mode splitting.

The raw nine-site permutation representation has no `A2` constituent, even though the five-species irrep/orientation atlas includes an `A2` role. The latter therefore requires an orientation-sensitive refinement rather than being inferred from `5 * 2 = 10`.

## Bridge discipline

The module records cardinality-preserving bridges separately from action- and interpretation-preserving bridges. It blocks the following promotions:

```text
same numeral -> same group action
five irrep names -> five raw constituents
six-by-nine cardinality -> chosen Laplacian adjacency
coefficient arithmetic -> Moonshine module.
```

Any stronger identification must provide an explicit carrier map and the appropriate commuting action, observer, or intertwiner.
