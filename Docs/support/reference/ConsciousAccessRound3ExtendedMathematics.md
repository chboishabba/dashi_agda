## 1. Natural-growth algorithm atlas

`NaturalGrowthAlgorithmAtlas.agda` formalises the supplied family of generative mechanisms as distinct update laws rather than one generic organic-pattern label.

### L-system rewriting

The axiom

```text
apex
```

rewrites in parallel to

```text
internode, left branch, apex, right branch, apex
```

with an exact equality witness. A context-sensitive rewrite proves that the same apex expands differently in open and inhibited contexts. A parametric bud also proves that internode growth can consume a stored resource.

### Space colonisation

Three environmental attraction points are assigned to candidate tips. The left tip claims two points, the right tip one, and the root none. The left tip is proved maximal over the complete tip type.

### Phyllotactic inhibition

Four candidate primordium sites are scored by:

```text
transported activation - inhibition from existing primordia
```

The south site has score `4` and is proved maximal. This is a finite activation/inhibition theorem, not a literal golden-angle counter or calibrated auxin model.

### Eden growth

Three frontier cells carry occupied-neighbour counts `1`, `3`, and `2`. The cell with count `3` is proved maximal.

### Diffusion-limited aggregation

A finite walker moves:

```text
far -> near -> contact
```

and first contact changes the aggregate from seed to grown.

### Iterated function systems

Three recursive contraction applications are proved to produce contraction depth `3`. The module records IFS recursion as a separate generator from biological growth, despite possible shared fractal morphology.

### Functional-structural growth

A productive resource budget can grow after maintenance, while a starved budget cannot. This keeps physiology/resource allocation separate from grammar, attraction, and stochastic accretion.

Finally, L-system and space-colonisation algorithms are proved to share a branching morphology while remaining unequal constructors:

```text
same morphology != same generator
```

## 2. Signed-distance Boolean geometry


The canonical perforated room proves:

