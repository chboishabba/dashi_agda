This tranche formalizes the process-bearing interpretation developed alongside the Monster 3B/nonary work. It does not promote the interpretation to a theorem about the Monster, Navier--Stokes, Yang--Mills, psychology, or any physical wave system.

## Signed membership rather than Boolean complement

A Boolean predicate `isGood` proves membership in one selected set. Its `false` output does not distinguish semantic opposition from exterior, openness, or non-classification. The exact local carrier is therefore:

```text
memberOfInverse | notMemberOfEither | memberOfGood
```

Both `memberOfInverse` and `notMemberOfEither` project to `isGood = false`, while `isInverse` separates them. Zero remains fibred by `ZeroKind`.

## Layered 3/6/9 attractors

`Horizon3` contains state, transport, and result. `Depth6` contains immediate and medium horizons. `Depth9` contains the first six coordinates and the long horizon. The projections preserve the immediate layer definitionally.

A `HorizonDrift` may therefore be positive immediately and adverse at medium and long depth. Local completion is not promoted to long-horizon success.

## Continuous, wave, and n-ary fibres

`AddressedFibre` leaves the address and its dependent fibre arbitrary. A ternary address can therefore index a continuous field, wave, distribution, operator, or another finite/infinite carrier. Balanced ternary is used as a compact signed local address, not as an ontological restriction.

## Address symmetry and propagated asymmetry

`OperatorEquivariance` states that local operation commutes with an address transport. `PropagationEquivariance` is a distinct record. No theorem promotes the first into the second: a role swap can preserve the local operator while later authority, capacity, dependency, or environmental propagation breaks the symmetry.

## Tree, DAG, and braid geometry

`OpenHoleTree` represents unresolved typed refinement. `ProvenanceDAG` permits different histories to reach one retained state. `ConstrainedBraid` keeps strands distinct while restricting crossings and endpoint transport. The global carrier may contain all three; no single geometry is asserted universally.

## Process branches and attractor relevance

`ProcessBranch` carries accumulated state, provenance, liveness, servicing cost, information value, diversion cost, and immediate/medium/long direction. `GoalProcessState` distinguishes an absent terminal outcome from an empty process state.

`BeneficialBranchAddition` requires proof-bearing serviceability, attractor relevance, and controlled interference. Mere liveness or affordability does not construct branch benefit.

## Exact boundaries

- that every Boolean function is semantically ternary;

- that `11`, `369`, or a ternary path alone determines a physical theory;
