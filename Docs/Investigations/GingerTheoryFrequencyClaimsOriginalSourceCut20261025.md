### Underlying paper correction and positive RF research status

1. **Sirav & Seyhan 2009**, `Blood-Brain Barrier Disruption by Continuous-Wave Radio Frequency Radiation`, DOI https://doi.org/10.1080/15368370802608738, PMID 19811403. Rat 900/1800 MHz continuous-wave 20-min exposure; BBB permeability increased in **male**, not female, rats under that study protocol. No 20–250 Hz **sound** intervention, exposed human neighborhood or combined industrial source.
2. **Nittby, Grafström, Eberhardt, Malmgren, Brun, Persson & Salford 2008**, DOI https://doi.org/10.1080/15368370802061995, PMID 18568929, is a narrative literature review of RF/ELF BBB effects, not an independent data-centre acoustic field study.
3. **Taspinar Simsek et al. 2026**, DOI https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6560/ae6e17, PMID 42134407, identified **17** studies reporting increased BBB permeability or molecular alterations and **18** reporting no effects, with dose/temperature/sham weaknesses and sparse human evidence. Authors conclude a causal link remains unestablished. Do not count the review as 35 new independent studies without ancestry checks.
4. **WHO-linked 2024 RF reproductive evidence**, https://www.arpansa.gov.au/who-systematic-review-rf-eme-exposure-and-male-fertility, should be distinguished from commercial WaveBlock article and from a clinical proof that **acoustic** exposure causes infertility.
5. **Dinse et al.** corrected 2022 NHANES study (DOI `10.1002/art.42330`; public corrected manuscript https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10947520/). Original 2020 DOI `10.1002/art.41214` retracted/replaced for data corrections by the authors, journal, ACR and Wiley. Corrected denominators **13,519** samples, not original 14,211. Corrected total prevalence **11.0, 11.4, 16.1%** and therefore relative growth **(16.1/11.0 - 1)*100 ≈ 46.36%**, not 300%. Even 300% in one subgroup would not prove a national average, a diagnosis or an exposure link.
6. **Wheeler et al. 2026**, DOI `10.3390/cleantechnol8040126`, is a *narrative* data-centre LFN review explicitly calling for primary calibrated operating-facility spectral measurement. It supplies no direct source-specific exposure-to-clinical endpoint study.
7. **2026 systematic review**, DOI `10.3390/environments13100568`, includes **36 studies/112 quantitative estimates**, none with directly **observed** exposed-population health outcomes; this is a scoped literature negative finding, **not** proof the true health effect is zero.

## Physical discriminator and reproducible acceptance sequence

**A. Source identity:** machine/time/load log, microphone/magnetometer/accelerometer calibrations, exact apparatus version and provenance, including data/log hashes.
**B. Wave propagation:** actual frequency-resolved airborne pressure from source to receiver, structure-borne motion separately, background/road/wind/near-field controls, phase/coherence and spatial gradient.
**C. Exposure physiology:** pre-registered blinded active/sham or natural experiment under measured and comparable receptor doses; separate audible/infra, vibration, expectation and other changes.
**D. RF separately:** MHz/GHz electric/magnetic exposure + spatial polarization/duty and SAR/thermal context; distinguish original animal exposure protocol from observational human susceptibility and sound.
**E. *Interaction* claim:** a factorial 2×2 crossover, sham/sham vs sound-only vs RF-only vs combined, with calibrated matched exposure and pre-specified interaction contrast `Y11 - Y10 - Y01 + Y00`; any observed interaction is not automatically intentional manipulation. Protect participants and use ethical review where human exposures are contemplated.
**F. Historical/deployment claim:** original dated programme document -> apparatus -> operator -> technical waveform or exposure -> independently confirmed modern same-technology transfer and deployment. Association, rhetoric, supplier involvement and shared name do not suffice.
**G. Application response:** if independent acoustic harm/nuisance is observed, pursue mitigation without needing to prove extraordinary historical assertions. Industrial energy/heat recovery is an **optional alternative mechanism**, not downstream proof of B–F.

All checks retain null, ambiguous, positive and negative measured outcomes. A failed inference does not invalidate documented testimony, and a genuine complaint does not automatically identify its physical cause.

## Runnable interaction discriminator (separate from safety or experimental authority)

`scripts/frequency_joint_interaction_audit.py` computes the **descriptive** four-arm contrast
`(combined - sound-only) - (RF-only - sham)` on separate independent experimental units. Each row requires the same `study_id`, `protocol_ref` and `calibration_ref`, with unique unit identifiers, binary arm labels, finite outcomes and at least two observations per arm. It rejects cross-study/changed-instrument silent joins, duplicates, missing factorial cells and NaNs. Input receipts are locators, **not authenticated originals**. The tool reports a descriptive standard error but refuses causal, physiological, field-coupling or intentional-deployment promotion.
