- the theory/data she planned to present depended on foundational work generated by a team member while a NASA MSFC civil servant;
- that work had later been matured privately by The Institute;
- the NASA-origin work needed NASA approval for public release before the Institute could discuss its subsequent results publicly; and

A later provenance review (Troth Record TR-0013) independently notes the existence of the archived 2020 screenshot but also stresses the limits: the artifact supplies no paper title, NASA case number, NASA review receipt, approval decision, or located final publication.

Therefore the evidence supports a stronger state than generic disclosure advocacy:

`bounded/private (self-reported) -> release under review (self-reported)`

but it does **not** yet establish:

`bounded/private -> released publicly`.
