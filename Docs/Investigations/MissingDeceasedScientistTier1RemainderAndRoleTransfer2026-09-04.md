## Michael David Hicks

- AAS Division for Planetary Sciences and University of Arizona memorial material place Hicks at JPL from 1998 through 2022 and give his death as 30 July 2023.
- Therefore his departure from JPL preceded his death by roughly a year.
- Any claim that his **death** disrupted an active JPL role must first establish that he still carried active mission responsibilities after leaving JPL. Otherwise the appropriate disruption event is his 2022 departure, not his 2023 death.
- The located institutional memorials do not provide a public cause of death.

Sources:
- AAS DPS obituary: https://dps.aas.org/obituaries/michael-david-hicks-1964-2023
- University of Arizona LPL memorial: https://lpl.arizona.edu/about/memoriam/michael-hicks

**Current effect on hypotheses:** Hicks is a negative pressure point for any theory that assumes every death/disappearance removed an active technical bottleneck at the moment of the event.

# Cross-roster update

The empirical picture is becoming less compatible with a single coarse statement such as “a set of active irreplaceable scientists were removed and their programmes stopped.” At least four distinct situations now exist:

1. **Programme continued after event:** PSFC/Loureiro, Scorpius/Chavez, FSP/LeBlanc.
2. **Technical employment ended before death:** Hicks.
3. **Concrete local criminal explanation currently has direct official support:** Grillmair.
4. **Role is non-scientific or not yet established as scientific:** Casias, Garcia, Sullivan.

That does not eliminate a narrower common pattern. It makes the next test more demanding and more useful:

- identify the **specific local responsibility** lost in each technically relevant case;
- measure its transfer/replacement latency;
- compare that with ordinary matched departures;
- then ask whether the surviving subset is still unusually concentrated in one capability or observer surface.

The roster should be allowed to shrink if the evidence demands it. A smaller source-backed subset is more informative than forcing every listed person into a common theory.
