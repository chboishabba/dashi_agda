This pass tests the fixed viewpoint coordinates directly. Technical work does not create a viewpoint assignment. Positive cells require first-person or direct contemporaneous evidence; participation and later attribution remain separate provenance classes.

## Fixed coordinates

- **V1** — support for UAP/UFO disclosure
- **V2** — belief in suppressed/exotic propulsion
- **V3** — transformative fusion/energy expectation
- **V4** — critique of secrecy/classification/suppression
- **V5** — willingness to discuss sensitive/controversial technology publicly
- **V6** — belief that major technical capabilities exist but are hidden/suppressed

## Provenance classes

`SELF` > `DIRECT CONTEMPORANEOUS WITNESS` > `DOCUMENTED PARTICIPATION` > `LATER ATTRIBUTION` > `MEDIA PROJECTION`.

A lower-provenance statement is not silently promoted into a person's own belief.

## Primary evidence notes

### Amy Eskridge

The archived 2020 transcript is the strongest viewpoint source located so far. Eskridge says she started the institute as a public-facing persona to disclose anti-gravity technology and argues that public visibility provides protection where private work can be suppressed without notice. She also discusses prior anti-gravity discoveries as suppressed and explicitly frames publication/disclosure as an objective.

Source: Blocked Epistemology archive of the Amy Eskridge Zoom transcript: https://blockedepistemology.substack.com/p/archive-amy-eskridge-zoom

This supports V1/V2/V4/V5/V6 as **self-stated viewpoint evidence**. It does not establish that the underlying technical or suppression claims were true.

### William N. McCasland

The 2016 Podesta/DeLonge email chain documents McCasland's participation in a meeting/discussion network around DeLonge's disclosure project. DeLonge's follow-up says McCasland had called himself a skeptic, then asserts that he was not really one and that he had helped assemble the advisory team.

Sources preserving the email chain include Paradigm Research Group's chronological email reproduction and contemporaneous press discussion.

This supports:
- `participated in disclosure-related discussion` — **DOCUMENTED PARTICIPATION**;
- `McCasland personally believed the UAP/Roswell claims` — **NOT ESTABLISHED**;
- `McCasland personally supported disclosure` — **NOT YET ESTABLISHED**.

The BIDI acquisition target is a McCasland-authored statement, email, interview, memo, or direct witness account carrying his own position.

### Nuno Loureiro

A 2022 long-form interview with Loureiro discusses fusion as a potentially major future energy source, and MIT's 2024 profile quotes him describing the emerging burning-plasma period as the dawn of a new era for fusion.

Sources:
- 45 Graus episode 119 transcript: https://45gtranscripts.netlify.app/2022/04/13/119-nuno-loureiro-chegou-finalmente-o-tempo-da-energia-de-fus%C3%A3o
- MIT News, 1 May 2024: https://news.mit.edu/2024/nuno-loureiro-named-director-mit-plasma-science-fusion-center-0501

This strongly supports V3. It does not support V1/V2/V4/V6.

### Anthony Chavez

LANL's 2025 profile quotes Chavez describing a collaborative engineering model: physicists express needs, then the engineering team designs and builds solutions together.

Source: LANL National Security Science, Summer 2025: https://cdn.lanl.gov/files/nss-2025-engineering-online_f0e10.pdf

This is useful first-person evidence but not evidence for the disclosure/secrecy viewpoint set.

## What this does to the unifying-viewpoint hypothesis

The current evidence does **not yet** show one common V1–V6 viewpoint across the roster.

What it does show is a potentially interesting partial structure:

1. **Eskridge** is a strong, explicit disclosure/suppression viewpoint case.
2. **McCasland** is inside a documented disclosure-related interaction network, but his own beliefs remain unresolved.
3. **Loureiro** is a strong transformative-energy optimist, but current evidence does not connect him to UAP/suppression/disclosure views.
4. The remaining technical cases are presently mostly **unknown**, not negative.

So the high-value next search is not broader theorizing. It is first-person viewpoint recovery for **McCasland, Reza, LeBlanc, Maiwald and Chavez**, plus matched peers.

## Stop rule

Do not infer a person's views from their field, employer, clearance-adjacent work, or from someone else claiming to know what they believed. If the viewpoint hypothesis is real, it should survive direct-source acquisition and matched controls.
