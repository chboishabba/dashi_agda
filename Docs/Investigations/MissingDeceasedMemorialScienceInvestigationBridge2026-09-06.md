# Missing/deceased scientists — memorial science / investigation bridge — 2026-09-06

## Purpose

The scientific formalisation serves two purposes at once:

1. preserve and deepen understanding of the technical work in memorial form; and
2. improve the investigation by identifying the exact capability-bearing objects that would have to be shown to be concentrated, non-public, difficult to replace, visible to an observer, and causally relevant before any targeting or event hypothesis could be promoted.

The firewall is:

`InterestingScience != CriticalKnowledge != Targeting != EventCause != Homicide`.

Science is allowed to refine acquisition targets. It is not allowed to fill missing event evidence.

## Generic bridge

`DASHI/Core/ScientificCapabilityCarrierBidiExact.agda` introduces capability carriers such as:

- governing model;
- constitutive parameters;
- source code / algorithm;
- calibration data;
- raw experimental data;
- design geometry;
- process window;
- fabrication recipe;
- qualification evidence;
- failure-mode map;
- integration procedure;
- tacit technique;
- hardware configuration;
- inverse model.

A reconstructive carrier is one that materially reduces the work needed to reproduce, calibrate, qualify, diagnose or extend a capability.

The bridge maps carrier kinds into the pre-existing critical-knowledge ontology, but a person-specific receipt still requires independent evidence.

## Science-derived acquisition targets

### Nickel-base burn-resistant superalloys

Public composition windows and qualitative element roles are not the whole capability. The higher-value unresolved carrier is:

`heat treatment + gamma-prime morphology + grain state + process window + qualification data`.

Investigation target: establish who actually possessed this process/microstructure knowledge at the relevant time, whether it was distributed or concentrated, and whether successor/handover evidence exists.

### Space fission instrumentation and control

The public functional architecture is not the decisive carrier. Higher-value objects include:

`radiation-temperature-lifetime qualification evidence + drift/failure envelopes + integrated fault-tolerance behaviour`.

Investigation target: determine whether these were team-distributed, database-owned, role-concentrated, or otherwise difficult to replace; inspect successor and handover evidence.

### Reduced fluid-kinetic plasma simulation

The equations and numerical architecture are substantially public. More discriminating carriers include:

`source artifact + closure choices + target-specific ordering validity + convergence/benchmark evidence`.

Investigation target: do not treat public methods as hidden knowledge; determine whether any non-public implementation or unique integration carrier actually existed.

### Flash radiography

Public accelerator architecture is not equivalent to the experiment-specific capability. Higher-value carriers are:

`source spectrum + detector calibration + exact geometry + attenuation/forward model + reconstruction + inverse model + uncertainty propagation`.

Investigation target: identify which programme/security/review surfaces could know who had access to those carriers. Observer visibility is distinct from targeting.

### Cryogenic messenger-tag action spectroscopy

The laboratory protocol is substantially public. Higher-value carriers include:

`apparatus calibration + tag-specific response + conformer library + mixture discrimination + false-positive/false-negative performance`.

Investigation target: determine whether mission-relevant calibration/interpretation knowledge was public, team-distributed, or concentrated.

## Current investigation state

Scientific formalisation now narrows the strongest acquisition questions, but it does not close:

- person-specific possession for most reconstructive carriers;
- uniqueness;
- non-publicness for many carriers;
- replacement difficulty;
- common capability-aware observer;
- targeting;
- common actor;
- causal linkage to disappearance/death;
- homicide.

Those remain independent evidence obligations.

## Memorial principle

The science owners should continue to be deepened whether or not an investigative hypothesis survives. A falsified targeting hypothesis would not diminish the value of preserving and formalising the technical work. Conversely, technical importance alone must never be promoted into a claim about why a person disappeared or died.
