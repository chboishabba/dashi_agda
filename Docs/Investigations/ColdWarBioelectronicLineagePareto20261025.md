## Highest-value NEW exact historical bridge

The 2022 National Security Archive (NSA Archive at George Washington University, **not** the US National Security Agency) collection edited by **Peter Kornbluh and William Burr** releases 1964–1979 primary documents about the Soviet microwave transmissions aimed at the U.S. Embassy in Moscow and U.S. Project **TUMS** (Technical Unidentified Moscow Signal), **PANDORA** and **BIZARRE**. https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/briefing-book/intelligence-russia-programs/2022-09-13/moscow-signals-declassified-microwave

Original objects provide real **inter-agency** joins:
- Richard S. Cesaro, ARPA, `Justification Memorandum for Project PANDORA`, **15 October 1965**. Original 2-page scan https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/sites/default/files/documents/rhwvuj-tzy4b/03.pdf (NSA Archive object 28787). This names State Department (coordinator), CIA, DoD/ARPA, **Air Force**, **Applied Physics Laboratory**, and **Walter Reed Army Institute of Research**. The second page also names Ross Adey (UCLA), Milton Zaret, J. Johnston (CIA), H. Pollack (IDA), among consultants. Programme Plan **562** and an Air Force equipment study via ARPA Order **757** are followable administrative leads, not evidence of completed efficacy or contemporary transfer.
- Samuel Koslov, ARPA, `Biomedical Phenomena`, **13 May 1965**, object 28786 https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/28786-document-2-arpa-samuel-koslov-memorandum-state-department-biomedical-phenomena. He explicitly raised a speculative low-level neurophysiological hypothesis about the Moscow Signal. His contemporaneous strength estimate was subsequently corrected. That is a *proposal/intelligence concern*, not a finding.
- Joseph Johnston, **CIA representative** in PANDORA/BIZARRE, `Aid Memoir`, **25 September 1967**, object 28791 https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/28791-document-7-cia-memorandum-joseph-johnston-aid-memoir-sic-summary-statement-my. He discussed an intriguing behavioral result in **one** animal and a modulation-versus-carrier interpretation, but also wrote that follow-up trials at the corrected lower power found **no behavioural effects**; expressly asked for independent replication. CIA agency participation in the interagency project is real, but still no evidence this derived from CIA MKULTRA subproject 119.
- PANDORA committee, `Summary and Detailed Minutes of 21 April 1969`, **16 May 1969**, object 28794 https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/28794-document-10-arpa-meeting-summaryminutes-summary-pandora-meeting-attached-detailed; and `Minutes of 12 May 1969`, object 28795 (linked from parent collection). These discuss prospective **human** studies, possible Fort Detrick subjects and concealment/classification. **Planning is not execution**, and the project ended March 1970.
- Samuel Koslov, now at **RAND**, to ARPA Deputy Director Stephen Lukasik, `Review of Project PANDORA Experiments`, **4 November 1969**, original five-page scan https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/sites/default/files/documents/rhwvuj-tzy4b/12.pdf (object 28796). Its literal conclusion: insufficient convincing evidence of animal behavioral change due to the signal within reasonable scientific criteria. Specific possible confounders: equipment and food-pellet malfunctions, room shifts, night/day effects, **EEG electrodes possibly acting as antennas**, missing/sham continuous-wave controls. The author wrote separately that a *possible* weapon/interrogation line could be studied at SECRET level and that safety research should be separate. **That recommendation is not an implemented or validated weapon.** The programme involved substantial exposure chambers/protocols whose hardware and later disposition merit documentary snowballing.
- RAND/Samuel Koslov et al., `Review of Contract N00014-69-C-0358, Radio Frequency Hazards`, **16 August 1971**, object 28797 from 2022 collection. Critical protocol/animal-welfare review. Do not count RAND's two critiques as two independent full clinical replications.
- George H. Heilmeier, ARPA, **15 September 1977**, object 28798: retrospective oversight letter states PANDORA closure **March 1970**, expenditures **USD 4,615,000**. This is a specific budget/documentary object, not a device genealogy by itself.

**Experimental result vs original hypotheses:** PANDORA/BIZARRE was an actual RF/primate experiment programme evaluating alleged Soviet signal effects. The *tested outcome was negative/inconclusive at relevant corrected dose*. Early apparent effects on one animal must not be generalized. Importantly, **low-frequency modulation on an RF carrier is not low-frequency airborne sound.**

## The previously missing CIA-internal sibling leaf: MKULTRA Subproject 119

A 3-page declassified US government **draft of 17 August 1960 / approved 9 September 1960** is hosted as an unchanged 2016 Commons scan https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:MKUltra_119.pdf (original PDF https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4a/MKUltra_119.pdf). The original CIA CREST/FOIA accession and attached full contemporaneous literature bibliography remain unlocated: **Commons is a secondary access carrier, not the record custodian.**

The project memo approves **USD 6,370** for an annotated bibliography/interpretive survey on bioelectric recording, analysis, automatic processing, physiological correlation and remote electronic activation. Its source-exact purpose includes `activation of human behavior by remote means`; the five listed survey categories include `techniques of activation of the human organism by remote electronic means`. The attached proposal names neurophysiology, electronics, telemetrics and communications engineering as **bibliographic fields**. This evidence pays a **funded research-interest/survey** claim, not experimental stimulation, demonstrated thought control, a patented device, or deployed installation. Sponsorship was designed to be concealed from associates of the research contractor.

**Source-type collision:** MKULTRA #119 and PANDORA both investigate bioelectric or EM/biological phenomena under US government programmes. That is a real *topical overlap*; the documents presently inspected do not name shared apparatus, research grant, laboratory log, individual receiving both contracts, or original programme-to-programme instruction. Do not invent such a join.

## Independent original mechanism, separate from organisational genealogy

Allan H. Frey, **1962**, `Human auditory system response to modulated electromagnetic energy`, *Journal of Applied Physiology* **17(4):689–692**, DOI https://doi.org/10.1152/jappl.1962.17.4.689. Genuine microwave-auditory phenomenon research. Source mechanism and biological auditory perception are distinct from an airborne low-frequency industrial noise spectrum. The existence of perceivable RF-generated clicks does not demonstrate intelligible injected speech, reproducible mental-state control, injury at public telecom levels, or operator intent.
