## Highest-value NEW exact historical bridge

The 2022 National Security Archive (NSA Archive at George Washington University, **not** the US National Security Agency) collection edited by **Peter Kornbluh and William Burr** releases 1964–1979 primary documents about the Soviet microwave transmissions aimed at the U.S. Embassy in Moscow and U.S. Project **TUMS** (Technical Unidentified Moscow Signal), **PANDORA** and **BIZARRE**. https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/briefing-book/intelligence-russia-programs/2022-09-13/moscow-signals-declassified-microwave

Original objects provide real **inter-agency** joins:
- Richard S. Cesaro, ARPA, `Justification Memorandum for Project PANDORA`, **15 October 1965**. Original 2-page scan https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/sites/default/files/documents/rhwvuj-tzy4b/03.pdf (NSA Archive object 28787). This names State Department (coordinator), CIA, DoD/ARPA, **Air Force**, **Applied Physics Laboratory**, and **Walter Reed Army Institute of Research**. The second page also names Ross Adey (UCLA), Milton Zaret, J. Johnston (CIA), H. Pollack (IDA), among consultants. Programme Plan **562** and an Air Force equipment study via ARPA Order **757** are followable administrative leads, not evidence of completed efficacy or contemporary transfer.
- Samuel Koslov, ARPA, `Biomedical Phenomena`, **13 May 1965**, object 28786 https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/28786-document-2-arpa-samuel-koslov-memorandum-state-department-biomedical-phenomena. He explicitly raised a speculative low-level neurophysiological hypothesis about the Moscow Signal. His contemporaneous strength estimate was subsequently corrected. That is a *proposal/intelligence concern*, not a finding.
- Joseph Johnston, **CIA representative** in PANDORA/BIZARRE, `Aid Memoir`, **25 September 1967**, object 28791 https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/28791-document-7-cia-memorandum-joseph-johnston-aid-memoir-sic-summary-statement-my. He discussed an intriguing behavioral result in **one** animal and a modulation-versus-carrier interpretation, but also wrote that follow-up trials at the corrected lower power found **no behavioural effects**; expressly asked for independent replication. CIA agency participation in the interagency project is real, but still no evidence this derived from CIA MKULTRA subproject 119.
- PANDORA committee, `Summary and Detailed Minutes of 21 April 1969`, **16 May 1969**, object 28794 https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/28794-document-10-arpa-meeting-summaryminutes-summary-pandora-meeting-attached-detailed; and `Minutes of 12 May 1969`, object 28795 (linked from parent collection). These discuss prospective **human** studies, possible Fort Detrick subjects and concealment/classification. **Planning is not execution**, and the project ended March 1970.
- Samuel Koslov, now at **RAND**, to ARPA Deputy Director Stephen Lukasik, `Review of Project PANDORA Experiments`, **4 November 1969**, original five-page scan https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/sites/default/files/documents/rhwvuj-tzy4b/12.pdf (object 28796). Its literal conclusion: insufficient convincing evidence of animal behavioral change due to the signal within reasonable scientific criteria. Specific possible confounders: equipment and food-pellet malfunctions, room shifts, night/day effects, **EEG electrodes possibly acting as antennas**, missing/sham continuous-wave controls. The author wrote separately that a *possible* weapon/interrogation line could be studied at SECRET level and that safety research should be separate. **That recommendation is not an implemented or validated weapon.** The programme involved substantial exposure chambers/protocols whose hardware and later disposition merit documentary snowballing.
- RAND/Samuel Koslov et al., `Review of Contract N00014-69-C-0358, Radio Frequency Hazards`, **16 August 1971**, object 28797 from 2022 collection. Critical protocol/animal-welfare review. Do not count RAND's two critiques as two independent full clinical replications.
- George H. Heilmeier, ARPA, **15 September 1977**, object 28798: retrospective oversight letter states PANDORA closure **March 1970**, expenditures **USD 4,615,000**. This is a specific budget/documentary object, not a device genealogy by itself.

**Experimental result vs original hypotheses:** PANDORA/BIZARRE was an actual RF/primate experiment programme evaluating alleged Soviet signal effects. The *tested outcome was negative/inconclusive at relevant corrected dose*. Early apparent effects on one animal must not be generalized. Importantly, **low-frequency modulation on an RF carrier is not low-frequency airborne sound.**

## Independent original mechanism, separate from organisational genealogy

Allan H. Frey, **1962**, `Human auditory system response to modulated electromagnetic energy`, *Journal of Applied Physiology* **17(4):689–692**, DOI https://doi.org/10.1152/jappl.1962.17.4.689. Genuine microwave-auditory phenomenon research. Source mechanism and biological auditory perception are distinct from an airborne low-frequency industrial noise spectrum. ## 1979 original Senate committee print acquired: actual historical human outcomes (P1 source paid)

**Original root:** United States Senate, Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation, *Microwave Irradiation of the U.S. Embassy in Moscow: Review of Its History and Studies to Determine Whether or Not Related Health Defects Were Experienced by Employees Assigned in the Period 1953–1977*, April **1979**. A **33-page scanned copy with official report pages and source-page labels** is preserved at https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/sites/default/files/documents/rhwvuj-tzy4b/15.pdf ; US Government Printing Office govinfo version https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CPRT-96SPRT43949/pdf/CPRT-96SPRT43949.pdf . This is a committee **staff report and original retrospective synthesis of underlying studies**, NOT an independently executed fifth human trial. Named 1979 underlying human original-study authors, administrations and cohorts retain attribution.

- **1965 State initial chart review**: 139 employees and 268 dependants; **no control group**, variable clinic records/timepoints, poorly suited to inferring any rate. Later G.W. University formal cytogenetics study started **29 March 1966** under Contract **SCC-31252** and finished **30 June 1969** under **SCC-31759**. The final report accounts for approximately **250 coded blood specimens from more than 71 people**, including **13 pre-Moscow controls**, not simply the 1969 board's intermediate report of **140 samples**; do not sum them as separate studies. The 1969 meeting's four flagged samples were interim attributed observations and must not be reported as four caused injuries.
- **GWU evaluator's initial claim**: 43% of exposed subjects assigned a particular chromosomal-risk classification, versus **38% of pre-assignment controls**. Senate staff reports that independent specialists examined slides, questioned preparation quality/artifacts and failed to endorse clinical causal significance. US State Department decided June 1969 **not to renew** the study; accepted the conclusion that it had **not established adverse biological effects attributable to microwave exposure**, while expressly saying it **could not conclusively exclude** such an effect. This is not proof of no effect under any dose or other protocol.
- **PANDORA experimental dosage**: early lab `special` primate signal around **4–5 mW/cm²** compared with historical Moscow embassy levels commonly around **2–18 μW/cm²**, a roughly **222–2500-fold** power-density scale difference depending on denominator. The 1979 report notes an `Allied Research` contract to compile East European publications on RF behavioural effects published by December 1969. This **specific literature bibliography** is a newly identified high-value root; it must not be conflated with the separate 1960 CIA MKULTRA #119 annotated-bibliography survey merely because both were government-funded research surveys.
- **1969 `Big Boy`**: conducted shipboard radar-exposure comparative observational tests, largely negative at doses lower than forecast. The special 1969 WRAIR **human deliberate irradiation** project under discussion and June 1969 planning remained a proposal; contemporaneous 1970 reviewers did not establish the efficacy of the primate exposure programmes.
- **State Department lymphocyte study (mid/late 1970s)**: ~350 Moscow employees/dependants compared with ~1000 other foreign-service controls; mean lymphocyte counts **~41% higher** in Moscow as measured. Investigators specifically reported **no temporal or geographic gradient tied to Moscow microwave dose**; counts later fell without a matched source change, and study director suggested a non-specific environmental/microbial hypothesis, not an identified alternative cause. Elevated average is not a clinical hematologic disease or evidence of a controlled weapon.
- **Abraham Lilienfeld, Johns Hopkins University, Foreign Service Health Status Study, 1976–1978**: actual retrospectively controlled cohort of Moscow personnel and dependants, with Eastern European embassy-post groups selected as comparators. 1979 staff report states **4,179 traced employee records**, 4,033 with demographic time records, 3,094 with usable medical abstracts. Analysis found no convincing mortality or morbidity pattern **tracking the recorded RF exposure** as of the review, with several real *differences* (e.g. protozoal infections, reported eye and mood/cognitive symptoms) discussed and evaluated against alternative causes/reporting bias and dose gradients. **Important limits:** relatively young population, delayed disease development, uneven record follow-up, uncertain individual dose and various source-specific disease subgroups. The authors recommended ongoing monitoring; a null at these levels and this study design is NOT a universal no-risk proof.
- **1979 source conclusion**: the controlled epidemiology did not detect adverse health consequences clearly attributable to the low-level Moscow embassy microwave exposure, but separately documented real research ethics issues, incompletely disclosed public exposure and methodological uncertainty. This combined positive/negative historical result is more informative than a statement either that PANDORA never touched human health or that it demonstrated successful human manipulation.

**Methodological non-identity:** A 1979 Senate *review* citing Lilienfeld's study is a different authority from the 1976–78 investigator's original report/data, and the 1979 staff summary is not a new independently replicated participant cohort. The underlying original Lilienfeld report, tables and anonymized exposure/health linkage remain the stronger next retrieval targets.
