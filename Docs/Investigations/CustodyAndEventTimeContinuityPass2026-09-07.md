This pass follows the application-transformation model with two additional questions:

1. **Where does the capability physically/digitally live?**
2. **Was the person still in the relevant role or access state at event time?**

These are separate from scientific authorship, expertise, motive and event causation.

## Maiwald / action spectroscopy

Primary JPL sources:

- FY23 SURP poster, `RPC#sp23012`, clearance `CL#23-5018`, *Unambiguous Detection of Biosignatures by Action Spectroscopy*.
- FY24 SURP poster, same project title, successor PI Deacon J. Nemchick.

The FY23 poster names Frank W. Maiwald as PI, Robert P. Hodyss and Mathias Weber as co-investigators, and describes use of the active University of Colorado Boulder / Weber-group cryogenic action-spectroscopy apparatus.

The FY24 poster names Deacon Nemchick as PI, retains Hodyss and Weber, and explicitly says the work heavily leverages the Weber-group cryogenic ion apparatus. FY24 publications listed on the poster retain Frank Maiwald as a coauthor.

Therefore the following are now source-backed:

- same-project continuation;
- successor PI;
- overlapping team;
- same external physical-apparatus platform;
- continuing scientific lineage.

This **falsifies** any claim that the physical spectroscopy platform itself disappeared with Maiwald.

Still open:

- transfer of JPL-specific calibration archives;
- mission-integration state;
- qualification records;
- failure history;
- notebooks/repository state;
- tacit execution carrier.

`project continuity != exact calibration/qualification carrier transfer`.
