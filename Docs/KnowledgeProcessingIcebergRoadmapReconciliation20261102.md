## Three decisive *candidate* cycles (NOT source-proved whole paths)

`R1 — capital recovery → next capital cycle`:
financed initial GPU cohort → customer payments → available free cash AFTER operating cost/interest/leases → GPU residual/refresh capex → new financing and covenant/lender risk.
- Input paid: portions of contractual borrowing, RBA vulnerability, six-month cash and future commitments.
- Missing: actual GPU vintage, cash waterfall, renewal probability, cost/throughput, debt priorities and offsetting independent external-payer receipts.
- Rival: customer demand, hardware resale and contract prices support the renewal without subsidised revolving debt.

`R2 — dependency → policy selection → access`:
institution adopts proprietary AI stack → high data/migration/reaccreditation costs → procurement/regulatory standard → differential independent provider costs → practical loss/preservation of competing routes.
- Input paid: UK MoD's own lock-in justification; U.S. NSPM-11 publicly advocates multiple suppliers and sovereign no-disable guarantees.
- Missing: US/UK separate contract deployments, specific legal clauses, provider-class budgets, working comparable open/private providers, real decisions and correction/appeal rights.
- Rival: multi-vendor or publicly sovereign interoperable implementation *reduces* unilateral private control.

`R3 — financing stress → systemic rescue incentive → new control rights`:
spread/collateral/contract stress → correlated non-bank losses → systemic/critical service risk → intervention deliberation → actual government instrument → resulting ownership, interoperability, exclusive access or independent route.
- Input paid: RBA's *possible* contagion warning, documented financing/credit concentration, source-written stress code.
- Missing: demonstrated major creditor-loss transmission, supervisory finding, appropriation/guarantee/receivership/contract instrument, rights acquired and measured exit results. No state seizure is evidenced.
- Rival: private restructuring, lease renegotiation, project cancellation, ordinary credit write-down, or public continuity backstop conditioned on interoperability.

**Triadic residue:** Funding, state procurement/regulation and epistemic authority are not reconstructed merely from three pairwise connections.
