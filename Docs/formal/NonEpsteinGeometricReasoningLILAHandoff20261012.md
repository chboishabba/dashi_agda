## Working synthesis

The current candidate mechanism is:

`diffuse/redundant representation`

`-> circuit/operator formation`

`-> repeated nonlinear/refinement stages`

`-> symmetry-adapted reusable operators/fibres`

`-> more specialised/context-sensitive routing`

`-> reduced irregular/redundant components`

`-> visible grokking/generalisation`.

A smaller reusable operator family can in principle generate a much larger behavioural family by composition and context-dependent routing. This gives a precise meaning to “parameter waste”:

> redundant local approximation of relations that could instead be represented once as reusable actions/operators and routed/composed where needed.

This is a testable hypothesis, not yet an empirical conclusion.

Core temporal test:

`does structured action/routing alignment emerge before, with, or after visible grokking?`

Useful candidate orderings:

- `t_action_alignment < t50`: latent geometric crystallisation precedes behavioural grokking;
- `t_action_alignment ~= t50`: representation reorganisation and behavioural transition are coupled;
- `t_action_alignment > t50`: geometric-action explanation is weakened for that run.
