# Global climate-denial claim genealogy: original-proposition max-cut
**Corrected scope: worldwide**, not "Australian climate denial". Australia/BOM/gas-tax cases are a **separate local comparison**, not the target population. **As of 27 October 2026 AEST.**

## Original research question
Which **particular people, groups and financed producers** originated, adapted and circulated recurring *global climate denial or minimisation arguments*—for example, "the planet is cooling", "climate science predicted an ice age", "warming stopped in 1998", "CO2 is plant food", "more CO2 therefore cannot be harmful"—and how often did apparently numerous independent speakers reuse the same original claims and evidentiary artefacts?

Separate the following original objects, regardless of agreement or disagreement with their political implications:

1. **Underlying real observation** (e.g., warming of some terrestrial biomass under CO2 enrichment, a cooler single year, a subset of 1970s aerosol studies).
2. **Original rhetorical proposition** with precise date, speaker, denominator, window, logical quantifier and policy inference.
3. **Funding/production** (paid original film, archival funder, signed contract, donor, producer, editorial rights—not mere co-attendance or general beneficiary).
4. **Specific transfer** (named sequel, verbatim quotation, attributed syndication, cited paper, source manuscript redline—not shared themes alone).
5. **Scientific assessment** of the exact proposition with original measurements and uncertainties, not a copy of an advocacy text.
6. **Population/circulation**: reach, reprints and content clones versus **genuinely independent original claim producers**. Neither repeated exposure nor newspaper "sources" are a source-root census.

**Important limitation**: earliest original identified *within this purposively selected evidence set* is not guaranteed to be first ever. A correct botanical experiment is not "denial"; the unqualified inference from it to net absence of climate damages may be.

## Primary-source lineage and confidence

### "Global cooling": THREE separate claims, not one inherited slogan
- **1975 original publisher-authored journalism**: **Peter Gwynne**, Newsweek, *The Cooling World*, **28 April 1975**. The original text is accessible as an attributed archival reproduction at https://sealevel.info/newsweek_old.htm. At the time, it reported a debated short historical cool interval and predicted adverse consequences of further cooling. A magazine article is an original MEDIA claim, not the state of the peer-reviewed scientific consensus.
- **2014 original AUTHOR self-assessment**: Peter Gwynne, *Stop Citing My 1975 Global Cooling Article*, 22 May 2014, byline on an attributed republication at https://www.realclearscience.com/articles/2014/05/22/stop_citing_my_1975_global_cooling_article_108668.html. This is exact author-to-old-document identity and an explicit correction of modern misuse; it is not independent climate-data reproduction.
- **2008 original HISTORICAL LITERATURE TEST**: **Thomas C. Peterson, William M. Connolley and John Fleck**, BAMS, DOI https://doi.org/10.1175/2008BAMS2370.1. Their **1965–79 sampled papers** classified **7 cooling, 20 neutral, 44 warming**, not a 1970s cooling-consensus majority. The sample and classification are an actual published systematic historical audit; it does not prove every scientist's contemporaneous belief or directly identify the first disseminator of every cooling meme.
- **1998-base modern pause/cooling**: **Robert Fawcett and David Jones**, Australian Bureau of Meteorology (original independent analysis, April 2008), https://bravenewclimate.com/files/documents/waiting-for-global-cooling.pdf, explicitly distinguishes falsely inferring a multi-decade trend from a short 1998–2007 window and El Niño selection. **David R. Easterling and Michael F. Wehner**, 2009 GRL, DOI https://doi.org/10.1029/2009GL037810, show decade-scale flat/cooling runs are compatible with forced long-run warming. These are not mere restatements of the 1975 ice-age-media claim.
- **Current primary observation**: NASA/GISS original annual release 14 January 2026, https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-releases-global-temperature-data/ , says **2024** hottest calendar year and **2025 cooler than 2024 but near 2023**, consistent with multi-decadal warming, not a new era of net planetary cooling. Different institutes share some underlying physical observations; do not mistake several institutional analyses for wholly independent station datasets.

### "CO2 is good for plants": early sponsored positive original + repeated overgeneralisation
- **1992 Western Fuels Association / Institute of Biospheric Research** *The Greening of Planet Earth* documentary: an original promotional campaign archived and described at https://www.climatefiles.com/trade-group/1992-western-fuels-greening-planet-earth/ . The archive credits Institute of Biospheric Research as film producer, Western Fuels Association as **funder**, and original contributor **Sherwood Idso**. Direct studio spending ledger, first full original audiovisual/hash and full production contract **not** obtained by this cut. Sponsor involvement is archival-corroborated, NOT permission to attribute scientific photosynthesis discovery to coal firms.
- **1998 sequel and promotional package**: Greening Earth Society / Western Fuels Association, *The Greening of Planet Earth Continues*, archival transcript https://www.climatefiles.com/trade-group/1998-western-fuels-greening-planet-earth-continues/ and 1998 **The CO2 Issue** https://www.climatefiles.com/trade-group/1998-greening-earth-society-co2/ . This is an explicit, archivally documented SAME CAMPAIGN sequel and circulated promotional material—stronger than shared slogan alone. Transcript/film and actual individual delivery/financing records need further exact inspection before upgrading to primary transfer evidence.
- **1998 Oregon Petition OTHER campaign**: Arthur B. Robinson/OISM with **Frederick Seitz** original covering letter/petition text https://www.oism.org/pproject/pproject.htm . Its later-updated page currently asserts over 31,000 science signatories; that is an **organisation self-report, not an audited 1998 count, nor 31,000 independent climate-science research findings**. An archived January 1998 original manuscript titled *Environmental Effects of Increased Atmospheric Carbon Dioxide* credits **Arthur B. Robinson, Sallie L. Baliunas, Willie Soon, Zachary W. Robinson**: https://ossfoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Oregon_Petition-reduced.pdf. Avoid treating same title and similar conclusions as either evidence of WFA-funded OISM work or a single exact 1998/1999 manuscript file without version comparison.
- **1998 original National Academy negative provenance**: **NAS Council**, 20 April 1998, https://www.nationalacademies.org/news/statement-of-the-council-of-the-nas-regarding-global-change-petition, explicitly disavows the petition and says its mailed manuscript was NOT then a PNAS or other peer-reviewed journal publication. Later **September 1999 Energy & Environment** DOI https://doi.org/10.1260/0958305991499694 published a named-author paper with related title/argument; the later journal existence does NOT retroactively contradict the NAS's time-scoped 1998 statement.
- **Modern first-party repetition**: CO2 Coalition's **undated live institutional fact page** https://co2coalition.org/facts/co2-is-plant-food/ and **dated 31 October 2022 Vijay Jayaraj** https://co2coalition.org/2022/10/31/life-giving-co2-should-be-valued-not-demonized/ . The publisher explicitly uses plant-food and net-benefit framing; this is DOCUMENTED thematic recurrence and named original publisher/speaker, NOT a traced WFA→Coalition payment or verbatim copy. Do not backdate undated pages to the Coalition's 2015 founding.
- **Underlying scientific positive evidence is real, but narrower**: **Zaichun Zhu and coauthors**, *Greening of the Earth and its drivers*, 2016, DOI 10.1038/nclimate3004 (original PDF https://www.globalcarbonproject.org/global/pdf/Zhu_2016_Greening%20of%20the%20Earth%20and%20its%20drivers.NatureCC.pdf) finds large greening coverage 1982–2009 and models approximately **70% of observed greening TREND attributable to CO2 fertilisation**. This is NOT 70% global vegetation or 70% of all CO2 harm being beneficial. **César Terrer et al.**, *Science* 2016 DOI 10.1126/science.aaf4610 and *Nature Climate Change* 2019 DOI 10.1038/s41558-019-0545-2, show plant-growth responses depend on mycorrhizae, nitrogen/phosphorus and site conditions; the two analyses share personnel, so do not count as two wholly independent authorship teams. Other impacts—heat, water, acidification, nutritional quality, storms and mortality—must be evaluated for any GLOBAL net-benefit claim.

Reuses, rather than duplicates, existing:
- TypedGenealogyEdgeExact: source relation distinct from evidence strength; adds authorRetrospectiveCorrection, institutionalDisavowal and extrapolationBeyondEvidence without allowing these kinds to constitute strong historical transfer.
- ClimateGenealogyTypedCasesExact: explicit original dated SourceReceipts and exact bounded relations GC-01 through GC-04 (Gwynne original/correction; WFA named sequel; OISM/NAS disavowal; botanically positive partial result versus global-net-benefit overreach).
- IntersectionalNonFactorability: two finite thought-world witnesses, first **same positive plant growth / opposed net-global climate outcome**, second **same single 1975 magazine report / opposed historical scientific majorities**. Hence, the observed narrow premise cannot identify the general conclusion. These are logical nonfactorability witnesses only: not climate modelling, no actual probability, no universal statement about public deception.
- EmpiricalGenealogyRound3Regression: source-role and no-promotion consumers, not a second ontology.

## Reproducibility and corpus boundary
New selected-source register: Docs/support/reference/global_climate_denial_claim_genealogy_20261027.csv, **18 deliberately selected appearances**; distinct record, variant, source document, original-author role, argument-root family, claimed transfer and independently measured underlying evidence. Unlike the prior 57-case Australian political/regulatory ledger, these focus on the worldwide scientific/narrative content. DO NOT add them as if this were a sampled worldwide denominator.

Adopt existing **Coan, Boussalis, Cook and Nanko 2021 CARDS taxonomy**, DOI 10.1038/s41598-021-01714-4, as the top-level claim categories ("not happening", "not us", "not bad", "solutions won't work", "science unreliable"). Subclaims and time windows must be retained rather than collapsed under a bland "denial" tag. CARDS is first-party systematic SOCIAL SCIENCE about a selected think-tank/blog corpus; it is not every climate critic on the planet, nor an original botanic/temperature measurement.

## Severe test and stopping rule
A false "few global roots" hypothesis is weakened by a robust, representative, independently-authored global original corpus whose individual producers have unrelated source histories, independently designed experiments, distinct funders and earlier dates than any proposed bridge. A false "plant food therefore harmless" inference is tested by **combined** measured growth AND heat/drought/nutrient/ocean damages on the same time/geographic estimand—not by denying the measured growth. A false "1970s cooling consensus" inference is rejected by representative contemporaneous peer-reviewed articles and contextual documentation; not by retroactively claiming no scientist ever entertained cooling.

Pareto: trace EXACT original first with real source files; collapse same-campaign reprints, preserve distinct original papers; enrich only when a dated person→content→payment→distribution fact changes a proposed source graph. STOP popular celebrity hunting and local gas-policy detours that do not advance the GLOBAL cooling/greening proposition genealogy.
