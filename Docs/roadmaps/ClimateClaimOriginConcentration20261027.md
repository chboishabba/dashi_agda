# Climate-claim source concentration: original inquiry and gated roadmap
**Date:** 27 October 2026 AEST. **Owner:** Johl Brown (independent project framing, audit direction); OpenAI ChatGPT (assisted research, source-written code and formal audit). **Working branch:** research/bom-fordham-media-claims-20261024, PR #1251. All original historians, scientific researchers, journalists, modellers and institutions retain their own authorship.

## The original inquiry and its testable statement
Are apparently numerous climate-denial and climate-policy-delay narratives produced by comparatively few *independent original authors or commissioning institutions*, then amplified through think tanks, radio, publishers, parliamentary speeches and political parties? Can documented finance and communication explain the spread, and can any resulting policy or monetary benefit be measured? This is NOT resolved by finding the same wealthy person adjacent to two media events, two similar slogans, or an overlapping percentage.

Required directional evidence chain:
**original authored claim/argument/model → producer/commissioner → documented payment/editorial right → transmission or credited reuse → audience exposure → legally evidenced policy step → measured outcome.**

Every arrow has its own evidence grade. A draft strategy does not show that a plan was implemented; a funding proposal is not a bank transaction; a client right to edit is not a documented specific edit; a press story and credited corporate reprint are not two scientific origins; influence inferred from similarity or beneficiary status is not documentary causal control.

## Established, qualified and unproved
**Established at source-original scale:** 1998 API team had 13 named contributor relations to ONE publicly reproduced plan document, not 13 original models; 1991 ICE and API share method recurrence without a documented ICE→API exact copy; 1996–97 GCC/STAC–ACSH commissioned work is documented by an archival investigation, while the originals of commission and editorial-rights documents are indexed but unread here; ACSH publisher-original 1997 public argument is readable but the final commissioned manuscript/public output join unproved. An identifiable Stephen Johnson The Nightly gas-policy story was byline-credited and republished by Hancock Energy: an actual reprint, not independent reporting or evidence of paid script direction.

**Separate Australian model producers:** Future Group commissioned **Arthur D. Little**; Australian Energy Producers commissioned **Wood Mackenzie**. Both consultant-authored original reports have been acquired as public PDFs. One 25% label produces different levy bases, tax treatment, time horizons, contract assumptions and project measures. Reporter/client repetitions do not count as new independently verified models. An ACTU retrospective 2023–24 what-if is not a third equivalent forecast.

**14 October 2026 parliamentary originals:** Government published an original release stating three gas reservation bills were introduced. Minister Madeleine King's 14 Oct first-person second-reading speeches are accessible via *attributed OpenAustralia transcript mirrors*, distinct from direct retrieval of APH Hansard and the full bill text. Principal reservation: up to 20% of LNG exports (demand calibrated). Consequential proposal describes additionality and 90% actual supply plus narrowly conditioned 10% flexibility; separate *Domestic Gas Reservation Levy Bill* describes licence **regulatory cost recovery** by yet-to-be-made regulation, not a 25% ad valorem LNG export-revenue tax. **Introduced ≠ enacted ≠ commenced ≠ prices reduced.** Earlier Dec 2025 production-share, May 2026 exports, Sept exposure-draft and Sep departmental status are separate dated original publications. New G39 and G40 evidence IDs keep distinct ministerial versus departmental documents.

**Not proved:** nationwide share or number of independent climate-denial authors, a single all-purpose coordinator, climate-science denial inferred from mere gas tax or reservation opposition, audited actual gas model price/tax outcomes, direct lobbying→specified parliamentary text, original 1996 GCC final-to-public editing, a 2026 speech directed by a 1998 strategic plan, or any observed economic benefit from bills not shown enacted.

### Pre-registration and severe falsifiers
Let source roots be independently authored *claim-generating research/strategy objects*, appearances be delivered media/political messages, and original funding be actual recorded transaction or commissioning contracts. Count exposure separately from root generation. Choose a top-k share or effective number ONLY after building a representative corpus and defining classification. Use stratified sample by year/outlet/medium/policy topic, blind independent coders, adversarial original science/economic experts, sensitivity if unresolved links are all treated as same-source or all independent, and cluster-aware uncertainty. Avoid entropy or Pareto percentage computed naively from selected prominent cases.

Potential **disconfirming evidence** includes genuine independent original studies reaching same headline, counterexamples predating asserted origin, dated transcripts contradicting a suggested copy, an original client contract disproving an alleged editorial right, lack of the alleged transfer channel, a separately originated government policy draft, and observed outcomes contradicting policy-promotional projections. These must weaken rather than be silently absorbed into the hypothesis.

The established network is **real at bounded documentary points**, but neither small-world national root concentration nor a universal coordinated funding-command hierarchy is presently proved.


## OIA dashboard source acquisition 15 October — an original government countermodel and inconsistency

The 7-page [Australian Government Office of Impact Analysis Dashboard IA, 15 Oct 2026](https://oia.pmc.gov.au/sites/default/files/posts/2026/10/Dashboard%20IA%20%26%20Statement%20of%20Sufficiency.pdf), authored by DISR, was visually checked on original PDF pages 3 and 6. The **regulatory burden table** compares estimated **A$134 million over ten years** for reservation reform with **A$59 million** for the status quo (modelled difference **A$75 million**). These are **administrative/regulatory compliance costs**, not cash collected under the proposed regulatory licence levy, and emphatically not the A$17 billion LNG export tax scenarios. The model expects a *transfer from gas producers to domestic gas users* through lower prices. This is a CONDITIONAL GOVERNMENT APPRAISAL, not a measured benefit or a universal proposition that producers never benefit from climate-policy delay.

**Intra-government timing conflict preserved:** that 15 October OIA PDF page 6 says the principal legislative scheme is intended to commence **1 January 2027**, while Minister Madeleine King's 14 October original first-person principal Bill second-reading speech describes commencement by proclamation with a backstop of **1 July 2027**. Both state actual exporter DSO from **1 January 2028**. No silent reconciliation by dropping the earlier or later original, and no promoted legal certainty absent introduced bill text and commencement instruments. The source register G46–G49 and Agda cases RC-21–RC-22 now preserve this exact difference.

**Research consequence:** official producer/consumer incidence modelling adds an important *adversarial control* to simplistic assumed beneficiaries. It does **not** settle effects on net wealthy shareholder returns, prices, jobs, climate emissions, or lobbying inputs. The accepted next step is the actual legislative documents plus original impact-analysis assumptions and later regulator data, not more broadcaster names.
