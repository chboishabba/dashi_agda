## Executive decision

The project is **not** at "invent mathematical geometry" stage. It has separately developed (i) optics and optical inverse-model guardrails, (ii) physical and functional neural graph models, (iii) latent/consumer factorisation and trauma-memory dynamics, (iv) access-consciousness and integration proposals, (v) zero-point mode hyperfabrics, and (vi) generic physical-theory discrimination. The bottleneck is **same-object, modality-preserving calibration, intervention and held-out validation**.

"Geometry generates consciousness" is therefore decomposed into claims of increasing strength:

1. **Geometry constrains interactions:** physical connectivity, embedding, material and boundary conditions constrain admissible transitions. This is testable without assumptions about subjective experience.
2. **Effective geometry mediates state-dependent access:** the same anatomy can express different functional reachability, memory recruitment, behaviour and measured conscious-access correlates. This needs transition and perturbation receipts.
3. **A specified geometry is sufficient/necessary for a specified access phenotype:** stronger counterfactual evidence, appropriate consumer readouts and comparison with dynamical/substrate alternatives are required.
4. **A geometry constitutes/generates phenomenal experience:** NOT proven by 1–3, not equivalent to access, IIT Phi, teleodynamic coherence density, reconstruction accuracy, behaviour or morphology. Specify a theory's psychophysical bridge and an adversarial discriminator; do not mark resolved when no discriminating assay is possible.

Keep *geometry as descriptive mathematics*, *realised geometry as physical organisation*, *effective causal graph*, *information geometry*, and *phenomenal claim* in separate fibres; define all cross-domain maps explicitly.

## One common comparison object, without premature identification

Use a **tagged tuple** (not a forced shared scalar):
\`\`\`
R = (system_id, species_or_material, preparation, geometry_level,
     geometry_carrier, physical_ontology, realised_incidence, spatial_chart,
     constitutive_params, dynamics_K, intervention_u, state_x,
     observation_Q, consumer_T, time_grid, instrument,
     uncertainty, source_version, data_digest)
\`\`\`
Geometry levels include (a) abstract graph/topology, (b) realised spatial/material geometry, (c) dynamic/effective causal topology, (d) optical image geometry, (e) informational/latent geometry, and (f) a proposed phenomenal geometry. Never identify any two solely because both are "toroidal", "C3", "coherent", "integrated" or "wave-like".

The canonical same-object diagram is:
\`\`\`
        fine physical state x --K_u--> fine physical state x'
             | Q                           | Q
             v                             v
     measured/consumer state q --Kq_u--> measured/consumer state q'

     consumer target T(x)  -- requires -->  a decoder g(Q(x)).
\`\`\`
Formal obligations:
- **Consumer retention**: \`T = g ∘ Q\` on the declared admissible set. A collision \`Q(x)=Q(y), T(x)≠T(y)\` defeats exact decoding.
- **Dynamics compatibility**: \`Q ∘ K_u = Kq_u ∘ Q\` or calibrated statistical deviation with uncertainty; pre-register norm and tolerances.
- **Causation**: matched intervention that changes physical topology/weights/parameters independently of nuisance changes, together with mediated effect on a predeclared target.
- **Phenomenal claim**: independently stated mapping from measured/realised physical structure into an experience theory; a self-report is an observation subject to task, memory and report confounds, not direct readout of a universal phenomenal scalar.

## Strongest adversarial alternatives to preserve

- **Ferrocell:** magnetic field + field-dependent nanoparticle alignment + classical/Mueller optical response + camera nuisance; full 3D torus reconstruction is an alternative hypothesis, not forced by rings.
- **Neural geometry:** graph wiring alone vs geometry + synaptic biophysics + neuromodulatory state + effective connectivity + dynamical history. Identical anatomy with different states must be retained as a counterexample to rigid geometry-determines-mind accounts.
- **Memory/trauma:** altered access priors/attractor basins vs memory trace erasure/rewriting vs altered response threshold. Same output need not imply same latent or same historical cause.
- **Consciousness:** global availability, recurrent processing, integrated causal structure, teleodynamic relational coherence, purely task/behavioural confounds, and substrate-dependence. Compare matched consumers, don't equate distinct scalar definitions.
- **Quantum:** spin-dependent material microphysics, classical effective electrodynamics, classical stochastic correlations, genuine coherent quantum mechanism and unknown mechanism. An odd magneto-optical response does not uniquely select quantum physics; Bell tests are only applicable to appropriate two-party correlations.
- **ZPF:** zero-point oscillator energy/renormalised Casimir stress and extraction/consciousness claims differ in Hamiltonian, boundary, observables and supplied energy accounting.
