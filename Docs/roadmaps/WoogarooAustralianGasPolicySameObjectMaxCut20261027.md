## The big question and six separate typed objects

What can be *shown* from a sequence of original records linking **subdivision/building design → gas connection → particular distribution network → actual fixed/volume charges → wholesale gas market → export-policy effects**? Separately, what documents show **producer engagement → precise policy instruction or original accepted amendment → public decision**? Combining the two requires actual shared physical/commercial/procedural objects, not common policy vocabulary, lobbying adjacency or political beneficiaries.

## Two '15% reserves' even within March One Nation: same headline, different legal object

- [Hanson bill status as indexed by the original Parliament/tracked at OPAX](https://opax.com.au/bill/au-federal-s1489) — the *Offshore Petroleum and Greenhouse Gas Storage Amendment (Domestic Reserve) Bill 2026*, introduced **10 March 2026**, concerns agreements for **15% of exported petroleum from Commonwealth waters**. The bill is a **private senator's proposal**, not in-force gas legislation. Original APH bill PDF was not retrieved in this continuation.
- [Hanson March 12 Senate motion, first-person speech via attributed OpenAustralia Hansard mirror](https://www.openaustralia.org.au/senate/?id=2026-03-12.16.1) — asks to subject **all gas production** to a **15% minimum domestic reserve**. The motion and the private bill need not have identical geographic/denominator scope. **Murray Watt** moved parliamentary procedure at 9:47 am; procedural participation **does not** turn him into sponsor of the private bill, private adviser or beneficiary.
- [Hanson May 21 public conference first-person launch as originally reported by ABC](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-05-21/one-nation-proposes-new-tax-break-for-drilling/106707250) — acknowledged an earlier reservation stance and described a pivot **following consultation with industry**; reporter quotes a new optional Commonwealth equity/exploration-rebate/royalty approach. The **later official party-authored 9 June text** is a **distinct publisher original** ([One Nation's Gas Policy](https://www.onenation.org.au/gas-policy-ownership)), prescribing a 30% exploration rebate paired with possible up-to-30% equity stakes, new-project wellhead royalty, and rejecting mandated reservation and 25% gas export-value taxes. That June publication does not prove when policy text was drafted, which producer was consulted, or a causal Senex instruction.
- **Temporal exclusion:** Senex's **24 September response** cannot have caused One Nation's **21 May policy pivot** or the federal **10 September exposure draft**. Senex's **8 May** objection precedes the 21 May event but **date ordering alone does not supply private meeting/communications evidence**. Similarity of criticism of domestic price oversupply and smaller onshore producers does not identify a shared commissioned script.

**Finite Agda contribution (not historical proof):** \`March15PolicyObject\` and \`GasPolicyDenominator\` construct a two-world \`INF.NonFactorabilityWitness\`: the display \`15%\` has a single printed value, whereas the March **offshore exported petroleum volume** and **all gas production volume** denominators differ. This is a rigorous no-factorisation fact **about the declared finite representation**. It does not establish the bill's enacted meaning, statutory effect, or who instructed its drafting. Likewise, separate typed gas-system layers prohibit automatically treating an export licence as local network ownership or a developer inclusion contract.

## Third-order economic hypotheses and counterfactual checks

1. **Potential household benefit:** if federally required additional LNG-linked gas is effectively supplied into the relevant eastern wholesale market, contracted at lower prices, and passed through to the retail tariff of the actual Springfield gas account, a bill reduction could result. Each 'if' is an **unpaid** receipt; lower wholesale prices do not automatically change fixed access charges. Quantification must use an exact tariff and counterfactual.
2. **Potential industrial benefit:** manufacturers on east-coast gas contracts could benefit from a wider contracted gas supply; whether that reaches Springfield employment, developer land values or municipal revenue demands identifiable company/location/contract records. **No same-object beneficiary is proven** by nearby geography or national policy.
3. **Potential network/producer cost:** reserved gas could alter onshore producer competitiveness and distributor throughput, or preserve gas connections through existing local technical choices. Senex asserts a crowd-out risk, while the government asserts greater available supply and affordability. These are **competing original propositions**, not validated demand-response magnitudes. Neither direction demonstrates secretly coordinated interest.
4. **Political strategic benefit:** public debate and party positions may attract voters/donors, but intent, lobbying instruction, actual financing and resulting votes/approvals are separate source types. Murray Watt's gas procedural role, 2026 ministerial gas reforms and Woogaroo environmental responsibilities must not be retroactively joined into political quid pro quo without an original common payment/decision source.

## STOP rule and verification

The original commercial question is a **local operator/distributor/tariff join** and **dated lobbying/product adoption join**, not an open-ended famous-person proximity network. Ibrahim/Pareto stop rule: quotient producers and repeated press reprints first; acquire the shortest original that changes a defined consumer; drop paid source-locator questions; retain absence as \`notLocated\` unless a bounded complete archive establishes true absence. No conjectured criminal or covert-command attribution from social association.

**Actual implementation:** \`DASHI/Governance/WoogarooGasNationalReservationPolicyBridge20261027Exact.agda\` imports existing master Woogaroo/Allgas/First Nine owners and existing #1251 climate-gas source and no-factorisation owners, with source-typed local gas political-version witnesses and fail-closed 15-field boundary; \`DASHI/Governance/EmpiricalGenealogyRound3Everything.agda\` imports this thin consumer; source-only scoped Python regression added.
