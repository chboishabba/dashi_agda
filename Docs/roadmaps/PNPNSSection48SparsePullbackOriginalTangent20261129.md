## A. Exact sparse nonlinear symbolic closure
Represent each Boolean polynomial by the finite set of squarefree monomials with odd coefficient. Addition symmetric difference, multiplication support union with parity cancellation. For each original deterministic total action given as a multioutput gate DAG, compile its coordinate polynomials by exact gate semantics (const/not/and/or, plus an explicit xor macro). Pullback substitutes the coordinate polynomials into each monomial, using union products and exact cancellation. The representation preserves complete Boolean semantics by induction over gates and over support. Do NOT truncate degrees; substitution can lower degree.
Starting with source observation polynomials, incremental echelon elimination maintains a basis in the canonical ANF vector space. Each independent row is a linear combination of actual observation pullbacks, and all processed rows are closed under every action at completion. Hence the span equals the span of all finite-word observations, as Section47. Coordinate extraction by leading monomial yields reduced matrices M_a and consumer rows Cbar. Exact q(T_a x)=M_a q(x) and O(x)=Cbar q(x) follow by evaluation of the coefficient equalities. This gives full future-equivalence on the image of q, not an assertion that every vector in the reduced ambient space is reachable. Exact finite-word observation via ordered matrix products; repeated action via binary powering. This is a concrete sparse implementation of the existing observable-function closure, not a general polynomial-time algorithm.
The compiler charges individual term toggles, pivot scans, monomial-factor traversals and gate/wire accesses; cap rank, peak terms per individual polynomial and counted work. On any refusal return CENSORED with no exact output. Term/operation counts are not physical bit cost or peak total storage; JS Set/BigInt allocation, matrix power and construction of emitted DAGs are outside those counters. No claim of optimality. A dense control product of 12 independent OR pairs has 23 native gates and exactly 3^12=531441 ANF terms; with 1024-term cap the compiler refuses before closure. Thus a small circuit is not necessarily a small ANF.
## B. Original input map is paid, not an oracle
Emit explicit const/not/and/or creation-order DAGs for q, each M_a and the consumer. Cache shared monomials. Lower XOR(a,b) to (a OR b) AND NOT(a AND b), four gates. Input wires and output aliases are allowed; zero gates does not mean zero wire transfer or free output serialization. Every emitted gate evaluated in independent direct Boolean evaluator. Multi-output adapter follows existing check_pnp_dag_cutset.py ABI; not yet a native GateProgram/JointCut theorem, nor a measured physical bit-clock. Separate construction, q(x), reduced step, readout, and matrix-power costs; no lower bound inferred from their particular representation. Fixed-word or huge-horizon jump can save work, not automatically faster for one step.
