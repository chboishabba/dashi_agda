## Exact projection theorem

Otherwise select one detecting row (a,b); G forbids t=f(y)=b+a*u(y) over its projected domain D defined by the other rows after eliminating t with the selected row. Thus each detecting flat forbids exactly one t at each point of D, none outside D. A quotient y has no feasible lift iff an unaffected flat forbids y or two distinct detecting flats simultaneously forbid opposite t values. The latter is exactly D_i intersect D_j intersect {f_i+f_j=1}, another affine flat; inconsistent intersections discarded. Proof uses precisely two possible t values and requires no independence assumption. Retain all unaffected flats and all consistent opposite-pair flats. This represents existential projection pointwise: F'(y) iff F(u(y)) OR F(u(y)+v). Reopen by checking the first lift and, if it fails, the second. Retain full prior chart at each step; neither signs nor occupied zero silently discarded. This preserves existential support, not counts. Ordinary signed clause resolution is the v=e_i special case.
