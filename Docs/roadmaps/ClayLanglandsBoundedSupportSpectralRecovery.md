## Field and exact decoder

For n original bits, take h=3^t least power with m=2h>=n+1. K=F2[T]/(T^(2h)+T^h+1) is a field: the polynomial is Phi_(3^(t+1)), and ord_(3^(t+1))(2)=2*3^t by elementary lifting of the exponent (v3(4^(3^t)-1)=t+1). Hence irreducible over F2, explicit without searching for a primitive field element. m=O(n+1). Injectively label x by the binary representation of 1+int(x) interpreted as a polynomial-basis field element. The integer addition is not field addition.

Given bound k>=1, request s_j=sum_{x satisfies F} a_x^j in K, 0<=j<2k, including s_0 (count parity, not count). For 1<=r=#support<=k, H_r=(s_(i+j))_(0<=i,j<r)=V V^T with V_(i,x)=a_x^i. The square Vandermonde is invertible; det H_r=prod_(x<y)(a_y-a_x)^2 nonzero. H_l is singular for l>r because its rank is <=r. Thus find largest invertible leading block <=k. Solve H_r c=(s_r,...,s_(2r-1))^T (minus equals plus). The unique monic locator P(Z)=Z^r+sum_(i<r)c_i Z^i equals prod_x(Z-a_x), since the locator recurrence holds by summation and invertibility. Roots recover the original labels, then invert integer encoding and verify original F. No prior count needed.

Deterministically split P over K using gcd with Tr_(K/F2)(T^i Z) for each i=0..m-1, evaluated modulo each current factor by repeated squaring. Nondegeneracy of the field trace pairing ensures distinct field roots differ on at least one coordinate; on the promised source all factors become linear, without field enumeration or random factorization. Check all roots distinct and in original label range, verify F, and recompute supplied syndromes. Off promise return only verified candidates or UNKNOWN. In particular no assertion of full enumeration or UNSAT off promise, even if syndrome matches. For label set 8..15 in GF64 (n=4), all four syndromes j=0..3 vanish: the affine 3-bit chart sum kills Boolean degree <=2 for j=0..3 (Frobenius squares are F2-linear). This is occupied zero, not absent support. General cost polynomial in n,k with straightforward Gaussian elimination, field arithmetic and polynomial gcd/trace splitting; no dependence on field cardinality. Source verification charged for each candidate.

## Original-source spectral weld and exact remaining cut

Each bit of each a_x^j can be computed with an explicit polynomial-size Boolean circuit (integer increment, fixed field multiplication and successive powers) and deterministic gate-equivalence clauses, giving a unique auxiliary lift of every original assignment. Mark the output bits. Existing simultaneous first parity jet on that actual lifted source supplies their XOR sums, which are exactly the field syndromes, or use the existing signed rank-two residual (3A-D)/4 with one marked bit weight and reduce the recovered integral moment modulo 2. For the latter, weight a Boolean mark by 2 when it is one and 1 otherwise: W(2)-W(1) equals the count of marked witnesses, then reduce mod 2. Compute both residues mod 8 before exact division by 4; no division by 4 in characteristic two. The gate lift and parity mapping are mathematical constructions here, not an integrated implementation of the original Weil evaluator. Original zero mode, signed residual and source/revision/precision coordinates remain separate. No new arbitrary weight compiler.

A uniform polynomial producer of these syndromes on every source promised at most k witnesses would give deterministic bounded-support recovery. For k=1 this specializes to C2a, not a stronger universal theorem: s_1 alone suffices on singleton.
