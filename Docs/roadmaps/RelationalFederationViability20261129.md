Preserve alternative hypotheses, competing interpretations, dissent, freedom of association and exit, rights, due process, privacy, legal limits and distinct Indigenous jurisdictions.

No anthropocentric scalar terminal objective; no forced synthesis of knowledge traditions. Admissibility is a joint, situated relation, not a score or an assertion that one society is superior. Country, nonhuman ecological relations, non-substitutable ecological limits, present agency, future options, material sufficiency and actual authority remain distinct. Seven future inspection points are a DASHI-selected horizon, not a universal Indigenous doctrine. Extend System V with independent obligation/authority fibres rather than declaring an ecological sensor, model or majority sovereign. System V is the people is an interpretive formulation, not an unrestricted authority theorem.
