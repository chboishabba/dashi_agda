## Critical logical edge
Let X denote a source-root-anchored experimental run, S(X) its summary score, R(X) a learned representation, and A(X) its validated causal-mechanism classification. The programme must never assume either `S` or `R` factors `A`. A nonfactorability witness blocks this. A *calibrated* separating channel may narrow or break a particular equivalence class, but separability in a stipulated finite toy model is not evidence that the physical channel exists. LILA semantic equivariance can enable choice of discriminating experiments; it cannot replace experimental measurement.
