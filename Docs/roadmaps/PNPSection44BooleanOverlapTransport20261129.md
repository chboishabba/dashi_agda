## A. Global overlap formal lift (proof)
Given finite field (indeed any field) arithmetic circuit C with constants, addition and multiplication, syntactic support V_g, let W be union over reachable product gates of V_a intersect V_b. For each assignment alpha to W, restrict C to alpha. All products in the restricted circuit have disjoint remaining supports, by construction. Induction proves every restricted gate is formally multilinear. Let chi_alpha be the product over W of x_i for alpha_i=1 and (1-x_i) otherwise. Sum chi_alpha C|alpha. Each selector and its factor have disjoint variable sets. The result is formally multilinear and on each Boolean point agrees with C since exactly one selector is 1. Uniqueness: induction on n by writing f=f0+x_n f1 and evaluating x_n=0,1. Thus exact canonical multilinear representative, using O(n+2^w(L+w+1)) gates including the input nodes. No assumption that original polynomial has low formal degree.

## B. Adaptive overlap lift (proof and implementation)
The global carrier is sufficient, not necessary. For each gate g and partial assignment (K,alpha), construct L(g,K,alpha), the formal multilinear representative of its Boolean restriction. Canonicalize K to K intersect V_g and alpha to K. Constants and variables obvious. Addition is componentwise. Product g=a*b: let S=(V_a intersect V_b) minus K. For each beta on S, multiply L(a,K union S,alpha union beta) and L(b,K union S,alpha union beta); their remaining syntactic supports are disjoint. Multiply by chi_beta on S, disjoint from both factors, and sum. Induction on the acyclic gate order proves support contained in V_g minus K, syntactic multilinearity, and agreement on all Boolean extensions. No recursive branch is a decision about satisfiability. Cache by exact (g,K,alpha), share selectors by (S,beta). This is a constructive algebraic analogue of existing relative-support and separator reopening, not a replacement for those SAT producers.

Define H_g as union of intersections at all strict product ancestors of g on root-to-g paths, intersected with V_g. Each actual context has K subset H_g: induction on the recursion edge, including canonicalization. At most 3^|H_g| contexts per gate (unfixed/0/1 per variable), often much less. If b=max |H_g|, t=max local product intersection, gate construction cost is O(n+L*3^b*2^t*(t+1)) in a unit gate/mask-operation model, or the sharper measured O(n+number of states+sum over product states of 2^|S|*(|S|+1)). Actual bitset/key cost additionally polynomial in n and index widths; this is not a calibrated physical bit-TM clock. A polynomial-time promise holds e.g. b+t=O(log L) and n polynomial in L. Maximum local overlap t alone does not establish such a bound; ancestor accumulation matters. No claim of necessity, optimality or general FPT recognition.

## C. Actual conditional consequences
For a multilinear target polynomial F, a cube-correct circuit lifted by A/B is an ordinary formal circuit for F, so the hypotheses of E.4 and K.2 are restored for the new circuit, provided those upstream lemmas and their machine encodings are independently accepted. This does not require any spectral point to be computed by the normalizer. It does not imply a lower bound on Boolean circuits for SAT. Conditional on an arithmetic lower bound B(m), any such circuit has lifted size >= B(m). In global form this forces w+log_2(L+w+1)+O(1) >= log_2 B(m), accounting also for input count if not included in L. In adaptive form log L+b log_2 3+t+log_2(t+1)+O(1) >= log_2 B(m), with the same input qualification. This is a restricted overlap tradeoff, not a theorem that b or w is small for arbitrary Boolean circuits. K.2's weighted program still needs actual costed target integration and K.3 scale constraints; formal lift does not by itself accelerate deterministic decision.

## D. Negative controls
- Raw support-degree substitution in K.2 fails: repeated squaring of x has quotient support degree 1, local overlap 1, but unrolled formal multiplication tree has 2^depth leaves. This falsifies the naive leaf bound, not every compressed evaluator.
- (x+y)(x+z) in the Boolean quotient contains a linear x term absent in the formal product; disjoint splits miss it.
- Independent x_i*x_i factors have local overlap 1 and global carrier n. Adaptive contexts remain small; global branching is not an intrinsic lower bound.
- SAT presence and parity/weighted count disagree on an even nonzero number of witnesses. The shared symbolic state-sum compiler is not a deterministic decision-to-counting reduction.
- One-hot tensor agreement may be cheap despite large coefficient flattening rank; fixed-cut rank is not an unrestricted Boolean lower bound. No natural-proofs, relativization or algebrization barrier is asserted evaded.
