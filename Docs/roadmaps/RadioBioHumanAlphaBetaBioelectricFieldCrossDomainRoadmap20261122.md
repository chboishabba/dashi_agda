## Executive result and scope

The most important **literal cross-programme link** recovered is [Wang, Hilburn, Wu, Mizuhara, Cousté, Abrahams, Bernstein, Matani, Shimojo & Kirschvink (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1523/ENEURO.0483-18.2019), *Transduction of the Geomagnetic Field as Evidenced from alpha-Band Activity in the Human Brain*, eNeuro 6(2), ENEURO.0483-18.2019. Its original funding statement explicitly names **DARPA RadioBio grant D17AC00019**, supporting later analysis of the data; earlier research support is separately credited (Human Frontier Science Program and JSPS). This is a funded **source/analysis lineage**, **not** proof of functional radiative intercellular communication or an operational military application. The authors report stimulus- and polarity-specific alpha-band event-related desynchronization to controlled Earth-strength magnetic rotations; magnetic reception mechanism, perception, generalization and independent reproduction remain separate gates. Source: [publisher article including funding](https://www.eneuro.org/content/early/2019/03/18/ENEURO.0483-18.2019/tab-article-info?versioned=true); [PMCID PMC6494972](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6494972/).

DARPA's [2017 RadioBio announcement](https://www.darpa.mil/news/2017/radiobio) and [programme page](https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/radiobio) propose to **test whether purposeful EM-wave signalling between biological cells exists**, including theoretical modelling, experimental tests and later independent replication. The solicitation is a research question, **not** its resolution. Distinguish **near-field subwavelength coupling**, **radiative cell-to-cell communication**, **quasistatic induction**, **transmembrane voltage / gap junctions**, **neural alpha/beta EEG oscillations**, and **human magnetic responsiveness**.

No universal resonance, remote influence, healing, common covert programme, or source-to-human transfer is established by thematic similarity, similar frequencies or common departmental funding.

## Research architecture — existing code first, no blanket synthesis theorem

Source waveform **S(t,x)** (carrier, envelope, repetition, phase, exposure geometry) → measured or simulated physical field **E/B(t,x)** under a stated medium/boundary model → coupling mechanism **K** (antenna, induction, magnetochemical, ionic/electrochemical, acoustic or optogenetic; typed separately) → biological/neural state **X(t,x)** → observation operator **O** (EEG/SEEG/MEG, voltage dyes, cell chemistry, morphology, wound closure) → sham/negative and alternative-cause discrimination.

A useful operator schematic is **Y = O ∘ X_K ∘ M_field(S) + noise**. Equality of a measured EEG spectral peak with a source repetition rate never alone identifies K, M_field, source entity, subject effect or causality. Retain **f_carrier ≠ f_modulation ≠ f_EEG-response** and **source power ≠ target dose**. Energy and Maxwell passivity requirements remain attached to Filsinger/Smith physics.

### Universal evidence and promotion rules

- **S0** documentary pointer; **S1** original author/institution paper or archive inspected; **S2** quantitative original observed data with exact sample, geometry, instrument and comparator; **S3** independently reproducible analysis of actual original data; **S4** new sham-controlled physical experiment; **S5** independent external replication and scoped transport between systems. Formal/SymPy acceptance is a separate **M** mathematics/software gate, never a surrogate for S2–S5.
- Each claim stores source root, exact title/author/year/DOI or archival ID, content hash/version, earliest original study and first repository implementation SHA/date, measurement/subject identity, comparison class, contradictory sources, non-observation constraints, license, uncertainty and acceptable inference limit.
- **Cross-producer BIDI:** recover canonical claims from every original paper, field observation, program announcement or social post; reverse lookup canonical observations and unresolved discrepancies to originals. Same scientist, waveform, organization, research topic, similar term and similar target frequency are **not** same experimental object.
- Explicit null outputs are first-class: H03 26 GHz EEG null, 1969 PANDORA controls, nonresponding geomagnetic rotations, no-sham failures and genuine non-identifiability are results; do not bury them in positivity-biased citation accumulation.
- Proposed human/animal experiments require ethics, human-subject consent, RF/electromagnetic safety, dose limits, biosafety and independent oversight. This roadmap specifies research, not unsupervised stimulation or medical advice.
