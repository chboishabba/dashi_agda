## Foundational objective — compatible reduction density
The motivating paper is Inoue, Kawarabayashi, Miyashita, Mohar, Thomassen and Thorup, The Four Color Theorem with Linearly Many Reducible Configurations and Near-Linear Time Coloring, arXiv:2603.24880v3 (1 October 2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24880v3. Its abstract establishes linearly many pairwise non-touching D-reducible configurations or pairwise non-crossing obstructing cycles of length at most five in planar triangulations, including flat regions, leading to constant-factor reduction and O(n log n) coloring. It is not a general graph reduction theorem or P=NP.
