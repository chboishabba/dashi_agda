## Split the central composite into testable arrows

A. Facility machinery -> acoustic/structural pressure/vibration: **physically credible**; verify facility operating log, site spectra Pa/Hz and accelerometer m/s², background/wind controls, location and distance. `dBA` alone insufficient. 140 Hz is low-frequency SOUND, not sub-20 Hz infrasound.

B. Source signature -> receptor exposure: **must be measured**. Propagation geometry and buildings, coupling paths, mic response, directivity, distance, night/sleep exposure and true spectral dose. No direct extrapolation of a purple graph to every neighbour.

C. Receptor exposure -> annoyance, sleep, cardiovascular effects: general environmental-noise literature supports risks at relevant exposures. Site-specific effects remain empirically unassigned; demand matched longitudinal/intervention designs and uncertainty.

D. Receptor exposure -> infertility, autoimmune pathology, DNA damage, "lowered testosterone", BBB injury: **unsupported for data-centre low-frequency sound by the identified original studies**. These are separate endpoints, not automatic extrapolations from auditory irritation or stress.

E. Ordinary RF base station -> human RF dose: measurable but distinct from sound; requires frequency, power, geometry, duration and SAR/exposure. An animal 900/1800 MHz BBB experiment is not a measured 5G/6G effect.

F. RF dose -> BBB effect: experimental literature mixed, and updated 2026 scoping review does not establish general causal link in humans. No automatic transition into whole-population disease or increased suggestibility.

G. Documented secret historic drug/suggestion experiments -> today's specific equipment/operators: historically documented objectives and abuses **do not establish a continuous personnel/device/transaction/field mechanism**. Require original same-object device or operational instruction, authenticated attribution, emission measurements, mechanistic performance and actual corroboration. TEMPEST cannot substitute for that join.

H. CIA influence dispatch -> foil hats/internet ridicule: existence of a documented propaganda instruction is not the origin/provenance of the separate meme or a license to assume every criticism is intentional intelligence activity. Requires original chain-of-publication evidence.

None of B–H can be paid merely by shared use of the terms *frequency*, *vibration*, *control* or *energy*.
