coherence and evidence responsiveness; triadic and historical mediation;
ideological or ordinary repetition; identifiable causal effect; documented

religion, dissent, or Indigenous source is classified as an attack by label.

## Measured coherence design

Proposed source-validated vector, NOT a single uncalibrated metric:

C(t) = (logical consistency with scoped propositions,
        probabilistic constraint calibration,
        evidentiary accountability,
        response to corrected sources,
        consequential dissent/appeal authority,
        coordination without domination).

Each dimension requires independent source scope, missingness, baseline,
measurement reliability, sampling coverage, prediction horizon and
context-specific validation. Strong group agreement is not equivalent
to truth or autonomy. Open disagreement is not automatically incoherence.

## Experiment and causal identification plan

Choose publicly documented institutional/campaign events with archived
source revisions, funding and actor relations, observed actions and
outcomes. Predeclare distinct hypotheses (organic development, genuine
dissent, platform reinforcement, institutional power, coordinated doubt,
mixed mechanism). For each source-licensed observation use the existing
costed/sequential discriminator to find a real method that separates
some live hypotheses, then update only the dependent claims.

A causal claim requires evidence for treatment/exposure, comparison
units, sampling/selection, time alignment, consistency, confounding
control or justified natural experiment, exposure spillovers, negative
controls, sensitivity bounds and uncertainty. Mechanism and hostile
intent are *separate* consumers with their own direct evidence. Null
and underidentified results remain publishable.

John Brown's hegemony framework is relevant across institutions, but
must not assert universal religious harm; Indigenous cultural history
requires specific custodial and historical authority, not analogous
narratives recast as a universal cognitive-warfare taxonomy.
