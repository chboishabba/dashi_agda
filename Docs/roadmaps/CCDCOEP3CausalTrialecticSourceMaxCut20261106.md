## Experiment and causal identification plan

source revisions, funding and actor relations, observed actions and

dissent, platform reinforcement, institutional power, coordinated doubt,

units, sampling/selection, time alignment, consistency, confounding
control or justified natural experiment, exposure spillovers, negative
controls, sensitivity bounds and uncertainty. Mechanism and hostile
intent are *separate* consumers with their own direct evidence. Null
