## 1. General signed crossing certificate, consuming the existing Mellin owner

For any E/Q with normalized newform f(q)=sum a_n q^n, write c=2pi/sqrt(N), q*=exp(-c), G(q)=f(q)/q, F(t)=f(exp(-ct)), eps=(-1)^delta. Fricke pairs the lower tail with the upper. Lambda^(delta+2j)(1)=2 int_1^infty F(t) log(t)^(delta+2j) dt. G and F have the same sign for 0<q<=q*. The q=0 limit G(0)=1 is retained; the negative sign case has exact F(1)=0, not a numerically small value. If there are at most m distinct sign crossings inside t>1 then multiply by product(log(t)^2-log(t_i)^2) over the actual crossings. Since F eventually positive, the product times F is nonnegative and strictly positive somewhere. Consequently at least one of the first m+1 moments of the correct parity is nonzero and analytic order a<=2m+delta. For selected rank >=2, classical low analytic rank theorem forces a>=2; if root sign negative, a>=3. Thus one crossing proves exact rank 2 or 3, respectively, once lower bound >=2 has been paid. This is not a bound tying general arithmetic rank to sign crossings.

Exact computational recipe: by |a_n|<=d(n)sqrt(n)<=2n and R>=q*, R<1, truncation G_M=sum_(j=0)^(M-1) a_(j+1) q^j gives uniform bounds
T0=2 R^M [(M+1)/(1-R)+R/(1-R)^2],
T1=2 R^(M-1) [M(M+1)/(1-R)+(2M+1)R/(1-R)^2+R(1+R)/(1-R)^3].
These bound the omitted function and first derivative on [0,R], respectively, from geometric-series sums. Interval Horner with rational endpoints and outward rounding verifies sign on whole bins, not sampled points. More generally bounds of the same type for any derivative allow Rolle-based root counting, provided genuine sign/derivative certificates are found; no universal rank-dependent crossing bound is assumed.
