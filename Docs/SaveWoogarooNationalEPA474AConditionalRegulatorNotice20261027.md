## Existing primary authority (new P0 enforcement lane)

The **Commonwealth EPBC Act 1999, Compilation 70 effective 24 August 2026**, [Division 13A, section 474A](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A00485/latest) authorises the National EPA CEO to issue an **Environment Protection Order (EPO)** in urgent compliance circumstances. The CEO must reasonably believe that a contravention or likely contravention of the EPBC Act, regulations, or an approval/permit/exemption condition has occurred, is occurring or is likely; that serious environmental harm has resulted or is imminent; and that an order is necessary for compliance and/or avoidance or mitigation. [The regulator's own compliance explanations](https://www.nationalepa.gov.au/epbc/compliance/audit-outcomes) state these three requirements. The order is typically for up to **14 days**, with the statutory limited extension route. The exact legal text, person bound, service, spatial requirements and time are controlling.

**Proof of an actually exercised power, different action:** the National EPA lists its **7 October 2026 Environment Protection Order against Warrengail Pastoral Company Pty Ltd** in relation to intended activity on **Wombinoo Station**, expiring **21 October 2026**. This is **NOT** Woogaroo, not an order against Cherish or Stockland, and not proof of any offence at Springfield. It is a source-original positive comparator showing the instrument is operational. The original register includes requirements preventing certain site disturbance, which are case-specific.

[National EPA Rules 2026 F2026L01105](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2026L01105/asmade/2026-08-21/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html) schedule item 6 identifies s474A order contents as registrable. Item 7 addresses variations/revocations. A missing matching website hit is **not** proof no internal investigation or unpublished communications occurred.

**Never substitute:** s474A CEO administrative EPO ≠ s475 Federal Court contravention injunction ≠ ADJR s15 judicial-review stay ≠ Queensland Planning Act commencement / Nature Conservation Act ss102–107 conservation-order procedures. An original EPO request does not file a court proceeding or suspend the time for judicial review.
