## Highest-value extraction order

### Part Ai — Preliminary Documentation Report

Photograph/scan or transcribe with exact page numbers:

- executive summary and purpose;
- action-area and cadastral description;
- all lot/plan identifiers;
- development footprint maps;
- total vegetation/habitat area;
- proposed-clearing area;
- retained/conservation habitat area;
- koala habitat classification and quality scores;
- habitat described as critical to survival / critical habitat, preserving the report's exact terminology;
- grey-headed flying-fox, swift parrot, regent honeyeater, threatened flora and TEC impact tables;
- survey results and species-use statements;
- corridor/connectivity analysis;
- fragmentation and edge-effect analysis;
- cumulative-impact discussion;
- avoidance and alternatives considered;
- mitigation hierarchy;
- residual impacts;
- offsets, including area, location and ecological equivalence claims;
- references to recovery plans, conservation advice or statutory guidelines;
- proponent's final residual/significance conclusions.

**Consumers:** EPBC Part 9 decision; Queensland NCA s 13; NCA s 102.

### Part Aii — Vegetation Clearing & Fauna Management Plan — Clearing Directions

Highest priority:

- clearing-stage maps;
- clearing sequence;
- fauna-spotter/catcher procedures;
- habitat-tree treatment;
- exclusion/no-go areas;
- timing constraints;
- pre-clearance survey obligations;
- machinery/works boundaries;
- fauna escape/connectivity assumptions;
- any staged or sequential clearing that gives a concrete future threatening-process chronology.

**Consumers:** NCA s 102 interim restraint; EPBC compliance/conditions; enforcement backstop if later relevant.

### Part Aiii — Attachment A15

Do not assume what A15 contains from the catalogue title alone. First record its title, authorship, date and purpose. Then extract any:

- habitat-quality methodology;
- raw scoring tables;
- GIS/map outputs;
- species survey data;
- offset calculations;
- population/connectivity modelling;
- expert conclusions.

**Consumers:** determined after the attachment is identified; likely EPBC and potentially NCA s 13 if ecological.

### Part B — Referral Material

Extract:

- original 2019 action description;
- original footprint and lot identity;
- original MNES baseline;
- original proposed clearing;
- referral-era koala listing/status assumptions;
- project changes between referral and 2026 PD;
- original avoidance/mitigation representations;
- any differences between the referred action and current action.

**Consumer:** temporal/change audit and EPBC scope; potentially useful to the planning/grandfathering lane.

Never record only a bare number such as `128 ha`. Retain what the hectares measure, the map/table defining them, whether the figure is gross action area, habitat, direct clearing, indirect impact, retained land or offset land, and which species/community it concerns.

## Critical WrongType checks while extracting

```text
proponent says "critical habitat"
  != Queensland NCA s 13 critical-habitat finding

koala habitat score
  != viable-population essentiality by itself

offset area
  != ecological equivalence

remote offset
  != replacement of local corridor function

mapped biodiversity corridor
  != statutory critical habitat

referral footprint
  != necessarily identical to final-PD footprint

clearing direction
  != evidence clearing has commenced

legal-deposit copy
  != agency adoption of every consultant conclusion
```

## What would most reduce the legal residual

The highest-value same-object join is:

```text
exact final action polygon
  x exact habitat class
  x exact species/community use
  x exact clearing/retention area
  x corridor/connectivity function
  x avoidance/residual-impact analysis
```

That one join can feed both:

```text
EPBC 2019/8575 Part 9 significance/refusal analysis
```

and independently:

```text
Queensland NCA s 13 essential-to-viable-population analysis.
```

The next best extraction is the **clearing sequence and timing** from Part Aii, because that can convert the NCA s 102 lane from an abstract power into an exact threatening-process chronology.
