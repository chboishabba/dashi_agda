# NV SI metrology and SAXON Q source-root cross-PR audit (2026-10-26)

**Latest PRs inspected:** Agda #1270 (SAXON Q experimental paper, architecture and logical-register audits), #1269 (NV photon observer), #1262 (spin-one/C3/SUSY-QM), #1261 (tetragonal crystal, not NV site C3v), #1266 (revisable observer no-go); Lean #76/#85 (quantum mereology and channels). Do not duplicate #1270's candidate logical embedding or Grover model.

**Existing SI owner:** `DASHI/Physics/Units/SI.agda`; original SI source is BIPM, *The International System of Units*, 9th ed. (2019), revision 4.01 (June 2026), DOI **10.59161/AUEZ1291**. SI `Dimension`, fixed-point `Quantity`, `Measurement`, `MagneticFluxDensity`, `Frequency`, `Power`, `Time` and named SI units already exist. `SIMetrologyBridge.agda` preserves source/version/authority; `EmpiricalCalibrationBridgeUnits.agda` keeps arbitrary uncalibrated quantities from being promoted into measured physical values.

**New consumer:** `NVSIMetrologyReadoutAdapterExact.agda` imports those exact SI dimension-indexed types and MCG #1238's actual `AxialNV` model, requiring field, frequency, optical power, exposure time, units and source/version for an instrument calibration. It **does not** pretend to infer physical quantity arithmetic or gyromagnetic conversion from rational Hamiltonian coefficients. It leaves a named, inhabited-from-evidence forward-model obligation rather than inventing values.

**Dimension-sensitive discrepancy to resolve physically:** spectroscopic cyclic frequency `f` in Hz and angular frequency `ω=2πf` are conventionally distinguished even though rad is dimensionless in the SI base-dimension system; using a `γ` fitted in Hz/T with an angular-frequency Hamiltonian, or treating pT/sqrt(Hz) sensitivity as a magnetic-field measurement in tesla, can introduce scale/category errors. This must be explicitly resolved at the source-specific model-to-quantity bridge, rather than justified by matching dimensions alone. Existing MCG Omar et al. system magnetometer sensitivities (DOI 10.1126/sciadv.aeg5281) are not readout/gate fidelity and not input values for SAXON Q without same-system calibration.

**Other measurement obligations:** photon count rate is not identical to photon count or optical pump power; a binary detector likelihood does not derive from power and exposure without measured optical collection, absorption/fluorescence dynamics, detector efficiency/background and dependence. Physical source apparatus, isotope/hyperfine parameters, pulse schedule, angle/orientation and calibration uncertainties remain separate.

**Attribution boundary:** experimental results belong to Gemsheim and colleagues (arXiv:2609.13022v2); vendor SXQ128/SXQ512 claims belong to SAXON Q; existing NV models to diamond MCG PR #1238; BIPM owns the SI standard; newly written types are a DASHI/OpenAI-assisted integration, not a result attributed to the primary authors. Source-written only, no Agda kernel or CI verification and no same-device measured instrument receipt.
