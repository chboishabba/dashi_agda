## Source-specific physics and exact discriminator

### A. What is physically real

General relativity couples geometry to the **total stress-energy tensor**. Electromagnetic fields, acoustic/elastic deformation, charged matter, plasma and rotation can contribute to a variable source. A selected nonzero time-dependent *transverse-traceless* radiation solution with a physically conserved source could, in principle, generate extraordinarily weak high-frequency GW emission. Gertsenshtein conversion of electromagnetic radiation in an external magnetised region is an ordinary conditional GR/Maxwell effect. No extra postulated vacuum amplification follows.

Two electromagnetic fields are not exotic merely because they overlap. For total fields E=E1+E2 and B=B1+B2,

  u_total = u_1 + u_2 + epsilon0 (E1 · E2) + (B1 · B2)/mu0.

The cross terms follow *exactly* from ordinary Maxwell energy density. Measured mixing, beating or response at sum/difference frequencies can be entirely classical. They do not establish a propagating gravitational degree of freedom. In a material plasma further constitutive nonlinearities are allowed, but require their own calibrated species/ionisation/collision/current source; "generating plasma" in an IPC classification is not evidence of an actual plasma.

The patent notes inert gas (including xenon) **may** ionise under excitation. This must be measured independently; sound itself is not proof of ionisation. Counter-rotation may be a mechanical balancing technique, not a proof of quadrupole stress radiation.

### B. Model-specific Gertsenshtein scaling

The patent's Equation 4 copies the JASON conversion scaling F = (4 pi G / c^4) B0^2 L^2, and immediately comments that conversion to SI magnetic-field units requires a 1/mu0 dimensional factor. Using the stated illustrative 4pi prefactor and explicit SI units gives

  F_SI = [4 pi G/(mu0 c^4)] (B_T L_m)^2.

That is a SELECTED weak-conversion idealisation. Exact polarisation, coherent length, bandwidth, geometry, Q, material dispersion and mode matching remain independent physical inputs. Numerical prefactors also depend on electromagnetic unit conventions; this is not a universal rigorous bound.

At B=14 T, L=1 m: F=1.61949e-35. A hypothetical incoming EM power of 1 MW then gives GW power around 1.61949e-29 W *in that selected model*. This is not the prediction for any actual experimental device.

To obtain 10^10 W GW using this selected 14-Tesla configuration would require ~6.17e44 W of source EM power. Pais instead invokes 10^10 Tesla and 10^33 W/m^2 electromagnetic flux. The former alone has ordinary magnetic energy density B^2/(2mu0) ~3.97887e25 J/m^3; the latter is an unvalidated generating premise. It is **invalid to apply the weak-field formula as a reliable extrapolation** to 10^10 Tesla; demonstrating such a field/source and a self-consistent theory would already be extraordinary new physics.

This is an explicit comparison between the source's promised numerator and its unpaid generator input, not a mathematical claim that every nonperturbative extension is prohibited.

### C. Alternative failure modes and hostile controls

The patent asserts spin-2 gravitons cannot couple to spin-half electrons. That is not the GR coupling law: gravitons couple to the conserved energy-momentum of fields, including Dirac matter. Extremely weak scattering/interaction of GWs with ordinary matter is different from a spin-based prohibition. "High frequency" also does not replace the energy/momentum budget.

Possible mundane signals at the claimed apparatus's frequencies include: RF leakage, intermodulation from active electronics, acoustic harmonics, motor phase noise, piezo/magnetostrictive transduction, magnetic pickup, thermomechanical drift, plasma ionisation/optical light and EM radiation. These must be separated from a genuine spin-2 TT far-field observable, independent of drive-only readings.

SOTA **detector** papers on resonant microwave cavities concern h -> electromagnetic response in the presence of a field (inverse conversion); they should not be silently read as demonstrated efficient electromagnetic -> GW **emission**. Physical reciprocity of an appropriately selected linear mixing amplitude is not energy gain.

### D. Steelman, constrained to one original testable object

A steelman candidate is: a driven charged elastic acoustic cavity with two separately characterised EM modes and a measured background B; an independently calibrated source satisfying Maxwell + matter + stress-energy conservation; a source-to-radiative-TT map with explicit distance and geometry; and a gravitational readout separated from mechanical/RF pickup. A plasma extension must include measured electron density, temperature, dispersion and current; a nonlinear vacuum extension must supply an explicit gauge/diffeomorphism-invariant effective action, renormalisation regime and a predicted deviation from the classical null.

Every step is a potential model/experiment that can fail. No amount of historical association, formal vocabulary, rapid transient drive or resonant power enhancement promotes a physical GW detection without the same-object stress, TT waveform and held-out instrument readout.
