## ARCHIVED historical September purpose and draft (do not send unchanged)

This is a short records request for the substantive 2026 Preliminary Documentation and public-comment response material for **EPBC 2019/8575 — Springfield Residential Development**.

The public EPBC portal currently exposes the statutory notices but does not expose the substantive project files through its document pane. The section 95B(2) publication notice states that **1,786 public comments** were received and that the Preliminary Documentation and a summary of comments were made available for public viewing from **23 July to 20 August 2026**.

Australia's federal environment administration changed during 2026. The National Environmental Protection Agency commenced on **1 July 2026** and now performs project-assessment and approval-condition functions under delegation. Government transition guidance says projects already referred when the new laws take effect continue to be assessed under the current EPBC Act. Current delegation instruments dated **24 August 2026** exist both for Departmental and National EPA recipients.

For this particular project, however, the strongest project-specific evidence presently held is the **2 September 2026 section 130(1A) extension notice**, which names **Declan O'Connor-Cox, Branch Head, Environment Assessments Queensland**, as the authorised decision-maker and gives a decision period ending **1 October 2026**. Agency restructuring should therefore not be treated as proof that a different person has taken over this specific decision.

## Notes for the legal team

This request is deliberately narrow. It asks for existing public-assessment records rather than asserting that the agency has failed to disclose material or that the proposal must be refused.

The known publication notice is not the substantive Preliminary Documentation. The known count of 1,786 comments does not by itself show whether or how those comments were answered.

The National EPA's establishment does not, by itself, establish that the exact 2019/8575 decision-maker changed. The 2 September 2026 project-specific extension notice is later and more specific evidence of the authorised decision-maker presently held in the file.

The State Library of Queensland catalogue separately records a four-volume 2026 legal-deposit set for the project, which may provide a fallback acquisition route if the agency cannot promptly supply the digital material.
