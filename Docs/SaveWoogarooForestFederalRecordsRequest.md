# Save Woogaroo Forest — request for the full EPBC 2019/8575 record

## Purpose

This is a short records request for the substantive 2026 Preliminary Documentation and public-comment response material for **EPBC 2019/8575 — Springfield Residential Development**.

The public EPBC portal currently exposes the statutory notices but does not expose the substantive project files through its document pane. The section 95B(2) publication notice states that **1,786 public comments** were received and that the Preliminary Documentation and a summary of comments were made available for public viewing from **23 July to 20 August 2026**.

## Suggested email

**Subject:** Urgent request — EPBC 2019/8575 substantive Preliminary Documentation and comments summary

Dear EPBC Referrals / National EPA,

I am seeking copies of the substantive assessment material for **EPBC 2019/8575 — Springfield Residential Development, Springfield, Queensland**.

The section 95B(2) publication notice states that the Preliminary Documentation and a summary of the 1,786 public comments received were made available for public viewing from 23 July to 20 August 2026. The public portal currently shows the project as “Final Preliminary Documentation Published”, but the project-document pane is not exposing the substantive files.

Could you please provide, preferably electronically:

1. the complete 2026 Preliminary Documentation report;
2. all appendices, maps and attachments forming part of that published Preliminary Documentation;
3. the summary of the 1,786 public comments referred to in the section 95B(2) notice;
4. any document setting out the proponent's response to those comments;
5. any revised impact, avoidance, mitigation or offset material published with or incorporated into the final Preliminary Documentation; and
6. if a final Part 9 approval/refusal decision has since been made, the signed decision instrument and any statement of reasons or conditions available for release.

For identification, the project is **EPBC 2019/8575, Springfield Residential Development**, proposed by **Cherish Enterprises Pty Ltd**.

If any of these records are available at a public URL, a direct link is sufficient. If some records are not held by your office, please identify the current custodian or public-access route if known.

This request is time-sensitive because a dated section 130(1A) notice records a decision period ending **1 October 2026**.

## Notes for the legal team

This request is deliberately narrow. It asks for existing public-assessment records rather than asserting that the agency has failed to disclose material or that the proposal must be refused.

The known publication notice is not the substantive Preliminary Documentation. The known count of 1,786 comments does not by itself show whether or how those comments were answered.

The State Library of Queensland catalogue separately records a four-volume 2026 legal-deposit set for the project, which may provide a fallback acquisition route if the agency cannot promptly supply the digital material.
