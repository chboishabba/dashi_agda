# Woogaroo Forest preservation legal cutset

## Objective

Find the shortest source-paid legal routes capable of keeping Woogaroo habitat physically in place, rather than merely recording ecological value.

The governing provenance ladder is:

```text
external source proposition
  != DASHI legal reconstruction
  != DASHI cross-source application/inference
  != adjudicated legal finding
  != realised protective outcome
```

A project being delayed is tracked separately from protection:

```text
delayed
  != refused
  != prohibited
  != permanently protected
```

Delay can nevertheless create an intervention window before works or a final approval.

## Current highest-alpha legal lanes

1. Queensland Nature Conservation Act 1992 s 13 critical-habitat classification evidence.
2. Nature Conservation Act s 102 interim conservation order for an imminent threatening process.
3. Nature Conservation Act s 49 compulsory nature-refuge route for permanent tenure-style protection.
4. Nature Conservation Act Part 7A formal critical-habitat / area-of-major-interest planning constraint.
5. EPBC Act ss 18/18A and Part 9 threatened-species / ecological-community assessment and refusal route.
6. EPBC Act s 475 injunction as an enforcement backstop where a contravention is threatened.
7. Nature Conservation Act s 173D enforcement order where an exact nominated offence can be identified.

## Key WrongType boundary

A proponent or federal assessment document using the words `critical habitat` does not automatically establish the Queensland Nature Conservation Act s 13 legal classification.

The same ecological facts may be relevant to both legal consumers, but the two tests require separate application receipts.

```text
EPBC ecological critical-habitat description
  != NCA s 13 critical-habitat finding
```

## Minimal preservation chain

```text
exact project / parcel identity
  + exact threatened species / ecological community
  + exact habitat-function evidence
        |
        +--> NCA s 13 application evidence
        |       |
        |       +--> s 102 interim order request
        |       +--> s 49 compulsory nature-refuge request
        |       +--> Part 7A formal identification route
        |

                |

                +--> injunction if conduct threatens contravention
```

The shortest next acquisition is therefore not another general conservation argument. It is an exact parcel/project-footprint matrix binding the existing ecology to the land that can actually be cleared.

## Custodianship / intersectionality boundary

Even a successful ecological preservation result does not exhaust Country or custodial authority:

```text
ecological protection
  != custodial authority

nature refuge
  != Country authority

development refusal
  != complete relational justice
```

Those coordinates remain separately source-paid through the existing custodianship / Mabo / Two-Eyed / intersectionality stack.
