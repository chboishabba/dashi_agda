## New theorem surface

`EarlyYearsStakeholderCoverageExact` makes claims declare the stakeholder axes they require. A professional-only pilot exactly resolves a bounded professional-practice claim but cannot resolve family-experience or child-experience claims while their active evidence axes are missing. A professional-surface collision with different family outcomes proves every observer sufficient for that family consumer must split the collision.

`CapabilityRecognitionExact` separates capability present, enactment reachable, contribution legible and contribution recognised. It proves non-recognition cannot determine absence of capability and constructs a strength-based transition opening a route without manufacturing authority.

`CommunityConnectednessTopologyExact` separates formal from effective connection and effective connection from authority. It gives an exact two-hop family→peer→service path and a peer-catalyst role that facilitates situated inquiry without epistemic or decision authority.

`EarlyYearsEmpowermentConnectednessBridgeExact` packages `CapabilityExpandingWithoutDomination`: pre-existing capability remains present while a reachable/recognisable route opens, decline remains available, effective connection is witnessed and no authority is manufactured. Causal and whole-policy claims remain counterfactual-, outcome-, horizon- and provenance-indexed.
