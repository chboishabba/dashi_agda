### 1. Stakeholder-indexed claim coverage
`EarlyYearsStakeholderCoverageExact` makes claims declare the stakeholder axes they require.
A professional-only pilot exactly resolves a bounded professional-practice claim. The same evidence stage does not resolve a family-experience claim because the family axis is active and missing. ### 2. Capability recognition
`CapabilityRecognitionExact` separates:
capability present
!= enactment reachable
!= contribution socially legible
!= contribution recognised.
Exact finite countermodels prove both:
capability alone cannot determine recognition
non-recognition cannot determine absence of capability.
The positive `StrengthBasedRecognitionTransition` witnesses a pre-existing capability whose route becomes reachable and recognised while actor decline remains available and no institutional authority is manufactured.
### 3. Effective connectedness
`CommunityConnectednessTopologyExact` defines effective connection through formal connection, reachability, reciprocity, trust and contestability. It proves:
formal connection cannot determine effective connection
effective connection cannot determine authority.
A second countermodel keeps high travel burden fixed while changing dwell opportunity. This retains the rural observation that one situated coordinate can be an access burden and also coexist with a relational affordance for another outcome/consumer, without promoting travel burden itself to a benefit.
The `PeerCatalyst` role supports connection, situated experience and independent inquiry while keeping epistemic and decision authority false.
### 4. Transition-level synthesis
`EarlyYearsEmpowermentConnectednessBridgeExact` introduces the exact structural certificate:
CapabilityExpandingWithoutDomination
Its preconditions retain actor representation, real choice, non-coercion, admissible actor knowledge, explicit authority boundary, provenance, actual reachability, non-compliance-based evaluation, measurement horizon and a counterfactual frame when causal claims are made.

This certificate is deliberately weaker than a universal empirical claim that a real family is `empowered`. Semantic promotion requires additional evidence/interpretation authority.
