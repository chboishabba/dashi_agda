# Reopenable consumer/intervention cross-domain kernel

This tranche turns the SeaMeInIt / Animalexic / LES comparison into reusable theorem-bearing DASHI machinery rather than another layer of domain receipts. It is stacked on the live LES branch and deliberately reuses existing theorem owners whenever the repository already has the mathematics: `AdaptiveFidelityPruningExact`, `AffectedDependencyClosureExact`, `IdentifiabilityActiveInformationExact`, `GenericFuturePartitionRefinementExact`, `FiniteRankedRefinementStabilizationExact`, `CertifiedFiniteFutureQuotientCompilerExact`, `ExplodedGraphGeometry`, and the existing 369 ultrametric/topological-address modules remain canonical owners of their respective results.
## Governed future behaviour
`DASHI.Core.ConsumerIndexedGovernedTransitionExact` defines a transition system whose observations, declared actions, and authority are consumer-indexed. Its principal theorem is:
```text
same coarse fibre
+ action commutation
+ observation descent
+ authority descent
=> finite future equivalence for every requested depth
```
This is deliberately stronger than current-score equality and weaker than world identity. `AuthorityPreservingFutureAbstractionExact` adds a separate downstream decision projection and proves the stronger same-fibre package:
=> same bounded future observations
 + same consumer decision
 + same consumer authority
The decision coordinate is explicit because observation equality and authority equality do not, by themselves, license prose-level assumptions about every downstream decision.
Calibration references:
- Patrick Cousot and Radhia Cousot, **Abstract interpretation: a unified lattice model for static analysis of programs by construction or approximation of fixpoints**, POPL 1977, DOI `10.1145/512950.512973`.
- David Blackwell, **Equivalent Comparisons of Experiments**, *Annals of Mathematical Statistics* 24(2), 1953, DOI `10.1214/aoms/1177729032`.
## Counterexample-guided refinement and bounded exhaustion
`DASHI.Core.CounterexampleGuidedConsumerRefinementExact` gives the refinement rule: a typed consumer-descent defect plus a refined observer which splits that witness. A genuine split cannot itself factor through the old coarse observer. One split does not establish global separation or future safety.
`DASHI.Core.BoundedLanguageBehaviouralCompletenessExact` closes the language-scope issue. It binds a finite declared action list to a proof that every action admitted for that consumer is represented, and combines that language with consumer-specific behavioural stabilization. The resulting `LanguageRelativeBehaviouralExhaustion` is explicitly scoped by action language, depth, state-domain reference, backend, policy and dedup reference. `BeamTermination` is a separate type: no retained child is not a theorem that the behavioural partition stabilized, and finite stabilization is not global completeness of a continuous state space.
The existing `GenericFuturePartitionRefinementExact`, `FiniteRankedRefinementStabilizationExact`, and `CertifiedFiniteFutureQuotientCompilerExact` remain the owners of generic depth refinement/ranked stabilization/canonical future quotient mathematics; this tranche adds the consumer/language/runtime claim surface rather than duplicating those proofs.

## Selective authority and the lawful authority ladder
`DASHI.Core.SelectiveAuthorityCertificateExact` separates numerical certification from promotion and gives the exact three-way policy `promote | abstain | reject`. Abstention preserves an independently valid anchor; rejection produces no candidate; numerical convergence cannot construct authority without a separate promotion witness.
`DASHI.Core.AuthorityPromotionHierarchyExact` strengthens the existing authority vocabulary into a lawful hierarchy:
computed -> validated -> supported -> authorised -> actionable
Only adjacent promotions are primitive. Multi-stage promotion is the reflexive-transitive closure of those edges and carries edge-scoped policy/evidence/authority witnesses. Thus a generic record that merely names `computed` and `actionable` is not enough to skip the intermediate obligations.

## Adaptive fidelity and safe escalation
Upstream `AdaptiveFidelityPruningExact` already owns certified lower-bound pruning. `AdaptiveFidelityConsumerMarginExact` adds the complementary decision-preservation theorem: if the certified low/high discrepancy lies inside a declared consumer decision-invariance region, low- and high-fidelity decisions are exactly equal.
`ConsumerSafeFidelityEscalationExact` turns the complementary case into a proof-relevant execution rule. At an input either there is an inside-margin witness and the cheap decision is safe, or there is a proof that no such witness is currently available and high fidelity must be run. Escalation is an information obligation, not evidence that the low-fidelity result is false.
- Marc C. Kennedy and Anthony O'Hagan, **Predicting the output from a complex computer code when fast approximations are available**, *Biometrika* 87(1), 2000, DOI `10.1093/biomet/87.1.1`.
- Natalia M. Alexandrov, J. E. Dennis Jr., Robert M. Lewis, Virginia Torczon, **A trust-region framework for managing the use of approximation models in optimization**, *Structural Optimization* 15, 1998, DOI `10.1007/BF01197433`.
## Evidence polarity, independence and typed reopening
`TypedEvidenceDependencyExact` reuses upstream `AffectedDependencyClosureExact`; it adds provenance-root independence and typed reopen reasons (`budgetDeferred`, `ambiguityUnresolved`, `dependencyChanged`, `fidelityEscalation`, `policyChanged`). Multiple metrics from one evidence root are not automatically multiple independent confirmations.
`EvidenceReliabilityPolarityExact` gives the existing four-way evidence vocabulary an exact two-coordinate semantics:
no support + no opposition = ignorance
support + no opposition    = support
no support + opposition    = opposition
support + opposition       = conflict
Its reliability law is intentionally asymmetric: an untrusted item discounts to ignorance. Low reliability cannot manufacture opposition (or support) merely by changing a confidence flag.

## Consumer observer joins and minimal hot state
`ConsumerObserverJoinResidualExact` supplies the deterministic observer-join and hot/cold representation theorem surface. A product observer refines each component. `RecoverableHotCold` proves exact reopening from hot state plus cold residual. Consumer sufficiency is ordinary `ConsumerDescent` through the hot projection.
The stronger `MinimalConsumerSufficientHotState` does not infer minimality from byte count, dimension or compression ratio. Minimality is an explicit universal property: every other projection through which the same consumer descends must factor through the proposed hot state. `ReopenableMinimalConsumerState` combines this coarsest sufficient hot view with an exact cold residual.
This is the intended runtime architecture:
consumer-sufficient hot state + exact cold residual -> reopen full state
Changing the consumer may therefore require reopening rather than pretending one quotient is canonical for all roles.
Reference: David Blackwell, **Equivalent Comparisons of Experiments**, DOI `10.1214/aoms/1177729032` (calibration only; the deterministic universal-property construction is proved locally).

# Reopenable consumer/intervention kernel — round 4
This tranche turns the SeaMeInIt / Animalexic / LES cross-pollination into theorem-bearing reusable DASHI modules rather than another set of domain receipts.
## Core theorem spine
The shared state-transition object is consumer indexed:
fine state
  -- declared action --> fine state
      |                    |
      v                    v
 consumer observation   consumer observation
      |
      +-- authority is a separate coordinate
`DASHI.Core.ConsumerIndexedGovernedTransitionExact` defines finite-depth action/observation equivalence and proves that an abstraction which preserves action transitions, consumer observations, and consumer authority makes every same-fibre pair future-equivalent at every requested finite depth.
This is intentionally stronger than current-score equality and weaker than world identity. It is relative to the declared consumer and action language.
## Selective authority
`DASHI.Core.SelectiveAuthorityCertificateExact` separates numerical certification from promotion. A candidate can be `promote`, `abstain`, or `reject`; abstention falls back to an independently valid anchor, while rejection yields no canonical candidate. A numerical certificate cannot construct an authorised candidate without the separate authority witness.
- Richard H. Byrd, Peihuang Lu, Jorge Nocedal, Ciyou Zhu, **Algorithm 778: L-BFGS-B: Fortran Subroutines for Large-Scale Bound-Constrained Optimization**, *ACM Transactions on Mathematical Software* 23(4), 1997, DOI `10.1145/279232.279236`.
## Decision-relative multi-fidelity escalation
`DASHI.Core.AdaptiveFidelityConsumerMarginExact` does not demand globally tiny surrogate error. A domain supplies a discrepancy bound and a consumer-specific region on which the decision is invariant. If the discrepancy bound lies inside that region, low- and high-fidelity decisions are theorem-equal; otherwise the system has an escalation obligation rather than evidence that the cheap result is false.
## Counterexample-guided refinement
`DASHI.Core.CounterexampleGuidedConsumerRefinementExact` packages one consumer-descent defect and a refined observer which splits that concrete collision. It proves that such a genuine split cannot itself factor through the old coarse observer. The boundary is explicit: one repaired collision does not prove global separation or future safety; repeated refinement still needs a stopping theorem. Existing finite-ranked stabilization and certified future-quotient modules remain the owners of those global finite claims.
## Evidence and dependency reopening
`DASHI.Core.TypedEvidenceDependencyExact` separates provenance-root independence from statistical independence. Multiple downstream metrics from one evidence root cannot be certified as multiple provenance-independent confirmations. It also gives exact transitive dependency paths and typed reopen reasons (`budgetDeferred`, `ambiguityUnresolved`, `dependencyChanged`, `fidelityEscalation`, `policyChanged`).
This is the shared kernel behind:
- Animalexic: same-frame/multimodal evidence and hypothesis reopening;
- SeaMeInIt: body/ROM/panel/manufacturing invalidation;
- LES: assimilation followed by selective replanning.
## World actions and information actions
`DASHI.Core.DualEffectInformationActionExact` places both in one action language. An action can change the world coordinate, the information coordinate, or both. A pure information action is therefore a property of an action rather than a separate planner universe. `InformationSeparatingAction` captures the exact bounded form of active sensing/experimentation: two currently collapsed information states become distinguishable after the declared action.
`DASHI.Core.CompositionalComplianceExact` treats a stage receipt as a proof of a predicate over the actual input and output. Sequential composition retains the intermediate carrier in a dependent pair, preventing unrelated endpoint proofs from being spliced merely because their types match.
No cryptographic proof-system claim is made; the reuse is the compliance-predicate composition shape.
