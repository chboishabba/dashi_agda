# Reopenable consumer/intervention cross-domain kernel

This tranche turns the SeaMeInIt / Animalexic / LES comparison into reusable theorem-bearing DASHI machinery rather than another layer of domain receipts. It is stacked on the live LES branch: upstream `LESResearchCrossPollinationRound4Exact` remains the owner of the broad LES gap closures, while `LESResearchCrossPollinationRound5Exact` adds the new cross-project theorem welds.

## New theorem spine

`DASHI.Core.ConsumerIndexedGovernedTransitionExact` defines a transition system whose observations, declared actions, and authority are consumer-indexed. Its principal theorem is:

```text
same coarse fibre
+ action commutation
+ observation descent
+ authority descent
=> finite future equivalence for every requested depth
```

This is deliberately stronger than current-score equality and weaker than world identity.

`DASHI.Core.CounterexampleGuidedConsumerRefinementExact` gives the dual refinement rule: a typed consumer-descent defect plus a refined observer which splits that witness. A genuine split cannot itself factor through the old coarse observer. One split does not establish global separation or future safety; the existing finite-ranked stabilization/future-quotient modules retain ownership of bounded stopping theorems.

## Selective authority

`DASHI.Core.SelectiveAuthorityCertificateExact` separates numerical certification from promotion and gives the exact three-way policy `promote | abstain | reject`. Abstention preserves an independently valid anchor; rejection produces no candidate; numerical convergence cannot construct authority without a separate promotion witness.

References:

- J. Hendrickx et al., **Machine learning with a reject option: a survey**, *Machine Learning* (2024), DOI `10.1007/s10994-024-06534-x`.
- Richard H. Byrd, Peihuang Lu, Jorge Nocedal, Ciyou Zhu, **Algorithm 778: L-BFGS-B: Fortran Subroutines for Large-Scale Bound-Constrained Optimization**, *ACM TOMS* 23(4), 1997, DOI `10.1145/279232.279236`.

## Adaptive fidelity

Upstream `AdaptiveFidelityPruningExact` already owns certified lower-bound pruning. `AdaptiveFidelityConsumerMarginExact` adds the complementary decision-preservation theorem: if the certified low/high discrepancy lies inside a declared consumer decision-invariance region, low- and high-fidelity decisions are exactly equal. A missing margin proof means escalation remains open; it is not a refutation of the cheap model.

References:

- Marc C. Kennedy and Anthony O'Hagan, **Predicting the output from a complex computer code when fast approximations are available**, *Biometrika* 87(1), 2000, DOI `10.1093/biomet/87.1.1`.
- Natalia M. Alexandrov, J. E. Dennis Jr., Robert M. Lewis, Virginia Torczon, **A trust-region framework for managing the use of approximation models in optimization**, *Structural Optimization* 15, 1998, DOI `10.1007/BF01197433`.

## Evidence, reopening, and active information

`TypedEvidenceDependencyExact` reuses upstream `AffectedDependencyClosureExact`; it adds provenance-root independence and typed reopen reasons (`budgetDeferred`, `ambiguityUnresolved`, `dependencyChanged`, `fidelityEscalation`, `policyChanged`). Multiple metrics from one evidence root are not automatically multiple independent confirmations.

`DualEffectInformationActionExact` places world effects and information effects in one action language. `ActiveInformationDualEffectBridgeExact` then proves that an information-separating action is an instance of upstream `IdentifiabilityActiveInformationExact.DistinguishingExperiment`. This gives a theorem-level bridge from active sensing/interaction to the existing value-of-information/equifinality lane.

## Proof-carrying compliance

`CompositionalComplianceExact` treats a stage as a proof of a predicate over the literal input and output. Composition retains the intermediate carrier as a dependent witness, preventing proofs about unrelated representatives from being spliced by endpoint type alone.

References:

- George C. Necula, **Compiling with Proofs**, PhD thesis, Carnegie Mellon University, Technical Report CMU-CS-98-154 (1998), no DOI recorded here.
- Nir Bitansky, Ran Canetti, Alessandro Chiesa, Eran Tromer, **Recursive composition and bootstrapping for SNARKS and proof-carrying data**, STOC 2013, DOI `10.1145/2488608.2488623`.

No cryptographic soundness claim is made; the reused idea is predicate-carrying composition.

## Adaptive wearable instance

`DASHI.Geometry.AdaptiveWearableCompilerExact` is the SeaMeInIt-facing formal adapter. It supplies finite empirical ROM fields with exact max/total projections, an abstract-scalar discrete angle-defect/regional-curvature measure, curvature-accommodation modes, a panel action grammar, distinct seam/manufacturing/thermal/support consumers, and an adapter to the governed-transition core.

The ROM carrier intentionally starts with finite sampled poses. No probability distribution or CVaR semantics are invented until pose weights have a justified interpretation.

Geometry/fabrication references:

- Oded Stein, Eitan Grinspun, Keenan Crane, **Developability of Triangle Meshes**, *ACM TOG* 37(4), 2018, DOI `10.1145/3197517.3201303`.

The theorem boundary is explicit: a seam/dart/gusset redistributes planar-realisation burden; this module does not claim that a cut physically destroys Gaussian curvature or that angle defect alone proves manufacturability.
