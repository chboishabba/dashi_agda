## P0: mirror referent, reflection, observation

There are **four different referents** that the word *mirror* could select:

1. **Acoustic boundary reflection:** an ordinary, calibratable material/geometry/medium response; a separate physical transfer model must pay this claim.
2. **Echo/reverberation:** a measured or processed signal relation, not automatic evidence of any human affect.
3. **Figurative mirror:** a cultural, political, or perceptual interpretation. Goodman explicitly describes echo/delay/reverb as simulations of sonic reflections and virtual spaces in **chapter 29, printed pp. 159–160**; that is a source-owned account of dub aesthetics, not apparatus-specific experimental evidence.
4. **Kozyrev Mirror:** a disputed apparatus/phenomenon and its proposed non-standard mechanism, explicitly isolated in [open PR #1404](https://github.com/chboishabba/dashi_agda/pull/1404); its preprints, patent translation dispute, torsion typing and proposed sham protocol belong to that PR. It is **not** a sonic-reflection theorem; no branch-only module is imported.

The exact finite DASHI result in `DASHI/Culture/GoodmanSonicWarfareExact.agda` is: equal projection to the surface label `sameMirrorWord` admits distinct answers for the `mechanismQuery` consumer, hence no universal factorisation through that **label**. This is not a no-go theorem for all possible apparatus-specific relationships. Optical mirroring, time reversal, algebraic involution, auditory echo, psychoacoustic illusions, and geometric torsion are not interchangeable. Existing reflection-positivity, signed-kernel, and optical modules are *not* acoustic mirror owners.

**P0 payoff:** attach apparatus-specific receipts only when an experiment identifies a source, geometry, medium, microphone position, waveform, calibration, baseline/null and uncertainty; never infer a mechanism from a label or a shared signature. Do not optimize hostile sound delivery.

## Explicit exact result and its scope

For `Encounter`, the finite example has two states with the same selected acoustic projection but different answers to a response query. Reusing `Query.queryAdequacyDefectBlocksFactorisation` establishes:

```text
Identical selected acoustic features do not determine
every response in the explicitly constructed finite model.
```

The theorem says nothing about the prevalence of those response differences in real people. It cannot prove that all songs produce fear, all societies are acoustically controlled, or any mechanism is a novel force. Adding a microphone, biosignal or source citation without a causal model does not repair an omitted coordinate.

## Pareto / snowball / Ibrahim and attribution-safe max-cut

The shortest productive cut is:

1. **P0 — same-referent guard:** mirror referents and physical evidence. Use existing non-factorability owner. Zero imports of unmerged Kozyrev branch-only files.
2. **P1 — exact finite consumer:** sound-feature-to-affect insufficiency, with adequate feature-description control query.
3. **P2 — source snowball:** follow primary citations *backward* from Goodman to original rhythm theorists, cultural and musical authors, archived cases, acoustics/psychoacoustics. Distinguish source discovery from corroboration and independent execution.
4. **P3 — live empirical discriminators:** construct preregistered field/room/receiver model and calibration receipts before any nontrivial predictive claim.
5. **P4 — TOE integration:** import this one canonical Goodman owner into `DASHI/EverythingTOESymmetryResolved369.agda`, `DASHI/Unifier.agda`, `DASHI/Unified/Everything.agda`; keep theorem ownership in original modules.
