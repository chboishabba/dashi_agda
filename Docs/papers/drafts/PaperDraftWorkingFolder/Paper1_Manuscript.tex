\begin{abstract}
We organize four parallel routes to the three-dimensional Navier--Stokes Millennium problem in the exact A/B/C/D form of the official Clay statement. This draft is deliberately conservative: it separates completed mathematics, source-backed arguments still undergoing independent referee reconstruction, and genuinely open estimates. Alternatives A and B remain proof-production routes. A has been reduced to two physical same-object identifications. B has been reduced to five literal claims: two exact welds and three signed harmonic-analysis estimates. Alternatives C and D already have direct source theorem routes into an independently specified Clay C and D; for Goal 1 their remaining work is independent mathematical audit of the candidate constructions, uniqueness arguments, decay hypotheses, viscosity scaling, pressure periodicity, and terminal obstructions, followed by a referee-readable proof. Machine-checked artifacts are supplementary evidence, not a replacement for the human proof.
\end{abstract}

\paragraph{Claim boundary.}
This is a working submission manuscript and proof audit, not a claim of Clay acceptance, publication, or community validation. A route is called closed only when its displayed mathematical implication has been proved, or when a source theorem has been traced to the exact official clause and its source-specific proof has survived the stated independent audit.

\section{The official four-alternative problem}
We consider
\begin{align}
\partial_tu+(u\cdot\nabla)u&=\nuu\Delta u-\nabla p+f,\\
\nabla\cdot u&=0,\\
u(\cdot,0)&=u_0,
\end{align}
with $\nuu>0$. We use the exact quantifier order and data classes of Fefferman's official problem statement, including the erratum requiring pressure periodicity in the periodic alternative \cite{fefferman}.

\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.2cm,style=nextline]
\item[A] Global smooth bounded-energy solutions on $\R^3$ for every smooth rapidly decaying divergence-free initial datum and zero forcing.
\item[B] Global smooth periodic solutions for every smooth divergence-free periodic initial datum and zero forcing.
\item[C] For every $\nuu>0$, admissible rapidly decaying data and forcing for which no global smooth bounded-energy solution on $\R^3$ exists.
\item[D] For every $\nuu>0$, admissible periodic data and rapidly time-decaying periodic forcing for which no global smooth periodic velocity/pressure solution exists.
\end{description}

\section{Alternative D: periodic forced breakdown}
\label{sec:D}
Fix $\nuu>0$. The pinned source chain is
\begin{quote}\small\ttfamily
theorem\_1\_1\_with\_initial\_rest\\
$\downarrow$\\
compressedCandidate\\
$\downarrow$\\
PeriodicPaper.of\_compact\_candidate\\
$\downarrow$\\
PeriodicPaper.periodic\_corollary
\end{quote}
The intended mathematical content is that a compact whole-space pre-singular candidate is compressed into the interior of a fundamental cell and periodised, together with its pressure and forcing, without changing the local Navier--Stokes equation on the active support.

\begin{proposition}[D candidate package --- source-backed, audit pending]
\label{prop:D-candidate}
For every $\nuu>0$ there exist smooth periodic data $u_0$ and smooth periodic forcing $f$ such that the source construction produces a classical periodic solution $(u^\star,p^\star)$ on $0\le t<1$, with the same viscosity, initial datum and forcing, and whose speed is unbounded as $t\uparrow1$.
\end{proposition}

The decisive source-specific subclaims are:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(D\arabic*)]
\item \textbf{Exact periodisation.} \path{PeriodicLocalization.navier_stokes_periodize} must rule out cross-cell nonlinear contamination and preserve the exact forced PDE.
\item \textbf{All-derivative force decay.} \path{CandidateConsequences.futureJet_decay} must control every spatial and time derivative required by Fefferman, uniformly in space.
\item \textbf{Pressure periodicity.} The candidate and hypothetical global solution must satisfy the pressure-periodicity erratum.
\item \textbf{Terminal obstruction.} The actual selected periodised candidate, not a surrogate, must satisfy \path{SpeedUnboundedAtOne}.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{proposition}[Periodic pre-singular uniqueness --- source-backed, audit pending]
\label{prop:D-uniq}
If $(u,p)$ is a global smooth periodic solution with the same $\nuu,u_0,f$ as the candidate, then for every $T<1$,
\[
u(x,t)=u^\star(x,t),\qquad 0\le t\le T.
\]
\end{proposition}
The source endpoint is \path{PeriodicUniqueness.classical_uniqueness_on_Icc}. The referee check is that its assumptions are supplied by a hypothetical Clay solution and that it does not assume an equivalent global regularity result.

\begin{theorem}[Alternative D, conditional only on the source audit]
\label{thm:D}
If Propositions~\ref{prop:D-candidate} and \ref{prop:D-uniq}, including (D1)--(D4), are independently verified for the pinned source construction, then Fefferman alternative D holds.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Assume a global smooth periodic solution exists for the same data. By Proposition~\ref{prop:D-uniq}, its velocity agrees with the candidate on every compact interval $[0,T]$, $T<1$. Smooth periodicity of the global solution implies bounded velocity on a compact space-time slab containing $t=1$. Agreement on all pre-one slabs transfers this bound to the candidate as $t\uparrow1$, contradicting \path{SpeedUnboundedAtOne}. Hence no global smooth periodic solution exists.
\end{proof}

\begin{obligation}[D referee stop condition]
Directly inspect \path{navier_stokes_periodize}, \path{futureJet_decay}, \path{classical_uniqueness_on_Icc}, the selected candidate's \path{SpeedUnboundedAtOne} proof, and the arbitrary-$\nuu$ construction. If these checks pass, no exhaustive bespoke Agda reconstruction is required for the Clay-facing argument.
\end{obligation}

\section{Alternative C: whole-space forced breakdown}
\label{sec:C}
Fix $\nuu>0$. The pinned route is
\begin{quote}\small\ttfamily
ActualCandidate.selected\_candidate\_one\_with\_initial\_rest\\
$\downarrow$\quad viscosity scaling\\
NavierStokesR3.theorem\_1\_1\\
$\downarrow$\\
comparator\_of\_breakdown
\end{quote}

\begin{proposition}[C candidate package --- source-backed, audit pending]
\label{prop:C-candidate}
For every $\nuu>0$, the source construction supplies smooth whole-space data and forcing, with the exact decay required by Fefferman C, and a classical candidate on $0\le t<1$ possessing the terminal obstruction used by \texttt{NavierStokesR3.theorem\_1\_1}.
\end{proposition}

The source-specific audit is concentrated in:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(C\arabic*)]
\item the same selected candidate is retained under viscosity scaling and comparison;
\item compact support/smoothness implies the exact all-jet rapid decay required by the official statement;
\item the official bounded-energy competitor class supplies every hypothesis of the whole-space uniqueness theorem, with no stronger substitute inserted silently;
\item candidate production, uniqueness and terminal obstruction contain no circular dependence on the desired breakdown theorem.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{proposition}[Whole-space pre-singular uniqueness --- source-backed, audit pending]
\label{prop:C-uniq}
Every hypothetical global smooth bounded-energy solution with the same data agrees with the selected candidate on each $[0,T]$, $T<1$.
\end{proposition}
The source endpoint is \path{NavierStokesR3.WholeSpaceUniqueness.classical_uniqueness_on_Icc}; its exact finite-energy and local regularity hypotheses must be checked independently.

\begin{theorem}[Alternative C, conditional only on the source audit]
\label{thm:C}
If Propositions~\ref{prop:C-candidate} and \ref{prop:C-uniq}, including (C1)--(C4), are independently verified, then Fefferman alternative C holds.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A hypothetical global smooth bounded-energy solution agrees with the selected candidate on every compact pre-singular interval. The global solution is smooth through the terminal time, while the candidate has the source-specific terminal obstruction. This contradiction excludes a global smooth bounded-energy solution for the admissible data.
\end{proof}

\begin{obligation}[C referee stop condition]
Independently audit the selected candidate, viscosity scaling, all-jet decay, exact bounded-energy implication, whole-space uniqueness, terminal obstruction and non-circular provenance. Rebuilding generic integration-by-parts and Gronwall theory in the custom Agda carrier is not a Goal-1 prerequisite.
\end{obligation}

\section{Alternative A: unforced whole-space continuation}
\label{sec:A}
A remains an original proof-production route. The continuum and generic integrability infrastructure is no longer the active mathematical frontier. The current reduction packages the remaining work into exactly two physical seams.

\begin{obligation}[A1: physical kernel identification]
\label{obl:A1}
On one actual Euclidean Fourier interaction, identify the output frequency, pair and output resolvents, centered residual, signed projected Gram scalar, saturation coefficient, and scale-relative curvature quantity. Prove that the abstract kernel consumed by the existing near-origin saturation theorem and high-frequency curvature theorem is literally the kernel generated by the physical Navier--Stokes trajectory.
\end{obligation}

\begin{obligation}[A2: physical state majorant identification]
\label{obl:A2}
Prove that the actual low- and high-frequency physical state majorants are pointwise dominated by the finite-energy convolution envelopes already known to be integrable. In particular, instantiate the concrete high-region inverse-sixth multiplier on the same physical interaction and weld the physical weight to the existing $L^2\times L^2\to L^1$ convolution quantity controlled by kinetic energy.
\end{obligation}

\begin{proposition}[A two-seam compiler --- already constructed]
Once A1 and A2 are supplied, the existing low-frequency saturation theorem, high-frequency curvature theorem, and weighted convolution integrability results imply integrability of the actual physical majorant required by the whole-space endgame.
\end{proposition}
The formal owner \path{NSClayFacingATwoPhysicalSeamCompilerExact.agda} is a compiler, not a proof of A1 or A2. Thus the active A frontier is exactly
\[
\boxed{\text{A1 + A2}}.
\]

\section{Alternative B: periodic signed-covariance continuation}
\label{sec:B}
B remains the most developed original periodic route. The argument preserves signed cancellation through a fixed-output covariance, partitions the literal physical interaction fibre into deep far-low (DFL), deep high-high (DHH), and critical-core regions, and retains the exact six signed pair blocks instead of taking absolute values prematurely:
\[
\mathcal C_k=
\mathcal C_{\mathrm{DFL,DFL}}+
\mathcal C_{\mathrm{DFL,DHH}}+
\mathcal C_{\mathrm{DFL,Core}}+
\mathcal C_{\mathrm{DHH,DHH}}+
\mathcal C_{\mathrm{DHH,Core}}+
\mathcal C_{\mathrm{Core,Core}}.
\]
This decomposition is already proved on the actual filtered R236 lists without inserting norms or fibre-cardinality estimates.

\begin{obligation}[B1: physical DFL to literal infinity-shell support]
\label{obl:B1}
For every item of the R236-filtered DFL block, extract the actual shell index, duplicate-free \texttt{InfinityShellSupport}, coefficient, high energy, derivative coefficient and exact signed shell mass required by the existing literal shell Bernstein theorem. The shell-cardinality, finite Bernstein, $E\!\cdot\!D$ payment and finite shell fold are already proved.
\end{obligation}

\begin{obligation}[B2: DFL--DHH signed shell-pair estimate]
\label{obl:B2}
Prove a cardinality-free signed bound for each literal DFL--DHH shell pair, combining the far-low Bernstein gain with the DHH low-output/null gain before shell summation, while preserving the physical multiplier difference.
\end{obligation}

\begin{obligation}[B3: DHH intra-shell signed $\ell^2$ aggregation]
\label{obl:B3}
Prove the literal DHH intra-shell signed $\ell^2$ estimate needed to sum the high--high block without a fibre-cardinality tax. Existing cutoff-uniform gap summation and all-four-helicity low-output bounds may be used.
\end{obligation}

\begin{obligation}[B4: strict critical signed operator]
\label{obl:B4}
Construct a signed block-operator certificate with a uniform $0\le\theta<1$ such that
\[
\mathcal C_{\mathrm{touch},k}
\le \theta Q_{\mathrm{core},k}+C_{\mathrm{core}}ED_k.
\]
The substantive content is the displayed operator bound and the strict margin $\theta<1$; generic pair combinatorics are no longer part of the frontier.
\end{obligation}

\begin{obligation}[B7: literal R406 same-object identity]
\label{obl:B7}
Prove that the literal weighted R406 remainder is exactly
\[
R406=4\sum_k\mathcal C_k
\]
for the same fixed-output covariance objects paid by B1--B4. Output-fibre concatenation and the finite R432 summation compiler are already available.
\end{obligation}

\begin{proposition}[Periodic terminal reduction --- compiler complete]
If B1--B4 and B7 are supplied with uniform output-independent constants, the existing physical critical-region assembly, output-family summation and terminal compilers reduce B to the established continuation consumer without introducing an output-count factor.
\end{proposition}
Thus B's genuine remaining analytic risk is B2, B3 and especially B4; B1 and B7 are exact physical welds.

\section{Completion criterion and formal provenance}
A is Goal-1 complete when A1/A2 are proved on the actual Euclidean interaction and the endgame is written as a conventional proof of Fefferman A. B is complete when B1/B2/B3/B4/B7 are proved on the literal carrier and the continuation proof is written without opaque compiler names. C and D are complete when every source-specific claim above has been independently inspected and rewritten as a human proof, with all standard external theorems cited and their hypotheses checked.

The submission-facing Lean project contains an independent Clay C/D specification and direct source routes. The pinned upstream project is \texttt{openai/NavierStokesAndEuler} at commit \texttt{f9e8bc5b38b6e212696e8a30e3e91517af887bbd}. Its recorded formalization metadata reports zero \texttt{sorry} for the comparator C/D declarations and only standard Lean axioms. This is proof-term provenance, not independent referee verification. A/B remain in Agda because the exact physical carriers and signed-block decompositions are already represented there. Compiler closure is never counted as closure of the open mathematical obligations.

\section{Research and writing order}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{D audit:} selected periodised candidate's \path{SpeedUnboundedAtOne}, exact periodisation, all-jet force decay, and periodic uniqueness.
\item \textbf{C audit:} selected R$^3$ candidate, viscosity scaling, bounded-energy implication, whole-space uniqueness, terminal obstruction, and circularity.
\item \textbf{B production:} close B1 while attacking B2/B3 and the higher-risk B4; finish with B7.
\item \textbf{A production:} prove A1 and A2 directly on the physical Euclidean interaction.
\end{enumerate}
After each item, the corresponding \emph{Open obligation} should be replaced by the proved lemma and proof. The manuscript itself is therefore the live Goal-1 gap detector.

\section{Conclusion}
The programme is no longer blocked by generic proof-assistant infrastructure. A has two physical same-object seams. B has three substantive signed estimates and two exact welds. C and D have direct source theorem routes to an independently specified Clay statement and now require hostile-referee reconstruction rather than further wrapper construction. The submission discipline is: prove what is genuinely new, audit what is source-backed, and never confuse formal plumbing with mathematical completion.
