# Indigenous / environmental authority round-two source corrections

This tranche records a claim-level evidence split rather than source-level blanket promotion.

## Promoted bounded surfaces

- Leah Thomas (2022): authorial primary source for Thomas's own intersectional-environmentalism framework and activist programme; not empirical causal authority and not a replacement for Crenshaw's genealogy.
- Goldman Environmental Prize (2015): independent institutional recognition of Berta Caceres and the Agua Zarca campaign; not a judicial finding.
- Global Witness (2025): systematic defender-risk monitoring surface; not evidence that every case has one legal mechanism.
- Escazu Agreement Article 9: formal defender-protection obligations; treaty text is distinct from effective implementation.
- UNESCO Budj Bim World Heritage property 1577: independent Gunditjmara hydrological-engineering / kooyang-aquaculture / continuity evidence; no dependency on Pascoe's literary framing.
- Mithaka collaborative Antiquity research: large-scale quarrying, grindstone production, substantial dwellings and intensive regional economic practices. Village settlement remains a live archaeological question in the paper itself.
- Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework Target 3: conservation coverage explicitly recognizes Indigenous/traditional territories and rights; coverage does not transfer territorial authority and rights language does not prove implementation.

## Explicitly separated contested surfaces

- Shiva/Navdanya seed-sovereignty nonseparability remains structurally promoted.
- The national/direct Bt-cotton -> farmer-suicide causal claim is separately marked empirically contested, using Gruere & Sengupta 2011 DOI 10.1080/00220388.2010.492863 and Nature's 2013 evidence synthesis DOI 10.1038/497024a.
- Dark Emu is adjudicated claim-by-claim: independently evidenced local material practices remain supported through their own sources; source handling, classification labels and broad geographic generalisation remain contestable. No stable agreement percentage is asserted.

The governing rule is:

```text
source adequate for claim c1 != source adequate for claim c2
independent evidence for phenomenon != validation of every framing of phenomenon
legal/policy recognition != effective implementation != originating authority
```
