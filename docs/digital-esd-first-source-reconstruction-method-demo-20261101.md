# First source-specific Digital-ESD reconstruction: exact evidence boundary (1 November 2026)

This note is a **worked source-bound methodological illustration**, not a retrospective empirical ESD result or completed source admission. It uses the canonical *Getenet–Burke–Fanshawe–Brown* online-support paper currently registered in `DASHI/Biology/EducationCorpusSourceRegistry.agda`. Do **not** relabel it as an “online instructor engagement strategies” paper, which has not been source-identified here.

## Original, separately attributed source

- Getenet S, Burke K, Fanshawe M, Brown A. *Supporting online learning in teacher education: student-identified strategies and social constructivist implications*. `Distance Education` (2026), DOI `10.1080/01587919.2026.2669288`.
- Canonical registry paper key `online-teacher-education-support-strategies-2026`.
- The registry reports **584 pre-service-teacher responses**, from a regional Australian teacher-education context, and five student-valued candidate strategy families: autonomy/flexibility, accessible centralised resources, meaningful interaction, self-organisation, guided scaffolding.
- Original-author claims stop at source-context student-identified supports. They do **not** establish universally effective interventions, causal change, agency transfer, ecological impact or longitudinal sustainability.
