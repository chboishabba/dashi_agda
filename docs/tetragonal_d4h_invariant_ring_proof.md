# Constructive invariant-ring proof for tetragonal 4/mmm

## Established theorem and attribution

The polynomial invariant structure is classical invariant theory of
finite reflection groups (dihedral group I2(4) acting in xy, and A1
in z). The following is an elementary constructive proof, not a novel
classification and not a formal Agda kernel certificate.

Work over any commutative field K of characteristic other than 2
(and, for the simple symmetric-polynomial argument, arbitrary other
characteristic is fine). Write R=K[x,y,z]. Let G be generated by
R:(x,y,z)↦(-y,x,z), S:(x,y,z)↦(x,-y,z), and
H:(x,y,z)↦(x,y,-z).

1. H-invariance forces only even powers of z, so f lies in
   K[x,y,z²].
2. S-invariance forces only even powers of y. Combining with
   R²:(x,y,z)↦(-x,-y,z), S-invariance implies invariance under
   x↦-x. Thus f lies in K[x²,y²,z²].
3. R acts as (x²,y²)↦(y²,x²); invariance therefore means
   symmetric polynomials in X=x² and Y=y².
4. The fundamental theorem on symmetric polynomials in two
   variables gives K[X,Y]^{S2}=K[X+Y,XY].
5. Hence

   K[x,y,z]^G = K[x²+y², x²y², z²].

The generators are algebraically independent: the map
(X,Y,Z)↦(X+Y,XY,Z) is dominant over an infinite field, or
use the leading-monomial proof for the symmetric-polynomial basis.
For K=Q or R this is unproblematic.

No coefficient fitting, diffraction evidence, or specimen observation
is used. The proof is about an abstract group action.

## Representation-theoretic consequences

The Reynolds operator P=(1/16)Σ_gρ(g) exists over Q (characteristic
not dividing 16). The vector representation contains no nonzero fixed
vector, since -I is in G. The homogeneous quadratic invariant space
has basis x²+y² and z². The quartic invariant space has basis

 (x²+y²)², x²y², (x²+y²)z², z⁴.

Accordingly a D4h-compatible scalar Landau polynomial to degree 4 is

 F = a(x²+y²) + b z²
     + c(x²+y²)² + d x²y²
     + e(x²+y²)z² + f z⁴,

plus an optional constant. Terms and coefficients depend on the
physical interpretation and transformation law of the order parameter.
A crystal with D4h spatial symmetry does NOT mean an arbitrary magnetic
or spin order parameter transforms as this polar-vector representation.

## Crystallographic scope

Point group 4/mmm is not itself a unique space group. The tetragonal
Bravais lattice may be primitive (P) or body centred (I), and distinct
space groups share the same point group. A particular crystal requires
its lattice, atomic basis, and if applicable magnetic symmetry.

## Agda obligations

The proof above is a paper proof. An Agda development would need:
finite-sign invariance normalisation, parity extraction over rational
polynomials, two-variable symmetric polynomial generation, and a typed
equivariant comparison with the D4h normal form. None is silently
inferred from the exhaustive Python samples.
