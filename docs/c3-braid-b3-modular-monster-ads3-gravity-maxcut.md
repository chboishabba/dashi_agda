## Physically credible route and obstacles
Witten's AdS3/Monster c=24 proposal concerns torus modular boundary transformations, not an identification of a generic Ricci-flat five-dimensional solution with AdS3. The B3->SL2Z map could let the C3 geometry represent boundary mapping-class transformations and their central-extension/framing anomaly, while a selected 3D gravity/CS boundary theory carries a representation of those transformations.
Such a construction needs: (1) a genuine B3 geometric braid isotopy from continuous framed trajectories; (2) a representation rho:SL2Z->Aut(H) matching the actual Monster VOA modular action, including multiplier and chiral/gravitational anomaly; (3) explicit connection-preserving map to the KK circle if desired; (4) a well-defined AdS3 bulk action and boundary Hilbert space; (5) matching full partition function/correlators and saddle consistency. A scalar modular-invariant J alone has trivial invariant transformation and does not capture nontrivial operator monodromy.
An AdS3 x S2 candidate in five dimensions generally requires curvature-balancing flux/matter or a cosmological term. A direct product of AdS3 and round S2 is not 5D Ricci-flat (its Ricci curvatures have opposite signs), so it cannot come from the currently studied pure 5D vacuum action without extra fields or nonproduct geometry.
