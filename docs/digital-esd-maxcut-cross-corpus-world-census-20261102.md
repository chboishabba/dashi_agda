### Positive and negative reconstruction tests

1. **Negative:** if two situated worlds agree after flattening but disagree on the research question, `NonFactorabilityWitness` defeats every factorisation through that flattening. Re-labelling the same scores cannot restore missing learner/cultural/ecological coordinates. Source-specific witnesses remain to be constructed, not presumed.
2. **Positive:** `Feminist.PositiveRecharting` requires a *new residual observer* separating states the inherited chart collapsed. Merely allocating an intersectionality column or reweighting the old sample is not a successful repair.
3. **Higher-order:** the existing PR #1451 three-axis proof shows **even all three pairwise tables** of pedagogy, learner decision power and ecological observation can agree while the three-way interaction differs. A 19-/20-coordinate extraction table is not automatically enough for such a consumer.
4. **Memory/time:** observable mediation or engagement is not sufficient to infer motive or learning. Historical trauma and threat sensitivity retain source/participant authority and non-promotion; harmful experience is not validated because coping occurs.
5. **Governance:** explicit McCall method strategy; individual/student/community/Indigenous/parent voices and consent roles are not interchangeable; participant access and action authority require receipts separate from observation counts.
