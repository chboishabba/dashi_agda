## Audit rules

A claimed proof can improve the canonical route only when its load-bearing statement has:

- exact quantifier order;
- constants uniform in shell cutoff, Galerkin cutoff and maximal time;
- Navier–Stokes critical scaling;
- no target norm, BKM integral, alignment, finite residence or smallness condition silently assumed on the right;
- all positive sources named;
- a strict total viscosity cost below one;
- a proof before weak-limit passage;
- and a successful cumulative kernel check after formalization.

A failed terminal implication does not erase correct local identities. Those identities remain available for a repaired route.
