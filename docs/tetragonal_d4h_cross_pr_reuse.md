## Correct finite representation-theoretic statement

For the natural polar vector representation V=Q³, the character
χ(g)=tr(ρ(g)), the dimension of invariants is

  dim(V^G) = (1/16) Σ_g tr(ρ(g)) = 0.

For quadratic forms Sym²(V*), the invariants are exactly the span
of x²+y² and z² (dimension 2). Full polynomial invariants are
generated by x²+y², x²y² and z² by classical finite reflection-group
invariant theory; a standalone ring proof is not yet in the Agda kernel.

The restricted square-symmetry D4 part has a degree-4 anisotropy,
and the invariants satisfy an algebraic separation between basal-plane
fourfold anisotropy and axial z². This supports an exact bridge to
Landau free-energy candidate terms but does not prove any physical
phase transition, thermodynamic coefficient, magnetic order or
material-specific band structure.
