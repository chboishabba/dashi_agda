## Global Constraints

- Preserve attribution: raw Nongin transcript is historical/generative provenance only; later DASHI theorems remain DASHI-owned unless already externally attributed.
- Never promote `+10% / 1.1` into an empirical percentage law; the theorem is consumer-relative representational sufficiency/non-sufficiency.
- Reuse existing owners before introducing parallel carriers.
- `3-cell` means the repo's finite ternary three-axis/27-state cell where stated; do not silently promote it to a CW/topological 3-cell.
- Relational Grothendieck site, Čech comparison, attached face, hypersheaf and higher-stack claims remain distinct.
- If full 6/12/8 same-object equivalence fails, formalize the obstruction and strongest faithful comparison instead of weakening the same-object criterion.
- Do not make LILA/E8/Monster/Albert/teleodynamics open PRs prerequisites for Nongin completion.
- Do not claim kernel certification without an exact-head Agda receipt.

## Review Focus

- A coarse collision that the consumer does **not** distinguish must not be called an advantage; Task 1 tests this fail-closed case.
- A richer representation without a recovery map must not count as a 1.1 repair; Task 1 requires exact consumer recovery.
- Observer nesting must not be conflated with depth merely because both use `Nat`; Task 2 requires explicit truncation/reopening maps and round trips.
- The full boundary nerve must preserve the old empty dyadic triple intersection versus later explicit augmented `tripleABC`; Task 4 tests this distinction directly.
- A transport/presheaf/action-2-cell stack must not be called a hypersheaf unless general coherence/effective-descent obligations are actually inhabited; Task 5 keeps explicit false promotion fields.
