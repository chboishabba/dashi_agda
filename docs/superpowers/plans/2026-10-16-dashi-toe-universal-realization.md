**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-16-dashi-toe-universal-realization-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- DASHI is represented as one candidate physical theory; do not encode the result as a loose thematic atlas.
- The 369/C3 structure is the candidate universal refinement/orientation substrate.
- Prefer strong typed candidate identifications over vague prose analogies.
- A conjectural realization may compose, but it may not masquerade as a paid theorem, same physical object, or empirical fact.
- Reuse existing owners and open-PR theorem-source receipts; do not reimplement mature Monster, E6/F3, GR/QFT, CM, quantum-mereology or OpenAI ingress machinery.
- Preserve source attribution and cross-kernel authority boundaries.
- `TOECandidate` is intentionally constructible before physical closure; `PhysicallyClosedTOE` is not.
- No exact-head kernel-green claim without an observed Agda run.

## Review Focus

- **Conjectural-edge composition:** composing two grade-0/1 candidate relations must preserve their unpaid obligations rather than upgrading the result.
- **Different C3 actions:** shared phase/character must not silently become same action unless an intertwiner witness is supplied.
- **Cross-kernel receipts:** Lean theorem-source evidence must remain imported-formal-source evidence, never an Agda theorem.
- **Physical promotion:** representation/action agreement must not imply calibrated physical identity, stress identity, or experimental success.
- **Negative knowledge:** existing non-descent/refutation results must remain expressible and must block incompatible promotion paths.
