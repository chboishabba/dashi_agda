# Digital-ESD Political Economy, Provisioning and Durability Design

**Date:** 2026-09-17

**Branch:** `agent/digital-esd-paper-methodology-primary-sources`

**Status:** design specification. This file records implementation intent and source-role boundaries. It does not itself create scholarly evidence.

## 1. Purpose

Extend the existing Digital-ESD architecture so that political economy is not merely implicit in externality, procurement, ownership, platform, labour, open-commons and lifecycle analyses.

The extension must answer four new but source-compatible questions:

1. Which political-economic and provisioning arrangements structure educational benefits, burdens, duplication, lock-in, labour, maintenance and exit?
2. Under which scoped mechanisms can growth, accumulation, competition, turnover or externalisation produce waste pressure?
3. What non-classroom social provisioning is lost or changed when education changes form, including meals, community connection, care, third-space functions and informal support?
4. What keeps an educational/technical system alive through staff turnover, recurrent cost, maintenance, political change, funding change and institutional memory loss?

This is an extension of the current Digital-ESD consumer, not a new global political-economy ontology.

## 2. Provenance and attribution layers

The implementation must preserve three distinct layers.

### 2.1 Transcript provenance

The originating recording is evidence only that Johl Brown proposed the following research-relevant interpretations/questions:

- capitalism encourages competition and may generate waste/externalities;
- speculative AI value and strategic infrastructure may interact with community consultation and lock-in;
- institutions may duplicate work rather than share it;
- open source/shared knowledge may provide an alternative coordination surface;
- scholarship has a public/shareable knowledge function not reducible to profit motive;
- sustainability must ask who is absent from decision-making.

Transcript provenance does not prove the corresponding world claims.

### 2.2 DASHI synthesis

DASHI may construct consumer questions, finite countermodels, comparative-institutional fixtures and source-bounded mechanism types from the attributed literature. These remain DASHI synthesis and may not be attributed back to the transcript speaker or an external source as that source's theorem.

### 2.3 External source evidence

External sources pay only their bounded empirical, theoretical, normative or methodological claims. Exact source identity, source role, population/object, design, time, system boundary and claim ceiling remain attached.

The controlling rules remain:

```text
source proposition != DASHI reconstruction != DASHI theorem
political-economy label != causal mechanism receipt
one cultural example != universal alternative system
```

## 3. Capitalism and waste: scoped theorem design

The extension must not make the bare political-economy label `capitalism` sufficient for a universal world-level waste conclusion.

The SOTA does support stronger scoped mechanism families:

- treadmill-of-production / accumulation literature: expansion, resource withdrawal and pollution/waste under growth-oriented production;
- product-longevity/planned-obsolescence literature: competitive/product-turnover and business-model pressures can shorten useful life and increase material turnover;
- food-waste literature: food loss/waste has systemic drivers, and oligopolistic/competitive retailer models can generate endogenous food waste;
- material-flow literature: extraction, throughput, stocks, emissions and waste are physically linked to economic production/consumption;
- degrowth/post-growth literature: material throughput and waste can remain structurally coupled to growth-oriented provisioning systems.

Therefore the theorem-bearing object should be conditional, for example:

```text
CapitalistGrowthWasteMechanismReceipt
  = capitalist/growth-oriented provisioning context
  + explicit accumulation/turnover/externalisation mechanism
  + specified material/service carrier
  + measured/modelled waste/throughput outcome
  + source/object/time scope
```

A theorem may then state that the fully inhabited mechanism receipt supports a bounded waste-pressure proposition.

It must remain impossible to derive the same proposition from `capitalismLabel` alone.

This preserves both the user's structural thesis and the repo's existing `ComparativeInstitutionalMeaningExact` rule that a political-economy label is not a counterfactual or causal receipt.

## 4. Political-economy coordinates

Reuse the existing comparative-institutional coordinates where possible. Digital ESD additionally needs education-facing readings for:

- ownership/provision form: public, private for-profit, non-profit, cooperative/common, mixed;
- profit/revenue model;
- accumulation/growth pressure;
- market concentration and competition/cooperation;
- intellectual-property/licensing regime;
- procurement and capital-financing model;
- subsidy/public-financing dependence;
- vendor/platform dependence and practical exit;
- labour, moderation, teaching, support and maintenance arrangements;
- externality allocation;
- community/participant decision authority;
- recurrent funding and total cost of ownership;
- strategic/national-security designation where source-relevant.

Do not encode one scalar 'capitalism score'.

## 5. Educational provisioning beyond instruction

The sustainability consumer must retain functions that can disappear or move when schooling changes delivery mode.

At minimum audit:

- meals/food security;
- safe physical gathering/dwell space;
- peer and adult social connection;
- health/social-service linkage;
- family/community connection;
- informal observation/support;
- disability/access support;
- device/connectivity access;
- care/supervision burden transferred to households;
- transport/travel burden;
- extracurricular/community participation.

The key firewall is:

```text
same instructional delivery != same social provisioning
```

Online/hybrid/self-directed/community schooling therefore must be compared as bundles of educational and social provisioning, not only content-delivery modes.

## 6. Community, third-space and alternative-education cross-pollination

Reuse `DASHI.Education.CommunityConnectednessTopologyExact`: formal connection is not effective connection; effective connection does not create authority; travel burden does not determine relational affordance.

Candidate comparative examples may include:

- community schools / integrated student-support models;
- school breakfast/lunch/food-relief programmes;
- self-directed learning and learner-control interventions;
- democratic/participatory education;
- Zapatista autonomous education;
- Indigenous/local knowledge and relational education;
- open knowledge commons;
- pod/microschool arrangements only when source evidence pays a concrete claim.

Attribution rules are strict:

- Zapatista empirical reconstruction remains scholarship about particular autonomous communities, not a generic anti-capitalist education brand and not an EZLN primary quotation unless a primary locator is acquired;
- Indigenous knowledge is nation/community/source specific; do not use `Braiding Sweetgrass` as generic Indigenous authority or as proof for all Indigenous education;
- self-directed education evidence must distinguish structured/scaffolded learner responsibility from absence of teaching/support;
- community-school evidence must preserve the evaluated model, population and implementation context.

## 7. Food and school social-provisioning seam

Create an explicit audit question for education-delivery changes:

```text
If attendance moves away from a school site, which non-instructional services are lost, shifted or recreated, by whom, and with what resources?
```

School-meal evidence can support the importance of school-based food provision and connectedness for relevant populations. It must not be promoted to the universal claim that online schooling causes food insecurity.

The same pattern applies to counselling, disability services, peer interaction, transport, supervision and other school-linked supports.

## 8. Maintenance and institutional durability

Add a durability/maintenance audit that distinguishes launch from persistence.

Coordinates:

- named maintainer/steward;
- paid labour/time allocation;
- recurrent operating budget;
- hardware/software maintenance;
- security/update responsibility;
- documentation and handover;
- staff succession/turnover resilience;
- training/professional learning;
- institutional ownership/governance;
- vendor/community dependency;
- monitoring/evaluation feedback;
- data/export/migration path;
- political/funding shock resilience;
- defunding/decommissioning plan;
- service continuity for accessibility and social-provisioning functions.

Primary theorem boundary:

```text
successful adoption != durable institution
initial grant != recurrently funded service
technical maintainability != funded maintenance capacity
named maintainer != succession resilience
```

Reuse existing school-reform sustainability, TCO/procurement, lifecycle, open-commons, participant-governance and context/memory owners rather than creating duplicate theories.

## 9. Source acquisition families

Prioritise sources that discriminate mechanisms rather than merely repeat broad sustainability language.

### Political economy / waste

Acquire exact bibliographic/source objects for:

- treadmill-of-production / political-economy-of-environment reviews and scope-condition critiques;
- food-loss/waste systemic-driver reviews;
- retailer market-structure / competition food-waste work;
- product-longevity / obsolescence reviews;
- material-flow / throughput literature;
- post-growth/degrowth provisioning literature where it supplies a distinct consumer coordinate.

### School provisioning and community

Acquire:

- school meals/food security primary and review evidence, including Queensland-relevant evidence;
- community-school / integrated-support reviews;
- third-space/community learning evidence only where the construct matches the consumer;
- self-directed-learning meta-analytic/review evidence;
- participatory/democratic decision-making review evidence.

### Alternative/plural institutions

Reuse and, where needed, extend:

- `PluralSustainableLandManagementTraditionsExact` and Zapatista source lineage;
- Indigenous/local-knowledge sustainability-education reviews;
- community-specific primary sources before attributing claims directly to a movement/community;
- `Braiding Sweetgrass` only for propositions actually present in Kimmerer's work or scholarship explicitly analysing it.

### Durability

Acquire school-reform implementation/sustainability reviews and digital-education funding/procurement evidence that directly address recurrent resourcing, staffing stability, professional learning, maintenance, governance and adaptation.

## 12. Success criteria

The implementation is complete when:

- capitalism/competition/ownership/profit/commons/public provision are explicitly representable without becoming proof tokens;
- at least one source-bounded waste mechanism is constructively inhabitable;
- bare `capitalismLabel` cannot create a waste outcome;
- social provisioning is formally independent of instructional delivery mode;
- institutional durability is independent of initial adoption/launch;
- food/community/self-directed/Zapatista/Indigenous examples retain source-specific authority boundaries;
- every new external claim is represented through an exact attributed source object or remains explicitly unpaid;
- the Digital-ESD aggregate and roadmap expose the new seam;
- no Agda/kernel certification is claimed without an observed receipt.
