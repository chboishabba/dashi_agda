# Smith Interpretation Max-Cut Design

## Goal

Use the existing DASHI/SensibLaw/SLR machinery to make *Smith v Chief Executive, Queensland Corrective Services* [2026] HCA 37 the first source-pinned statutory-interpretation specimen in which operative PNF constellations, candidate interpretations, consumer-relative representation adequacy, counterexample/experimental discrimination, legal-follow acquisition, authority treatment, and adjudicative boundaries are explicit without creating a parallel legal ontology.

## Source and attribution boundary

Primary specimen: *Smith v Chief Executive, Queensland Corrective Services* [2026] HCA 37, judgment 14 October 2026, supplied by the user. Opinion-level propositions remain attributed to the relevant reasons. In particular, Edelman J's terms `literalism` and `contextomy`, his treatment of s 48 Human Rights Act 2019 (Qld), and his distinction among conforming interpretation, severance and partial disapplication remain Edelman J's reasons unless another opinion independently supplies the same proposition.

DASHI authors the finite formal structures, countermodels and cross-domain reuse. Existing source-owning modules remain the authorities for Two-Eyed Seeing, Kimmerer, intersectionality, Lacan/Irigaray, Justice Lee, Mabo, experimental design and investigative Pareto. Cross-pollination does not transfer source authorship or domain authority.

## Architecture

1. Reuse PNF/source ancestry as the source world.
2. Extract a query-indexed operative constellation from the PNF graph rather than treating one token or doctrine label as sufficient.
3. Represent candidate interpretations as consumer-relative projections/factorisations of that constellation. The candidate universe is not exhausted by Australian precedent.
4. Reuse `AdmissibleConsumerMDLHyperfabricExact` and `IntersectionalNonFactorability`: admissibility and consumer adequacy are hard gates; description length/eloquence is not authority; a quotient that erases a consumer-material distinction cannot recover it by later rewording alone.
5. Add a consumer-relative informativeness preorder. `A` is at least as informative as `B` for a declared query when every query-relevant distinction preserved by `B` is also recoverable from `A`. Equality of expressive complexity is neither required nor sufficient. Simple English, full English and multilingual surfaces may be parity-equivalent, incomparable, or strictly ordered for a given consumer.
6. Reuse `RobustExperimentInferenceFrontierExact` / discovery machinery to turn a failed factorisation or unresolved interpretation residual into an explicit discriminator-design obligation. Experimental-design priority creates no truth or legal authority.
7. Reuse `ITIRInvestigationAcquisitionParetoExact` to choose among lawful source/acquisition routes by the existing five-axis mixed Pareto relation: information gain, dependency closure, residual coverage and provenance novelty maximise; lawful resource cost minimises. No scalar ranking is introduced.
8. Reuse `SensibLawLegalFollowProofSearchBridgeExact`: residual -> typed search intent -> jurisdiction/time/source-role/authority constrained legal-follow -> bounded acquisition -> same parser/PNF re-entry -> reviewed treatment/payment. Acquisition alone does not pay a residual.
9. Treat precedent and legislation as high-authority legal strands for an Australian legal consumer, not as the generator of the whole logically/semantically compatible interpretation universe. Preserve Two-Eyed-style coordination-without-fusion across lexical, logical, statutory, precedential, rights, historical, affected/situated and comparative strands.
10. Preserve the Justice Lee boundary: reconstruction, search and synthesis do not determine credibility, ultimate fact, normative weight or final judgment.

## Smith fixture

The exact statutory question concerns `participate in assisted reproductive technology` under Corrective Services Act 2006 (Qld) s 22. The fixture keeps at least two candidate constructions:

- wide: procedures connected with or capable of assisting possible reproduction, including fertility-preservation procedures;
- narrow/direct-assistance: technology that directly assists conception or reproduction; egg extraction/freezing preserves fertility rather than itself achieving/directly assisting reproduction.

The formal fixture does not claim these exhaust every compatible interpretation. It proves only that the supplied wide and narrow candidates are separable by material examples and that a coarse purpose-connected representation can collapse distinctions relevant to the statutory consumer.

Edelman J's endometriosis/laparoscopy/twisted-ovary/cycle-app/cancer-treatment examples are represented as attributed discriminator/counterexample material against the wide construction, not as a generic theorem that counterexamples decide statutory meaning.

## Sophistry / expressive-power boundary

The primitive is not `Sophistry : Bool`. The reusable formal objects are expressive asymmetry, consumer-relative informativeness, erased-distinction counterexamples and interactional/strategic receipts. Greater complexity, vocabulary or argumentative degrees of freedom create no semantic/evidential/legal authority. A simpler or other-language representation may be at least as informative, or strictly more informative, for a declared consumer.

A structural distortion may be proved when a representation transformation erases a distinction required by the consumer. Calling that use `sophistry` requires a separate interactional/strategic attribution receipt; structural inadequacy alone does not establish intent.

## Indigenous / Two-Eyed / intersectional boundary

Reuse only the repository's source-bounded structural lessons:

- coordination of distinct knowledge systems does not require epistemic fusion;
- same extracted proposition does not recover provenance, authority, permission or obligation;
- translation may preserve, add, erase or leave coordinates unresolved;
- admissibility is query-indexed;
- an inadequate projection may fail to factor through an operative situated decision.

No universal Indigenous epistemology, cultural permission, or Indigenous authority is inferred for Smith. The formal legal consumer remains Australian statutory interpretation with its own authority receipts.

10. SLR runtime carries corresponding receipts without claim-truth or adjudicative promotion.
