# DASHI TOE Universal Realization Architecture

Date: 2026-10-16

## Intent

DASHI is treated as one candidate physical theory, not as a federation of analogies. The mature 369/C3 layer is the candidate universal kinematic/refinement/orientation substrate. Existing Monster, exceptional, gauge, QFT, GR, quantum-information, matter, chemistry, CM/arithmetic and continuum-dynamics programmes are sector realizations, reductions, phases, observers, or effective theories of one universal DASHI object.

Agda and Lean are the consistency, provenance and falsification harness. They do not cap conceptual synthesis. The architecture must permit aggressive candidate identifications, but every identification must expose the exact proof, transport, action, stress, calibration or empirical obligations required to keep it.

Success means the repo can state a strong TOE candidate in one canonical place, classify the status of each sector realization, aggressively compose currently separated structures, and reduce disagreement to explicit fail-closed leaves instead of informal caution.

## Aggressive TOE synthesis rule

The integration policy is deliberately permissive at the hypothesis layer:

- make strong cross-sector candidate connections when there is a mathematically meaningful common structure;
- prefer a bold typed conjectural realization over a vague prose analogy;
- compose realizations aggressively to derive new candidate predictions or theorem obligations;
- let formal incompatibility, missing intertwiners, source mismatch, wrong dimensions, failed stress transport or empirical disagreement kill bad connections.

What remains prohibited is silently relabelling a conjectural relation as a proved same-object theorem or empirical fact.

## Validation ladder

Every realization has an explicit maximum attained grade:

```text
0 conjectural structural connection
1 shared carrier/coordinate map
2 representation equivalence/intertwiner
3 dynamics intertwiner
4 same action/Hamiltonian
5 stress/metric-variation/observable preservation
6 calibrated same physical object
7 held-out empirical prediction
8 independent replication
```

This is not a claim-confidence score. It is a typed statement of which transport obligations have been paid.

## Terminal objects

### `TOECandidate`

Constructible when:

- all declared sectors have a realization path or explicit conjectural socket;
- the universal 369/C3 substrate is wired to the canonical realization graph;
- all cross-sector claims have typed validation grades and unpaid obligations;
- known non-descent/refutation results are retained;
- the complete candidate remains internally inspectable.

`TOECandidate` is intentionally obtainable before all physics is proved. Its purpose is to let DASHI state the strongest coherent theory currently imaginable and expose exactly where it can fail.

### `PhysicallyClosedTOE`

Requires at minimum:

```text
universal physical state
-> universal dynamics/action
-> controlled sector reductions
-> QFT stress-energy
-> Lorentzian/Einstein geometry
-> matter/measurement observables
-> calibrated same-object prediction
-> held-out empirical success
-> independent replication
```

No cardinality match, character agreement, theorem-source citation, kernel proof of an abstract structure, or scalar entropy analogy substitutes for these physical obligations.

## Falsification and non-collapse

The architecture must make failure productive. A conjectural unification edge can be rejected by:

- impossible carrier dimensions or type mismatch;
- failure of representation character/action compatibility;
- failed dynamics or Hamiltonian intertwining;
- failed action restriction;
- failed metric-variation/stress commutation;
- observer non-descent;
- scale/calibration incompatibility;
- empirical prediction failure.

Rejected edges remain recorded as negative knowledge where useful.
