# Nongin semantic-depth capstone design

## Goal

Complete the Nongin programme at its honest mathematical boundary on current `dashi_agda/master`, without making later 369, exceptional-group, teleodynamic, Monster, LILA, physical, or empirical results prerequisites for the historical Nongin claim.

The core target is to replace the historical shorthand “1.0 -> 1.1 / +10%” with a precise consumer-relative theorem:

- a coarse observer may identify two fine states;
- a declared consumer may distinguish those states;
- therefore that consumer does not factor through the coarse observer;
- an enriched/frame-bearing observer that retains the missing distinction can factor the consumer;
- no numeric performance advantage or universal epistemic superiority follows.

The second target is to turn the current syntactic nested-observer term into a typed depth/truncation/reopening tower reusing the repository's existing observer, residual, presheaf, bidescent, carry, and inverse-system machinery.

The third target is a new Nongin capstone that imports the strongest current descendants on master while preserving the provenance firewall: the raw Nongin source contributes historical/generative intuition only; downstream theorems retain their own owners.

## Design

### A. Consumer-relative “1.1” theorem

Add a focused reasoning/philosophy owner, tentatively:

`DASHI/Philosophy/NonginConsumerRelativePowerUpExact.agda`

The owner should reuse existing `FactorsThrough` and non-descent machinery rather than introduce a new sufficiency ontology.

Define a generic witness around:

- `fineObserver : Fine -> Coarse`;
- `consumer : Fine -> Answer`;
- `left right : Fine`;
- `fineObserver left = fineObserver right`;
- a proof that `consumer left != consumer right`.

Compile this to the existing `ConsumerNonDescentWitness` / factorisation obstruction. Then provide an enriched observer carrying enough residual/frame information to reopen the distinction and prove positive factorisation for the same consumer.

Instantiate the generic theorem at least twice:

1. the original Nongin debate toy (`DebateState -> Bit`), where the public binary frame erases the `other` coordinate;
2. a mature repo-owned descendant, preferably the exact `T^9 <-> T^4_local x T^5_complement` factorisation, where the local chart alone is insufficient for a consumer that depends on the complement, while local+complement reopens the global state exactly.

This yields the formal reading:

`1.1 advantage = retained consumer-relevant distinction under a richer representation`.

Explicitly retain false/uninhabited promotions for:

- literal 10-percent advantage;
- guaranteed decision superiority;
- “more dimensions is always better”;
- universal consumer sufficiency;
- empirical psychological or strategic advantage.

A whole inverse limit should remain optional unless the existing owner already pays it definitionally. Do not manufacture all-depth coherence from one-step carry.

If the finite site collapses some of these obligations definitionally, prove that rather than introducing needless abstraction.

## Non-goals

This tranche does not require or claim:

- E8, F4, Albert, Monster, LILA, RH, teleodynamics, neuroscience, physics, or consciousness realization;
- a universal numeric “10%” law;
- psychological superiority of meta-cognition;
- truth from representational richness;
- a Monster/exceptional interpretation of the ternary carriers;
- full higher-stack/hypersheaf promotion if the canonical coherence obligations remain unpaid.

Those programmes may consume the resulting generic theorem later, but they do not determine Nongin completion.

## Success criterion

The Nongin programme is considered structurally complete when A, B, and C are paid on current master-compatible owners:

1. the historical 1.0->1.1 intuition has become a generic consumer-relative factorisation/nonfactorisation theorem with an exact repair;
2. recursive observer nesting has an exact finite-depth truncation/collision/reopening semantics linked to existing depth machinery;
3. a current-master capstone records those results and the strongest downstream descendants without attribution leakage.

D closes the mature finite trialectic geometry lane if possible; otherwise it must end in the strongest exact no-go/comparison theorem supported by the incidence structure. E is an optional stronger categorical promotion programme and must stop at the first genuine coherence wall.
