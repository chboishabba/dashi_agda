# Lombardi / Australian Colonial Finance / Strategic-Land Provenance Design

## Intent

Integrate the attached Montefiore/Rothschild Australian-colonial-finance, gold-rush, Maralinga/Pine-Gap, and Mark-Lombardi/BCCI material into DASHI without creating a conspiracy-graph ontology, without collapsing Jewish identity into a causal relation, and without promoting visual/network adjacency into causation, coordination, control, culpability, or institutional continuity.

The implementation must reuse DASHI's existing evidence, provenance, observer, Pine Gap, and Epstein machinery wherever that machinery already owns the relevant semantics.

## Existing canonical owners to reuse

1. `DASHI.Governance.EvidenceGradedGenealogyCore`
   - owns evidence grades A-E;
   - owns typed relations including direct documented transfer, personnel/institutional continuity, funding/organisational link, adjacency, conceptual borrowing, shared method, and analogy;
   - keeps provenance edge-local;
   - already proves one strong edge does not establish a universal coordinated network.

2. `DASHI.Physics.Foundations.IndigenousMilitaryIntelligenceCircuitExact`
   - owns Pine Gap as a joint/global strategic site on Arrernte Country;
   - owns the abstract `Country -> military installation -> remote-conflict legibility` circuit;
   - already blocks shared topology -> institutional identity and protest demand -> verified specific targeting chain.

3. `DASHI.Governance.AustraliaUSAllianceSovereigntyGenealogyExact`
   - owns Pine Gap/AUKUS sovereignty/dependence coordinates and the boundary that alliance dependence does not definitionally erase sovereignty.

4. `DASHI.Cognition.EpsteinPsychedelicSourceAtlasExact` and the associated Epstein network tranche on `agent/epstein-psychedelic-network-maxcut-20261012`
   - provide the model for source role, exact source identity, bounded source claim, and non-promotion firewalls;
   - corpus presence, correspondence, proposed participation, completed participation, funding instruction, transfer statement, bank settlement, purpose, and wrongdoing remain separate coordinates.

5. Existing consumer-relative/coarse-fine observer machinery
   - a network drawing is a consumer-facing projection of a richer historical state;
   - equality of coarse diagrams must not imply equality of fine archival states.

No competing definitions of these canonical concepts are to be added.

## External-source authority and attribution

External sources retain authority for their own historical/art-historical claims. DASHI owns only the typed reconstruction, provenance adapters, non-promotion theorems, observer semantics, and cross-case synthesis.

### Montefiore / Rothschild / Victorian gold

Seed the source atlas with at least:

- National Portrait Gallery of Australia, Angus Trumble, `The Rothschilds, the Montefiores, and the Victorian Gold Rush` (2017), documenting N. M. Rothschild & Sons correspondence with Montefiore relatives and their close involvement in the Victorian gold-rush trade.
- Australian Dictionary of Biography / People Australia entries for Jacob Barrow Montefiore and Joseph Barrow Montefiore, for family relationships, colonial offices, Bank of Australasia / mercantile roles, and the historically documented relationship to Moses Montefiore and the wider Cohen/Rothschild family.
- Rothschild Archive material concerning Australian gold purchasing/shipping where exact archival identifiers are available.

The formalization must correct the social-media shorthand: Jacob/Joseph Barrow Montefiore are not to be represented merely as `marriedIntoRothschild`. The wider family relation runs through specific kinship/marriage edges, including Moses Montefiore's marriage to Judith Cohen and Judith's sister Hannah's marriage to Nathan Mayer Rothschild, while Jacob/Joseph have their own cousinship/business/agency/correspondence relations. Every relation remains typed.

### Maralinga

Seed with National Archives of Australia sources establishing:

- British nuclear testing in Australia with Australian-government agreement/support;
- Maralinga's development as the permanent proving ground;
- testing on Pitjantjatjara and Yankunytjatjara traditional lands without their permission;
- exposure/removal consequences and the later Royal Commission record.

### Pine Gap

Reuse existing Pine Gap source owners rather than building a parallel historical record. Additional source entries are permitted only where they add a genuinely new historical coordinate (for example, the 1966 Australia-US agreement or a specific facility function not already represented).

### Mark Lombardi / BCCI

Seed with:

- Whitney Museum of American Art entry for Mark Lombardi's `BCCI-ICIC & FAB, 1972-91 (4th Version)` and the Whitney's explanation of Lombardi's public-record research and `narrative structures` practice;
- U.S. Senate Foreign Relations Committee report `The BCCI Affair` (Kerry/Brown, 1992) for BCCI/ICIC/First American factual claims, including ICIC nominee/financing functions and BCCI's concealed First American control.

Lombardi is an authority for the artwork and narrative-structure practice, not automatically for each underlying factual allegation. Underlying historical claims require their own source receipts.

## Source-atlas design

Create a case-specific source atlas with:

- exact source identity;
- source role;
- source URL/reference;
- bounded source claim;
- authority class (government/archive/scholarly/museum/congressional/artwork/secondary);
- directness (primary / archival interpretation / secondary synthesis / artwork representation);
- date/interval;
- named entities;
- claim-level promotion ceilings.

The atlas must follow the Epstein pattern: source presence is not claim truth; a source author's statement is not automatically an independently verified transaction; an archival interpretation is not a primary transaction record; an artwork edge is not an underlying historical receipt.

`Country/land -> colonial legal/administrative control -> extraction -> merchant/bank/insurance/shipping intermediation -> metropolitan capital`

This is a composition schema, not a claim that every case instantiates every edge.

## Lombardi narrative-structure adapter

Create a representation adapter over evidence-grade edges rather than a new evidence calculus.

A diagram edge must retain:

- source and target;
- typed historical relation;
- evidence grade;
- provenance/source identities;
- temporal interval;
- contested/confirmed/unresolved status where applicable;
- whether the edge is direct evidence, derived path, or artistic representation.

Required theorems/boundaries:

- `visualAdjacency != causalLink`
- `graphPath != causalChain`
- `nodeDegree != culpability`
- `visualCentrality != commandAuthority`
- `sharedCounterparty != coordination`
- `samePersonInTwoNetworks != institutionalIdentity`
- `artworkRepresentation != underlyingHistoricalReceipt`
- `diagramCompleteness = false` unless explicitly proven for a declared corpus.

The adapter should support a consumer-relative projection from a fine archival/evidence graph to a coarse narrative diagram.

For fine states `h1`, `h2` and diagram observer `pi`:

`pi h1 == pi h2` must not imply `h1 == h2`.

Provide an inhabited collision witness where two fine edge states differ in provenance/status but intentionally project to the same coarse visible relation. Reuse existing coarse/fine consumer machinery if its interface admits this directly; otherwise build only the thinnest adapter necessary.

A Pareto/acquisition list may rank these for research effort, but the ranking is not evidence, truth, culpability, or causal strength.
