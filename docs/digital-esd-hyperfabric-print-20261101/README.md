### Explicit formal boundaries

- **Attribution:** original source finding `≠` source-specific DASHI model `≠` cross-paper inference `≠` new formal result `≠` independently validated empirical outcome. Authorship is not affiliation or programme ownership.
- **Question-relative sufficiency:** a coarse observer may identify two worlds that a question must distinguish. Then the question does not factor through that observation. This constrains claims that access, activity, or a global visibility score determine participation, learning, identity, or total situated audit state.
- **Same-source indexing:** `SourceAuditHyperfabric source` requires observations, admissibility fibres and intersection receipts to carry proof that each component's source matches the indexed original. A top-level label is insufficient.
- **Human / clinical / community authority:** data, AI classification, consultation and abstract ecological indicators do not automatically confer a participant's meaning, decision rights, clinical diagnosis or community consent.
- **Reciprocal sustainability:** education *for* sustainability and the sustainability *of* digital education remain two distinct obligations. Ecology, disability and intersectionality, political/economic burden and institutional durability are not collapsed into engagement.
- **EfS independence:** EfS themes are nominated before importing the digital principles. A comparison receipt is a candidate author interpretation with unresolved evidence; it is not automatically independent human coding or proof of intervention effect.
- **Candidate source cases:** the 16 new/reused source cases have an explicit `admittedToFullReview ≡ false` flag. Every case contains a source locator, bounded original contribution, two-way inquiry, rival reading and evidence debt. A citation cannot admit a paper.
- **Manuscript use:** the integrative conceptual review is aimed at **retrospective critical re-examination of existing digital-learning/SoTL work** and **prospective, sustainability-aware research design**. The seven principles may be retained, narrowed, split, merged, extended or rejected after actual review and coauthor adjudication.
- **Research status:** real ERIC acquisition and deduplication work is reported elsewhere; external database completion, reviewed source spans, source admission and full framework adjudication remain unpaid. No current-head Agda kernel or local preflight receipt was obtained in this print session.
- **Domain boundary:** **ESD stays outside Fleet and TOE**. The source print connects only the Digital-ESD umbrella. JMD/meta-introspector materials are independently attributed technical references, not ESD source-authority substitutes.

### Exact composition boundary: what is *not* yet a populated hyperfabric

The printed formal owners already relate the Alice loom, the independently nominated EfS concepts, seven digital principles, six comparison receipts, seven survival receipts, and sixteen additional-source cases. **But source-attributed candidate bridges are not the same object as admitted source-audit fibres.** The new `DigitalESDEfSSourceCrossPollinationExact` cases each contain an `AttributedSource`, a `ReciprocalPedagogicalBridge`, a same-locator equality and `admittedToFullReview ≡ false`. They do **not** each contain an instantiated `SourceAuditHyperfabric source` populated by same-source observations, full-text source-span receipts, intersection fibres and independent reviewer decisions.

The existing `DigitalESDSourceAuditHyperfabricExact` is the reusable source-indexed **type**, not a certificate that every paper in this new atlas has passed that type's evidence and admissibility requirements. The umbrella file imports the candidate cases and audit owner separately. This is a **source-connected candidate overlay**, not one automatically fused, empirically admitted Alice–EfS world.

The print includes complete code text for 35 specified central owners and the whole Digital-ESD direct-import manifest. It does **not** print the entire transitive repository closure (including every support module imported by the Alice loom and ESD umbrella), nor does it claim a completed Agda kernel check on the draft branch.
