## New admission invariant

No source may enter final Digital-ESD synthesis merely because it has been found, cited, screened as relevant, assigned a DOI/QID/Dewey coordinate, or placed on a Pareto frontier.

Every admitted source must first carry a `DigitalESDSourceAuditAdmissionExact.SourceAuditAdmission` for that exact `AttributedSource`.

The admission contract requires:

1. complete ten-axis 0–5 visibility coverage;
2. source-specific reasons, locators and limitations for every axis receipt;
3. situated observer/provenance information rather than a view-from-nowhere score;
4. query-relative admissibility and retained claim ceiling;
5. standards/framework applicability declarations;
6. required sparse intersection checks;
7. preserved tension receipts where admissible observers diverge;
8. canonical snowball attribution/provenance receipt;
9. explicit WrongType/no-promotion boundary; and
10. scoring-protocol version.

Zero scores are admissible and analytically useful. No authoritative grand total or forced consensus score exists.

## Sparse intersection rule

Intersectionality is not an additive total. Component-axis visibility does not determine interaction visibility.

A higher intersection fibre is added only when:

- an admitted source studies it;
- a declared manuscript consumer requires it; or
- a constructive `FactorsThrough` collision shows the existing projection loses a decision-relevant distinction.

This preserves the existing least-refinement / consumer-relative repair discipline.

## Standards/framework role

The source-written normative atlas currently treats ISO/NIST/ITIL/Six Sigma as versioned normative/process observers. Standard mention, asserted conformity and certified conformity are distinct states, and none creates educational-effect evidence.

Copyrighted ISO text is not reproduced. Only public bibliographic identity/scope and legitimately acquired propositions are retained.
