## 1. Start without prior DASHI knowledge

The educational question is whether and how insights from digital education can contribute to transformative Education for Sustainability (EfS)/Education for Sustainable Development (ESD), while also testing the environmental, social, epistemic, institutional, temporal and economic sustainability of the digital educational activity itself.

DASHI is a proof- and provenance-oriented research infrastructure, not a machine that has discovered an empirically true teaching method. It can retain typed distinctions among source assertions, our interpretations, mathematical representation results, counterexamples and empirical claims that still require study. **Nothing in this history implies that an Agda-typed relationship is a measured pedagogical effect.**

A *source fibre* is a retained paper-specific evidence surface: who authored the result, what was studied, what was said, in which setting, what the limitations were, and how that source differs from neighbouring sources. A *cross-paper braid* relates these fibres (e.g. complementary, in tension, scope-different) without fusing their authorship or claims. A *consumer* is a particular question that evidence is being asked to answer. A *projection* is a simplified view; it is insufficient for a consumer if two states share that projection but demand different answers. A *candidate principle* is a proposed generalisation subject to later challenge, not an outcome.

## 2. Epistemic register: what kind of origin claim is this?

| Register | Example | What it licenses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H: historical repository observation | PR #341 was opened 26 July and merged 27 July 2026; its PR description records seven source papers | Dated statement about repository history, not a study effect |
| S: source-bound scholarly claim | An individual paper describes a bounded outcome or conceptual argument | Only its own reported scope/claim ceiling |
| I: DASHI interpretive inference | Later student-voice scholarship can challenge an earlier AI-feedback reading | A proposed cross-paper relationship, not an original author's finding |
| F: formal representation / theorem | A coarse projection cannot answer a query that differs inside its fibres, given an explicit collision | The stated mathematical result under assumptions, not empirical ESD success |
| P: prospective hypothesis | A candidate Digital-ESD relationship might transfer into a specific institution | A question for independent empirical review |
| U: unresolved archaeology or reviewer decision | Why these seven labels rather than a six/eight-way scheme | Remains open until evidence/decision receipts exist |

An academic Methods section must identify each claimed conclusion's register. S + I + F does not automatically become an observed outcome. PR history supports H but does not prove a psychological causal motive for the authors' decisions.

## 5. How the synthesis was actually constructed, step by step

**Step A — preserve source claims.** Use the registry and source-specific owner for each paper; retain methods, participants, settings and negative boundaries before relating sources.

**Step B — make proposed cross-paper relations explicit.** The [dialectical-development owner](../DASHI/Biology/CrossPaperDialecticalDevelopment.agda) defines distinct registers and relations such as AI feedback → student-voice governance challenge, humour audience/context → person–place ecological refinement, voice → student-chosen support, and allyship → multiple observer positions. These arrows are **our interpretations**; the individual papers did not collectively run the purported combined experiment.

**Step C — formulate candidate abstractions.** The [principle owner](../DASHI/Education/DigitalESDTransferablePedagogicalPrinciplesExact.agda) contains seven explicitly source-backed rows. The labels are a synthesis decision, not seven automatically inferred clusters or seven separate observed intervention effects.

**Step D — attach conditions, rather than declare transfer.** The [transformative matrix](../DASHI/Education/DigitalESDTransformativePrincipleMatrixExact.agda) pairs candidate pedagogy with **independently sourced** sustainability constraints (equity, agency, lifecycle, procurement, openness, durability, future options) and educator/institutional/policy enactment conditions. Digital pedagogical support, scaling and sustainability are three distinct evidential obligations.

**Step E — reverse the direction of critique.** EfS/ESD literature, including GreenComp's published competency framework and Tilbury/other source-bounded scholarship, can define concepts independently before seeing the digital seven. The reciprocal comparison records convergence, mismatch and tensions, not an automatic fit.

**Step F — test with reviewable evidence.** External search/metadata → explicit screening → selected and verified full text → exact revision/span → parser/PNF candidates → human review → independent SourceAuditAdmission → principle challenge. A candidate mechanism never becomes a general ESD effect merely because it appears in a source register or passes Agda's type checker.

## 7. Why seven? Reproducibility and alternative-grouping debt

**Known:** there is a concrete seven-row mapping and a six-stage [declared derivation method](../DASHI/Education/DigitalESDTransferablePrincipleDerivationMethodExact.agda). **Not established:** a prespecified algorithm, independently recorded alternative partitions, rejection decisions, coder agreement, or a proof of minimality/completeness/unique optimality of seven.

The following are **new alternative hypotheses for future adjudication**, emphatically **not historical decisions already made**:

| Alternative candidate partition | What it might improve | What it might erase / required discriminating evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Combine feedback and iterative evidence return | One coherent reflexive-feedback cycle | Single feedback event vs longitudinal governance/revision may demand separate observables |
| Combine engagement with adaptive support | Smaller relational-inclusion construct | Reachable participation vs response to specific unmet support needs can differ |
| Split individual learner agency from collective/community decision authority | Better EfS collective action and institutional power | Distinguish individual refusal, shared problem framing, community consent and real decision rights |
| Split contextual custodianship into epistemic data curation and ecological place relations | Avoid reducing ecological knowledge to data practice | Source-specific ecological pedagogy/kinship and material relationships may have distinct authority |
| Merge plural observers and contextual custodianship | Simpler situated epistemology | Observer identity/authority may differ even with matching contextual descriptions |
| Introduce explicit values/futures/educator praxis as new principles | Fits independently developed EfS scholarship | Test whether they are genuinely new principles, subdimensions or independent constraints rather than adding labels by preference |

**Adjudication protocol:** (1) define source inclusion/exclusion before inspecting alternatives; (2) independently code EfS sources blind to digital-seven labels; (3) record proposed concepts, source spans, disagreements, and *rejected* alternatives; (4) reveal the seven only after EfS coding is frozen; (5) compare partitions against consumer-specific collision cases, overlap, explanatory coverage, context transport and practical usability; (6) retain minority interpretations and explicit decision owner/time; (7) revise/split/merge/defeat where receipts require. Neither formal consistency nor coder consensus substitutes for empirical effect evidence.

## 8. Why sustainability? Two reciprocal questions, not a bolted-on topic

1. **Education for sustainability:** Which digital pedagogical mechanisms contribute to systems thinking, critical values reflection, futures literacy, collective action and ecological/community learning, in which circumstances and with what observed outcomes?
2. **Sustainability of digital education:** Who receives learning benefits; who bears lifecycle, labour, energy, procurement, dependency, privacy and long-term burdens; who can contest or exit; what remains sustainable over time?

The original [manuscript draft](digital-esd-integrative-review-draft.md) and [transformation matrix](../DASHI/Education/DigitalESDTransformativePrincipleMatrixExact.agda) express this dual question. The historical *decision* to choose this specific publication venue/topic still needs meeting/commit/email decision archaeology. Its current fit is not proof of its historical origin.

**EfS is a generative independent pedagogy, not just the B-side environmental filter.** See [reciprocity methods](digital-esd-efs-pedagogical-reciprocity.md): GreenComp; Vikane/Høydalsvik (Norwegian ECE, context-bounded); Sue Elliott (practitioner/theoretical); Tilbury 2002 case collection vs Tilbury/Wortman 2004 distinct framework; and new source cases on #1448.

The Tilbury collection's diverse pathways can share goals without one universal method. The existing many-to-many support, intersectional non-factorability, situated memory and evidence-braid owners already permit that distinction. *Analogical structural reuse is not study-level EfS validation.*

## 10. What the ~44k ERIC programme can and cannot establish

Repo-reported search result: **46,597 raw hits → 43,996 deduplicated ERIC metadata/abstract records**. This is **not** 43,996 full texts or 43,996 admitted studies. Last in-thread observed execution snapshot was 200 explicitly reviewed; 28 retained; 10 verified/materialised/parsed, with 18 full-text residuals. **These are historical observations, not a live database census for 1 November.** The 43,996-row SLR synthetic scale fixture is distinct from the real ERIC source set.

Canonical path: source → revision → selected full-text artifact/digest → addressable regions → parser/PNF candidates → 19 manuscript extraction coordinates (+ separately bundled 16-field study claim ceiling) → independent reviewed evidence → source audit/admission → corpus-level challenge. PostgreSQL is canonical state, JSON is bounded receipt/inspection. Human/source authority firewalls stay intact; the complete five-database review, independence adjudication and principle-survival result are outstanding.

For any claimed relationship, use a reviewer-facing receipt:
- exact source/revision/span and source role;
- context/population, pedagogical practice, actual measured outcome and time horizon;
- source claim ceiling and rival/negative interpretations;
- specific digital principle and independent EfS orientation;
- convergence, productive tension, non-equivalence or unresolved;
- reviewer decision, dissent, source-family/dependence, missingness and transfer restriction;
- current status: candidate / reviewed observation / admitted / synthesised.

No final supported/narrowed/split/merged/defeated label is paid merely by an Agda constructor or candidate source count.

### Never silently infer

Seven *source papers* in PR #341 =/= seven *principles*. Nine registry papers =/= nine independent empirical studies. Six EfS themes =/= complete EfS pedagogy. A source/case entry =/= reviewed source. Similar pathways =/= same learning mechanism. Same observed behaviour =/= same motive/agency. Collaboration email =/= publication permission. Source-written Agda =/= exact-head typecheck =/= empirical finding.

**Research honesty criterion:** a collaborator with no DASHI background should be able to reconstruct every asserted inferential step from a named source, attributed relationship and explicit unpaid obligation, and should encounter an explicit **unknown** rather than an invented bridge.
