## A rigorous obstruction to a claimed site isomorphism

Let X be the existing nine-cell grid and X° = X excluding centre.
Let Y be the tetragonal eight-site direction × height carrier.

Both have eight sites, both admit D4 actions generated by
90-degree rotation r and vertical mirror s. Nevertheless, they
are **not isomorphic as D4-sets**.

For s in X°: exactly north and south are fixed (2 sites).
Corners are permuted without fixed points.
For s in Y: north/above, south/above, north/below, south/below
are fixed (4 sites).

If an equivariant bijection f:X° → Y existed, then f would
restrict to a bijection between the fixed-point sets Fix_X°(s)
and Fix_Y(s), impossible because 2 ≠ 4.

Even more structurally, the two four-element orbits of X° are
edge and corner actions with different reflection stabilisers,
whereas Y is two copies of the edge action. Thus cardinal
equality 4+4=8 is insufficient, and an untyped site rechart
would silently erase stabiliser data.

This is compatible with **a useful shared D4 subgroup**, not
with an equivariant identity of its two site carriers. Also,
the horizontal mirror H in D4h acts as a height swap on Y;
there is no canonical corresponding height coordinate in X°.

## Correct reuse

(1) Reuse NineCell's actual D4 permutation and orbit-compression
lemmas for planar chart geometry; retain the centre/edge/corner
stabilisers. (2) Reuse the T4→T9 cover/descent for 3x3 trit-valued
configurations; lift coordinate permutations to configuration pullbacks,
with proofs that a cover mapping is equivariant *only if* local
chart labels are transported as well. (3) Keep the C3 cyclic
left/right/synthesis groupoid distinct from spatial C4; an
additional action would require a specified commuting/semidirect
compatibility law. (4) T18 is a boundary of six independent
T3 cells, not 18 sites of D4h or a crystal supercell. It may
admit compatible actions after specifying the six-cell permutation.
