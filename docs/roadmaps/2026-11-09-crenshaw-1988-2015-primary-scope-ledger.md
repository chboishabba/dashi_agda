## Crenshaw 1988 original authorial standpoint

Primary publisher PDF: https://harvardlawreview.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Crenshaw-Race-Reform-and-Retrenchment-pdf.pdf
- Printed page 1331, prefatory footnote 2, accessible parsed-text line range 26–31: Crenshaw discusses using "Black" as a named cultural category and the dilemma between detached "they/them" and first-person "we" in scholarship. This is an authorial statement about professional knowledge production, not biographical evidence that she appeared as counsel in DeGraffenreid, Moore or Payne.
- Printed page 1331, article abstract, parsed lines 11–13: Crenshaw criticizes both colourblind formalism and accounts dismissing the pragmatic value of rights; formal legal reform may coexist with material racial subordination.
