# Kitzerow BH4 priority acquisition

This note records the acquisition boundary for the Kitzerow BH4/autism evidence pass. It is an evidence/provenance note, not a clinical endorsement and not a finding of research misconduct.

## Primary Kitzerow material acquired

- Kimberly Kitzerow, *Autism & the Comorbidities Along the BH4 Pathway* (ResearchGate preprint, January 2024), DOI `10.13140/RG.2.2.23124.37761/1`.
- Kimberly Kitzerow, *The BH4 Pathway as an Allostatic Mechanism in the Pathology of Autism and Systemic Comorbidities* (ResearchGate preprint, August 2024; multiple revision/upload records are visible on ResearchGate).
- Kimberly Kitzerow, *Neurodivergent Biochemistry and the Autism and the Comorbidities Theory* (ResearchGate preprint, July 2025), DOI `10.13140/RG.2.2.30248.89606`.
- Kitzerow's public documented timeline, including linked 2023 public disclosures and a claimed 2023-11-27 Stanford Neurodiversity Project outreach.
- Kitzerow's 2025-11-15 public statement that the Japan, Brazil, Stanford and Princeton convergent findings remained **uncited** with respect to her work.

## Prior-art subtraction

The implementation does **not** treat BH4 stress/redox biology, NOS coupling/uncoupling, AAAH biochemistry, AGMO/ether-lipid biology, allostasis, cellular stress response, or E/I hypotheses as Kitzerow-originated facts. Representative pre-Kitzerow anchor: Eichwald et al., *Tetrahydrobiopterin: Beyond Its Traditional Role as a Cofactor*, *Antioxidants* 12(5):1037 (2023), DOI `10.3390/antiox12051037`.

The priority question is narrower: whether Kitzerow publicly assembled a distinctive selection, ordering, and proposed causal architecture before later works with specific structural overlap.

## Later evidence mapped in this pass

- Ojha et al., NO -> TSC2 S-nitrosylation/degradation -> mTOR dysregulation across autism models (2026, *Molecular Psychiatry*): strong bounded mechanistic support for one downstream edge; not whole-graph confirmation. The acquired Nature full text contains no `Kitzerow` match.
- Jang, Takahashi & Huguenard reticular-thalamic hyperexcitability intervention (2025, *Science Advances*): causal circuit/E-I evidence; upstream BH4 provenance remains open. The acquired PMC/full-text record contains no `Kitzerow` match.
- 2026 *Nature Neuroscience* autism connectivity-subtype work: supports heterogeneous hypo/hyper-connectivity state structure; does not uniquely identify BH4.
- Nomura et al. 2025 *Cell Genomics*: supports cell-type-specific translation/regulatory vulnerability across autism-linked genetic perturbations; stronger universal-stress wording remains an adapter claim.
- Colpani Filho et al. 2025 *Brain Sciences* BH4 systematic review, DOI `10.3390/brainsci15020151`: supports relevance of BH4/NO/neopterin literature but reviews studies from 1967-2021 and reports that its analysis was conducted in 2020-2021. The acquired article does not establish a Kitzerow citation; Kitzerow herself describes it as uncited.
- Naviaux 3-hit metabolic-signaling model (2025): substantial structural convergence, evaluated against the much older cell-danger-response lineage.
- Leisman, Melillo & Alfasi developmental neuroimmune cascade (2026): systems-model convergence, not an experimental test of BH4 centrality.
- IACC 2026 developmental-systems framing: institutional/policy convergence only.

## Contact, publication-reference, and attribution boundary

The public Kitzerow timeline states that researchers affiliated with Stanford's Neurodiversity Project contacted her on 2023-11-27 requesting information about the autism framework and links an email screenshot. In this pass that is encoded as `explicitContactClaim`, **not** `verifiedPrimaryContact`, because the linked Adobe artifact could not be fetched here and the underlying correspondence, headers/content, participant identities, and relation of those participants to the later Jang/Takahashi/Huguenard paper have not been independently verified.

This is importantly different from an article citation. The acquired Stanford and Ojha article texts do not contain a `Kitzerow` match, and Kitzerow's own November 2025 public statement says the Japan/Brazil/Stanford/Princeton studies remained uncited. The formalization therefore has a separate `PublicationReferenceStatus` channel: contact does not imply citation; absence of citation does not disprove contact; either one alone does not establish derivation or misconduct.

Likewise, Kitzerow reports a 2025 Princeton research-integrity complaint and a December 2025 institutional response reporting no evidence of wrongdoing. Both are provenance events, not proof tokens for either plagiarism or absence of scientific overlap.

## Promotion rules

The formal owners keep five coordinates independent:

1. scientific support / convergence;
2. temporal priority;
3. contact or exposure evidence;
4. publication citation/reference evidence;
5. attribution status.

No rule permits node support -> whole-model confirmation, chronology -> access, contact -> article citation, article citation -> derivation, contact -> derivation, or contact/overlap -> plagiarism. A stronger attribution result requires primary-source verification of access/use plus sufficiently specific overlap after subtracting inherited prior art.
