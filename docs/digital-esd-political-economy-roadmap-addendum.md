For every political-economy, social-provisioning and durability extraction, the consumer must still ask:

- who was sampled and who was eligible but missing;
- who was excluded by access, language, disclosure, platform, place, time or methodology;
- who was affected but unsampled;
- which intersections were unreported;
- who defined categories and interpreted evidence;
- who had decision authority;
- whose future options were affected;
- which disability/access supports, household burdens or maintenance costs were shifted;
- who benefits, bears burden, controls and can exit.

Broad equity, sample size, formal accessibility or participation count cannot pay this audit.

## Political economy / waste source tranche

Current exact source fixtures include:

- Frederick H. Buttel (2004), *The Treadmill of Production: An Appreciation, Assessment, and Agenda for Research*, DOI `10.1177/1086026604267938` — review/theory and scope-condition context;
- Dean Curran (2017), *The Treadmill of Production and the Positional Economy of Consumption*, DOI `10.1111/cars.12137` — bounded production/consumption growth mechanism context;
- Luca Correani, Patrizio Morganti, Cecilia Silvestri and Alessandro Ruggieri (2023), *Food waste, circular economy, and policy with oligopolistic retailers*, DOI `10.1016/j.jclepro.2023.137092` — theoretical oligopoly model where food waste is endogenous to retailer/consumer strategic interaction.

The implemented positive path is intentionally narrow:

```text
regime/context
+ explicit mechanism
+ specified carrier
+ measured/modelled outcome
+ source scope
-> bounded waste-pressure proposition
```

A bare `capitalismLabel` cannot construct that receipt.

## Social provisioning source tranche

Current fixtures include:

- Rebecca Duell, Danielle Villoresi, Nomxolisi Malope-Rwodzi and Danielle Gallegos (2026), *Beyond Breakfast: Exploring What Works for Schools Delivering School Breakfast Programs in Queensland, Australia*, DOI `10.1002/hpja.70193`;
- Marieke Heers, Chris Van Klaveren, Wim Groot and Henriette Maassen van den Brink (2016), *Community Schools: What We Know and What We Need to Know*, DOI `10.3102/0034654315627365`.

The formal consumer now separates instructional delivery from meals/food security, safe gathering space, peer/adult connection, health/social-service linkage, family/community connection, informal support, disability/access support, device/connectivity access, household care/supervision, travel and extracurricular/community participation.

The key theorem is:

```text
same instructional delivery surface != same social-provisioning adequacy
```

School-food/community evidence does not imply that online education universally causes hunger or social loss.

## Durability source tranche

Current fixtures include:

- Felicity I. McLure and Jill M. Aldridge (2023), *Sustaining reform implementation: a systematic literature review*, DOI `10.1080/13632434.2023.2171012` — review of 249 empirical studies; source reports school-level importance of leadership, budgeting/resourcing, continuing professional learning, data/evaluation, stakeholder engagement and staffing stability;
- OECD (2023), *Shaping Digital Education: Enabling Factors for Quality, Equity and Efficiency*, DOI `10.1787/bac4dc9f-en` — institutional source distinguishing capital and recurrent digital-education expenditure and retaining maintenance, technical support, professional development, procurement and funding design.

The durability consumer now retains stewardship, paid labour/time, recurrent budget, technical maintenance, security/update responsibility, handover, succession, professional learning, governance, dependency, monitoring, migration/export, funding/political shock resilience, decommissioning and accessibility/social-provision continuity.

Constructive boundary:

```text
successful launch != institutional durability
initial grant != recurrently funded service
technical maintainability != funded maintenance capacity
named maintainer != succession resilience
```
