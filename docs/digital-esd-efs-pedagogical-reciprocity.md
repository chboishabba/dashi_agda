# Reciprocal EfS--digital pedagogy formalisation (candidate stage)

## Why this cut exists

The seven Digital-ESD principles arose by cross-paper interpretive synthesis from an initial purposive digital-education corpus; they are not automatically derived from the full literature. Existing `DigitalESDTransformativePrincipleMatrixExact` pairs those principles with sustainability constraints and enactment conditions, but does not treat EfS's own pedagogy as an independent challenger. This cut does.

## Source roles and attribution

1. Jan H. Vikane and Torhild Erika L. Høydalsvik, *Teachers' expressed understandings concerning sustainability in the Norwegian ECE context*, *Environmental Education Research* 30(3), 450–461 (2024), published online 2023. https://doi.org/10.1080/13504622.2023.2273785 — situated early-childhood empirical evidence about teachers' understandings and pedagogy, **not** an Australian higher-education effect estimate.
2. Sue Elliott, *Education for sustainability*, Early Childhood Australia (2019). https://www.earlychildhoodaustralia.org.au/the-spoke/education-for-sustainability/ — practitioner-facing and theoretical framing of ecocentric relations, critical reflection, action and current/future children's options, **not** a causal intervention study.

Their claims belong to their authors. The proposed bridge vocabulary, correspondences, and counterfactual questions are explicitly DASHI cross-paper interpretations.

## Reciprocal method

Two independently coded pedagogical corpora are required: digital-education findings and EfS/ESD educational commitments. The seven existing digital principles are **not** treated as the final categories. EfS orientations introduced here are an initial coding vocabulary, not a claimed complete EfS taxonomy. A source-bounded candidate bridge records: digital principle, EfS orientation, attributed source, relation status, hypothesised mechanism, questions in both directions, competing reading, evidence debt, and candidate-only promotion status.

The seven initial candidate links are:
- contextual custodianship ↔ systems relationships;
- revisable feedback ↔ values and critical reflection;
- learner agency ↔ collective participation/action;
- evidence return ↔ futures and intergenerational thinking;
- plural observers ↔ ecological / more-than-human relationships;
- situated engagement ↔ transformative educator praxis;
- adaptive local support ↔ collective action (productive tension).

In particular, individual personalisation can increase accessibility **or** displace collective inquiry; the formalism does not assume one resolution.

## Science-facing falsification and missing evidence

A fully reviewed source may **narrow**, **split**, **merge**, **extend**, **support** or **defeat** any candidate. Critical cases include: simulations that do not improve systems thinking, values reflection without decision rights, personalised pathways that degrade group inquiry, teacher awareness without transformative practice, and ecological representations falsely substituted for affected observer authority.

The interpretation needs prespecified coding/alternative-theme registers, independent author review, exact source-span receipts, context-transfer tests, and eventual corpus admission before claiming effectiveness. There is no theorem here that the bridge works empirically or that the seven principles are complete.

## Second cut: EfS-first, independently nominated themes (21 October 2026)

The initial bridge was intentionally provisional but was still keyed from the seven digital principles. The second cut adds `DigitalESDEfSIndependentDerivationExact`, which **does not import the digital principle owner**. It preserves the Joint Research Centre's actual GreenComp taxonomy: four areas and all twelve named competencies, available in the [official JRC conceptual model](https://joint-research-centre.ec.europa.eu/scientific-activities/key-competences-lifelong-learning/greencomp-european-sustainability-competence-framework/greencomp-conceptual-reference-model_en) (Bianchi, Pisiotis and Cabrera Giraldez, 2022).

Six **author-defined, not uniquely source-determined** EfS-side study themes are nominated: (1) values and justice, (2) systems reasoning and problem framing, (3) futures, (4) participation and action, (5) ecological relationships, (6) educator enactment. The first four are scaffolded by GreenComp. Elliott contributes source-bounded ecological/practitioner interpretation; Vikane and Høydalsvik contribute a situated ECE empirical challenge about educator understandings and enactment. GreenComp is a competence/reference framework, not evidence of the effectiveness of any particular digital intervention.

Only **after** that EfS-side definition does `DigitalESDEfSIndependentChallengeExact` import the existing digital principles. Six explicit comparison receipts then identify: two candidate convergences (systems/context and futures/revision), three candidate tensions (values/feedback, collective agency/individual agency, educator enactment/engagement), and one unmatched EfS theme (ecological relationships). These are interpretive coding decisions to be independently reviewed, not automated discovery outcomes. The unmatched ecological theme does not automatically become an eighth digital principle. The adaptive personalisation versus collective-action tension in the earlier bridge remains independently open.

### Required empirical and co-author protocol

1. Have two or more EfS-competent researchers code EfS papers without visibility of the seven digital principle labels; record exact pages/spans and source-role limits.
2. Record competing codes, disagreement, rejected/merged themes and adjudication provenance.
3. Reveal the digital source/derivation ledger only after EfS-side decisions are frozen and compare convergence, collision, tension and unmatched obligations.
4. Add cross-context empirical evidence and enactment outcomes before promoting conceptual correspondences to effectiveness.
5. Rerun a source-admission and literature-search completeness check. The candidate six EfS themes and seven digital themes may split/merge/extend/be defeated.

An Agda structural inhabitant guarantees that the typed receipt contains its declared data and boundaries; it does **not** guarantee factual source agreement, independence of actual human reviewers, a complete literature search, or empirical validity. These owners are source-written, kernel-pending.
