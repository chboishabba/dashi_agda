# No-promotion checklist

Checked source-written boundaries:

- nodule presence does not entail active fixation;
- `nifH` identity does not entail fixation rate;
- nitrogenase identity does not entail realised catalytic/protected state;
- limiting nitrogenase stoichiometry does not entail in-vivo flux;
- fixed-N flux does not entail plant assimilation;
- plant assimilation does not entail ecosystem soil-N outcome;
- soil-N outcome does not entail restoration success or deployment authority;
- Lotus/barley receptor evidence does not become Acacia same-object mechanism;
- Azotobacter FeSII evidence does not become Acacia nodule mechanism;
- #980 water-carbon evidence does not become BNF measurement.
