## Separate Queensland offsets-register negative control

Queensland Government's [Offsets Register](https://www.qld.gov.au/environment/management/environmental/offsets/registers) explicitly says it **does not include offsets under Commonwealth legislation**, and its published data is only current to **28 August 2026** (as publicly displayed 9 Nov 2026). Thus 'no Queensland offsets-register records for this EPBC offset' is **not** evidence there is no Commonwealth-approved offset, no VDEC, no environmental obligation, or a breach. VDEC registration must be searched in the correct Queensland Vegetation Management Act / land-title registry, not inferred from this unrelated register.
