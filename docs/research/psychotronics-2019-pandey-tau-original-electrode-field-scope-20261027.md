### What original source authors actually tested

- **Molecular targets:** synthetic tau-core hexapeptides **Ac-VQIVYK-am** (PHF6) and **Ac-VQIINK-am** (PHF6*), not the 2017 diphenylalanine FF nor the 2019 Saikia Aβ16–22/Aβ1–42 system.
- **Conditions:** control **0 V/cm** versus original **AC and DC electric fields at 150 V/cm and 300 V/cm** in electrode/circuit assemblies. The original full-body Fig.1 describes a two-plate EF setup, including a full-wave rectifier for DC and AC apparatus without it; it does **not** describe 2024 Rodin or Helmholtz coils.
- **Original readouts:** ThT fluorescence kinetic profiles, DLS and other molecular assays; authors report field-exposed samples exhibiting **sixfold lower ThT fluorescence intensity versus untreated controls** under described experimental incubation conditions. Lower reporter fluorescence is an original assay observation, NOT a 6-fold health benefit, nor 60% Rodin comparison.
- **Original biological assays:** author-reported MTT cell viability readouts in SH-SY5Y, IMR-32 and PC-12 **cell-line** models on tau-peptide solutions initially treated with or without electric field. Author manuscript describes field-treated sample viability differences in the range **18–45% depending on conditions**, with the numerical table/wording needing original denominator verification before independent quantitative meta-analysis. This is not an in-vivo human clinical effect.
- **Original prospective device:** section *Future work* proposes a later experimental device, explicitly **conditional on in-vivo and clinical trials**. Proposal ≠ manufactured approved medical therapy. That article's clinical extrapolation is the authors' interpretation, not newly verified efficacy.

Original source: [RSC 2019 article](https://doi.org/10.1039/C8RA09993F). Original PMCID [PMC9060620](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9060620/).
