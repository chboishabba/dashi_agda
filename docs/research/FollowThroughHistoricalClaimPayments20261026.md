Independent research: Johl Brown (direction), OpenAI ChatGPT (AI-assisted synthesis). Original authors retain their respective original claims.

The 22 October 2026 APM episode, https://www.apmreports.org/episode/2026/10/22/sold-a-story-s2-e3-the-forgotten-experiment , reports Direct Instruction's strongest average academic results in the 1977 evaluation, and favourable cognitive and affective scores. It also reports House and colleagues' reanalysis changed some results without dislodging DI's aggregate lead; the panel rejected the inference that there was a universally best intervention because site effects differed. This is APM's historical reconstruction until the actual tables and calculations are reproduced.

House, Glass, McLean and Walker, "No Simple Answer" (1978), https://files.ascd.org/staticfiles/ascd/pdf/journals/ed_lead/el_197803_house.pdf , themselves dispute a simple overall programme victory. The publication acknowledges Ford Foundation funding; this does not decide the merits of its critique.

APM reports that officials withheld first-year data later located by journalists at the National Archives. The final evaluation was nevertheless published in 1977. Withholding some data is distinct from hiding the entire study, and neither alone proves intent or national causal effects.

APM's separate Olivia Chilkoti East St Louis report (22 October 2026), https://www.apmreports.org/story/2026/10/22/east-st-louis-project-follow-through , records seven former participants' retrospective testimony and describes school data excluded from the final national report for technical reasons. This interview evidence is meaningful but not a representative measured sample of all programme participants.

The 350,000 figure is cumulative programme data coverage as APM describes it, not the 1977 comparison's analytic denominator. The podcast calls the Texas school the subject of its *next* episode, so the original Instagram chronology must not be silently harmonised.
