### A genuinely dated *earlier* design-family original: Wong 2019 patent application

[Dennis Yat Wong, US20190269877A1, **Electromagnetic resonant frequency coil ion emitter**](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20190269877A1/en), application filed **5 March 2018**, published **5 September 2019**, status indexed as **abandoned** (legal status from patent aggregator, not a legal opinion). The original text discloses **star-like conductive poles and copper wire driven from audio files through an amplifier** to a room-scale apparatus. Figures and description distinguish area-wide and wand forms; one implementation describes sleeves disposed at *up to 14 points*, not the 24-point Starship maker architecture. The text claims rotating EM vortices, ion output, wellness and plant/insect effects: those are applicant assertions in a **published patent application**, not USPTO physical certification.

**Chronology:** Wong's 2018/19 text is *prior original published conceptual design-family comparator* to the presently marketed coils. **NO inventor-to-current-maker transfer, licensing, borrowing or matching exact winding geometry demonstrated**. It also cannot be a historically documented ancestor of Croft's **2002** quartz/Mobius Succor Punch because its priority is 2018. Likewise the published application is not proof of Rife's contested therapeutic claims; those are the applicant's background language. The example 14-point illustration is **not a 24-point same-winding demonstration**, but the application uses nonlimiting language and may claim a broader family. Neither this difference nor the common audio-amplifier architecture settles claim overlap, borrowing, licensing or whether the two businesses are related; avoid both an invented connection and a blanket patent-scope exclusion.

**Consequence:** The source search had a stronger answer than expanding Pavlita's psychotronics genealogy: explicit modern manufacturer and dated formal design-family comparator, while respecting the distinction between prior-art resemblance and documented inheritance.

## Physics and quantum discriminator audit anchored to the original devices

1. **Audio to magnetic field and moving sphere (Starship and iTorus)**: a driver waveform (I(t)) through a winding produces an ordinary field (B(x,t)) determined by actual winding geometry; a permanent magnet can experience force proportional to field gradient and torque from field alignment. Observed sphere motion could be mechanically supported vibration, magnet translation or camera artefact. Treat source waveform, measured current/voltage, vector field, motion, mechanical mounting and acoustic/vibration signatures as **jointly required to infer a causal mechanism**, not the words *quantum*, *Rodin* or *harmonic*. Existing DASHI Biot–Savart and metrology owners are reusable *conditional theory*, not same-geometry measured calibration.
2. **Low-voltage Croft/Aurumaya**: a 15 Hz square wave contains harmonics; it is an ordinary time-dependent source. Compare actual winding/circuit, field spectra and sham under matched cues. A scalar potential can be part of classical EM gauge description, but a purported independent free-standing *scalar healing wave* or thought-addressable quantum channel does not follow. Quartz piezoelectric response, if driven under suitable stress/geometry, is not a validated mind–matter effect. Quantum entanglement does not enable controllable faster-than-light communication without a classical signalling resource.
3. **Visible violet corona / Oudin**: high-voltage ionisation, electrode heating, airflow, electronic excitation and ion wind can create moving luminous structures. Visual branching is **not** evidence that a permanently coherent plasma torus has detached and become self-sustaining, or that human intention moves it. Only safely instrumented high-voltage lab work can resolve the current/power/motion budget.
4. **Static piezoelectricity and power**: static stress may polarise quartz and produce transient charge, but cannot sustain positive work into an external load indefinitely without energy input or a changing physical reservoir. Test time-resolved input/output electric and thermal energy, not pressure labels such as '12,000 PSI' (the store's unsupported number). No 'vacuum/zero-point harvesting' accepted without such a same-device energy receipt.
5. **iTorus / MAYU / health**: measured EM field, dissolved oxygen or changed pH are each their own endpoints, not an efficacy effect or 'improved cellular hydration'. The 5D-ASC is a psychometric instrument (and not 5th spatial dimension), `hoe_math` developmental vocabulary is distinct from HeartMath, and controlled PEMF outcomes do not automatically generalise across coil/sham architectures.
6. **Psychic telepathy / brain quantum / timeline**: extraordinary outcome requires an independent, blinded, prospectively registered intervention and physically bounded information path. Hardware electrical operation and consumer testimony are **not** an intermediate proof step toward psi or a quantum portal.
