## Max-cut/Pareto frontier: actual next decisions

1. **2019 ACS exact 60% comparator.** The author's original ACS publisher page [DOI 10.1021/acschemneuro.8b00490](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acschemneuro.8b00490) names free Supporting Information including MF effects on secondary aggregation; **original specific SI PDF bytes/text not acquired**, 2019 main article paywalled. The question is a strict source-support join: *Does it experimentally use Rodin AND matched Helmholtz coils, at comparable exposure, with a 60% metric and explicit denominator?* Patent WO2019012556A1 documents a proposed different AC/DC parallel-electrode device, **not** definitive proof that the unseen ACS body lacks further hardware. If full text is not accessible, mark **UNRESOLVED**, not misquotation or misconduct.
2. **1953 historic hourly original weather.** NOAA original [1953 Bangor ISD station-year archive](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/noaa/1953/726071-14606-1953.gz) exists but full bytes unacquired. NOAA 2018 ISD original PDF shows record length = **105 + variable length field**; prior incorrect synthetic assumption was explicitly retracted. New [GHCNh](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/global-historical-climatology-network-hourly) is a separately disseminated archive, **not an independent gauge**. Actual July UTC rows must be parsed with precipitation groups and 1953 local time before any 22h comparison; Bangor is not exact Ellsworth site, and onset would still not prove causation.
3. **Gentry original source.** [2003 RVML organiser/uploader](https://vimeo.com/92437258) publicly identifies Gentry Smith speaker and *promotional* biographies referencing Reich and Pat Flanagan. The 2003 lecture video source remains **unheard / no verified original transcript** in this audit, and promotion cannot establish Navy employment, author-built coil dimensions, 1999 transfer or psi effectiveness.
4. **Rodin raw provenance.** Original published 2013/2024 tables have shared rounded B values; source-identity collision under hypothetical rounding prevents inference of identical or independent measurement-session traces. Only original lab/session metadata and raw logs can settle source reuse.
5. **Hardware image identity.** When an initial screenshot has no source-native serial, drawing or trusted original clip, mark **candidate family, same-device unverified**, not a new historical root.

Stop spending Phase-1 effort on lower-value thematic analogies or unproductive general web searches once all high-value blockers are explicitly source-typed and assigned to a phase. A future P2/P3 physical design requires a different acceptance/ethics/safety gate.
