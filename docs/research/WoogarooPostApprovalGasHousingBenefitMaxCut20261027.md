### Critical original-regulator gas-network fork (current October 2026)

AEMC's [AGN Queensland Gas Distribution Network register](https://www.aemc.gov.au/energy-system/gas/gas-pipeline-register/qld-agn-queensland-gas-distribution-network) identifies **Ipswich** in the Australian Gas Networks (AGN) footprint; the distinct [Allgas register](https://www.aemc.gov.au/energy-system/gas/gas-pipeline-register/qld-allgas-gas-distribution-network) identifies Allgas Energy with **Stonepeak ownership after the March 2026 GDI sale**, operated by AGIG, covering **southern Brisbane and other areas**. The [AER's distributor guide](https://www.aer.gov.au/consumers/understanding-energy/who-your-distributor) also separates AGN (Ipswich) from Allgas (Brisbane south of river). The records are original **regional network identity facts**, not an exact-cadastral/site connection certificate.

**Material falsifier / correction to the inherited gas-asset snowball:** do **not** automatically follow the historical Energex gas alliance into the Allgas→GDI→Stonepeak asset chain. Australian Gas Networks and Allgas are distinct current owned networks even where related operating service providers are involved. Springfield borders geographic descriptors that can mislead without a pipe- or GPR-level join. Resolve the actual 2019/8575 subdivision reticulation/service address, network map and provider quotation first; only then pursue beneficial title and investment returns for that **specific physical gas asset**.

## Judicial-review and legal-critical cut (highest immediate information gain)

The older [legal priority roadmap](../../DASHI/Law/SensibLawWoogarooLegalPriorityRoadmapExact.agda) previously used the superseded **1 October 2026** decision-extension target; updated it to the **15 October actual approval notice**. The last published pre-decision extension expired **16 October 2026** rather than 1 October; approval was dated 15 October. An approval is not automatically irreversible; the proper cause, standing, time and interlocutory restraint require qualified counsel.

- **P0 — time-critical, before any broad network snowball.** Obtain the signed 15 October approval and conditions, authorised signatory/decision-maker and proof of notification to affected applicant(s). Work with environmental administrative-law counsel **immediately** on Federal Court route, standing, relevant versions/transitions of EPBC law, application deadline, and interim protection. This project needs a real lawyer-computed date, **not** an assumed universal 28-day-from-portal-publication rule. The [ADJR Act](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A01697/latest) contains different time/reasons mechanics, including sections 11 and 13, with procedural context. Public register publication, personal notification and reasons furnished are distinct time events.
- **P0 — primary decision file.** Request the complete **section 135A recommendation report** from the Secretary (subject to permitted exclusions), section 95B material and comment copies provided to the decision-maker, final PD/response materials, decision briefing, applicable conservation advice/recovery plans and site plans. Relevant EPBC 2025–26 amendments/transitional commencement must be fixed **as at the decision date**, not by today’s prospective standards.
- **P1 — ecological legal issue.** Construct same-footprint/same-date project ledger: actual January vs final PD, comment-response, cumulative Item 4.6(c) analysis, approved clearing envelope (separate 133.9 ha gross land uses versus **maximum 128 ha disturbance** as described in proponent January PD), protected matters, functional corridor/habitat, offsets (additionality/time-to-gain/performance), adjacent references 2014/7306, 2016/7676, 2017/7875, 2018/8350, 2020/8629, 2020/8651. - **P2 — first/third-order commercial receipts.** Same legal parcel's registered title, beneficial ownership, option and developer agreement, contemporaneous valuations and state infrastructure/development-contribution obligations. *Stockland proposed developer* ≠ *Cherish proponent* ≠ *registered owner* absent receipts.
- **P3 — gas induced-demand chain.** Exact modern Springview 2/3 civil/hydraulic drawings, gas reticulation design (if any), appliance schedule, individual dwelling gas choice/mandate, service registration, distributor, connection date, gas network asset owner (test **AGN Ipswich versus Allgas/Stonepeak** against actual address), relevant tariff. Stress test all-electric counterfactual; don't assume each residential lot requires gas simply because 2004 Greater Springfield planned 8,000 gas connections.
- **P4 — lobbying, donations, ministerial interest.** Original ECQ and AEC records with electoral jurisdiction and financial year; Australia/Queensland corporate identifiers; original Parliament senator declarations; lobbying diaries/client declarations and actual message/work product. 2. **Countermodel B:** project builds all-electric dwellings, no new gas connections and no gas asset transaction. Historic 2004 alliance remains true but present induced-gas hypothesis fails.
