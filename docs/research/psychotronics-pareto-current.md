The [Wellcome archived diagram](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/kgngz6kz) is a 1956 photographic carrier of the 1906 printed diagram. Distinguish creator/publication/custodian years. 2. **Oleg D. Jefimenko, *Electrostatic Motors* (1973)**, [scanned primary text](https://rexresearch1.com/Electrostatics/Electrostatic%20MotorsJefimenko.pdf), printed pp.114–117 (PDF 0-based page indices 69–70): an *author-reported* experiment drove small electret and corona motors from earth-electric-field antennas. **Jefimenko himself states larger motor utility needs an antenna extracting appreciable power**. This materially strengthens the atmospheric-drive *mechanism* branch but does not authenticate the viral **“Pignatelli motor”** name. Earlier expert or university demonstrations remain separate independent class references, not same-device measurements.

They may describe different SKU/configuration/revision, source-copy drift, impedance measured at different frequencies, or may simply be marketing errors. No operational definition of `coverage` (sensitivity threshold, amplitude, frequency, duty, distance, background floor) is supplied; hence no quantitative healing or field-radius conclusion is admissible. **Do not average 55/30/24 into a single estimate.**

### Pareto stop/branch rules

1. If no original native reel/circuit exists, **stop at the exact acquisition residual**. It is not useful to invent an electrical schematic or keep renaming it RNNN. Do not claim failure to find means the object does not exist.
2. If a source or image is **reposted**, maintain one origin unless independent acquisition/provenance establishes otherwise; a manufacturer saying a device works is primary evidence of the *marketing claim*, not primary experimental evidence for its truth.
3. A **real electrostatic atmospheric-energy mechanism**, historical Oudin resonator, medical PEMF indication, or water vortex does not entail any general scalar/timeline/remote-viewing mechanism. All those statements require separate empirically discriminating witnesses.
4. Demand *actual observed measurements* for quantitative promotion. A typed empty evidence slot, a note describing a potential future control, a citation or a `refl` regression does not itself produce a physical result.
5. Keep high-voltage procedures in appropriately qualified laboratory settings; do not instruct untrained readers to approach exposed terminals or operate these demonstrations as therapy.
