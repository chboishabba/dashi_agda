## Outcome of source investigation

These counts are *not* 21 independent confirmations or 18 separate experiments. Crosswalk preserved: earlier batch 10 stills / 2 accounts; later batch 8 stills / 6 more accounts.

There are four different, non-identical objects that superficially share a coil or ring:

The **seventh** image `1000018567.png` is a different open-loop clip with a creator reply naming an **Oudin Oscillator**. The historical resonator *family* is well documented; no reel/circuit continuity receipt shows the two are one construction.
- iPyramids **iTorus i9**, commercial torus-shaped coil with claimed PEMF field/health effects. Maker specifications are not independent spatial field measurements.
- Aurumaya **Succor Punch** quartz + winding + low-voltage, 15 Hz circuitry; creator explicitly attributes the modern design tradition to Don Croft. A name and apparent construction similarity do not establish that two coils share a measured transfer function.
- MAYU vortex glass, electrostatic-motor bench, and a fictional biopunk diagram are distinct consumers. Ordinary hydrodynamics, electrostatics, and creative fiction must retain separate source roles.

### Two newly inspected high-value primary comparisons

1. **Guilleminot, *Handbook of electricity in medicine* (1906)**, Oudin unipolar resonator diagram. The [Wellcome archived diagram](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/kgngz6kz) is a 1956 photographic carrier of the 1906 printed diagram. Distinguish creator/publication/custodian years. A historical resonator family does not identify the Bernd tower's concealed wiring.
2. **Oleg D. Jefimenko, *Electrostatic Motors* (1973)**, [scanned primary text](https://rexresearch1.com/Electrostatics/Electrostatic%20MotorsJefimenko.pdf), printed pp.114–117 (PDF 0-based page indices 69–70): an *author-reported* experiment drove small electret and corona motors from earth-electric-field antennas. **Jefimenko himself states larger motor utility needs an antenna extracting appreciable power**. This materially strengthens the atmospheric-drive *mechanism* branch but does not authenticate the viral **“Pignatelli motor”** name. Earlier expert or university demonstrations remain separate independent class references, not same-device measurements.

Additional apparatus comparators: [Oudin Coil 2010](https://electrotherapymuseum.org/2010/Oudin2010/index.htm) and [Oudin sphere topload](https://www.electrotherapymuseum.org/2008/CopperOudin/index.htm), both attributed to **Jeff Behary**, one authorial origin. ### Pareto stop/branch rules

1. If no original native reel/circuit exists, **stop at the exact acquisition residual**. It is not useful to invent an electrical schematic or keep renaming it RNNN. Do not claim failure to find means the object does not exist.
2. If a source or image is **reposted**, maintain one origin unless independent acquisition/provenance establishes otherwise; a manufacturer saying a device works is primary evidence of the *marketing claim*, not primary experimental evidence for its truth.
3. A **real electrostatic atmospheric-energy mechanism**, historical Oudin resonator, medical PEMF indication, or water vortex does not entail any general scalar/timeline/remote-viewing mechanism. All those statements require separate empirically discriminating witnesses.
4. Demand *actual observed measurements* for quantitative promotion. A typed empty evidence slot, a note describing a potential future control, a citation or a `refl` regression does not itself produce a physical result.
5. Keep high-voltage procedures in appropriately qualified laboratory settings; do not instruct untrained readers to approach exposed terminals or operate these demonstrations as therapy.
