Its source-described electrode assembly is **not** the 2024 paper's 12-sided copper **Rodin coil**, a matched Helmholtz comparator or a source-identical magnetometer. Google Patents automatic `Ceased` legal-status metadata is not a verified legal determination.
4. **Original human authorship:** the six named WIPO inventors are also original credited authors in the [Pandey et al. 2017 *Scientific Reports* article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-017-02609-z), DOI 10.1038/s41598-017-02609-z, and [2019 Saikia ACS article](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acschemneuro.8b00490), DOI 10.1021/acschemneuro.8b00490. *Three different original publications/objects* ≠ three independent human groups or a fresh second biological replication.
