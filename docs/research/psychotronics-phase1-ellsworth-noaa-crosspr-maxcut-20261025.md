## 1 — First independently instrumented historical observation

**Original custodial source:** NOAA/NCEI [Bangor International Airport, Maine, GHCN-Daily station identifier `USW00014606`](https://www.ncdc.noaa.gov/cdo-web/datasets/GHCND/stations/GHCND%3AUSW00014606/detail), at latitude 44.79791° N / longitude 68.81852° W. NOAA's current database covers 1953-01-01 onward and does not attribute the weather to Wilhelm Reich. The [original `.dly` historical station archive](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/ghcn/daily/all/USW00014606.dly) contains a line beginning `USW00014606195307PRCP` with daily original **PRCP integers in tenths of millimetre**. The [same NCEI GHCN-Daily v3.35 original README](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/ghcn/daily/readme.txt) documents fixed-width fields, 5-digit values and three quality/source/measurement flags, and cites original method authors **M. J. Menne, I. Durre, R. S. Vose, B. E. Gleason and T. G. Houston (2012)**. README, metadata and station file have one **institutional data lineage**, not three distinct weather-observing stations.

| Bangor observation-date label | Original NOAA PRCP (tenths mm) | Precipitation (mm) | Inches, rounded |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 5 July 1953 | 0 | 0.0 | 0.00 |
| **6 July 1953** | **66** | **6.6** | **0.26** |
| **7 July 1953** | **193** | **19.3** | **0.76** |
| **8 July 1953** | **46** | **4.6** | **0.18** |
| 9 July 1953 | 74 | 7.4 | 0.29 |

The three **July 6–8** Bangor daily counts sum to **305 tenths of mm = 30.5 mm**, equivalent to **1.20 inches** after decimal rounding. These are **observed regional station records in an independently maintained NOAA archive**, not one more Reich republication. They support occurrence of **rain somewhere in the broader Bangor/Ellsworth region in the period**, independently of his published story. They cannot establish **exact Ellsworth/Graham Lake rainfall**, **his claimed start at ~22:00** (daily observations and cutoff times differ), **a pre/post causal counterfactual**, or **original 1953 gauge sheets unaltered by later digitisation**. Treat source-retrieved current version and 1953 measurement vintage as separate dates. The station raw value `66` is **6.6 mm**, not 66 mm or .66 inch; flags and missing values must be honoured by a parser.

**Original evidence endpoint distinction:**

1. **Reich 1954 original self-report of Ellsworth 6 July 1953** (original later-text carrier, source [CORE OROP Ellsworth PDF pp57,59](https://www.quebecorgone.com/wilhelm_reich_books/Wilhelm-Reich_-_Cosmic-Orgone-Engineering-1954.pdf)): tubes and water cable experiment **10:45–11:52**; rain by 22:00 *claimed*; source also **explicitly acknowledges a western frontal system** and attributes its behaviour to his intervention.
2. **NOAA Bangor 1953 rain series**: independently maintained physical rain observation, correct **Maine** station, broad temporal correspondence only. Neither instrument location nor the observation window is identical to Reich's apparatus and account.
3. **Original independent historic weather chart/front/forecast**: still **unpaid**; Reich's stated Weather Bureau forecast is a quote **by Reich**, not verified from the Bureau's original forecast. The NOAA data do not by themselves adjudicate a forecast or the atmospheric front.
4. **Weather-control causal intervention**: unsupported; no documented direct causal bridge, and the World Meteorological Organization's [2025 source](https://public.wmo.int/content/wmo-statement-weather-modification) concerns specific meteorological seeding interventions, not Reich's purported orgone mechanism.

**Important negative spatial source-control:** [Extreme Weather Watch "Ellsworth Weather in 1953"](https://www.extremeweatherwatch.com/cities/ellsworth/year-1953) is explicitly **Ellsworth, Kansas** on its original page, not Ellsworth, Maine. Its July 6–7 precipitation=0 cannot be used to contradict the Maine records. This is an exceptionally clear example where a name-only source join would manufacture a false disconfirmation. Do not infer physical proximity from matching municipality text; the **station ID, state and coordinates** are necessary.

## 2 — Earlier 8 July 1953 *Ellsworth American* item, not an earlier independent rain experiment

Before the source graph's previously earliest identified named newspaper report, **Anthony R. Shannon, Bangor Daily News, 24 July 1953**, there is a source-specific **8 July 1953 *Ellsworth American*** item with an anonymous headline commonly transcribed as *Rain Monday Night Caused By Rainmakers*. An image/transcription of that newspaper is **reproduced in Reich's [*CORE* later full publication compilation](https://rexresearch1.com/ReichOrgoneLibrary/ReichCORE.pdf)**, section **'Report on OROP Ellsworth in the Ellsworth American, July 8, 1953'**, followed by the July 24 Shannon account. The full 1953 original newspaper ink/issue has **not** been verified independently and the full compiled PDF was not page-screenshotted in this search; statement derives from indexable reproduced text.

The earlier report describes Orgone Institute's operation on Monday 6 July, cloud development and rain beginning around 10 p.m. Text largely adopts the **Orgone Institute's own framing** and lists no independent observation/instrument or attributed staff reporter in the encountered reproduction. It is therefore a **different newspaper publisher**, an **earlier dated publicity carrier**, **not another rainmaking event**, nor an independent causal test. The 24 July Bangor Daily News article is a later **named byline** and includes interview-style grower reporting, though its original newsprint was not read. [Bangor Public Library](https://bangorpubliclibrary.org/digital-content/newspapers-magazines/) identifies an in-library/partner digital archive covering *Bangor Daily News* **1832–2011**; this is a concrete original issue access route, not original content obtained. The original [Reich Museum finding aid](https://wilhelmreichmuseum.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Index-of-the-Wilhelm-Reich-Archives.pdf) separately identifies 1953 clipping holdings in Orgone Institute boxes 24–25.

**Specific source-control caveat:** the [92-page Quebecorgone digital transcription](https://www.quebecorgone.com/wilhelm_reich_books/Wilhelm-Reich_-_Cosmic-Orgone-Engineering-1954.pdf) explicitly marks some documentary records *not included*. Do not silently promote a reproduction in a **different complete compilation** to an item inspected in that shorter volume, or count original newspaper and Reich's later reproduction as two reporter roots.

## 4 — Programmatic conditional acceptance / receipt hierarchy

New repo-local Python [`scripts/check_psychotronics_ellsworth_1953_sources.py`](../../scripts/check_psychotronics_ellsworth_1953_sources.py) implements the original [NOAA GHCN .dly 269-column schema](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/ghcn/daily/readme.txt), checks station `USW00014606`, year/month, flags, observed 5–9 July quantities and **305-tenths-mm** exact total, and audits CSV IDs/references/URL structure. It is **offline by default**. Modes:

```bash
python3 scripts/check_psychotronics_ellsworth_1953_sources.py --self-test
python3 scripts/check_psychotronics_ellsworth_1953_sources.py --ledger-only
# After separately acquiring original NOAA station file:
python3 scripts/check_psychotronics_ellsworth_1953_sources.py --dly /path/to/USW00014606.dly --self-test
```

Without `--dly`, result **`data_original_bytes_checked: false`**. This is intentional: the values were **web-inspected** at the official source, but a search browser excerpt must not be advertised as an executed original NCEI machine-data byte replay. A generated synthetic fixture tests the parser, **not meteorology**. Structural source/edge JS audits likewise cannot substitute for Agda kernel verification. The typed Agda atlas count now **65** and makes four no-promotion source/claim relations plus a finite copied-data arithmetic equality; those are **source-written**, kernel pending.
