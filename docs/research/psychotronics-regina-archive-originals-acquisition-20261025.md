   - **Box 2, item 32**, *Psychic Energy—Its Characteristics and Dynamics*, **1973 conference draft**, catalogued separately from the *published proceedings paper* (printed pp.6–7; PDF p.5).
   - **Box 2, item 31**, *Nature and Dynamics of Psychic Energy*, various June and Duncan Blewett **1973 drafts**; separate title/variant.
   - **Box 2, items 27–28**, Duncan Blewett *A Handbook for Psychotronic Engineering*, **1985 draft** and undated research notes. Later genre and timing, not proof of physical efficacy.
   - **Box 2, items 29–30**, Duncan & June Blewett and R. Ochshorn, *An Induction–Resonance Model of Psychic Energy Dynamics*, one dated **1980**, one undated. They are two catalogue entries, *not* two independent field measurements.

   - **Box 2, item 43**, *Handbook of Psychotronic Engineering*, updated August **2002**.
   - **Box 2, item 44**, *An Induction–Resonance Model of Psychic Energy Dynamic*, undated manuscript.

3. **US NTIS [JPRSL50221](https://ntrl.ntis.gov/NTRL/dashboard/searchResults/titleDetail/JPRSL50221.xhtml)**: **1974 JPRS English translation**, *Proceedings of the Conference on Psychotronic Research, Part I*, **225 pp**, original Czech Prague 1973 conference materials. Its catalogue currently specifies **paper copy available to US Government recipients only**. No online Part I body has been authenticated here.

An absence in Part II does not establish the original paper is lost.
