**Welz's *own* archived author text** says generator invention **1991** and resin/metal orgonite **1992**: [generator narrative](https://www.orgone.net/i_5_the_orgone_generator.html), [orgonite narrative](https://www.orgon.org/english/i_6_my_invention_of_orgonite.html). Likewise Welz's proprietary formula and claims to create new life-force energy are **author assertions**, not independently authenticated process performance. The existence of metal shavings cast in resin and a copper coil is ordinary hardware; energetic properties beyond Maxwell need distinct evidence.

**Croft's own *dated 18 May and 22 July 2002* Succor Punch texts** on [Croft Zappers](https://www.croftzappers.com/succor-punch) describe quartz with a Mobius coil and low-frequency 15 Hz zapper. ## Landscape 2 — Rodin's numerical/winding idea and its early technical self-qualification

The strongest recovered **original paper** is **Marko Rodin & Greg Volk**, '[The Rodin Number Map and Rodin Coil](https://www.naturalphilosophy.org/pdf/abstracts/abstracts_5352.pdf)', *NPA Long Beach 2010 Proceedings*, **7 pages**. Original printed **p.1 and p.7** were independently inspected as page images, and the complete extracted paper was examined. Authorial credit belongs **jointly to Rodin and Volk**; do not attribute all explanatory mathematics to one. The paper constructs modulo-nine maps, discusses winding a periodic planar pattern on a torus, presents a photo captioned 'levitating magnet above a Rodin coil', cites experimenters and nonstandard physical interpretations, and **explicitly concludes on printed p.7 that disciplined controlled quantitative tests had not been published**. A conference proceedings contribution is not independent experimental replication, and an image label does not supply a force trace. In particular, the period-six unit sequence `1→2→4→8→7→5→1` modulo 9 is valid elementary modular arithmetic; it does **not** logically entail a 3D force field, free energy or an exclusive natural topology.

## Ibrahim/snowball investigation protocol: explicit graph, no adjacency fallacy

**Seed objects:** screenshot-specific accounts and maker/product names; **first outward frontier:** author-credited original product descriptions and dated publications; **second frontier:** footnotes, patent forward/back citations, coauthor papers, contemporaneous reprints and manuscript custody. Every extracted edge is classified in [the 53-edge ledger](psychotronics-phase1-lineage-edges-20261025.csv) as:

1. **Direct document-to-document citation** (e.g., 2026 patent lists Nunez 2017 and Dennis Wong 2019; 2023 paper references Rodin/Volk 2010). This proves **bibliography inclusion**, not reading/copying/testing by an inventor.
2. **Named first-person influence or contemporary maker acknowledgement** (Croft's stated 2002 design; Aurumaya credit; named 2023 builder references). The proposition proved is *the person said they used or followed X*, not objective efficacy.
3. **Publisher/reprint/archival custody** (Adachi index, government translation, Rice/Regina fonds). Counts as **circulation and available witnesses**, never independent experiments.
4. **Technical class, nomenclature or image analogy** (Nunez nesting, Wong star, Rodin torus, GoldenEye Starship; Tesla/Oudin; Reich/Croft), always explicitly **unproved inheritance** unless a dated original transfer/citation is found.
5. **Opposing interpretation or negative evidentiary finding** (Reich Museum distinction, Rodin/Volk acknowledgement of untested physics, Cancer Research UK). A contradiction is retained **at proposition level**, not erased by dismissing one entire author.

**Cycle and common-root handling:** do not let article A quote book B that quotes article A become two independent observations. Same maker variant pages are *one manufacturer source root*. Multiple Rodin lectures/reprints share a Rodin root; Rodin-and-Volk's coauthored 2010 paper deserves dual credit, not a Volk-less alias. Croft May/July/anthology share a Croft origin, while Adachi's editorial intervention is **new transmission evidence** but not new psychic data. When a name is ambiguous, keep distinct person nodes.

**No guilt-by-network:** historical CIA/DoD papers and Soviet/Nazi programmes have real archive traces, but a shared vocabulary/person/event adjacency is not a secret common technology, a missing-scientist programme or evidence of unlawful conduct. Keep user-established attribution and operational-discriminator rules intact.
