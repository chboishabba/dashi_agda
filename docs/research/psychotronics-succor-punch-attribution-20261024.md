### Pareto / Ibrahim frontier, reprioritised by consumer-relevant residual reduction

1. **Same-object visual acquisition:** Obtain *original reels* for each account, original audio and successive frames showing electrodes/rotor. For the "floating spinning orb", test a static high-voltage tip, a truly rotating electrode, ionic-wind rotor, and camera-exposure aliasing. This is the highest discriminating next acquisition: the stills cannot establish genuine mechanical or plasma rotation.
2. **Source genealogy of the builders:** Extract creator-specific credits, descriptions, kits, suppliers, tagged accounts, and reverse-citations. Determine whether a post names Croft, Succor Punch, Welz, Rodin, Merkaba, Rife, Lakhovsky or another builder. Require a *direct, dated reference* before promoting historical influence.
3. **Signal/field separation:** Identify the actual high-voltage driver, coil configuration, and whether 7.83 Hz, 15 Hz or 333 Hz refers to modulation, sound output, fundamental driving frequency or a claimed biological response. No `7.83 Hz → 333 Hz` conversion is paid by screenshot text; require time-series/spectrum, calibration and control. Signal generation ≠ effect on health/reality.
4. **Source-object custody:** Verify original 2002 educate-yourself pages against contemporaneous archive snapshots; distinguish authored date, publication date, later edits, contemporary copying and anthology. Croft's authored chronology can be retained while server custody remains unresolved.
5. **Effects falsifier:** An independent blinded experiment with sham/signal-on conditions, matched geometry, quantified power/EM exposure, fluorescence/corona thermal artefacts, predeclared outcome, and independent replication. Do not perform unsafe high-voltage work without appropriate laboratory supervision. No psychological or medical effect is supported merely by observing electrical activity.
6. **Lower-alpha metadata:** Verified same-object Wikidata/Dewey/DOI coordinates only when useful for an already identified source, not keyword expansion or concept conflation.

Recompute the Pareto frontier after each acquisition. Priority is about possible residual reduction and discriminating power, never source authority. Ibrahim traversal supplies candidates and topic coverage; no traversed link becomes proof, causation, independent corroboration, or an historical first-link edge merely by adjacency.
