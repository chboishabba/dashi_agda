## Critical statutory/policy firewall

The [2012 Environmental Offsets Policy principles](https://www.nationalepa.gov.au/epbc/approvals/offsets/guidance/offset-policy-principles) specifically require a defensible conservation outcome for the same *attribute*, usually at least 90% direct, additionality, scientifically robust/timely outcomes and transparent, enforceable monitoring. It is not satisfied by four-species OAG numerical output alone. The exceptions and assessment discretion still need actual policy/approval evidence.

**Do not wrongly apply the 2026 standard to this approval.** [National Environmental Standard (Environmental Offsets) 2026, F2026L01093](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2026L01093/asmade/details), registered **21 August**, commenced **22 August 2026** as an instrument; [DCCEEW reform guidance](https://www.dcceew.gov.au/environment/epbc/epbc-act-reform/stronger-environmental-protection-restoration) explicitly says new Standards **do not apply to assessments/approval decisions until the new approval tests commence (on or before 1 December 2026)**. The **16 October 2026** decision therefore cannot be called contrary to the 2026 standard merely because the instrument's registration predates it. Verify any saved/transitional law separately. Its express 8 principles can inform a prospective comparator, **not a retroactive legal violation**.

### Why D01–D05 are more decisive than one offending number

A perfect 56-row OAG output might satisfy an *internal* arithmetic relation while failing to measure the attributes QUT and QCC identify: existing corridor continuity, a particular local resident population, mature structurally complex forest and shaded heat refugia. The 2012 policy specifically demands conservation benefit for the impacted protected attribute, not just a spreadsheet percentage. That is a falsifiable question; not an automatic conclusion that no offset anywhere could ever work.
