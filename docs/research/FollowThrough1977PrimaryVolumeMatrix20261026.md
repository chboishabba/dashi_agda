## Newly resolved source-grain issue
APM's October 22 2026 historical reporting describes 22 programmes selected/piloted across the original programme. The original 1977 volume IV-B/IV-C bibliography describes **17 models represented in that particular national analysis**. Distinct collection definitions, not automatically a contradiction. Do not use 22 as the 1977 denominator without checking inclusion/exclusion histories.
