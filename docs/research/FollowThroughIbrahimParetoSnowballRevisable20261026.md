## Pareto + snowball policy
For a given source action, compute (1) exact missing evidence objects paid, (2) exact claims reopened, and (3) three explicitly declared nonnegative ordinal costs (effort/access/interpretation). An action dominates only if it covers **at least** the same missing evidence objects AND claims with no greater costs and at least one strict improvement. Since these targets are different objects, original Abt, House, St. Pierre, follow-up, and original policy records can all remain incomparable; a cheap unrelated document must never crowd out the decisive Abt tables. This is a **conditional planning frontier**, not a prediction that documents exist or will be obtainable.

**Snowball:** primary Abt IV-A/B/C numerical page → original cohort/site/model mapping → House's exact exclusions/reanalysis → St. Pierre's reliability/uncorrected alternatives → sensitivity of domain rankings. A second provenance-independent trail is date-stamped federal policy allocations and internal publication/dissemination decisions; these are not children of a positive classroom result. Each newly acquired object reopens only dependent claims, without rewriting source roots, historic authorship, or passed certificates. Prior admissible claims remain source-scoped rather than superseded as false.

**Stop condition:** until a genuinely new original numerical table or primary dated policy decision is acquired, extra secondary summaries or generic observer countermodels have low marginal value. Do not fabricate rankings, interview prevalence, cover-up intention, or learning-decline effect.
