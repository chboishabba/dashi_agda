## Why this changes the inquiry

The testable object is **sponsor → dated commissioned work / advocacy → recipient and identified bill/policy version → enacted measure → measured outcome**, not similarity of names or policy slogans. Earlier documentation already found Rinehart's 22 November 2021 speech, separate HPPL/IPA 2022 lobbying, the November 2022 limited Act, One Nation's 15 January 2025 unlimited-work pledge, and Hanson's 2026 first-person attribution to Rinehart.

**New temporal discriminator:** The Coalition's **Social Services Legislation Amendment (Workforce Incentive) Bill 2022** was introduced on **10 February 2022** and lapsed upon the dissolution preceding the 21 May election. HPPL/IPA submissions to a distinct September 2022 Senate inquiry therefore **cannot have originated that earlier February legislative text**. That exclusion does not rule out Rinehart's earlier November 2021 public advocacy, independent contemporaneous advocacy, later lobbying, influence on particular amendments, or a private discussion whose original dated record has not been located.

The later Albanese-government **Social Services and Other Legislation Amendment (Workforce Incentive) Bill 2022** was introduced on **28 September 2022**, passed on **22 November** and assented to on **23 November 2022** as **Act No. 55 of 2022**. It re-used much of the prior bill's Schedules 1–2 and added a separate temporary $4,000 Work Bonus provision. The 2022 limited temporary mechanism is not the 2025 One Nation unlimited earnings proposal; no observed employment/price change or private commissioning inference follows automatically.

**Phase D — optional outcome test:** recover government policy evaluation, work participation series, amendment-origin ministerial briefs, or commission/creative-approval records tied to the *same* policy version. An outcome trend alone does not identify one sponsor's causal effect.

**Separate US-side highest-value originals:** FTC v Bannon **1:20-mc-00111-CRC** final procedural docket (the FTC public site ends at 18 December 2020), DEA/OCDETF **NY-NYS-0829** authenticated final disposition, and the Black 2016 original transaction classification. New evidence must be severe in both directions. This preserves the 2026 source-world ledger's independent roots and deliberately avoids importing the unmerged #1251/#1268 frameworks.
