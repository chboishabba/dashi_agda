## 9 November further original-source result — shared management benefits versus new offset credit

The original [One Environment *Offset Area Annual Report – Year 2* embedded in Providence EPBC 2018/8347's 7 March 2025 ACR, printed pages 10–15](https://www.stockland.com.au/globalassets/residential/qld/providence/11487-providence-east-epbc-acr-year-2-07.03.2025_opt.pdf) gives a **stronger management additionality test without requiring full GIS reconstruction**:

- Page 10: the original manager says **perimeter fencing upgrades occurred on an adjoining offset and also benefited the 2018/8347 offset**. This directly substantiates *cross-offset shared operational benefits*, but does not prove that the 2019/8575 proposed new Avonvale area is the adjoining one or that any benefit was double-booked.
- Pages 11–12: original manager says the wildfire plan used **50-metre firebreaks** and coordinated low-intensity/cool burns with neighbouring property owners in **2024**. These are cross-property operational coordination claims, not a legally established 2019/8575 habitat gain.
- Pages 13–15: original manager reports **96 hectares planted in May 2023**, with trees **one to two metres tall** at reporting. These numbers describe actual *reported* historical works for **2018/8347**; planted trees are not independent survival, canopy maturity, species occupancy or twenty-year offset outcomes.

**Decision rule:** a common landholding and shared property-wide fence/fire infrastructure may generate non-exclusive ecological benefits without necessarily violating additionality. The actual test is a management-action **baseline and cost/outcome allocation ledger**: which dated tasks were already required/funded under 2018/8347 (and 2015/7530, 2015/7609, 2021/8975), what new acts were added specifically for the **2019/8575** 168ha, whether the same benefits were counted toward both approvals, and the independent result under no new 2019 action. A supplier's own Year-2 report cannot replace independent field checking. Original owner \`SensibLawWoogarooAvonvalePriorDeclaration20261109Exact\` now encodes separate prior-project management claims and explicit **no proven re-credit**.
