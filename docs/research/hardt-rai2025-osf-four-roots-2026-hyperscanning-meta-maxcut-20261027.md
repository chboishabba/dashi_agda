The original authors conducted a synthesis of **54 unique prior studies**, not a new 54-study experimental cohort. **Condition contrasts use Hedges' g:** core *k=14*, **g=0.887**, CI [0.533,1.241]; expanded *k=36*, g=0.691. **External outcome associations use Fisher's z:** core *k=6*, **z=0.456** (r approximately 0.426), expanded *k=30*, z=0.474 (r approx 0.441), restrictive *k=26*, z=0.418 (r approx 0.395).
