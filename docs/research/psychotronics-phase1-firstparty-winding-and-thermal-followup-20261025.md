## New original contemporary research object: thermal confounding recognised by the Rodin group

[**International Conference on Magnetism 2024**, **1 July 2024**, session/poster 2627](https://www.mitcongressi.it/abstracts-new/inc/make_pdf_abstract.php?IDabstract=2627), titled *Thermal characterization on vortex magnetic stimulation devices for biological systems*, by original authors **Teodoro Córdova-Fraga, Ángel David Ramírez-Galindo, Huetzin Aaron Pérez-Olivas and Gustavo Basurto-Islas** (P1-43). The original conference organiser's indexed PDF abstract text was read in search results; **direct PDF fetching was disabled and screenshot inspection was unsuccessful; neither poster nor completed full paper/thermal traces acquired**. The abstract discusses **Rodin versus Helmholtz** device design/characterisation and explicitly identifies **temperature reduction and physiological control** as necessary for interpreting in-vitro magnetic-stimulation effects.

This conference abstract follows the same investigators' 2024 *Revista Mexicana de Física* original ([Ramírez-Galindo et al., DOI 10.31349/RevMexFis.70.030901](https://rmf.smf.mx/ojs/index.php/rmf/article/view/6973), P1-37) that cites ref24 ([Saikia et al., *ACS Chemical Neuroscience* 2019](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acschemneuro.8b00490), P1-40) for a purported '**up to 60%**' Rodin-versus-Helmholtz effect. Accessible Saikia original abstract concerns **electric and magnetic field exposure of amyloid peptides**, with **15–38% less toxicity** for electric-field-treated samples in selected cultured-cell assays and **no explicitly named Rodin/Helmholtz 60% matched comparison**. The **full Saikia paper is not open/read**, though its publisher lists freely accessible **supporting information**. Consequently:

1. The 2024 RMF statement is an **author claim** with **specific original reference 24**.
2. The available Saikia abstract is **not a corroborating same-geometry comparison** and cannot independently support the 60% number.
3. The July 2024 **same-Guanajuato-team** conference abstract shows the subject of **thermal-controlled Rodin/Helmholtz comparison was actively recognised**, but does **not** publish paired effect sizes, controls, temperature curves or a 60% confirmed measurement.
4. **Do not turn a possible citation mismatch into an allegation of deliberate miscitation or fabrication.** The complete full-text, supplement, source author clarification and actual poster/methods are the acceptance objects.
5. 2013 Pérez et al. and 2024 Ramírez-Galindo et al. reported **three exactly matching rounded magnetic-field/current pairs**; [source-independent-measurement audit](psychotronics-phase1-rodin-2013-2024-magnetometry-provenance-audit-20261025.md) E54 still classifies reuse as **unresolved**, not fraudulent or independently replicated. No clinical evidence or special scalar field follows.

**Physical interpretation:** temperature, sample fluid chemistry, actual magnetic dose, induced electric field, field gradients, sham conditions and coil geometry are distinct causal variables. [Biot–Savart](psychotronics-biot-savart-prior-art-weld-20261025.md), already owned in repo, models ordinary current-generated magnetic field; a vector `B=∇×A` is not a measured **gradient tensor** and is not a biological-treatment causal coefficient. 2013–2024 multiple articles share named authors; conference poster and journal paper add topic/document evidence, **not independent institutions or controlled clinical replication**.
