## Strongest *genuine operational nonlocal* steelman: no-signalling boundary

For arbitrary bipartite quantum state `ρ_AB` and a local completely positive trace-preserving operation with Kraus operators `K_a` on A:

```text
ρ'_B = Tr_A Σ_a (K_a ⊗ I) ρ_AB (K_a† ⊗ I)
     = Tr_A ρ_AB = ρ_B     when Σ_a K_a†K_a=I .
```

Thus ordinary entanglement alone cannot produce *controllable remote outcome changes*. Conditional states can differ on a **postselected** Alice outcome, but the label is available to Bob only via subsequent ordinary communication. Bell violations demonstrate restrictions on classical hidden-variable accounts, not a remote-control dial.

A demonstrated arbitrary-distance, intervention-controlled, hidden-channel-free transfer would be **new physics**, not an application of ordinary entanglement. This is an operational theorem of standard quantum mechanics, not a theorem ruling out every imaginable alternative theory. Quantum-reference note: [no-signalling formulation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8299552/).
