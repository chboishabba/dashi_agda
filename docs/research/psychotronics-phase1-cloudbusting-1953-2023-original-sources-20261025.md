## 2. Highest-Pareto recovered primary source: Reich's **own** July 1953 chronological weather log published 1954

[**Wilhelm Reich, *CORE: Cosmic Orgone Engineering / OROP Desert*, Part 1, Publications of the Orgone Institute vol VI nos 1–4, July 1954**](https://www.quebecorgone.com/wilhelm_reich_books/Wilhelm-Reich_-_Cosmic-Orgone-Engineering-1954.pdf) is an author-attributed historical publication in a **later 92-page PDF transcription/carrier**, which explicitly marks some omitted appendices and figures. **Its PDF pp57 and 59 were viewed as page images**; this is source-owned text, **not** access to untouched original 1954 printing, independent cloud measurements, complete underlying data, or publisher-neutral replication.

Reich's own printed chapter *OROP Ellsworth* and his **protocol reportedly written 8 July 1953** give exact event claims:

- **5 July 1953**, Orgonon preparatory operation **10:45–11:45**.
- **6 July 1953**, Graham Lake Dam near Ellsworth, Maine, **10:45–11:52**, one apparatus, **10 fully extended tubes and two water cables**.
- Author's narrative says cloud changes, shifting wind, a coastal fog front and western weather front; **rain in Ellsworth reported at ~22:00** on 6 July; later light/heavy rain through morning 7 July.
- The original claimant directly acknowledges that an ordinary weather front arrived **from the west**, from the Great Lakes direction, and interprets it as the *effect* of his earlier intervention. An **arriving weather front is a possible natural explanation**, not an invented modern rebuttal. Reich's inference that the device "engineered" the front is **his own interpretation** and is not licensed merely by temporal sequence.
- His quoted weather forecast was reportedly dry, but no original independent local Weather Bureau forecast, station log/time series, raw maps, intervention pre-registration, comparison region or independent instrument record was inspected here. The statement about forecast being contradicted is **Reich's attributed report**, not independently demonstrated meteorological impossibility.
- The digitised edition contains extraordinary claims about state attention/secret interference (e.g. military aircraft). These are **Reich's own allegations**; no automatic inference of state involvement, organised suppression or secret deployment.

A named contemporary news carrier exists: **Anthony R. The original newsprint issue **was not independently read page-by-page**; the **Reich Museum original finding aid** explicitly locates copies in **Orgone Institute Box 25**. ## 5. Physical and historical proof boundaries without diverting Phase 1 into experiments

The [World Meteorological Organization's **14 June 2025** original institutional *Statement on Weather Modification*](https://public.wmo.int/content/wmo-statement-weather-modification) (scope information through June 2024) treats **specific physical cloud seeding** and **local fog dispersal** as legitimate weather-modification research with limited evidence in particular regimes; it explicitly warns that the energy needed for large weather phenomena rules out casual creation of rain-bearing systems and large-scale alteration. This is a more precise scientific comparator than saying all weather modification is impossible. **It does not itself test the Reich device, Croft modern device or a mind-only claim**. The causal inference is narrower: none of the provided documents produces a scientifically warranted **instrument→atmospheric field→rain** or **intention→cloud dissolution** chain.

Cloud formation/dissipation is influenced by water vapour saturation, heating/cooling, turbulence, atmospheric advection, mixing and condensation/evaporation. A targeted cloud vanishing while nearby clouds remain is **not in itself** a demonstration of intention acting over distance. Reddit before/after selection and Reich's observed temporal sequence each lack an independently established counterfactual causal explanation; this is a literature-appraisal conclusion, **not** a demand to perform new bench or human trials as a Phase-1 requirement. Keep conventional meteorology, purely electromagnetic field observations, psychokinesis, popular "quantum observer effect", orgone energy and aesthetic fiction in **separate proposition classes**. Quantum measurement effects in microscopic systems are **not** evidence human attention controls the sky.
