**Distinct earlier bibliographic coordinate for Kitaygorodsky, separate from his now-recovered 1974 author text.** The [declassified sixteen-page bibliographic scan](https://documents2.theblackvault.com/documents/cia/stargate/STARGATE%20%231%20173/Part0001/CIA-RDP96-00787R000400010012-6.pdf), commissioned for a **6 June 1978** FBIS translation, identifies compilers **L. V. Vilenskaya and V. M. Ivanova** and on **PDF index 4, section II item 5**, explicitly lists **A. I. Kitaygorodskiy, “Tricks are Tricks, But Science...”**, *Sovetskaya Kultura*, **14 September 1973**. This is an exact **contemporary publication bibliographic coordinate** conveyed via a later translated official source, not an inspected 1973 newspaper text. **We therefore cannot yet tell whether this 1973 article describes Pavlita, independent experimental work, telepathy in general or some other tricks**, and cannot equate it with the **now-authenticated 1974 *Vesmír* → *Nauka i Zhizn* same-author essay** without its original content. The apparent 1973/1974 chronology now becomes a concrete original-paper acquisition problem, not an invitation to select one convenient account.

**Two already-existing negative controls inside the original 1975 Maire–LaMothe US report can be paired.** We visually inspected the original scanned [printed p.34 / PDF index 35](https://www.theblackvault.com/documents/parapsychology/sovczech.pdf), where the authors report that Rejdák had called water-purification and plant experiments **“ineffective” in 1972**, in the very paragraph that relays alleged positive outcomes. On **printed p.58 / PDF index 57**, the same analysts state that *effects on suspended magnets were reportedly reduced by electrostatic shielding*, contrasting their account of Soviet subjects Kulagina/Vinogradova. The reported shielding contrast is an **analyst's representation of prior work**, with original experimental apparatus and source details still unpaid. The writers speculated that different kinds or alterations of a biological energy might explain the contrast, but *that theoretical inference is theirs and not warranted by the observed description*. The conventional alternatives are familiar: electrostatic forces, electrical charging/grounding, air movement, capacitance, humidity, shielding geometry, mechanical motion and camera or instrumentation behaviour.

**Water-specific inference:** An object affected through or in water, an electrically induced water-chemistry change, and water directly exposed to atmospheric-pressure plasma are three **different same-object hypotheses**. Historical sources do not currently supply Pavlita's discharge, water aliquot, plasma feed gas, measured nitrate/nitrite/peroxide, baseline/final chemistry, or controls. Modern DASHI PAW studies already specify many of those receipts; until a matching historical reactor and chemical trace appear, the appropriate conclusion is **unknown historical chemistry**, not evidence of plasma, negative evidence against plasma, or validation of stored psychotronic energy.

**Revised original-source priorities:** (1) the exact **Pavlita/Pavlitová-Zapadlová 1973 Prague Part I p.64 experiment body** and plant/water control notes; (2) **Kitaygorodskiy, Sovetskaya Kultura, 14 September 1973**, then the recovered 1974 Russian author text and original Czech *Vesmír* printing; (3) the 1972 StB/Soviet evaluation and Rejdák plant/water statement with original tests; (4) original individual **UNESCO 1974 articles** where they contribute controls or definitions. More abstract formalisation and hypothesis-generation is **lower Pareto value** than these named, dated primary sources.

## Executive synthesis

The central question is not whether a secret Nazi technology was directly inherited by modern copper-coil sellers. It is how **several distinct families of electrical engineering, physiology, disputed psychical experimentation, spiritual interpretation, government attention and commercial wellness** converged on the rhetoric of invisible energy, resonances and constructed devices.

**Six conclusions emerge.**

1. **Multiple origins, not one hidden science.** German dowsing/earth radiation and historical electric therapies; Russian suggestion/mental influence; French psychotronique terminology; Czechoslovak psychotronics; Western public reportage and counterculture; later radionics/orgonite and modern PEMF marketing are not the same source or scientific proposition.
2. **The word is older than the Czech institute.** Souček (2020) and the French Institut Métapsychique International identify engineer **Fernand Clerc** proposing psychotronique in **November 1954**, discussed by **Toute la Radio no.192, 12 January 1955**. Rejdák adopted and institutionalised the terminology through the late 1960s and Prague conference of 1973. The 1954/55 actual printed pages have not been acquired; the Institute article retrospectively quotes them.
3. **Pavlita is a contested experiment, not just a legend.** Ostrander–Schroeder (1970), Krippner (1977) and Maire–LaMothe (1975) described or repeated extraordinary generator claims. A rival, critic-authored historical tradition (Kholodnyi 2017, reported by Kuchinov 2021) recounts a **1972 Soviet–Czechoslovak examination by Alexander Kitaygorodsky and Yuri Azarov**, with purported reproductions of effects through familiar forces (the accessible 1974 author text specifies iron contamination, hand-generated airflow and thermal convection). Original 1972 logs and the original Czech edition/experimental records remain unpaid; later narration alone is not an independently performed replication by this review.
4. **Real documentary transfers exist.** The 1970 public book is cited in the original September 1975 US report, selected bibliography **printed p.69 item 5**. Cultural historian Stephen Canner (2021) reports Max Toth's reading of the book, visit to Karel Drbal and connection to the 1973 conference/1974 pyramid-power market. The second route depends on recollections and secondary reconstruction, not an inspected 1970 contract or diary.
5. **The physical counter-literature matters.** Kirlian corona photographs and Oudin/Tesla discharges are real electrical effects. The original Pehek–Kyler–Faust Science paper (1976) attributes most observed image differences to moisture and circuit effects; Mills (2009) supports the ordinary high-voltage mechanism. Jefimenko documents actual electrostatic motor engineering. None entails a new psychic energy, human mind control or a fifth physical dimension.
6. **Positive psi claims must be reported, not collapsed.** Tressoldi–Storm (2024) meta-analyse Ganzfeld data and report a small positive aggregate effect around 0.08; Mumford–Rose–Goslin's final 29 September 1995 AIR evaluation says remote viewing had no demonstrated operational intelligence value. These are *different populations, tasks, outcomes and evidence questions*. Neither result tests Pavlita generators or modern toroidal healing devices.

**Scope restriction:** original experiments, authors' reported observation, state-authorised inquiry, independent critique, modern seller copy and present-day psychological theory have fundamentally different proof ceilings. No source count equals an independent-replication count.

## Literature-review questions and approach

R1 — Which original authors, dates and technical apparatus underlie a claim, and what was the actual evidence available to them?

R2 — Which *dated* citations, letters, translations, conference exchanges, visits and patents establish influence? Which are independent observations and which repeat the same root?

R3 — What ordinary electrical, optical, fluid, physiological or psychological mechanism could produce the observations without novel psychic causation?

R4 — Which studies directly test the extraordinary physical or biological claims, including favourable findings, null studies, competing explanations and methodological weaknesses?

R5 — How did politics, military interest, disciplinary ambitions, counterculture and contemporary commercialization redistribute and change the claims?

The method is an **attributed critical/scoping review**, with original author/source-role classification, named archive custody, source-root independence, version/dating, explicit limits of inspection, an opposing-interpretations matrix and a consumer-specific Pareto. It reuses DASHI's existing AttributedSourceCore, SnowballAttributionProvenanceInvariant, SnowballOSINTAcquisitionInvariant, EmpiricalSourceDiligenceAdmission, Ibrahim investigative-claim/source-lineage and source-genealogy/evidence-synthesis modules. It does **not** purport to have screened every indexed paper or pooled subject-level data.

## 1. Intellectual and political prehistories

### 1.1 German dowsing and electrical-medical traditions

The SS-associated Ahnenerbe's genuine dowsing archive is narrower and more instructive than the later Nazi Vril-machine myth. [Josef Wimmer's 30 October 1940 assessment](https://digitalcommons.ursinus.edu/dowsing/25/) considered confiscated pendulum/dowsing publications and Ahnenerbe oversight. [Wolfram Sievers's letter of 20 November 1940](https://digitalcommons.ursinus.edu/dowsing/13/) referred to Wimmer's assessment. Yet [Wimmer's June 1941 account](https://digitalcommons.ursinus.edu/dowsing/2/) reports a police search and confiscation of his dowsing materials and magnetic theodolite. The archive documents **contested administrative interest, censorship and coercion**, not a uniform Nazi policy of sponsoring successful paranormal physics. There is **no original Nazi-to-Soviet or Nazi-to-Croft engineering transfer document in our inspected corpus**.

Wilhelm Reich's later orgone devices form a different anti-fascist biography and theory, not an Ahnenerbe device. Tesla, Oudin and Lakhovsky represent actual high-voltage or resonant electrical apparatus with their own inventors and specifications; the validity of a circuit is distinct from any therapeutic claim attached to it. The [Wellcome Guilleminot 1906 Oudin circuit](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/kgngz6kz) is useful here because exact winding topology—not copper or purple light alone—specifies an engineering family.

### 1.2 Soviet suggestion, physiological investigation and contested mind–matter experiments

[Leonid Vasiliev's Experiments in Mental Suggestion](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/ygf3wazt) exists as a **1962 Russian original / 1963 authorised English edition**. Its specific shielding, distance, control and inference claims still require primary page-level inspection; catalogue existence is not an experiment receipt. Later Soviet investigators combined physiological, electrical and psi interpretations in ways that cannot be reduced to one intelligence programme. Souček (2020) describes ideological constraints and a strategy of framing contested psychic phenomena in materialist or medically applicable terms. Some reported military uses were aspirational or speculative.

### 1.3 The name and organised Czech field

The [Institut Métapsychique International history](https://www.metapsychique.org/la-parapsychologie-hier-aujourdhui-demain/) retrospectively quotes Fernand Clerc's term psychotronique from **1954/55** and gives a **1967 Prague coordinating group** and **June 1973 international congress**. Ivan Souček's [2020 study of medical pluralism](https://doi.org/10.21104/CL.2020.1.03), pp.57–61, discusses Rejdák, the growth of research institutions, later international congresses, clinics and the materialist search for a new explanatory mechanism. Rejdák is a documented programme organiser and terminological transmitter; he must not be retroactively credited with Clerc's original term or every generator built by Pavlita.

The [1973 Prague conference Part I](https://ntrl.ntis.gov/NTRL/dashboard/searchResults/titleDetail/JPRSL50221.xhtml), translated for JPRS in 1974, gives a useful cross-sectional sample: biological electrodynamics, central-nervous-system magnetic-field effects, high-frequency photography, induced altered states, charges and photographic illumination, alleged contactless motion and neutrino-based ESP. The [Part II digitisation](https://archive.org/details/JPRSL502226September1974ProceedingsOfTheFirstConferenceOnPsychotronicResearchPartII) is discoverable. **Neither conference metadata nor its later English translation validates the papers' hypotheses**, and original Czech paper attribution must survive the translation.

## 2. Pavlita as the high-information dispute

The **claim-supporting tradition**: Ostrander and Schroeder's 1970 book described small metal devices, allegedly charged by humans, influencing motion or organisms. Stanley Krippner's 1977 first-person [chapter, Future Science pp.420–430](https://stanleykrippner.weebly.com/1975-1979.html), reports a later encounter; this is **a different person's testimony**, not a replicate performed under the same blinded controls. The original [Maire–LaMothe US defence-intelligence analysis](https://www.theblackvault.com/documents/parapsychology/sovczech.pdf) relays claims about Pavlita and notes serious information gaps, including the absence of construction details and unanswered questions about the promised patents. Its appearance in an intelligence file validates **institutional attention and an analytic text**, not the biological power attributed to the hardware.

The **competing physical explanation tradition**: [Eugene Kuchinov's historical essay](https://sreda.v-a-c.org/en/read-01), citing **Yuri Kholodnyi, History of Psychotronic Weapons (2017), pp.144–150**, describes a **1972** Soviet investigation. On this account, **Alexander Kitaygorodsky and Yuri Azarov** examined Pavlita demonstrations, filmed devices, and attempted conventional re-creations of rotating objects and insect effects. The account says they explained demonstrations through conventional physical effects. In the separately accessible author-original 1974 critique, the three specifically described alternatives are **magnetic contamination, hand-generated airflow and thermal convection**, not a single electrostatic force. The Russian 2017 text is indexed and excerpted, but [its original PDF locator](https://klnran.ru/wp-content/uploads/2017/04/BVZN_19.pdf) returned an error on direct open. The **same-author 1974 Russian *Nauka i Zhizn* full text**, explicitly introduced as a republication of Czech *Vesmír* no.2, is now inspected; obtain original Czech pages for edition-level comparison.

**Review assessment:** This is a genuine **opposing report** worth prioritising. It is **not** equivalent to our independently auditing the 1972 controls, recording traces or confirming the account's "most of 30 experiments" assertion. Nor should the original 1970/1975 claims be promoted simply because a physicist or government analyst discussed them. Acquire original Azarov/Kholodnyi and Kitaygorodsky sources, apparatus drawings, prior controls and witness notes, then compare claim by claim. Note that the 1972 claimed investigation and the *different* 1985 Czech ministry cooperation enquiries are not one event.

The 1975 report's **direct** citation of the 1970 public book, selected bibliography p.69 #5, shows why bibliographic provenance matters: a widely repeated claim can move from a popular book to an intelligence analysis without becoming an independent experimental demonstration. Conversely a competing reported re-creation can be omitted, missed or unavailable; the record has not established what the 1975 authors knew, so **no intentional suppression is inferred**.

## 3. Western diffusion, counterculture and later commercial uptake

One edge is directly inspected: **Ostrander–Schroeder 1970 → Maire–LaMothe 1975** printed reference. Another is historian-mediated: [Stephen Canner's 2021 investigation](https://mediatedsignals.com/2021/04/26/the-comet-the-typist-and-the-psychotronic-think-tank-a-tale-of-the-new-age/) says **Max Toth** read the 1970 book, visited Czechoslovakia, met Karel Drbal and Rejdák, helped link Western speakers to the **1973 conference** and later promoted **Pyramid Power** (1974). Canner used recollections and related archival material, but this review has not acquired the original Toth diary, agreement or dated correspondence: call it **reported social transmission**, not an inspected contemporaneous letter.

Krippner, Kirlian-aura communities, borderland-science publications and later Tom Bearden references demonstrate later Western circulation. A 1979 Borderland/Bearden discussion explicitly **cites Krippner's 1977 original chapter**; that is a **copied-source lineage**, not independent hardware verification. The same applies to 1970 Ostrander/Schroeder originals, the 1973 UK PSI printing, and later combined reprints: **one authorial root across multiple carriers**.

**Modern descendants still need explicit lineage receipts:** Don Croft's original 2002 Succor Punch self-description and Aurumaya's later named acknowledgement form an actual *modern maker-to-maker attribution*. In contrast, Pavlita→Croft, Reich→every device, Nazi→Soviet, Soviet→Rodin, and 1970 book→the iTorus app have **not** been established as direct design transfers. Shared vocabulary—orgone, radionics, copper, torus, scalar, psi, Rife, vortex—is a source-search lead, not an inheritance conclusion.

## 5. Empirical psi controversy is not one pooled experiment

A modern favourable result exists: [Tressoldi and Storm's 2024 Stage 2 registered-report Ganzfeld meta-analysis](https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.51746.4) summarises **113 studies** from 1974–2020 and reports a small approximate mean effect **0.08**, with bias analyses the authors regard as reassuring. It is proponent-authored and must be assessed for shared data, protocol heterogeneity, outcome selection and alternative explanations; it does **not** directly confirm psychotronic generators, 5D physics or an electromagnetic telepathy channel.

A different programme-specific independent finding is [AIR's final 29 September 1995 remote-viewing assessment](https://irp.fas.org/program/collect/air1995.pdf), by **Mumford, Rose and Goslin**, which found no established useful intelligence operation and did not regard the disputed observations as a settled paranormal mechanism. It is not necessarily a statistical replication of any Ganzfeld protocol, so it does not straightforwardly negate the 2024 meta-analysis. Likewise no Ganzfeld summary verifies Pavlita's apparatus claims.

**Inferential hierarchy:** a reported perceptual anomaly or questionnaire score ≠ a measured physical EM field ≠ a device-specific EM causal effect ≠ a demonstrated fifth dimension or information transfer. The existing DASHI HoeMath, HeartMath, 5D-ASC and masking-experiment formal boundaries already cover this; do not create another consciousness "energy" ontology.

## 8. Limits, authorship and attribution

Original actors own their correspondence, experiments and reported conclusions. Later historians own their reconstructions; archivists own the custody/catalogue notes; device makers own their advertising. A paper's original publication date, English translation date, CIA later declassification, archived PDF mirror and repeated testimonial must not be collapsed into "five corroborations". **Source quality is proposition-relative.** A military document is primary evidence that an agency discussed a hypothesis, not primary evidence that it worked.

Important corpus limitations: the full original Pavlita files, Azarov lab logs and 1974 Czech critique, initial Fernand Clerc magazine pages, 1973 original conference papers and 1985 correspondence were not all obtained. Kholodnyi 2017 is presently partly search-indexed but original PDF opening failed; his account is reported with this limit. Russian/German/Czech/French source selection and translation availability bias the sample. This review is **not a registered systematic review, peer-reviewed manuscript, quantitative meta-analysis, clinical trial or proof of hidden forces**. The [CSV corpus](psychotronics-literature-corpus-20261024.csv) provides reproducible search/navigation anchors for a future deeper review.

**DASHI authorship:** original claim-specific provenance mapping, critical synthesis, literature dependency graph, first-unpaid-source Pareto and explanatory boundaries only. No extra "RNNN" theorem families, invented QIDs/DOIs, synthetic experiments presented as real, or forced physical unification of geometrically similar devices.
