# Egely–Vértesy / Pavlita: first-party magnetometry and adverse-source max-cut (25 October 2026)

## Scope and decisive change

This is an **original-claim/experimental-method audit**, not a confirmation of anomalous magnetism. G. Egely and G. Vértesy's *Experimental Investigation of Biologically Induced Magnetic Anomalies* is now available as a **26-page scanned author-attributed Hungarian Academy of Sciences report**, with original figures and apparatus plates. Previously, the psychotronics review concentrated on the 1973–75 Pavlita account and Kitaygorodsky's 1974 first-person *different demonstrations*. The later magnetic-material test warrants its **own claim family**: it uses **vibrating-sample magnetometry (VSM)** before/after handling rather than an uncalibrated moving vane.

**Inspection:** original scanned cover and title/figure pages visually inspected; full author-attributed paper's experimental paragraphs and tables/figure descriptions independently read in a mirror transcription. The scanned cover carries the authors, institute and title, and a title leaf has **HU ISSN 0368 5330**, but it does not visibly give an unambiguous *full-report publication year*. A **1986 convention presentation** is separately bibliographically attested; a **1988 printed summary in Research in Parapsychology 1987**, pp.87–92, is a later derivative account. The full report cites a **1986** Jahn/Dunne item and thus is not to be assigned a confident earlier publication date without inspecting the original imprint/custodial record. The reported multi-year programme is not five independent experiments because the authorial, sample and instrumentation dependence is not resolved.

Original scan: https://rexresearch.com/egely/egely.pdf (26-page scan); transcription: https://www.rexresearch.com/pavlita/pavlita.htm ; author site catalogue: https://egely.hu/documents/?lang=en ; Rice University institutional archival object: https://archives.library.rice.edu/repositories/2/archival_objects/326138 .

**Attribution:** G. Egely and G. Vértesy own their measurements, conclusions and apparatus/report text; Robert Pavlita developed and operated the reported activation devices and personally handled samples. The third-party scan/transcription hosts and subsequent intelligence/archive and newsletter mirrors are **carriers, not co-authors or independent experimental replications**.

## Extraction by material and outcome (all numbers are *author-reported*)

| Material / sample | Reported observation | Explicit controls/uncertainties mentioned | Unpaid discriminators |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wood and PVC | Off-site initial set: 20 wood and 4 PVC reportedly changed, 5 wood did not; a later on-site 5 wood and 4 PVC reportedly changed | Authors prepared/marked samples; VSM baseline; tape and holder background; claimed handling safeguards | Operator/blind randomisation, metal microtrace assay and independent material sample handling |
| Wood signal scale | Ferromagnetic-like VSM curve; author estimates **0.02 mg** iron can mimic signal; deliberate fresh drilled-iron abrasion test gives **8 mG**, while reported activated wood average is **45 mG** | Authors acknowledge wear pollution possibility and uncertain active sample volume, roughly 20% estimated uncertainty | The single abrasive-drill example **does not bound all particle sizes, residues, transfer pathways, magnetic histories, or different sonde surfaces** |
| LiTaO3 crystals | Two crystals reportedly show approximately **85%** change in absolute diamagnetic susceptibility | Linear slopes compared before/after; samples prepared/marked by authors | Temperature, alignment/demagnetisation, holder remount reproducibility, blinded sham and second VSM |
| Bismuth | Five specimens: three changes one way, one opposite way, one unaffected; reported recovery by day 3 | Comparison of before/after VSM curves | Separate normal temperature/texture/strain/oxidation effects, time-matched shams |
| Aluminium, praseodymium | Three each reportedly show changed magnetisation curves | Same measurement apparatus family; oxidation difficulty explicitly noted for praseodymium | Humidity/oxide/time control, known reference specimen and calibrated response |
| Permalloy, nickel, Fe3Al | Two samples of each reported; different sign of saturation change for different materials | Different VSM configuration; hysteresis curves | Full repeated remount and instrument-configuration error budgets; independent observer |
| Two metglass compositions | Five Fe40Ni40Si6B14 and two Fe75Cr5B20 reported; within one same-sonde material family sign and shape differ; some reported 2–4-day recovery | Original graphs; sample materials identified | Field history, induced stress, domain pinning, sample alignment and blind order |

The paper explicitly **grounded samples prior to VSM** to reduce electrostatic pickup, and describes using the **same tape**, operator-prepared samples and separated activation/measurement rooms. These are *real author-reported controls*, not grounds to label the entire experiment uncontrolled. However the account does **not** establish a sealed, independently blinded and independently re-identified chain of custody from activation through readout. Its conclusion that contamination was excluded relies significantly on an **unmatched abrasion comparison**, the visual cleanliness of a sonde, and a proposed sign-inconsistency argument. The paper also reports **only Pavlita** performed activation, limiting operator generalisation and making operator/material/time coupling hard to separate.

1. **1986 presentation family:** Hansen's original published critical review lists *Egely & Vértesy (August 1986)* as a Parapsychological Association poster/paper. This supports a historical presentation, not that every graph of the long report was presented then. https://www.tricksterbook.com/ArticlesOnline/DeceptionBySubjects.html .
2. **1988 printed summary:** D. H. Weiner's abstracted *Research in Parapsychology 1987* report, pp.87–92, is actually present in a declassified fragment, showing Egely and Vértesy with Central Research Institute for Physics affiliation and a **John Beloff in-absentia presentation**. Government declassification *does not* make the original measurements governmental observations. https://documents2.theblackvault.com/documents/cia/stargate/STARGATE%20%238%20237/Part0007/CIA-RDP96-00792R000701020004-4.pdf .
3. **1990 challenge:** George P. Hansen's *Deception by Subjects in Psi Research* footnote attributes a **March 1987 personal communication** to Zoltán Vassy reporting that Vassy observed Pavlita use trickery in a device demonstration. This is **Hansen's report of Vassy's claimed observation**. It is not a direct obtained Vassy report, a finding of misconduct in Egely's laboratory, or identification of the **same specimen/devices**. Serious counter-source, requiring Vassy's original notes and session identity—not a licence to assert fraud as proven. https://www.tricksterbook.com/ArticlesOnline/DeceptionBySubjects.html .

**Same-apparatus distinction:** Kitaygorodsky's ~1974 first-person iron-particle, airflow and thermal-convection alternative reproductions concern *a magnetised match and moving suspended vanes*, not these particular 1980s VSM wood, bismuth, lithium tantalate or metallic-alloy samples. His mechanism informs the controls; it is **not** a direct experimental refutation of each later claim. Conversely, the later authors' VSM signal does not retroactively validate his contested 1974 vane observations, historical water treatment, claims about an original brain-mediated force or any subsequent alternative-energy products.

## Minimum *source-defined* replication, if ever resourced

The 1980s experiment's precise estimand is the **interaction** between concealed genuine/sham PAD exposure and *within-material* magnetisation-curve change after controlling calendar time, sample identity, instrument remount and unknown sonde contamination:
```
(delta M | blinded real handling) - (delta M | indistinguishable sham handling)
```
not a naked post-minus-pre difference. Compare full signed M(H) loops, raw magnetometer voltage and orientation, temperature, holder/tape, field history and wear particles across randomised, concealed, independently relabelled samples. Include an analyst and operator blind, matched contact/abrasion geometry, time-matched no-contact, serialised transfer, magnetite/iron mass detection substantially **below the reported 0.02 mg mimic scale**, known magnetic standards, dual orthogonal VSM/remount checks, and prospectively stated detection/false-positive limits. An independently operated or automated sonde could test whether the alleged mental state contributes *conditional on same hardware*, rather than conflate material contact with biology.

The existing PR's source-join, covariance/GLS, rank and calibration consumers are **reusable templates**; they do not automatically become VSM data certification. Magnetic field/current/force receipts from Field Loom S1 are **different physical observables** from magnetisation-curve response of a separately prepared test material. No need for another source-written Agda ontology or synthetic discovery p-values.
