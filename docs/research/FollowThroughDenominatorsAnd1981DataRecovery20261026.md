# Additional denominators and post-evaluation data-recovery lead

This addendum qualifies the independent Follow Through source matrix. Original authors have full credit: St. Pierre (1981), evaluators Stebbins/Bock and later commentators; Johl Brown directed independent research and OpenAI ChatGPT assisted with this reconciliation. No ESD-team action.

- 22: APM's programme-wide number of selected/piloted approaches, reported in 2026 (Haavik and Chilkoti).
- 17: 1977 original Abt IV-B/IV-C analytical-volume representation as reported in ERIC annotated bibliography ED248995. This reference is a secondary bibliography of the original volume, not original table inspection.
- 13: A separate subsequent description of the evaluation in ERIC ED215010 speaks of 13 instructional models. The precise classification/subset that produces 13 is NOT established by this retrieved passage, and 13 must not be substituted for either 17 or 22. Look for inclusion, sufficient-data and pooled-category rules in source IV-A/B.
- 350,000: cumulative population reported by APM, not comparison analytic sample.
These are four nonidentical denominator claims. Never use one count as an effect-estimate denominator for another.

A newly identified ORIGINAL authored source: Robert G. St. Pierre, *Three Ways of Learning More from Follow Through: Secondary Analysis of Extant Data, Compilation and Analysis of Follow-Up Data, and Completely New Studies*, Abt Associates, 15 January 1981, ERIC ED244731, https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED244731 ; fulltext https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED244731.pdf . The ERIC summary says it recommends reanalysis of extant data, follow-up compilation and new studies; that is a 1981 proposal and does NOT establish dataset availability in 2026. Need original report page inspection and modern archive custody before promising replication.

Additional earlier critique: ERIC ED086371 (1970s Follow Through evaluation discussion) raises the problem that comparison schools may receive other services under different programme labels; citation needs original author/title/page acquisition before use as a primary source claim.

Important conflict to resolve: APM 2026 states data cannot be reanalysed with modern tools after anonymity-related destruction, while the 1981 St. Pierre proposal discussed extant-data secondary analysis. These statements concern different dates and potentially different data artifacts, not a proven contradiction. Obtain original custody/de-identification/destruction timeline and distinguish aggregate tables from raw student data.
