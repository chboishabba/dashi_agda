## Attribution protocol

1. **Claim != source statement != external-world truth.** Document-date and event-period remain distinct. A named document's existence pays only its attributed contents and the bounded findings its institutional role authorizes.
2. **A reprint is not another root.** `MindWar` 1980 and Aquino's 2003 annotation share an author lineage; reposts of Wylie's testimony share a Wylie root. The House hearing and Wylie's submitted statement are not independent witnesses to the same first-person account.
3. **Source type is binding.** Witness testimony, court administrative disposition, regulatory finding, contradictory institutional investigations, and personal proposals are not interchangeable.
4. **Graph adjacency never pays a command hierarchy.** In particular: `Robert Maxwell != Robert Mercer`; `MindWar != Project Stargate`; Aquino's alleged criminal involvement cannot be inferred from investigative inclusion; Bannon CA role + later Epstein correspondence does not establish re-deployment of CA methods.
5. **Preserve the contrary records.** The DOJ/FBI July 2025 memo's 'no credible blackmail evidence found' is an agency review position, not a universal negative theorem; the 1992 House INSLAW conclusion and 1993 Bua report conflict on material questions. Record them as independent author roots and adjudicate claim-by-claim.
6. **No guilt from presence.** An email, address-book entry, travel invitation, social network, charge or allegation cannot on its own establish participation in any other person's abuse or criminal conspiracy. Protect survivors and third parties; do not publish victim-identifying material.
