## The crucial model omission the earlier arithmetic-only round did not settle

The 2012 OAG's **0.2% vulnerable / 1.2% endangered koala**, area/quality, confidence and 20-year inverse-compound calculation **does** reproduce the proponent's 108.35% original source credit (conditional 1.2% gives ~88.83%). That proves arithmetic/conditional numerical vulnerability only, **not ecological adequacy**. A proponent can have **perfect source spreadsheet arithmetic** yet provide NO validated bridge to the following ecological functions:

1. Woogaroo's **existing mature feeding-canopy and breeding/hollow structures** versus future planted hectares/features.
2. **Local koala dispersal and population viability**, including same-site, same-history indexed movement before and after action; distant offset koalas do not undo corridor severance.
3. **Heat and drought refuge / shaded riparian microclimate**, which an undifferentiated 20-year habitat-quality number may omit.
4. **Cumulative fragmentation, indirect habitat unusability and shifted edge effects**, which may not be represented by a simple 128 ha direct-impact scalar.
5. **Season-specific bird/flying-fox use and mature nectar resources**, as opposed to planting projected 80% of preferred-forage saplings without an observed survival/reproductive-stage measurement.

There are independent negative and falsification pathways: require direct same-function, same-time impact/offset ecological comparisons. Do not assume functional equivalence from a full OAG score. The new golden module defines an *empty proof carrier* \`SameFunctionValidation proxy ecologicalFunction\` and shows that no mere calculator attribute inhabits a proper same-function certificate. ## Evidence hierarchy — independence of *observations*, not social prestige

**Original Commonwealth instruments/statute**: authoritative for legal decisions and operative conditions but **not proof of real-world offset success**.

**Original dated independent field evidence** (actual GPS/monitoring/koala rescues/telemetry, suitable survey design) and **independent professionally authored site-specific opinions**: primary for what was observed/authored, subject to original methods and possible counterevidence. QUT/QCC independently author their **critiques**, but do not automatically originate all underlying observations they reference.

**Community first-person reports**: direct, potentially valuable local observation if an original observer, time, place, photo/GPS/case record and adequate species ID can be checked; absence of credentials is not grounds for dismissal. Repetition of one observation across 1,786 comments is not 1,786 independent field observations.

**Proponent-side numerical tables / survey estimates**: interested source assertions; their verifiable arithmetic/negative admissions are admissible **as claims about that model**, but proposed future gains, 10/10 presence categories, confidence and flora maturity remain unvalidated ecological outcomes until confirmed independently.

**Judicial/agency determination**: a distinct procedural/legal evidence tier, only where an original 2019/8575-specific filing, actual order, s95C brief, condition monitoring or legal finding is received. No such event is inferred from the QUT/QCC opinions or any score.
