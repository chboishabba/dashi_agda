## Exact transcript claims and acceptance discriminators
1. **22 models** (00:11–00:16). Distinguish models funded across programme history from models/sites included in the 1977 national model-comparison estimand. Do not silently replace one denominator with another.
2. **Reading/math and abstract reasoning/problem solving** (00:18–00:29). Abt supports unusually high basic-skill results and APM reports positive cognitive-domain comparisons. Need model × domain × site × cohort × time estimate and correct instrument definition; do not make universal student-level or every-site claims.
3. **Self-esteem, self-confidence, feelings about school** (00:25–00:50). Preserve separate psychometric constructs, instruments and reliability; affective superiority as reported is not proof that competency caused self-confidence. Becker 1978 explicitly urges caution on self-reported affect.
4. **Government funded worse models** (00:50–00:53). Needs specific appropriations/distribution, date and administrative decision relative to publication and objectives; programme continuation and evidence-based dissemination are different policy acts.
5. **Scripts, locked classroom doors, transfers, fidelity** (00:53–01:19). Testimony reported by APM supports that those events were described, not their general prevalence nor causal contribution to heterogeneous impacts. Reconstruct dose, teacher preparation, turnover, deviations, fidelity definitions, site-level effect and confounders.
6. **Site heterogeneity** (01:19–01:23). House critique documents design problems, non-random site/comparison selection, heterogeneity; compare competing analyses on identical estimands and uncertainty before ranking robustness.
7. **350,000 children, announced in 1967 vs began in 1968** (01:23–01:31). Distinguish programme-wide cumulative count, evaluation records, tested children, effective sample, cluster sample and model-specific analytic denominators.
8. **Great Falls Precision Teaching omitted** (01:31–01:38). As-of S2E3 only. An episode's omission is not proof of historical irrelevance; first verify the separate original project and whether it belonged to the same evaluation.
9. **Race / Texas school / Episode 4** (01:38–01:48). Transcript internally says only three episodes released then says episode four *was* about a Texas school. Verify episode publication chronology and underlying school before using this anecdote as evidence.
10. **Ford Foundation commissioned House, DI still first** (01:48–02:04). APM interviews and contemporary critique support commission/reanalysis and contested aggregate ranking; recheck exact adjustments before declaring rank stability under all alternatives.
11. **Government cited local control** (02:04–02:08). Require government decision memo and authority allocation; do not infer exclusive reason or deliberate deception without independent contemporaneous evidence.
12. **'Government covered up the largest study' and caused nationwide degradation** (02:32–02:42). Decompose disclosure, publication, suppression of particular early data, communication choices, national adoption counterfactual and aggregate educational outcomes. A published 1977 evaluation does not establish complete erasure; archival withholding of some early findings would not, by itself, identify a national causal impact.

## Sharp model-specific hypotheses
H_basic: Direct Instruction's model-weighted mean adjusted basic-skills effect is greater than each comparator on a specified analytic population.
H_cognitive: same for each explicitly defined problem-solving/reasoning instrument.
H_affective: same for each *particular* affective scale under its reliability/construct assumptions.
H_transport: advantage persists under new sites, cohorts and curricular fidelity distributions (not entailed by any aggregate historical rank).
H_fidelity: intervention on measured programme adherence changes student outcomes (requires identification beyond correlation).
H_competence: experimental or longitudinal mediation identifies competence → later confidence separate from teaching assignment, teacher relationships and pre-existing engagement.
H_policy: documented decisions diverge from a declared policy decision rule; intent and downstream learning-loss effects are additional hypotheses.
