# Follow Through — 1978 methodological disagreement: attributed primary claims

Research direction: Johl Brown. AI-assisted audit: OpenAI ChatGPT. Historical authors retain their original claims. This is independent Follow Through research, not team Digital ESD integration.

## Same-source denominators and study grain
House, Gene V. Glass, Leslie D. McLean and Decker F. Walker (1978), "No Simple Answer: Critique of the Follow Through Evaluation", *Educational Leadership*, https://files.ascd.org/staticfiles/ascd/pdf/journals/ed_lead/el_197803_house.pdf , directly describe a comparison of **13 models** involving **over 20,000** pupils taught over four years. These are their described evaluation subjects, NOT the 22 approaches across programme history, NOR the 350,000 cumulative participation count reported by APM in 2026. The separately reported 17-model denominator from later ERIC bibliography remains subject to original-table inclusion audit. Model count and child count both require original year/cohort/population qualification.

House et al. explicitly state that Abt analysed the evaluation and authored the final report but did not design the study or collect data. They note that many measurement and sampling limitations originated in the politically constrained design before Abt's analysis. Thus "Abt conducted the whole experiment", and "all methodological defects were introduced by Abt", are unsupported reductions.

## Becker (1978) technical answer to House
Wesley C. Becker, "The National Evaluation of Follow Through: Behavior-Theory-Based Programs Come out on Top", *Educational and Urban Society* 10(4), doi:10.1177/001312457801000403, is a programme-associated contemporary rebuttal, not independent empirical replication.

Becker explains two "grayout" rules (data designated by the evaluator as untrustworthy):
1. Entire site column if FT/non-FT preschool participation differed by more than 50 percent.
2. Specific effects when covariance adjustment shifted a site mean by over one-half standard deviation.

Becker disputes the inference that every signed result meeting such a condition should be disregarded; he argues a positive result against a bias favouring negative could still be informative. His argument requires modelled bias direction and uncertainty before its substantive correctness can be established. Becker also AGREES with House that Raven's is not an appropriate measure of academic cognitive skill. This qualified agreement should not be suppressed.

## Distinct outcome constructs
Basic skills (reading/math/spelling, as actually assessed), cognitive-conceptual measures (which instrument?), affective/self-concept measures (which scale?), and external observer narratives are distinct coordinates. AAPM's chart tracing cannot create originally unreported effect size numbers. "Competence caused confidence" remains a mediation hypothesis.

## Highest-value mathematical/empirical reanalysis
Construct a table at the observed site×model×cohort×test domain grain with (i) raw and adjusted effects, (ii) FT/non-FT preschool percentage differences, (iii) standard-deviation scale of adjustment, (iv) grayout designation, (v) analytic inclusion decision, (vi) alternative House/Becker admissibility, (vii) cluster sizes/standard errors, and (viii) sensitivity to omitted sites. Before ranking programmes, compute effect weights and uncertainty with consistent denominators.
