## Empirical max-cut and discriminator design
Make an original-document index with title, original date, author, archive locator, scan hash, quotation offsets, redactions and relation to reprints. Then build a bounded dataset table with observation grain (pupil, school, site, cohort, year), model-as-assigned vs received, baseline characteristics, missingness and sampling design. For agency, measure directly; do not infer from a self-concept proxy or academic score.
