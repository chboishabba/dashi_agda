## Finite theorem and scientific boundary
A two-history *synthetic* witness has identical assessed mastery but different learner voice. Thus the voice projection cannot factor through the academic projection in this model. This is a proof-facing mathematical counterexample, NOT an empirical result about Direct Instruction, educational equity, or actual children.

Relevant unresolved research: extract model-by-site data, clarify analysis population and outcome definitions; reproduce competing adjustment choices; distinguish model assignment, treatment received, fidelity and teacher adaptation; independently measure learner agency; estimate heterogeneity and transport uncertainty. Neither course-effect causation nor mechanism mediation is inferred from scores alone.
