## Bidirectional severe discriminators against the original episode claims
D1 (strong DI claim): In the exact common population/outcome/instrument and the specified alternative House and St. Pierre adjustments, compare corrected estimates and uncertainty. A sourced sign reversal, loss of a clear lead, or demonstrable model-count mismatch narrows the robustness claim. A stability certificate must identify the **set of tested specifications**, not all conceivable analytical models.

D2 (strong House claim): If the criticism only challenges universal extrapolation and not the ranking of a defined aggregate estimand, allow both claims to be true. Require an original same-sample reanalysis producing a different sign/order to claim actual rank reversal. House's critique of Raven's as an academic-cognitive outcome is construct-specific; do not generalise it to reading and mathematics.

D3 (competence causes confidence): Affective score association alone cannot pay causal mediation. Require temporally ordered competence/confidence measurements, confounder control and a clearly specified intervention/mediation estimand. A strong alternative is that programme context, teacher behaviour or selection affects both.

D4 (fidelity explains heterogeneity): Demand original site-level fidelity instruments, cross-school assignment scheme, timing and measured violations. Test whether site ranking changes after adjusting for baseline and teacher turnover. Lock-door/transfers are interview evidence pending independent contemporaneous frequency.

D5 (policy suppressed successful results): Separate the first-year nonrelease, 1977 published final report, contemporaneous appropriations and explicit decision justifications. Strong pro-criticism test: an original dated memo directly instructing concealment or misreporting. Strong alternative: independent records showing continued dissemination or different declared programme goals. Neither alone establishes a national downstream education counterfactual.

D6 (long-run durability): Five-site follow-up outcomes are not full national programme randomisation, and national percentile trajectory differs from local exposed-vs-control effects. Audit cohort selection, attrition and post-programme curricula.
