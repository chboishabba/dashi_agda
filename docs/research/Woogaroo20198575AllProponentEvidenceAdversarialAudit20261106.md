## Uniform evidence test — every substantive claim must survive this path

~~~text
original dated source / field record
   -> independent sampling frame + raw trace + location + detection conditions
   -> correctly joined assessment unit / species / season / population
   -> correctly computed component scores, units, weights and decision thresholds
   -> validated causal counterfactual / expected future change
   -> independent uncertainty/success rate / maturation time / reversibility
   -> conservation equivalence to a specific *2019/8575* impact function
   -> genuine registered security and measurable delivery
   -> statutory adoption/consideration (separate government source)
   -> actual merits, enforcement, or court finding (never inferred)
~~~

**No source promotion:** a source-authored assertion is primary *evidence that the author asserted it*, not independent proof of the assertion. The author's own low-scoring/barren-habitat admissions constrain positive projections, but do not become independently confirmed ground truth. A project approval is a government act, not proof that planted trees grew, birds visited or conditions were complied with.

**Asymmetric treatment by causal direction, not by party:** If uncertainty favors a higher score, ask whether that raises the quantified *impact*, *offset baseline*, or *offset future*. A higher baseline at the offset may **reduce** incremental offset credit, while a higher future score may **increase** credit. An impact score increase may increase total required offsets. Audit every use of the rule by stage and direction. The A16 Table 25 text explicitly proposes choosing the higher score for subjective criteria when uncertain; that does not independently justify any particular AU's higher score.

## Hard first-order source defects (reproduced exactly)

### H1 — Mount Walker West A16 Table 45 arithmetic contradiction

| Row, maximum | AU1 | AU2 | AU3 | AU4 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Presence (10) | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 |
| Usage (15) | 5 | 5 | 15 | **5** |
| Approximate density (30) | 0 | 0 | 10 | 10 |
| Population role (15) | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| **Mathematical sum of source row entries** | **20** | **20** | **40** | **30** |
| **Printed total** | 20 | 20 | 40 | **40** |
| Printed rescaled /4 | 1.14 | 1.14 | 2.29 | **2.29** |

**Proven:** As literally printed, **AU4 rows total 30/70, not 40/70**. On 4-point species-stocking scale, a 30-point total is **(30/70)×4 = 12/7 ≈ 1.71**, while printed 40-point total gives **16/7 ≈ 2.29**: conditional numerical gap **4/7 ≈ 0.5714**. The preceding prose says AU4 usage 15/15, not table's 5/15, which would sum to 40. **UNRESOLVED:** Is the table, the prose, the selected computational input, or a document revision wrong? One must identify the actual approved worksheet and raw source. AU4 has **0% koala offset credit in A16 Table 13**, so this discrepancy alone does **not** establish that the claimed 50.02% compensation changes.

### H2 — MWW SAT labelling conflict

A16 Table 44 and §6.1.3 describe SATs **T1,T2,T3,T7,T8**, tallying **0,2,0,1,0** scat-positive tree slots out of five separate 30-tree SATs. Later A16 §6.2.1 prose names **T1,T6,T7,T8,T10** — the latter is also the Avonvale A15 Table 33 sequence. This is an internal **source-label inconsistency**. Cause unknown; investigate raw GPS / transect record, not assume copying or falsification. The sampling only occurred where adequate trees were available: not a randomized full-area survey.

### H3 — impact site area discrepancy between source tables

**A16 Table 2** lists **110.89 ha** (AU1) + **16.13 ha** (AU2) = **127.02 ha**. The main PD **Table A26** instead gives **111.87 ha** + **16.13 ha** = **128.00 ha**. The discrepancy is exactly **0.98 ha**. Do not merge values without checking the map polygon, geometry stage, revision, and whether the descriptions represent different impact accounting scopes.

## Substantive proponent-model challenge, paid numerical inputs

### M1 — presence / detection / AU scope

A16 reports **3/150 scat-bearing tree-search slots** across **2/5 positive SATs**, all **low koala use**. AU1 and AU2 explicitly receive **0/30 density** with sparse trees/no koala evidence, while all four AUs get **10/10 “presence detected on or adjacent to site”**. This is a **binary on-site/adjacent categorical score** (Table 25: 0=no, 5=adjacent, 10=on-site), **not 100% koala occupancy**. Even **1 positive of 1 tree would be 100% of sampled trees**, not 100% habitat function; actual SAT protocol demands a suitable 30-tree sampling frame. The challenge is **scope propagation**, biological interpretation, and quantitative downstream consequence, not confusing the denominator with animal abundance. Presence anywhere in the offset does not establish breeding or equivalent local connectivity in every AU.

Independent falsifier: GPS-linked transects, all detection/absence histories, unsampled AUs, original Table 25 scoring implementation, a separately qualified ecologist's same-AU interpretation, and the precise input cells used in the certified OAG calculation.

### M2 — entire 50.02% koala offset credit concentrated in the low-use/open-paddock AUs

A16 **Table 13** reports:

| AU | Area ha | Baseline quality (original) | Forecast future quality | Claimed share of total koala loss offset |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 50.8 | 3.40 (rounded 3) | 6.86 (rounded 7) | **16.34%** |
| 2 | 104.7 | 3.44 (rounded 3) | 6.71 (rounded 7) | **33.68%** |
| 3 | 62.0 | 6.51 (rounded 7) | 7.10 (rounded 7) | **0%** |
| 4 | 8.1 | 6.71 (rounded 7) | 7.10 (rounded 7) | **0%** |
| **Total** | **225.6** | | | **50.02%** |

**Proven source arithmetic:** AU1+AU2=**155.5 ha** by the rounded Table 13 AUs and **100% of claimed koala credit**; 155.4-ha restoration headline elsewhere is a separate quantity and must not be silently forced into equality. These are precisely the AUs with the source's **0/30 density score**. This is a **future-uplift prediction**, not empirical recovery of existing Woogaroo koalas. AU3 and AU4's mathematical issue does not directly explain the 50.02% because they receive zero credited koala offset.

### M3 — ecology extrapolation, future gain and zero risk-of-loss

A16 Table 12 explicitly sets **risk of loss without offset = 0%** and **with offset = 0%**, so its proponent-reported protected-area credit is not justified by an avoided-loss difference in this table; benefits are modelled primarily from future habitat-quality changes. It assumes **20 years** until ecological benefit, **75% future-quality confidence for non-remnant AU1/AU2**, **85% for remnant/regrowth AU3/AU4**, and a **90% confidence** for the zero-risk-loss assumption. These are published inputs, **not independently observed success probabilities**. No simple numerical confidence interval follows without a time-series model.

A16 cites reaching **50% canopy cover and 70% canopy height** relative to regional benchmarks as a basis for remnant-regrowth status by year 20. That benchmark is **not** a demonstrated equivalence with existing mature trees, flowering capacity, koala refuge/foraging, breeding and Woogaroo corridor function.

### M4 — Avonvale, Esk, birds, flying-fox and source symmetry

- **Avonvale A15 Table 33:** **11,5,0,0,1** scat-positive tree slots /30 each (**17/150**, 3 positive SATs); report also claims a koala observed. It propagates **10/10 presence across all six AUs**, some predominantly paddock. Same AU scope/rubric criticism applies; a high-use tree patch cannot establish all-AU use. Its baseline notes pest threats and patch-scale vegetation variation.
- **Esk A14/Main PD §6.3.3:** reports scats in treed AUs and **low SAT usage**. Detailed equivalent sample table still needs extraction and direct AUDIT, rather than imputing one.
- **Avonvale bird records:** Main PD §6.4.4–6.4.5 expressly reports **no Swift Parrot or Regent Honeyeater records within 10 km** in searched databases while counting **projected future preferred foraging trees** toward OAG credits. Missing record is not proof of no visiting bird; projected trees are not documented visits, established nectar or protected bird survival.
- **MWW bird tree-value projections:** A16 Tables 47–51 rely on sampled stem counts, area extrapolation, planting-density scenarios and an **80% future planting survival assumption**, with tree maturity/flowering and species use separate gates.
- **Grey-headed Flying-fox:** reported habitat-quality score, flowering-resource mix, seasonality and future forage values are proxies for available food, not actual confirmed visits/roosts or compensatory population outcomes.
- **Impact site symmetry:** Main PD Table A26 identifies **128 ha**, Koala MHQA **6.78/10**, flying-fox FHA **7.47/10**, **9,983** Swift Parrot and **9,868** Regent Honeyeater mapped foraging features. Do not assume they are artificially low; evidence of low valuations must be established by comparison, not inferred from party interest.
