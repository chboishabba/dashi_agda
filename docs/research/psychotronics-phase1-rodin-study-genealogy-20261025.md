## Material finding and corrected state of literature

A prior status implied that the 2010 Marko Rodin and Greg Volk conference article's admission of missing quantitative controlled studies remained the last word on Rodin coils. That is false **as a statement about the later literature**. **By September 2013** an author group centred on **Teodoro Córdova-Fraga / Universidad de Guanajuato** published an instrumented Rodin-type field source with magnetic intensity estimates, source-current/impedance table and laboratory cell-response assays. Another publication followed in **2014**, then **2020/2021**, **2022**, and a **2024 issue** (the SciELO electronic carrier itself says 2025) provided a 3D field model and Hall sensor map. Ordinary magnetic fields and biological exposure effects are physically researchable; no later study establishes scalar waves, orgone energy, free energy, a quantum consciousness channel, gravity engineering or unusual "3/6/9" numerology.

**Source-specific critical tension:** 2020/2021 authors report reduced Aβ fibril formation and cell protection in one *in vitro* stress/amyloid context; 2022 authors report increased apoptosis and changed cytoskeleton in SH-SY5Y in another exposure. These are **not the same intervention/outcome or an automatically direct contradiction**, but they defeat any blanket inference that the geometry necessarily promotes healing. Likewise a *different* 2014 HEK+gadolinium report's stated `p < 0.5` is far too permissive for conventional `p < 0.05` statistical inference; do not silently 'repair' the paper's printed p threshold as though the significance test were known.

### 2010 Rodin and Volk — joint mathematics/source commentary

[Marko Rodin and Greg Volk, *The Rodin Number Map and Rodin Coil*, Proceedings of the NPA, Long Beach 2010, seven pages](https://www.naturalphilosophy.org/pdf/abstracts/abstracts_5352.pdf). Original page images cover and printed p7 visually inspected. Their paper introduces modulo-nine maps and proposed topological winding relations. Its printed p7 labels a photo *'Levitating magnet above a Rodin coil'* attributed in bibliography to **Jamie Buturff** and separately an iron-filings field image to **Jean-Louis Naudin**; **a photograph with a credited contributor is not a quantified independently witnessed magnetic force measurement**. The same page says quantitative controlled coil studies had **not been published 'to date'**. Credit the two **joint paper authors** and the separately credited photographic sources, without assuming any device or measurement belongs to the paper authors.

Modular doubling follows `1,2,4,8,7,5,1` in `Z/9Z`; mathematical recurrence is real. Claiming that this number sequence forces a particular external field, unique torus, special 3D resonance or quantum universe is an extra premise, not a proof.

### 2013 Pérez et al. — instrumented original ordinary Rodin source, peer-reviewed

[H. Pérez et al., *Portable device for magnetic stimulation: assessment survival and proliferation in human lymphocytes*, *Review of Scientific Instruments* **84**, 094701 (2013); DOI 10.1063/1.4819796, PMID 24089844](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24089844/). The original journal article is available in [a research-paper PDF carrier](https://www.rfsafe.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/RSI-Portable.pdf): printed **094701-2** original page and Table I visually inspected on 25 October 2026. Its apparatus comprises **two series-connected Rodin-type coils, each 21 turns and 2.2-cm average diameter**, low-voltage waveform generator/audio amplifier, and applied sinusoidal frequencies **100, 800, 1500, 2450 and 2500 Hz**; source fields reported **1.13–4.13 mT**, confirmed by gaussmeter. One example: at **100 Hz**, the authors tabulate **3.80 mT theoretical versus 4.13 mT measured**, not some previously unknown electromagnetic scalar field.

The authors investigated cultured, donor-derived lymphocytes plus **paramagnetic ferrofluid** and reported changed survival/proliferation especially for *mitogen-stimulated* cells. These are genuine author-reported instrument and *in vitro* assay descriptions; no patient treatment, independent replicated clinical trial, isolated quantum mechanism or demonstration that fields were geometry-specific after equalising complete field/dose gradient controls. The article labels a **1000-W amplifier**; that nameplate is **not** necessarily delivered biological exposure or coil power.

This is an **important direct source correction**: the 2010 'no controlled studies published' sentence is a time-bounded historical claim, not a justification to omit all later measured studies.
