## Paper-type decision

Current primary type is **reflexive, source-governed integrative conceptual review**, with author-nominated bounded HE/digital-scholarship corpus and structured external EfS challenge. The seven principles are provisional intermediary syntheses. Methods must distinguish this from both independent human action research and statistical meta-analysis. A quantitative pooling sub-study is conditional on source-admitted independent effect estimates of compatible estimands and designs, with heterogeneity, bias and uncertainty checks; otherwise provide context-indexed narrative synthesis.

## Weekly operating rules

1. Thursday: circulate a one-page decision packet stating live options, evidence, exact unresolved questions and accountable owners. Friday: resolve or explicitly defer with reason and date; do not let 'more reading' substitute for an explicit decision.
2. Maintain a decision ledger with version, owner, date, source/email/meeting provenance, alternatives and impact on abstracts/Methods/results; decisions are not empirical evidence.
3. Keep the working paper in the group's Teams document as collaborative writing authority. DASHI/Agda owns claim-bound evidence, source/provenance and formal comparison, not editorial team consent.
4. Track three lanes separately: (A) team/reviewer decisions, (B) source and corpus execution/admission, (C) manuscript writing and governance. Completion in one lane does not pay another.
5. Holiday weeks (25 December, 1 January) are deliberately low-load/no assumed meeting. Obtain agreement to substitutes or rolling asynchronous decisions if desired.
6. Risk gate: full ERIC universe is metadata, not fully reviewed full text. Other database exports and exact passage review remain open. Do not let an optional quantitative pooling ambition prevent submitting a defensible integrative review.
