## Native mathematical construction introduced in #1261

Module `DiamondFccPrimitiveGlideExact.agda`:
In coordinates scaled by four relative to the conventional cubic
lattice, fcc primitive coefficients (u,v,w) produce
  L(u,v,w) = (2(v+w),2(u+w),2(u+v)).
The diamond motif is A(u,v,w)=L(u,v,w),
  B(u,v,w)=L(u,v,w)+(1,1,1).
The represented ideal host therefore has two FCC sublattices.

Signed-coordinate flip and two coordinate swaps act by **actual
integer primitive-coordinate maps**:
  flipX(u,v,w)=(u+v+w,-w,-v),
  swapXY(u,v,w)=(v,u,w),
  swapYZ(u,v,w)=(u,w,v).
Unlike the abstract point-group catalogue these maps act on a
crystallographic lattice, not just arbitrary 27 test points.

The affine x-glide
  Gx(r)=(1,1,1)+(-rx,ry,rz)
maps A(u,v,w) to B(flipX(u,v,w)) and B(u,v,w) to
A((1,0,0)+flipX(u,v,w)). Consequently
  Gx²(r)=r+(0,2,2).
This is a *genuine nonzero* FCC translation and proves this chosen
generator is not a zero-translation point reflection of the
primitive lattice. The source-written Agda module includes
ring-solver proof terms for these mappings; Agda kernel status open.

Module `DiamondNVOrderedBondStabilizerExact.agda`:
A specified geometric bond A(0,0,0)→B(0,0,0) points along [111].
Its spatial stabilizer is generated by C3 cyclic coordinate
permutation and one XY reflection, satisfying c³=s²=1 and
scs=c^(-1). It has six operations (C3v ≅ S3).
A site-stabilizer without marking its bond is larger (24).
This module also uses `AtomicPeriodicTable369GenerativeExact.AtomicIndex`
to distinguish a substitutional N endpoint from an actual vacancy:
a vacancy is a separate `Occupant` constructor, not fake Z=0.
Neutral N-14/carbon-12 atomic labels are *indices*, not a proof
of the physical NV charge state or a full defect Hamiltonian.

## Independent exact computational verification
`scripts/check_diamond_fcc_glide_nv_bond_exact.py`:
- Primitive map fcc→quarter coordinates and exact inverse on all
  343 coefficient triples in [-3,3]^3.
- All x-glide/site transforms, glide²=fcc primitive translation.
- 48 signed cubic point operations, with a concrete translation
  coset t_g=(1,1,1) for odd coordinate-sign parity; products obey
  t_g+g*t_h-t_(gh) ∈ FCC.
- Four conventional FCC centring translations, giving 192
  distinct affine operations modulo conventional translations.
- 8 A/B diamond sites in one conventional cell are preserved.
- All 192×192 affine products close exactly modulo 4.
- Exact fixed-point counts: atomic-site stabilizer 24, ordered
  [111] bond stabilizer 6, consistent with tetrahedral
  site symmetry and C3v ordered defect geometry.
The substantive local Python independently confirmed these results.
This is an exact finite model check, not an Agda compiler
certificate or measured diamond specimen receipt.

## Phase readiness and acceptance tests

**A. Tetragonal P4/mmm** — group and integer affine mathematical
source implemented; Agda kernel and source-setting comparison open.

**B. Diamond Fd-3m** — concrete FCC primitive, A/B motif and
nonsymmorphic generator implemented; executable finite 192-operator
source-equivalent checks pass. Still needed: kernel checking,
a finite Agda B3/48 representation, full 192-operation
Seitz quotient and inverse law *inside Agda*, origin-choice
reconciliation with IUCr and precise Wyckoff-site action.
Avoid calling a six-operation generator proof a 230-group
classification.

**C. Atomic/defect and quantum** — the ordered N-vacancy bond is
an actual decorated crystallographic site pair. Next prove
the ordered-site stabilizer is exactly C3v **within the full
space-group action** and connect its representation to the
native spin/orbital Hilbert module, strain and Zeeman terms.
Only then examine NV qutrit encoding, ODMR/readout and
multicore coherence under actual Hamiltonian covariance.

**D. Full crystallography programme** — reuse B4/Weyl,
7/14/32 catalogues, actual Bravais metric/dual lattices,
Wyckoff actions, nonsymmorphic phases, source-normalized 230
operation tables, and measurement null-models. Correct
reciprocal fcc/bcc pairing is a priority.

## Steelman and falsifiable discriminators
The defensible advance is a *single model-consumer interface*
that respects original crystal lattice, group action,
atomic species, defects, quantum Hamiltonian and measurements,
while retaining provenance and any counterexamples.
A coincidence of 3, 9, 13, 27 or shared C3 labels is not
an experimental observation; a symmetry of diamond sites
is not generally a symmetry of three spin eigenstates.
The concrete glide square and the 24-vs-6 stabilizer
split are strong nontrivial mathematical discriminators.
