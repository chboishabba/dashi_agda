# Digital-ESD PNF, intersectional-absence and material-substrate audit

Status: working review-method addendum. This document does not create final study inclusion or empirical payment.

## Core review object

Each admitted study now contributes through the following chain:

```text
exact attributed source
-> literal reported result
-> paid Predicate Normal Form assertion
   (population, context, intervention, comparator, outcome, time, inferential force)
-> study design / analysis-set receipt
-> n / effect magnitude / CI-SE-p / uncertainty semantics
-> strongest admissible implication
-> intersectional absence audit: "who is not at the table?"
-> material/environmental substrate audit
-> bounded synthesis contribution
```

Paper discussion rhetoric is not itself the paid assertion. A stronger causal, mechanistic, transport, prevalence, normative, policy or system-transformation predicate requires an additional receipt.

## Predicate-level proof accounting

The canonical owners are:

- `DigitalESDStudyResultPNFProofSearchExact` for quantitative/longitudinal studies;
- `DigitalESDStudyEvidencePNFExtensionExact` for qualitative, review, conceptual, LCA, monitoring and mixed-method evidence;
- `DigitalESDDisabilityStudyPNFExact` for disability-specific primary studies;
- `PredicateNormalFormEvidenceAuditExact` for quantifier, scope and promotion obligations;
- `ExperimentalAssertionPNFImplicationConeExact` for the result -> bounded contrast -> association -> causal effect -> mechanism -> transport -> recommendation ladder.

Examples already retained:

- Deng et al.: n=1408, paired mean gain +9.25, 95% CI [7.58,10.92], dz=.289; paid comparison is immediate pre/post change, not a causal treatment effect because there is no untreated comparator.
- Bräßler/Braßler: study N=409 and Time×Group F(1,191)=22.4, p<.001, partial eta-squared=.105; the exact inferential analysis denominator remains unresolved and no educational-effect CI is manufactured.
- Descamps et al.: 164 attended, 107 complete pre/post cases; active-scenario contrasts are paid, but complete-case missingness and the absence of an untreated control remain live.
- Green, Molloy & Duggan: randomized factorial assignment is retained, but post-randomization analysis exclusions keep the current paid result below automatic causal promotion.
- Collado et al.: longitudinal quasi-experimental predicates and one-year follow-up are retained with their source-specific selection/attrition/interval receipts.

The review therefore distinguishes:

```text
effect magnitude != confidence interval
confidence interval != causal identification
randomization != admissible analysis set
causal identification of a measured outcome != construct completeness != transport
```

## Who is not at the table?

The recorded research conversation explicitly asks this question. It is now a mandatory study-level audit overlay rather than a discussion-only prompt.

`DigitalESDStudyIntersectionalAbsenceAuditExact` asks:

1. who was sampled?
2. who was eligible but missing?
3. who was excluded by design?
4. who could not access participation?
5. who had to disclose disability/need/identity to be counted?
6. who is affected but unsampled?
7. which intersections are unreported?
8. who defined the categories?
9. who interpreted the evidence?
10. who had decision authority?
11. whose future options are affected?

It reuses rather than replaces `DigitalESDDisabilityIntersectionalityAuditExact`, `IntersectionalNonFactorability`, `EarlyLearningIntersectionalCapabilityExact`, the trauma/memory authority boundary and the externality-incidence audit.

A finite collision proves:

```text
same reported n != same representation adequacy
```

and the audit explicitly blocks:

```text
engagement telemetry -> disability or trauma diagnosis
formal accessibility checklist -> realised usable access
participation count -> epistemic voice
one marginalised axis -> intersectional adequacy
```

The canonical disability lane retains exact primary studies on disabled learners, GenAI, technology-enhanced learning and assistive-technology sustainability, plus review/institutional context. Trauma/memory, political and Amalek lanes are structural donors only: they cannot create disability facts, diagnoses or political conclusions.

Current SOTA also makes the absence audit empirically important. A 2025 systematic review of disabled students in online higher education included 14 studies and found that physical conditions dominated the represented samples while neurodivergent and intellectually disabled students together represented less than 1% of participants. Online flexibility could expand access while support gaps, accommodation barriers, isolation and stigma remained live. This is review-level evidence about the literature corpus, not a universal prevalence estimate.

## Digital education has a material substrate

`DigitalESDMaterialEnvironmentalSubstrateExact` makes the physical system boundary explicit. "AI", "online", "cloud" and "digital" are service labels, not dematerialised educational systems.

Eight lifecycle stages are retained:

1. semiconductor fabrication;
2. device manufacture;
3. procurement/distribution;
4. networks and data centres;
5. operational electricity/water;
6. maintenance/repair/upgrade;
7. replacement/end of life;
8. recovery/recycling/disposal.

For each relevant study/deployment claim the audit asks about devices/accelerators, compute location, grid context, water/cooling, embodied fabrication/materials, service life, repair/upgrade/reuse, end-of-life route, affected workers/communities and whether the environmental quantity is measured, inventory-derived, modelled, scenario-projected or merely contextual.

The owner directly reuses:

- `DigitalESDExternalityIncidenceAuditExact`;
- `TSMCHBMManufacturingDemandPolicy2026Exact`;
- `LESResearchCrossPollinationExact.TaskFactorisation` as the task-relative sufficiency donor;
- the corrected Pinzone education-LCA source;
- IEA energy/AI context;
- ITU/UNITAR e-waste context;
- TSMC primary sustainability/annual-report context.

No contextual aggregate is allowed to become a named deployment footprint:

```text
AI label != deployment footprint
device count != environmental sustainability
global data-centre average != school AI usage
TSMC manufacturing report != education deployment LCA
modelled impact != measured impact
recycling collection != recovered material
infrastructure efficiency != absolute burden reduction
```

The general context is still materially relevant. IEA estimates data-centre electricity use at roughly 415 TWh in 2024, with a base-case projection of about 945 TWh in 2030, while explicitly retaining substantial scenario uncertainty. The Global E-waste Monitor reports 62 billion kg of e-waste generated in 2022 with 22.3% formally collected/recycled. TSMC's own 2025 reporting retains large water/material/waste-management surfaces and explicitly links expansion and advanced-process complexity to resource/waste management. These are infrastructure/manufacturing context, not the footprint of a particular educational intervention.

## Attribution / authority rule

The same-object discipline applies at every level:

```text
same paper != one analysis set
same DOI != every metadata field is correct
same construct label != same measurement
same result rhetoric != same paid PNF
same demographic label != same situated capability
same service label != same material substrate
same contextual infrastructure statistic != same deployment footprint
```

For political/religious/cultural donor lanes, role-binding and analogy remain separate from identity and empirical transfer. They may inform the structure of an audit but never supply disability facts, environmental measurements, political judgments or participant diagnoses.
