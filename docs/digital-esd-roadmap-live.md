# Digital-ESD paper live roadmap

**Branch:** `agent/digital-esd-paper-methodology-primary-sources`

**Status:** working research/paper roadmap. This document records design intent, implementation state and evidence debt; it is not a scholarly source and does not create review evidence.

## 1. Recovered research brief

The originating research conversation proposed a translational paper rather than a generic paper about digital sustainability. Its generative question was:

```text
What can established research on online pedagogy, engagement, feedback,
learning technologies, student support, voice, professional practice and
institutional adaptation contribute to building capacity for sustainable
development?
```

The proposed output was a conceptual framework / set of transferable principles grounded in the team's existing research, with independent sustainability expertise and literature brought in rather than treating the digital-education corpus as sufficient sustainability authority.

The same conversation also introduced a reciprocal constraint: digital systems used for ESD are themselves environmental, social, economic, epistemic, pedagogical and infrastructural objects. Hardware replacement, resource use, open source, duplication, externalities, younger learner perspectives and long-horizon option preservation therefore belong inside the problem rather than outside it.

This internal conversation is retained as **design-intent provenance only**. It is not peer-reviewed evidence and cannot pay a scholarly claim.

## 2. Current paper centre

The paper is now organised around:

```text
Corpus A: mature digital-education research
        ↓ source-attributed cross-paper synthesis
seven candidate transferable principles
        ↓
pedagogy / institutional practice / professional development / policy
        ↓
Corpus B: independent sustainability/ESD constraints and evidence
        ↓
candidate transformative digital-ESD framework
        ↓
searched + screened corpus challenge and revision
```

This replaces the weaker framing in which the principal contribution was merely a reciprocal or twin transformation between digitality and sustainability.

## 3. Prior-art contraction

Two close antecedents are explicitly retained:

- **Chugh (2026)** pays the sustainability-paradox antecedent: digital education may expand educational/social opportunity while also creating environmental and social costs, motivating lifecycle/procurement/circularity responses.
- **Böhme (2026)** pays the coupled/twin-transformation antecedent: sustainability/ESD and digitality can be conceptualised as mutually dependent educational transformations.

Therefore the paper does **not** claim novelty for either bare proposition.

The candidate contribution is narrower: an evidence-governed architecture for translating a source-attributed digital-education corpus into candidate transformative principles while retaining enactment conditions, participant authority, sustainability constraints, same-object/context distinctions and non-skippable search/extraction lineage.

Global publication novelty remains unpaid until the declared search and screening are complete.

## 5. Seven candidate transferable principles

1. **Situated relational engagement** — meaningful interaction in context rather than access/presence alone.
2. **Feedback as revisable signal** — analytics/classification informs inquiry but is not student meaning or authority.
3. **Constitutive learner agency** — learners may shape questions, categories, interpretation, design and evidence return.
4. **Adaptive support with local choice** — support is routed through context and learner choice rather than universal intervention tables.
5. **Plural situated observers** — no learner/parent/teacher/institution/researcher/model projection is the whole educational system.
6. **Contextual custodianship** — person/place, affordances, effort and value flows remain part of educational interpretation.
7. **Iterative evidence return and recharting** — interventions/classifications remain contestable and revisable as evidence changes.

These are **candidate DASHI cross-paper synthesis**, not empirical findings attributed to any one source paper.

- institutional practice;
- professional development; and
- policy.

Professional development is therefore an enactment condition rather than an invented eighth principle.

The controlling separation is:

```text
pedagogical principle support
!= enactment/scaling capacity
!= sustainability evidence
```

## 7. Corpus B: sustainability constraints

Current constraints include:

- social equity and inclusion;
- epistemic agency, deliberative governance and public accountability;
- lifecycle, service life, repair, reuse and circularity;
- total cost of ownership and procurement;
- openness, interoperability, portability, migration and exit;
- institutional durability and monitoring; and
- intergenerational option preservation.

Primary/method/context sources include UNESCO, UNECE, OECD, the UNESCO-UNICEF-ITU Charter, ITU-T L.1410/L.1023, lifecycle/circularity studies, participatory ESD work, Chugh, Böhme, Holst, Martínez García et al., platformisation and edtech durability cases.

No B-side sustainability source is retroactively promoted into an Alice-source finding.

## 8. Transformation ladder

The paper currently requires the following states to remain distinct:

```text
technology presence
!= implementation activity
!= input integration
!= task performance
!= learning/outcomes
!= institutional durability
!= system transformation
```

UNESCO, UNECE, OECD, Holst and Martínez García et al. currently support different parts of this distinction while retaining their respective populations and source roles.

## 9. Candidate-principle derivation method

Six stages are source-written:

1. source-bound corpus extraction;
2. cross-paper relation synthesis;
3. candidate principle derivation;
4. scaling-condition attachment;
5. sustainability-constraint pairing; and
6. structured-corpus challenge and revision.

The methodological firewall is:

```text
candidate principle generation may precede search closure
```

but:

```text
pre-search candidate framework != final review result
```

and final principle promotion before search closure remains blocked.
